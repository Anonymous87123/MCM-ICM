1.1 Background

While bringing convenience to life, single-use or disposable plastic products also pose a severe threat to the environment, like 83 trillion tons of plastic products are generated in the world by the 1950s, resulting in 63 trillion tons single-use or disposable plastic product waste [1]. According to the statement from the U.N. Environment Programme (UNEP), 500 billion plastic bags are used worldwide every year, and at least 8 million tons of plastic product waste is injected into the ocean [2]. U.N. secretary-general Antonio Guterres claims that the amount of plastic product waste in the ocean will be much larger than the number of ﬁshes by 2050 [3] [4] [5]. Therefore, it is desirable to improve the way we mitigate single-use or disposable plastic product waste and develop a plan to signiﬁcantly reduce the amount of single-use or disposable plastic product waste in the world.
