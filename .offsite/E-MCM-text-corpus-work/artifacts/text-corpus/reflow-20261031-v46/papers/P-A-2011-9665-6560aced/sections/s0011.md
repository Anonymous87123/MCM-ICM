3.3.1 Assumptions and Notations

For the sake of convenience of the following discussions, we firstly assume that: (1) The “ vertical air “ for an specific athlete is a constant. (2) The snowboard and snowboarder are an entity and the internal action of them can be ignored. (3) The air resistance is ignored. (4) The coefficient of sliding friction of the half pipe surface is fixed.
