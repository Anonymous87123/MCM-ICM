A3. The solution of width of half-pipe

a=5*pi/180;rx=16.44;o=51.66*pi/180;smax=19.27;rx=16.44;

hx=6.241;u=0.1;m=75;g=9.8;vmax=23.31;wx=19.18;n=7;

AB=rx*o/cos(a);

CD=smax+2*AB;

vs=sqrt(2/m*(0.5*m*vmax^2-u*m*g*AB-m*g*hx));

vx=vs*cos(o);

vy=vs*sin(o);

tt=2*vy/g;

a0=CD*sin(a) +2*rx*o*sin(a) +vx*tt; % the solution for horizontal distance ax=a0*n
