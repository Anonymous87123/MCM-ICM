7.3 Immortality and unbounded growth

By the Second Law of Thermodynamics, entropy always increases. So as dragons keep growing by feasting on their preys, disorders appear as damages to cells. There is simply no escape to mortality. As for growth, animals appear to obey the 3 4 scaling law of metabolic rate change to body mass change [8]. The relation is sub-linear, which means, by an imaginary experiment, suppose a dragon quadruples its weight in its ﬁrst year of life, then the metabolic rate only increases by 3 4 ≈2.8 times. Therefore the energy needed for maintenance outpaces the increase of energy only 4 supply. Eventually, the amount of energy available for growth reduces to zero. Hence, unbounded growth doesn’t exist in real animals.
