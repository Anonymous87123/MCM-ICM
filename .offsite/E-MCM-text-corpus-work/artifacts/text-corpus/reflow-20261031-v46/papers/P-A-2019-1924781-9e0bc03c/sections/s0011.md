Now that there are two dragons left they are able to both grow with renewed speed, while also not draining the community they are in of its resources. Notice that the only population that is aﬀected by the dragons is the Barbary Sheep population. This is because the Barbary Sheep is two orders of magnitude more massive than all the other animals and has the smallest initial population out of any other animal. This means that once the dragon becomes larger he will be more likely to go after the Barbary sheep, and when he does, the eﬀect it will have on the population of the sheep will be greater. The two dragons now reach a new equilibrium and achieve a steady state with the community with a combined mass of 200,000 kg.
