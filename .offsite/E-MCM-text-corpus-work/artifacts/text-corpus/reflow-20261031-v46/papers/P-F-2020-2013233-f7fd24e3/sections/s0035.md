8.1 Strength

(1) In the refugee optimal flow model, consider the environmental responsibility of the receiving country. More importantly, we measure based on consumer-side carbon emission indicators to avoid unfair distribution caused by trade-off emissions. (2) Dividing culture into three types: income culture, loss culture and swing culture, which simplifies the problem of cultural protection that is difficult to quantify. (3) Taking into consideration the perspective of nationalism. (4) Consider the long-term impact caused by the policy, in order to improve the accuracy of the model, incorporate new influencing factors and further optimize the dynamic results.
