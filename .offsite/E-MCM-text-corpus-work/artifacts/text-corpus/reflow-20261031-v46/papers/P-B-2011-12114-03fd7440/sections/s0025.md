6.2.3 Additional Notes

This algorithm can also handle instances in which the search area surrounding the vari- ous potential repeaters is non-circular, although the various sequential optimizations (i.e. all those besides the parallelization) noted in 6.2.2 are eliminated, and the tight runtime is much more diﬃcult to calculate (though it can easily be shown to be no worse than O(s4 max(a) min(a) ) where a is the array of areas of the various regions covered by diﬀerent loca- tions).
