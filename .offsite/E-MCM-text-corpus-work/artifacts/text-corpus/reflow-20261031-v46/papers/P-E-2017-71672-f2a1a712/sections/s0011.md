4.3 Comparing Initiatives

Increasing public transportation (C) and decreasing the number of vacant properties (D) both improve the smart growth of Boulder with the given metric. We rank our initiatives for Canberra from best to worst in the order E, D, A, C, B. Better, more accessible community engagement (E) is key to smart growth success in Canberra, as is improving the public transit system (D). Building new developments on open space (B) is, ultimately, slightly detrimental to the city’s smart growth success level because it involves new development on open space, but we feel it is unrealistic for this not to occur in Canberra.
