4.1 KPIs for Three Components

To meet the need of ICM Corporation, we deﬁne KPIs respectively for three components to evaluate their success. It is widely acknowledged that KPIs need to be “SMART”, that is, speciﬁc, measurable, attainable, relevant and time-bound. We attach great importance to measurablity of our KPIs, while avoiding overlooking other standards. 12 KPIs, 3 for people, 4 for technology and 5 for process in total are deﬁned.

Now we make some justiﬁcations for our KPIs. In order to satisfy the need of measurablity, all KPIS are deﬁned by either formulas or ratios. Meanwhile, all KPIs are set to be nonnegative; if the calculation shows some KPI is negative, then it is treated as zero. Additionally, all KPIs are normalizes and set to be of beneﬁt-type, that is, the closer to 1, the better.
