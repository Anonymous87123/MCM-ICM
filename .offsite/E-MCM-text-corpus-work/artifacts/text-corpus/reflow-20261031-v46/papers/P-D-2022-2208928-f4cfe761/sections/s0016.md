4.3.3 Intergrated weight calculation

We respectively calculate two sets of weight vector by seperately using AHP and EWM. Now we take both into consideration, and obtain ﬁnal weights W𝑖, 𝑖= 1, 2, 3 as:

W𝑖= 𝜔WAi + (1 −𝜔)WEi. (20)

The 𝜔here stands for the coeﬃcient of two methods, representing the importance of each. Since our KPIs are all measurable, we attach greater signiﬁcance to EWM, since EWM is more objective than AHP. We set 𝜔= 0.2, and the ﬁnal weights for each components are shown in Figure 2.
