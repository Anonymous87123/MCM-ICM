3.1.3.1 The Sea Surface Simulation By P-M Spectrum

The turbulent ocean can be regarded as the combination of infinite harmonic waves that have different amplitudes, frequencies, directions and phases, the contribution of the harmonic waves consist of the sea spectrum. The sea spectrum is the statistical property of a random process, contains each harmonic component about the distributions of frequency and directions.

There are a lot of previous researches about sea spectrum, among which Pierson-Moscowtc spectrum, JONSWAP spectrum and Elfouhaily spectrum were widely used. Now we are using the first one as our ocean reflection surface.
