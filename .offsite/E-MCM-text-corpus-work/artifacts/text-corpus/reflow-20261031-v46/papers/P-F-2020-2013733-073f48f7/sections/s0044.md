4.3 Impact on Economy, with Specific-Factor Model

4.3.1 Basic idea

关注数学模型 A large amount of EDPs flow in will absolutely shock the country who receive them. In the market of employment, EDPs may enter nation’s industry and make their influence. Specific-Factor model is adopted to describe the impact on the EDPs-import country, under the assumption that EDPs have the opportunity to find a job.

4.3.2 Assumption

⚫ The quality of EDPs labor is the same with of native labor ⚫ There are two industries in a country, high-end industry and low-end industry.

EDPs have both high-skill and low-skill, so they can choose both industries. ⚫ Each industry has two production factors: labor(L) and capital(K), and the output is a Cobb-Douglas production function. In high-end industry, more capital is needed for the increasing labor so as to maximize the output.
