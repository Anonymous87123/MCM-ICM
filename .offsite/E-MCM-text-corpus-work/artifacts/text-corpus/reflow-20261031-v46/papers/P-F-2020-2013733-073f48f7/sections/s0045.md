4.3.1 Basic idea

关注数学模型 A large amount of EDPs flow in will absolutely shock the country who receive them. In the market of employment, EDPs may enter nation’s industry and make their influence. Specific-Factor model is adopted to describe the impact on the EDPs-import country, under the assumption that EDPs have the opportunity to find a job.
