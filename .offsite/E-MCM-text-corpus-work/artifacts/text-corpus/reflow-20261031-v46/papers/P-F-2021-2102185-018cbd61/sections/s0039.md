7 Sensitivity analysis

We conducted a sensitivity analysis on the ﬁve indicators. The ﬁgures of each indicator were changed by 5% and 10% and we obtain a new score for each indicator. This process illustrated a linear relationship between the scores and ﬁgures of indicators. And the total score for a higher education system also response to the ﬁve scores linearly.
