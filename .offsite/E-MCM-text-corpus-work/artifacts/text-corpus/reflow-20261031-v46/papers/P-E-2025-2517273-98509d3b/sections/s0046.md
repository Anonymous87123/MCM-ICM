4.4.4 Cost-Benefit Distribution Analysis

The stacked bar chart reveals the complex financial structure of each farming system. Regenerative agriculture shows the highest total positive value (approximately 580 USD/ha), with significant contributions from ecosystem service benefits (orange) and premium pricing (red). While it incurs the highest initial investment and certification costs (purple and gray), these are offset by superior revenue streams. Organic farming occupies a middle ground with moderate costs and benefits (total around 440 USD/ha). Conventional farming demonstrates the lowest total value (around 280 USD/ha), primarily due to minimal ecosystem service benefits and absence of premium pricing, despite having lower operational costs. This analysis illustrates how the higher costs associated with regenerative practices are justified by enhanced revenue streams and ecosystem service benefits.
