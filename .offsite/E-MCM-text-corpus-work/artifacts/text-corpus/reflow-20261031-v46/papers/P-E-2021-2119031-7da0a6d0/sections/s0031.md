7.1 Sustainability Indicator

7.1.1 Indicator Selection

Sustainable development, as Mrs. Brent famously said, it is "development that not only satisfies the needs of contemporary people, but does not endanger the ability of future genera- tions to meet their needs." It generally includes economic sustainability, ecological sustaina- bility and social sustainability [4][5]. For the food system, it is more embodied in ecological sustainability, that is, to measure some of the environmental impacts caused by the food system.
