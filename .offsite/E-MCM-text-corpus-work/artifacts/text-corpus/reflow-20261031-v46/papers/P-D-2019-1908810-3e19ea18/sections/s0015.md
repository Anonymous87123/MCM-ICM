1 Introduction

The Louvre is a famous art museum that houses many incredible artworks, including the Mona Lisa. As a result, the Louvre attracts many tourists and enthusiasts all throughout the year. In an event of an emergency, it is crucial that people are able to evacuate eﬀectively while also allowing emergency personnel to get to a target location. In this paper, we model the Louvre as a network and analyze how evacuation occurs in diﬀerent scenarios with multiple models. We identify bottlenecks, potential threats, and possible solutions – which we use to propose a policy and make recommendations to the Louvre’s emergency management team.
