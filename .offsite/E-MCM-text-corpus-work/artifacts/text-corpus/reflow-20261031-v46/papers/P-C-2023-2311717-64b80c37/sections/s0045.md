10.1 Strengths

⚫ We decompose the time series into trend signs and stochastic fluctuations and then make separate predictions. The model built according to this has a good interpretation of the time series patterns. ⚫ We do not define the difficulty of words by ourselves, but generalize the difficulty based on the results of user play. Therefore, the difficulty rating we obtained is more relevant to the user's play experience. ⚫ We applied the integration algorithm to weaken the generalization error caused by chance due to neural network training. Eventually, better prediction accuracy than individual neu- ral networks is achieved.
