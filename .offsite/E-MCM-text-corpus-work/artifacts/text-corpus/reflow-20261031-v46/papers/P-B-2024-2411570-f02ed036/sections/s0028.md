3.4 Water Resistance Modeling

We use Stokes’ formula from fluid dynamics to describe the drag force on a submersible in water:

𝐹= −𝑘𝑣 (7)

where 𝑘is fluid resistance coefficient; 𝑣is the flow velocity of the submersible concerning the fluid, then 𝑣= 𝑣−𝑣𝑐, which can be obtained by substituting into eq.2:

𝐹= −𝑘(𝑣−𝑣𝑐) = −𝑘+ 𝑘𝑣𝑐 (8)
