This means that 52.8 kWh are needed for a full charge, or .44kWh per mile of travel. This translates to 1501.3 BTU needed a mile in an electric vehicle. The average pas- senger car can travel 31 miles on a gallon of gasoline. Since a gallon of gasoline contains roughly 116,090 BTU, the average gas car runs on 3744.84 BTU per mile [1]. This is more than twice as much energy required by electric cars, so switching to electric cars decreases the amount of energy required worldwide for transportation purposes, but also requires a switch from the 100% gasoline power source for gas cars, to the medley of power sources used for electricity.
