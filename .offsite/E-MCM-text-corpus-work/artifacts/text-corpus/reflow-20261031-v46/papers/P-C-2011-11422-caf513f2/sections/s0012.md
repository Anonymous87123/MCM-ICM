6.2.1 Cellular automata

Initial seeding is important as there is no point to seeding more cars if fewer cars will get you to your goal percentage of electric cars on the road by a certain year. By using both the global and the local model, we determined that the ﬁnal percentage of cars that are electric, for a given number of seeding, is less in the local model than in the global model, meaning that these local interactions seem to slow down the distribution of cars.
