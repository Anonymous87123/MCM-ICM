6.2 Weaknesses

• Roughly Decision in Fitting. For log(Hyphal Extension Rate)-log(Decomposition Rate) curve, from original data, we can only get the regression information of T = 10 ◦C, T = 16 ◦C, and T = 22 ◦C. We choose to use the linear interpolation of horizontal intercept for each temperature since decoration rate may be positively associated with temperature, but indeed this can be determined more meticulously, like parabolic in- terpolation or spline interpolation.
