7 Organic Agriculture Impacts Evaluation (OAIE) Model

The adoption of environmentally sustainable practices is an inevitable consequence of the pur- suit of sustainable development. In this section, a comprehensive fuzzy integrated evaluation sys- tem of the impacts of organic agriculture is established. This system is based on real data collected on organic and conventional agriculture, selecting multi-level indicators. Building upon the find- ings of prior studies, this analysis will assess the broader ecosystem impacts of organic agriculture.

7.1 Indicator Identification and Data Collection

A comprehensive data set was meticulously assembled, encompassing conventional agriculture prior to 2010 and organic agriculture following 2020. This endeavor involved a synthesis of in- formation from the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) database and relevant specialized literature. The data were categorized into four major aspects: ecosystem, crop growth, environmental protection, and cost-effectiveness. And ten secondary indicators were selected below in Figure 13.
