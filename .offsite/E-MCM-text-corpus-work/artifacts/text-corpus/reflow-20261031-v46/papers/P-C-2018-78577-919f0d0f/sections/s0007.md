1.1 Background

Energy is an important part of people’s daily life, and the cornerstone of the development of society. The formation of an industrial society brought fossil fuels into our lives. However, with the development of society, people have been gradually aware of the shortcomings of fossil fuels: high pollution and limited quantity. People began to seek to create a clean, renewable energystructure.

Along the U.S. border with Mexico, four states hope to form a realistic energy contract that focuses on cleaner, renewable energy. We are asked to analyze the composition of the energy structure of these states, and predict the trend of their development over the next 50 years. We will finally put forward some feasible suggestions for the compact.
