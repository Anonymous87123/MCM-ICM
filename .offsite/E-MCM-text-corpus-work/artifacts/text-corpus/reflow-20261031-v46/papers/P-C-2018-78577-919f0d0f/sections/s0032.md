5.1 Model of ARIMA

ARIMA model is a widely used time series analysis method, which first put forward by Box and Jenkins in 1970.[5] The basic approaches of ARIMA are:

• Firstly, smooth the original time series with difference method;

Secondly, determine the type of model, the order of model and undeter- • mined parameters by analyzing the characteristics of ACF (Autocorrela- tion Function) and PACF (Partial Autocorrelation Function) of stationary sequence;

• Thirdly, test the validity of the model;

• Finally, analyze and predict the future time series.

ARIMA model can be known as ARIMA(p, q, d), where p is the autoregres- sive order number and q is the sliding average order number. This model repre- sents making differences on the non-stationary random sequence variable Yt for d times, and then we obtain the stationary series Xt.
