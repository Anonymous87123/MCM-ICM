6.2.1 Establish a Fund

The first action is to establish a fund. The fund will be used to invest com- panies to build facilities relative to wind or solar energy. To achieve goals in compact, the four states have to contribute a certain amount of money to the fund.

Generally, the cooperation of the four states can create some extra profit. So this approach can be understood as a problem of the distribution of profit in a cooperation. We use the Shapley Value Regression Model to determine the opti- mized share of the funding from each state.
