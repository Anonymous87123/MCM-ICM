Therefore, developing countries should pay more attention to economic development and eﬃciency in constructing food systems, and are not sensitive to the optimization of equity and sustainability. Excessive consideration of equity and sustainability will aﬀect or even limit the development of their economies. For developed countries, because their level of economic development has reached a certain level, the pursuit of interests and eﬃciency will not have much impact on the stability of their food system, and considering fairness and sustainability is more conducive to improving the food system.
