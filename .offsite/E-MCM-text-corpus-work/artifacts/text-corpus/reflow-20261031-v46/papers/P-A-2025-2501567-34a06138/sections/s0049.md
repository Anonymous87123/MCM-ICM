4.9.1 Chaboche Model

In structural engineering, fatigue damage is a critical consideration, especially when components are subjected to repetitive loading. For staircases, two common types of loading scenarios are short-duration high-frequency stepping and long-duration low- frequency stepping, each of which affects the structure in distinct ways [11]. The former causes rapid, high-magnitude impacts that may lead to sudden localized damage, while the latter involves prolonged, lower-magnitude stresses that accumulate over time.
