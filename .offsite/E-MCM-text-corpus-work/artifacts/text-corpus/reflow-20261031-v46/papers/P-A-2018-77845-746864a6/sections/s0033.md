5 Moving-Ship-Receiving-Signal Model

In order to ensure that the ship can continue to receive signals at sea, we assume that radio waves are not a single frequency at launch, but have a certain bandwidth. For example, the frequency of the emitted waves consists of all frequencies between 21M Hz and 21.45M Hz.

Because the frequency of electromagnetic waves in a given frequency range is con- tinuous, and electromagnetic waves with different frequencies will have different prop- agation path, the intersection of electromagnetic waves and the sea is no longer a point, but becomes a range.
