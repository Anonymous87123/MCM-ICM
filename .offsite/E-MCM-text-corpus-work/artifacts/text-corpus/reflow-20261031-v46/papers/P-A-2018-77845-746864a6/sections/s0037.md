7.2 The Height of The F Layer

We analyze the propagation of the multi-hop HP radio waves with the height of the F layer is 289.8m, or 339.3m, or 418.6m. As shown in Figure 16, as the altitude of the F-layer ionosphere increases, radio waves can travel higher and the maximum propagation distance is increased. However, the maximum number of hops does not change, and the propagation paths looks similar in geometry.
