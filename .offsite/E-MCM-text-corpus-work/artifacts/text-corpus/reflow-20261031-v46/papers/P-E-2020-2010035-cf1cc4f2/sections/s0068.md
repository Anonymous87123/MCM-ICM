2. The Target

• Target Goals Based on prediction of IPWH, we set two achievable targets.

Target Goal 1: decrease of annual plastic waste generated by single-use or disposal plastics; Target Goal 2: decrease of annual harm done by plastic waste to the environment.

• Time-Lines Based on IPWH, Target 1 can be achieved by year 2125 and Tar- get 2 by year 2131.
