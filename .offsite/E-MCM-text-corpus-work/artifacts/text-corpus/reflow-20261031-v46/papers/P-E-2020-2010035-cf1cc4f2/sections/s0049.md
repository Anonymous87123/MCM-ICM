8.2 Equity Issues

We calculate the score of all countries and regions considered by using the afore- mentioned metric. We made Figure 13 to analyze the regional distribution of plastic waste level.

The distribution is geographically uneven. On a continental level, the most se- vere levels of plastic waste crisis appear principally in South-East Asia and Mid-East Africa. An overall better situation is observed in North America and Southern and Northern Europe.

The distribution is polarized. As we can tell from Figure 12 and Table 5, countries are somewhat concentrated at two poles, and relatively few countries are categorized at the “Moderate” level. This may be due to the disparate economic levels worldwide that leads to the imbalanced measures and awareness to deal with the plastic waste crisis.
