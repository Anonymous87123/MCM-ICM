5.2.2 Score of Protection Measures

 Low: For low-grade 𝑀𝑠𝑐𝑜𝑟𝑒buildings, basic conservation measures, such as routine maintenance and inspections and, where necessary, minor repairs, are undertaken. The risk or value of these buildings is low, so the measures taken are mainly preventive and low-cost.

 Medium: For medium-grade 𝑀𝑠𝑐𝑜𝑟𝑒, moderate protection measures are implemented, in- cluding enhanced structural inspections, improved safety features, and disaster preparedness programs. These measures aim to increase the resistance and resilience of buildings and require moderate investment.

 High: For high-grade 𝑀𝑠𝑐𝑜𝑟𝑒, implement comprehensive and high-intensity protection measures. This may include comprehensive structural reinforcement, installation of advanced security systems, and customized risk management plans in cooperation with external experts. Given the high risk or value of these buildings, the goal of the measures is to minimize potential losses, even if this means higher initial costs.

Note that: The "one-size-fits-all" approach to disaster protection measures ignores the im- pact of regional differences, architectural characteristics and socio-economic factors, and can lead to poor protection and resource utilization. So specific protection measures still need to be derived from a thoughtful local analysis by natural disaster experts.
