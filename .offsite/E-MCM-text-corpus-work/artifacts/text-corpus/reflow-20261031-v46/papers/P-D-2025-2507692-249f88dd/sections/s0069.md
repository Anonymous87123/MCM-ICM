2. Multi-Model Synergy: Builds multiple models to tackle Baltimore’s transportation issues from

diverse angles. The Adaptive Transportation Network Model simulates bridge-collapse impacts;

the Upgraded Bus Network Model optimizes the bus system; the Integrated Multimodal Transport Model integrates bus and rail networks; the Pearson - based model aids in strategy optimization.

This multi-model cooperation comprehensively boosts the transportation system’s analysis and improvement capabilities.
