1. Introduction

Ecological services are the various kinds of beneﬁts humans receive from the natu- ral environment and a properly-functioning ecosystem, such as the puriﬁcation of air and water; nutrient cycling; resources like timber, fuel and ﬁber. However, natural ecosystems worldwide are under enormous pressure from human activities, especially from many land use projects. Therefore, it is important to remind people that the loss of these services will leave us worse oﬀ.(IUCN, 2005)
