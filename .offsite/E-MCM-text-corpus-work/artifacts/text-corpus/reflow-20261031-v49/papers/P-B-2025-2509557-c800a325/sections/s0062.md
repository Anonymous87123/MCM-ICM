5.5 Tax Revenue Model

Total tax revenue is modeled as:

T = τx + fotherx |{z} | {z } Tax Fixed revenue fees

(5)

where τx represents tax revenue, with τ being the tax rate and x the taxable base, while fotherx represents fixed fees proportionally linked to x. By distinguishing between tax-based income and additional fees, this equation clarifies how revenue is generated for reinvestment in the system.
