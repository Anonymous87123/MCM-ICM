7.2 Weaknesses

• No Verification of Raw Data: We have no guarantee of the accuracy of given data [5].

No Involvement of Other States: We do not consider states other than CA, AZ, NM, • and TX, due to the lack of relevant data.

No Consideration of State Self-Interest: We set our targets based on maximizing total • renewable energy consumption, but in fact each state may care more about its own development while regardless of others, thus not following our mutualtargets.
