4.3.1 Strategies Implemented by the Community

We solved for these three parameter values for extreme events in North America using scipy and present the results in Figure 5. We observed that in the face of extreme weather conditions, these three metrics exhibit a positive correlation. The greater the potential loss that may arise from risks, the higher the cost should be for the community’s investment in protective measures.
