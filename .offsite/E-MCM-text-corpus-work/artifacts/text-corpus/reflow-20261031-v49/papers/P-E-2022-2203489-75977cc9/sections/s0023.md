5.2.1 Intermediate cuttings intensity(𝑰𝑰𝑰𝑰𝑰𝑰𝑰𝑰)

Intermediate cuttings refers to regularly cutting down some trees in immature forests to preserve the good environmental conditions of the forest and promote tree growth and devel- opment. At the same time, part of the wood can be obtained to expand carbon sequestration and create more economic benefits. The intensity of intermediate cuttings will directly deter- mine the number of retained trees and the degree of improvement in forest environmental con- ditions. Excessive or low harvesting intensity, will be bed for trees, either.
