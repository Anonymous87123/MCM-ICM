4.2 NetLogo Model

Since the NetLogo code is based around the ability for the agents to make autonomous decisions, many procedures needed to be developed that would govern the motions of the cars. To give a better representation of the procedures follow and the way in which NetLogo functions, a pseudo-code ﬂow chart is provided below. Notably, NetLogo works best with two diﬀerent procedures working in tandem.
