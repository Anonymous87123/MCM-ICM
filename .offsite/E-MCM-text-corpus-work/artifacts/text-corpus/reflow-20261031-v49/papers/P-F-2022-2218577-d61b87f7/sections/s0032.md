2. Regional Equity

The level of disparity between some regions is relatively large. From Figure 4, we can ﬁnd that the countries in East Asia, Northern Europe and North America have higher scores, and most of them are developed countries or developing countries with better development trend, and they have great impact on the promotion of global economic, social, technological and energy equity. In contrast, Southwest Asia and Africa are mostly developing countries due to their backward economic and environmental conditions, and their equity scores are low.
