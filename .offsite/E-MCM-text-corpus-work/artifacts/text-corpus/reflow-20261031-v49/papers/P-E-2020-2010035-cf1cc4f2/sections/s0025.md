4.3 Harm Model

This section describes the process of waste management when plastics become wastes (expire their usage life-time). Currently, there exists several ways of disposal: incineration, recycling, landﬁll, discarded into rivers and oceans. Among processed wastes, those who pose overwhelming threat to the environment are mismanaged wastes.

Mismanaged waste is material which is at high risk of entering the ocean via wind or tidal transport, or carried to coastlines from inland waterways. Mismanaged waste is the sum of material which is either littered or inadequately disposed. [1]

In our model, it is equal to wastes that are neither recycled nor incinerated. Thus we deﬁne the annual “Harm” done to the environment H as the total amount of mis- managed plastic wastes,
