7.2.2 Impact on Traditional Plastic Industry

Firstly, due to the appearance of plastics’ alternatives, the growth rate of production APo(t) and APs(t) are slowed down, especially the latter.

Secondly, the increase of recycle rate ro(t) plays a role to decrease the manufacture cost of plastic products. Because the recycled plastic are used in next year’s production.
