5.2.1 Introducing the Parameters

Part I : Parameters of the forest itself

In order to calculate the amount of carbon sequestered by the forest itself per hectare per year, we introduced the following parameters.

CABi t: Carbon in above-ground biomass in the i th trees per hectare in year t, t/ha.

CBBi t : Carbon in below-ground biomass in the i th trees per hectare in year t, t/ha.

CDW i t : Carbon in dead wood in the i th trees per hectare in year t, t/ha.

CLi t : Carbon in litter in the i th trees per hectare in year t, t/ha.

SCi t : Soil carbon in the i th trees per hectare in year t, t/ha.

By summing the above parameters, the following equation can be obtained.

TFCSi t = CABi t + CBBi t + CDW i t + CLi t + SCi (1) t

TFCSi t ,whose unit is t/ha represents the total carbon sequestration per hectare of the i th trees in yeart.
