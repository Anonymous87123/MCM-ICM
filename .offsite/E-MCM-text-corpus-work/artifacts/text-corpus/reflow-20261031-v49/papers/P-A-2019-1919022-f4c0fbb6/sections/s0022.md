3.1.2 Action and Rest

Animals will adapt their metabolic rates q to different motion states, so will the dragons do. In Equation (1), the adaption is reﬂected in the characteristic constant q0. The motion state of a dragon can be divided into two types: rest state and action state. The rest state includes sleeping and relaxing while the action state includes ﬂying, preying and so on. Dragons consume much more energies in the action state than in the rest state.

By this classiﬁcation, we calculate the characteristic constant q0r for the rest state and q0a for the action state. According to the Assumption. 1, dragons’ motion mechanism is similar to birds and bats, so we use the metabolic rate of different kinds of birds and bats to ﬁt the q0.
