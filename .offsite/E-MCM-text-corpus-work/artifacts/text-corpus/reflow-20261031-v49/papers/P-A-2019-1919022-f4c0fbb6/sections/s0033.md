4.4 Human being’ Restoration to the Ecosystem

The introduction of dragons may cause the destruction to the existing ecosystem, so it is necessary for human beings to help reconstruct the environment. In this part, we quanta- tively calculate the impact factory µ of human’s conservation.

Human beings reconstruct the environment by repairing the habitat that dragons have destroyed. However, human beings cannot completely repair the destruction the dragons make. We set human beings’ impact factor as µ.
