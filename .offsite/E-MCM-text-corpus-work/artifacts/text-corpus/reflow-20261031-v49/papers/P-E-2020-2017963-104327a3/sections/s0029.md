5.3.1 Laws and regulations

In 2019, at the Congress of the Basel Convention, 187 countries around the world revised the convention to include plastic waste as an import and export restriction object, deciding to include plastic waste pollution as one of the globally recognized environmental problems[21]. In March 2007, San Francisco became the ﬁrst city in the U.S. to ban the use of non-biodegradable plastic bags, encouraging using recyclable and biodegradable plastic bags [22]. After that, although global countries started to implement ‘plastic limit orders’, proposing to restrict and ban the use of disposable plastic products, there were diﬀerences in the implementation eﬀect between countries. Therefore, as a global target, governments should revise laws and regulations, extend the ‘plastic limit orders’ to all industries.
