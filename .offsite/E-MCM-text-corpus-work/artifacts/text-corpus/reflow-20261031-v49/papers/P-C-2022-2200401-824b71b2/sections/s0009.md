1.4 Our work && model overview

We proposed an APGM(Adaptive Periodic Grid Model) to decide when and how to change our posi- tions in the Gold and Bitcoin markets. This model uses ARIMA predictions and AM to shift the grid. This approach enables grid model to make decisions when market price change immediately. We also design a backtesting process to adaptively adjust the parameters of our APGM. At the end, we write a memorandum to communicate your strategy, model, and results to the trader.
