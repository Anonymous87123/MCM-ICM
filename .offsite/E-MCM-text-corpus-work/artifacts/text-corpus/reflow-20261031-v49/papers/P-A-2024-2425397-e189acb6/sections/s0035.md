7.1 Add Parasite Populations

When we study the parasites of lampreys’ populations, more than 80% of the reports (includ- ing life stages) originate from lampreys’ mature bodies, and only 11% of the reports originate from lampreys’ larvae. This may indicate that parasites of lampreys’ populations are predominantly para- sitized by lampreys’ mature bodies [7].
