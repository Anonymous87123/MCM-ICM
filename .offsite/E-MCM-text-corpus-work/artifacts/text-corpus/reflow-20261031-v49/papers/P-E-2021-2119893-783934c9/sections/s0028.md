Both processes should take into account speciﬁc regional diﬀerences and diﬀerent stakeholder perspectives, while scaling should also consider generating systematic eﬀects when implemented in a wider range.

6.2 Discussion of Adaptation of Our Model

Many of our indicators are selected based on the national level, and these indicators may be meaningless or diﬃcult to obtain data for some small regions or large areas.

Developing and developed countries have diﬀerent priorities in their food systems.
