Weaknesses

• When changing the area of the model application, the inﬂuence of each indicator on the food system will change accordingly, and the neglect of this change in the model makes the result produce some deviation.

• In the model, we consider the impact of emergencies on the food system, but when a country or region produces serious emergencies such as war, the model is not applicable.
