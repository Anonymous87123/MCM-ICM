1.2 Restatement of the Problem

Our team will measure the impact of digital currencies on the legal tender and financial markets at the individual, national and global level. We will also include the motivation of nations to limit or encourage the development of digital currencies in our study. Last but not the least, we will explore the regulatory objectives and means of this free-flowing digital currency at the global level, as well as the monetary behavior game among countries.
