7.2 Weaknesses

1. The base equivalent has a certain timeliness. The basis value is judged by the willingness to pay the questionnaire survey, and the resulting value equivalent table can evaluate the ecological value in a short period of time.

2. There is a problem of double counting in value. For example, there is a certain cross-value between biodiversity and aesthetic landscape, culture and education, which makes the ﬁnal evaluation value large.

3. Part of the value is difﬁcult to quantify or the accuracy may not be high after quantiﬁcation. For example, the value of genetic inheritance, human disease regulation and other functions is difﬁcult to quantify, which will lead to bias in the ﬁnal evaluation results.
