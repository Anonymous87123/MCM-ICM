6.5 Evaluating the Model

One strength of this model is the fact that it is successfully able to model a potential number of people that would be able to sustain the dragons. It also is very optimized in its consideration for the least amount of resources necessary. However, at the same time, this model is also have several weaknesses. Implementing such a system where cows would be raised for the sole purpose of feeding dragons would likely spark many ethical concerns and anger among the global population. Additionally, this model’s sensitivity to variation in assumptions and averages makes it potentially diﬃcult if it were to be applied directly to the real world. It is also likely that it may be diﬃcult to realistically secure this amount of land and cows in one area. Thus, one potential extension of this model would be to account for transportation time and manpower, allowing cows to be exported from across the world. This would allow several of the assumptions made in the model to be more realistic.
