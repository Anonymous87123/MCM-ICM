5.2.1 Principal component analysis dimensionality reduction

By searching the VERIS community database, we found that there are 136 indica- tors related to cybercrime attacking countries and 825 indicators related to cybercrime victim countries. Considering that when the number of features is very large, mod- el training may become complicated and the computational cost is very high, and in 微信公众号：数学模型 order to remove redundant information, principal component analysis is performed on the indicator data to reduce the dimensionality, and it is ensured that the selected principal components can explain at least 90% of the variability in the original data, thereby reducing the attacking country indicator to 8 dimensions and the victim coun- try indicator to 12 dimensions.
