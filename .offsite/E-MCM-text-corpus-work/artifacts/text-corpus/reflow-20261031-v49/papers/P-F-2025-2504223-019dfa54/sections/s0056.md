6.4 Hypothesis Testing

The most important assumption in the Cox model is the proportional hazard as- sumption, that is, the effect of covariates on survival time does not change over time. Graphical tests (observing whether the survival curves are parallel) show that the sur- vival probability of the "Rejection" group is higher than that of the "Adoption" group, with the former having a longer median survival time of about 14 years, while the latter has a median survival time of about 14 years. In addition, the Log-rank test (p = 0.0013 < 0.05) shows that there is a significant difference in the survival probability between the two groups.
