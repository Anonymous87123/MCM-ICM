Ecological Recovery Phase Model

 During the recovery phase, we expand the model by introducing crop dynamics. The model emphasizes interspecific competition between ryegrass and crops through modified Lotka-Volterra competition terms, capturing resource partitioning and competitive exclusion. The Holling Type III functional response for aphid-ladybug interactions is retained to ensure continuity in biological control mechanisms. Additionally, the model incorporates the long-term ecological memory of chemical interventions, simulating the persistence and degrada.
