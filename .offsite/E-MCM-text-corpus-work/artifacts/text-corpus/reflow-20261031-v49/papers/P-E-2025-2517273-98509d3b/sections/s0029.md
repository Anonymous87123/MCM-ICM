3.3.1.6 Natural enemy diversity

dN N dt = rnN 1 − Kn + γNBNB −δNN −βNPCpN (32)

The natural enemy population dynamics (dN/dt) follow a logistic growth pattern modified by interactions with beneficial insects (γNB). The equation incorporates density-dependent mortality (δN) and the effects of chemical controls (βNP), representing a sophisticated approach to biological control modeling within the agricultural system.
