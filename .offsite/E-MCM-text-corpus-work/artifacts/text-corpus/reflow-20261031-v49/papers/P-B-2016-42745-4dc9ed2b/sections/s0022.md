Three key distinctions are listed to help better construct the model:

Initial cost and following cost. The land-based approach has a relatively high initial • fixed cost for the construction of utilities; the expense of each strike, yet, is relatively low. The laser broom, for instance, has a vast installation cost [9], whereas the energy used for each strike of space debris is minute. Inversely, the space-based approach has a more even cost allocation in regarding to time series, since every time a space rocket is employed to send the space debris catcher into working range.
