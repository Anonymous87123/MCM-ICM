• Human Health Aspect Analysis Through Figure 9(a) we can visualize that the level of human health security in these four regions is not very different, and our analysis suggests that medical security is good in cities, and the impact of light pollution on human health in cities can be compensated by technological leadership in medical care.

• Social Aspect Analysis The low light in protected areas and rural areas subliminally affects crime rates, and the high crime rate leads to a much lower level of social safety in these two areas than in urban communities, consistent with the fact that low light levels in communities may lead to increased crime. The positive contribution of light is also affirmed.

• Ecological Aspect Analysis The ecological impact of light pollution is the most significant, with significant dis- parities among the four regions. High population density, nighttime activities in commercial areas, and human activities lead to high artificial light brightness in ur- ban and suburban areas and serious ecological damage.
