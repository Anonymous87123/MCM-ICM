6.3.1 The Management Strategies without Consider of Territorial Sea

For the solution result of question 2, the result marked with migration direction is shown in Figure 11, It can be seen that in the next 50 years, the temperature of ocean water will gradually increase, which led to the migration of ﬁsh to the north, and the density of ﬁsh in the offshore area will gradually decrease, which led to the gradual proﬁts decline of small ﬁshing companies. Referring to the migration direction of ﬁsh, transfer the initial position of the port to Iceland, and repeat the simulation process of section 6.2.2 with other conditions unchanged. The change trend of annual proﬁt of ﬁshing companies over time is shown in Figure 12,

As the location of the new port is closer to the migration direction of the ﬁsh in the future, the proﬁts of ﬁshing companies will gradually increase and eventually stabilize with the migration of the ﬁsh.
