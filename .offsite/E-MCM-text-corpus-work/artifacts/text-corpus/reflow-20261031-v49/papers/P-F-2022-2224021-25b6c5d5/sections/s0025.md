4.2.5 Health & Education Equity

1) CV of the Completion Rate for Compulsory Education (CVCRCE) We use CV of the completion rate of compulsory education around the world to measure the status quo of countries in compulsory education. It measures education equity.

2) CV of Infant Mortality (CVIM) We measure health equity by CV of newborn infant mortality around the world to measures the current state of newborn health in each country.

3) CV of Access to Healthcare Resources (CVAHR) We measure the accessibility of medical and health resources around the world. We collect the number of health technicians per 1,000 people, the number of beds in medical institutions per 1,000 people, and the number of hospitals per 10,000 people. We calculate these three indicators separately. Then we get the average for each country. We get CVAHR by calculating CV of all averages.
