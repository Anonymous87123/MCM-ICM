4.2.2 Economic Equity

1) Gini Coeﬃcient of Wealth per Capita (GIW) Gini index measures the extent to which the distribution of income among individuals or households within an economy deviates from a perfectly equal distribution. We regard the world as a whole and get its Gini index.

2) Theil Index of GDP per Capita (TIGDP) Theil index is a special case of the generalized entropy index (GE). It is a measure of inequity. We use GDP of all countries to calculate Theil index.
