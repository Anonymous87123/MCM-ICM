4.1.5 Internationalization index

INT = INB (5) UNI Where INT is Internationalization index, UNI is the number of universities in a country, and 微信公众号：数学模型 INB represents the number of inbound international students in a country.

The Internationalization index of a country is quantiﬁed by the ratio of total number of foreign students to the total number of universities. Although there are diﬀerences in inter- national enrollment policies in various countries, the health of higher education is still the primary attraction to most foreign students when they are choosing which country to study in. Therefore, the Internationalization index reﬂects the international recognition of the nation’s higher education system.
