4 Stability and Sensitivity Analysis

In the reflection model we established in 3.1, the changes of height, shape of sea surface are mainly caused by wind speed. What’s more, in different sea areas, the temperature and salinity of seawater couldn't be the same, which could change εr and σ of seawater. So, we mainly analyze the influence of wind speed, εr and σ to the stability and sensitivity of our model.
