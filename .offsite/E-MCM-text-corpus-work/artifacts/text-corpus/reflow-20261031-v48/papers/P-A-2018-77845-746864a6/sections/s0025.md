3.5 Free-Space Path Loss

We assume that the energy loss of electromagnetic waves caused by the influence of free-space path loss is always exists as long as the electromagnetic wave is spreading. According to [4], the free-space path loss can be described as:

F SP L(dB) = 20log10(d) + 20log10(f) + 32:45 (13)

where d id the propagation distance (km) and f is the frequency (M Hz).
