4.1 Light Pollution Composition and Impact

Light pollution is a large and complex concept, the consensus of most studies is that the source of pollution is divided into natural light pollution and artificial light pollution, according the composition of pollution is divided into white bright pollution, daytime pollution, colored light pollution, according to the common form of pollution can be di- vided into glare, spill light, and light trespass...
