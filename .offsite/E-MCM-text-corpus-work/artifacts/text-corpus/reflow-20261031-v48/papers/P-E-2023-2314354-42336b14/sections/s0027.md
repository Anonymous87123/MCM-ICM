The above bar chart more directly reflects the differences in the degree of light pollution in the four regions under different main indicators: ➢ Protected Land: The low level of road traffic reflects the limited human activity and development in the area. The level of social activity is relatively low because the area is not usually densely populated or developed. ➢ Rural Community: The presence of moderate levels of road traffic reflects the presence of some human activity and devel- opment in the area. Has a relatively high level of ecological health, which may be due to lower levels of development and more natural habitats. There is a moderate level of social activity, reflecting the pres- ence of a community, which is more dispersed and has a lower population density. ➢ Suburban Community: Road traffic levels are higher than in rural areas, reflecting the higher level of development and popula- tion density in the region. Has a higher level of social activity than rural areas, reflecting the more densely populated and developed nature of the location.

➢ Urban Community: Has a very high level of road traffic, reflecting the area's high population density and level of develop- ment. The level of ecological health is low because natural habitats are often replaced by artificial envi- ronments and there is less green space. Has the highest level of social activity, reflecting the high popu- lation density and diverse activities and amenities in the urban environment.
