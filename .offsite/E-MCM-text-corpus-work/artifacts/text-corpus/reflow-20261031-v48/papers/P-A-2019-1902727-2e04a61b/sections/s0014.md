4.2.2 Energy intake

In 4.2.1, we have already got the calorie consumption of the dragon at 1 year old, and when the calorie intake is equal to the consumption, the dragon’s weight remains the same. [4]To increase the weight of the dragon, the calorie intake must be greater than the consumption.

We assume that the dragon’s body is composed of water, protein, fat, carbohydrates, minerals, etc., and the proportion of protein, fat and carbohydrate is p1, p2, p3, respec- tively. We believe that the proportion of protein, fat and carbohydrate in the weight gain of the dragon is also p1, p2, p3. According to the basic knowledge of nutrition, we believe that the energy required to increase 1g protein, fat and carbohydrate in the dragon body is 4, 9, 4kcal.
