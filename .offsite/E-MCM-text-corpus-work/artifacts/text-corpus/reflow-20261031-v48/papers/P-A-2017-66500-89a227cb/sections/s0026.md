With analysis to the possible sites, we find that the landforms can be categorized into 3 classes:

• Highland at upper reach of Victoria Fall, marked as area A.

• Steep terrain by the midstream, marked as area B.

• Plain between Gwayi Confluence and Kariba dam, marked as area C.

Because of the differences in landforms, the dams built can serve different major functions. Flood hits area A frequently, so the dams built here need a greater 𝑉89::". Area B is steep, which benefits the electricity generation. The storage capacity of dams here is comparatively smaller. Area C is close to human active areas, so the dams in area C are mainly designed for water usage in agriculture, industry and domestic water. Therefore, we divide Zambezi river into three domains, Flood control, Electricity generation and Water supply.
