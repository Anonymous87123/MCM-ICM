3 Data Sources and Variable Description

3.1 Data Sources

The selection of data is of great significance to the model. The authenticity and extensiveness of the data make the model more practical. After repeated search, We found the real data involved in the Acquisti article 5 [1]. This data come from the US Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the US Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC). Although the authority of these data is beyond doubt，due to the variety of variables will be used in different areas in the process of model building, these data must be varied. These data due to sample size and capacity constraints, cannot meet the needs of our model. So we look for an alternative method that uses some of the data that is already in McSherry's article [2] .Then use computer simulation part of the data that modeling needed according to objective laws. The combination of the two types of data becomes the data used by our model. At the same time, we use the real data involved in the Acquisti article [1] to test our model.
