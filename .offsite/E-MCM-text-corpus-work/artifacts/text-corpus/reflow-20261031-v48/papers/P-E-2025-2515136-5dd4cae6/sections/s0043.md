5.2 Maturity Assessment of Ecosystems

In the context of ecosystem development, a gradual transition is observed, accompanied by alterations in species richness, biomass (total bioenergy), P/R ratio, energy utilization, and food web complexity.

In order to assess the stability and maturity changes of the previously mentioned wheat field ecosystem (transformed by forested areas), the maturity index of the ecosystem, ΩEcoMat, was de- fined and the meaning of the indicators in it was explained as follows,

Index Description SR Species Richness, measures the stability of the ecosystem BM Biomass, measures the development level of the ecosystem P/R Ratio of total production to total respiration Energy Utilization Rate, specifically photosynthetic efficiency EUIR FWC Food Web Complexity

The maturity index is derived from the weights of the aforementioned indicators, which are evaluated by experts. However, it should be noted that the weights employed in this process are exclusively utilized to regulate the order of magnitude consistency, as demonstrated by the follow- ing formula:

ΩEcoMat = ω1 · S R + ω2 · BM + ω3 · P R + ω4 · EUR + ω5 · FWC (14)
