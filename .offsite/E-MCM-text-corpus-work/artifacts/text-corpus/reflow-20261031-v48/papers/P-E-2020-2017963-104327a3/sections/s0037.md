6.3 Application to Singapore and Italy

To verify the validity of the model, we will analyze two examples. In 2018, the PMC value of Singapore was 0.7052, while that of Italy was 0.7049, showing a small gap between the two countries. Both from a PMC perspective and from a common-sense perspective, one would argue that the two countries should undertake similar plastic waste reduction tasks. But in fact, because Singapore has a smaller land area than Italy and diﬀerent geographical locations, it can carry diﬀerent emissions, the calculated MPMV is quite diﬀerent. The MPMV of the two is multiplied by the corresponding PMC to obtain the SPMV which is of referable signiﬁcance to the relevant plans and measures of the two countries.
