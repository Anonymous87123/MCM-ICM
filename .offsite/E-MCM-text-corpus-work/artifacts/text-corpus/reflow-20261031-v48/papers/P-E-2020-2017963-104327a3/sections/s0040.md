7.2 Weaknesses

• Possible landﬁlls were not considered when studying the timeline.

Our model does not take into account landﬁlls that could put further stress on the envi- ronment, which could happen in practice.

• Some areas are not included in the analysis due to data missing.

Due to the limited time, we did not interpolate the missing data.
