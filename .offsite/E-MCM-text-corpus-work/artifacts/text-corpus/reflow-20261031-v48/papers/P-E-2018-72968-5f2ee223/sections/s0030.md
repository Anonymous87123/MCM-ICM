5.5.1 Larger "states"

To obtain a modification of SPEC Model, a ex-State model is required. The following facts need special consideration.

Big countries have more human and energy resources and can be fully deployed dur- ing times of emergency.

As a combination of countries, continents have inherent advantages in developing re- silient and flexible policy-making. All regions have a higher degree of coordination and complementarity of policies when faced with unrest.

Policy silos are more impossible to exist, it is essential rearrange evaluation system in a broader context of education, healthcare, good governance, and societal resilience.
