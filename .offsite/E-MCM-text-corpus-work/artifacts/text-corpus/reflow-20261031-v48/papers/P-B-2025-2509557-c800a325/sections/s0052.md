3.5 Tax Allocation

• Tax Sources:Tax revenue mainly comes from tourist-related taxes (e.g., tax rate and per capita tourist spending) and other fees (e.g., tickets, service fees).

• Allocation Ratio:It is assumed that tax revenue is allocated at fixed proportions to environ- mental protection, infrastructure, and public services, with the sum of allocation ratios equal to 1.

• Investment Effect:It is assumed that the effects of tax-allocated investments (e.g., envi- ronmental investment efficiency, infrastructure investment efficiency) are constant and take effect immediately.
