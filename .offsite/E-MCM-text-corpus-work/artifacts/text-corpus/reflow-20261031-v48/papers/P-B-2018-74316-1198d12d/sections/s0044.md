6.2 Weaknesses

a. There is two main drawbacks in Monte Carlo Simulation. If a random number which may not be random as expected have to be put in the particular part of system, some subtle non-random patterns will appear. And there would be deviate from the authentic simulation results. And Monte Carlo simulation with static characteristics has trouble to complete long-term dynamic simulation. b. The division of 4 economic centers is rough and simple. And the division of grid shapes issubjective.
