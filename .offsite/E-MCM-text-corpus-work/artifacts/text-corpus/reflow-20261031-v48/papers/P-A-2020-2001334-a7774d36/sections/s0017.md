4.1 Description of Temperature Field

According to the Assumption 2, the change of ocean temperature in the vertical plane is not considered. Therefore, for the target ocean area, based on longitude and latitude, a coordinate system is established to describe the location of each point. Therefore, the temperature u of any point A ∈Ωat time t can be expressed as

u(x, y, t) (1)

where (x, y) is the coordinate of the point A, the abscissa represents the longitude and the ordinate represents the latitude.
