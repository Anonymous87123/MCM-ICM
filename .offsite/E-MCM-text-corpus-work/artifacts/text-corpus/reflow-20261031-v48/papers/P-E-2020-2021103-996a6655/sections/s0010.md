2.2.2 Costs of Plastic Production Policies

Assumption: Countries will more readily accept a policy that minimizes (1) the quantity of cumulative saved plastic from the ocean and (2) the rate of decrease of their plastic produc- tion.

Remark: Countries are shown to adopt and enact plastic reduction policies that are easy to achieve and do not change much over time [3].
