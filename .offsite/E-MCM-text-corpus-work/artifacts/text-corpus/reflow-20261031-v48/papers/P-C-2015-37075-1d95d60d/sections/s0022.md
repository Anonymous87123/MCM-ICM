We now calculate the loss of organizational productivity associated with a person’s resignation and decompose it into direct and indirect effect. We run our simulations over next ﬁve years for 50 times and average the results for presentation in Figure 4.

It is obvious that direct and indirect effect on organizational productivity closely fol- low the number of resigned staff. An individual’s resignation will cause about 20-unit direct effect and 25-unit indirect effect. Total organizational productivity is 2000-2500 units monthly, so a person’s resignation costs 2% of total organizational productivity.

Another observation is the ratio between indirect and direct effect. In the simulations, the former is larger than the later with a stable ratio. The parameter α can reﬂect how a person view the resignation of another individual and we will return to it later.
