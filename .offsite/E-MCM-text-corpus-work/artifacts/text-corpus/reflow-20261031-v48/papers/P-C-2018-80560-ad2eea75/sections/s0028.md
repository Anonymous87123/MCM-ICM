7 Strength and Weakness

subsectionStrength

Integrity. All the data in the given dataset have been screened and checked carefully, for either missing information or incorrect information.

Fair evaluation system. Our evaluation criteria oriented from the return on earnings (ROI) rate. Thus, it has very clear meanings on the cost and earnings of certain energy.

Mathematical and Economical Model: Our models and parameters have direct economic meanings, while subjecting to mathematics laws at the same time.

Dynamic Models and Programing: sophisticated economical models and Time Series pro-gramming is included as to simulate the growth of energy consumption and price.

Science for Policy. We have added supply and demand shocks in models in order to sim-ulate the effect of policies. Thus, the policy makers can use our model for quantitative analysis and clearly observe market dynamics.

7.1 Weakness

Simplifying Assumptions. Simplified assumptions are adopted for a solvable model, so the result may slightly digress from the ground truth.

Lack of possible cost. We did not have time to take construction costs of plants into con-sideration and nor did we add into the model the constraints of technology and natural resources. Local maxima or minima. Our model have considerable amount of parameters. Although it fits good to predict the future, it is inevitable that local maxima or minima is reached in predicting some factors.
