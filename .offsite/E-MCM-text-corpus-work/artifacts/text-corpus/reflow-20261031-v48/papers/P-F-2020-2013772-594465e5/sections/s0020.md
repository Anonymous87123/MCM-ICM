5.4 The Impact of Environmental Policies

The Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs), as part of the heart of the Paris Agreement 2015, embodies each countries’ post-2020 climate actions. We designed our policies for UN based on the analysis of Intended nationally determined contributions (INDCs, anticipation of NDCs submitted by each country).[12] Here we deﬁne an in- dicator EDP Accommodation Rate (AR) to prepare for the next steps

AR = EDPj (20) TPj

If the destination country receives a group of EDPs, what will be affected environmen- tally? We searched the indicators displayed on the World Bank website and ﬁnd that PM2.5 air pollution (PAP) and Carbon Dioxide Emissions (CDE) can be used to mea- sure the implications. When EDPs from place i move to placej, the PAP and CDE of place j will alter accordingly.

As we assumed (in Assumption 2), before and after the migration, the carbon diox- ide emissions per capita and emissions of other pollutants remained unchanged. We have the following formulas to calculate new PAP and CDE.

PAPj×TPj+PAPi×EDPi = PAPj × (1 −AR) + PAPi × AR PAP = TPj+EDPi (21) CDEj×TPj+CDEi×EDPi = CDEj × (1 −AR) + CDEi × AR CDE = TPj+EDPi

Therefore, to quantify the environmental policy, we suggest UN to raise the standards of EDP destination countries for greenhouse gas emissions and emissions of other pol- lutants according to formula 21.
