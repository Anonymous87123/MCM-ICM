7.2 Weaknesses

1. The AHP method can only ﬁnd the best solution from existing solutions and can not provide new solutions.

2. In the data transmission queuing system, the input rate and output rate could be time-varying in the real society, which leads to a nonhomogeneous Markov chain. In this situation, the effectiveness protocols are hard to determine.

3. To analyse the beneﬁt of the seaport from the costumers’ use of the maturity metric, we assume the utility for all costumers are the same, as well as the cost.

In real society, there are different utility and cost.
