4.2.2 Academic Progress Rate(APR)

Despite the great emphasis on the sports teams, the majority of the colleges are still aimed at improving the overall academic performance of the students,especially for those Division I schools. Thus, Academic Progress Rate(APR) is recently in- troduced by NCAA to evaluate the overall level of the player’s academic per- formances, which in turn aids in the assessment of the player’s comprehensive qualities.[16]
