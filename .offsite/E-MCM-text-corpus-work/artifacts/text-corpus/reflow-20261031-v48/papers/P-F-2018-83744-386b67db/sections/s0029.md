5.1.1 Strengths

• We use a traditional sequential game theory model to depict the market equilibrium. It is concise and powerful. By adding several parameters to the base framework, we can apply the model to many realistic settings.

• Scientiﬁc methods are adopted in deciding the actual form of parameters. We combine AHP, graph theory, psychological theory and ﬁtting methods with our economic model, and achieve satisfactory result.

• We use undirected scale-free network to creatively simulate the process of private information disclosure and quantify the eﬀects of externality. This is quite new in related ﬁelds. • We give direct and viable policy implications to government from comparative statics in our model, which are convincing and scientiﬁc.
