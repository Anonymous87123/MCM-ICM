6. Largest Eigenvalue of Adjacency Matrix

The largest eigenvalue λ1 of a weighted adjacency matrix of a network is a standard metric of network strength. The weighted adjacency matrix of a passing network is a matrix with a zero diagonal and entry ai,j equal to the number of passes from player i to j. A larger λ1 suggests strong connections between the most important players in the network.
