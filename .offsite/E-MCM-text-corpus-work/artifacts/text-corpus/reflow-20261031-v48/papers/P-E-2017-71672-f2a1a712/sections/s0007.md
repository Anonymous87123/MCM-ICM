3.2 Canberra, ACT, AUS

The latest growth plan for Canberra was proposed in 2012 by the Aus- tralian Capital Territory Government, which outlines outcomes for 2030.

1. The ACT’s current plan to increase mixed land uses includes devel- opment of existing city centres to create more concentrated centres of commerce and living. The plan does not outline an exact target for land designed for mixed use, but it is clear from the city land map that very little land area is currently zoned for mixed use [5]. 3. The ACT introduced an update to the Affordable Housing Action Plan Phase III in 2016, outlining cost thresholds for affordable hous- ing [6]. This plan maintains the requirement that 20 % of new housing must meet these thresholds [6].
