# P-C-2018-72969-f2349d15 — distilled rules

- [caption.position] Figures are captioned below and tables above, with no exception across all 13 figures and all 8 grid exhibits - the side is fixed by exhibit type, not by page pressure.
- [caption.numbering] Every exhibit number is followed by a colon, 'Figure N:' or 'Table N:', never by a period and never by a bare space; the caption then closes with a period only when it is a full clause.
- [caption.words] Captions stay short and noun-phrase shaped - 17 of the 21 run under twelve words - and the one long caption (32 words, Table 3) is long only because it has to define the arrows and the letters it prints in its cells.
- [table.rule_style] All eight grid exhibits are booktabs: one heavy rule under the header block, one heavy rule at the foot, and no vertical rule anywhere in the paper.
- [table.zebra] No table shades alternate rows; row tracking is done by the stub column, which is why the widest grid (10 item columns) stays readable without banding.
- [table.merged_cells] Merged cells occur exactly once in the paper, as Table 8's two header spans where NM and TX each sit over two columns of inline expressions.
- [table.header_rows] Two grids carry two header rows and both are pairs of stacked blocks over one shared stub set - Table 3's ten item names twice, Table 4's eight factor names twice; the other six grids carry a single header row.
- [table.rows] Grid size is read off the claim: the three grids of two or three rows hold a single comparison each, while the 5- to 21-row grids enumerate something the prose has just promised to enumerate.
- [table.width] Six grids run the full measure and two are narrow inserts at 299.6 and 336.5 pt against a 520 pt text block; width follows the column count a claim needs, not the claim's importance.
- [geometry.family] Section kind picks the family before the data does: model sections take the schematics, the two raw scatter panels and the heat maps, result and sensitivity sections take the multi-panel line rows, the grouped bars and the radar.
- [geometry.legend] Legends are omitted wherever the caption or the italic sub-captions already name the elements - 6 of 13 figures carry no legend box - and the ones that do carry one place it inside at upper left except when three panels must share a key.
- [geometry.panel_count] Panel rows are the paper's default: 10 of 13 figures are multi-panel and the 1x3 row is the standard, used five times whenever three items are compared - here always the same three Arizona items.
- [geometry.three_d] Nothing in the paper is three-dimensional; all 57 regions were cropped and inspected, and no axis, projection or depth cue appears in any of them.
- [geometry.colorbar] Colour carries the value in exactly one figure: Figure 6's two colourbarred matrices are the only place where a reader decodes a ramp rather than a position.
- [mention.first_page] Every exhibit but one is cited by number in the body; the single uncited figure is also the only one whose neighbouring prose names a metric instead of the figure, so citation is the paper's default and its omission is local, not systemic.
- [mention.lead_pages] Pointer position is not stable: six citations print on the exhibit's own page, four one page early and two one page late, so 'cited before it appears' is not a rule this paper obeys.
- [mention.lead_pattern] Two pointer idioms alternate and neither dominates - the flat 'we plot / we present ... in Fig. N' form (7 exhibits) and 'as shown in Fig. N' (5) - with the number always spelled 'Figure N' only on first use.
- [mention.read_sentences] A third of the figures (3 of 13) hand the reader no interpretation sentence at all, and they are the small-multiple strip, the heat maps and the mechanism diagram - the three exhibits whose reading is supposed to be self-evident.
- [place.solo_page] No exhibit is given a page of its own: all 21 share their page with prose, and the two full-page-scale grids still leave room for the paragraph that reads them.
- [place.page_zone] Exhibits are spread through the column rather than pushed to one end - 7 in the top third of a page, 8 in the middle, 6 at the bottom - so page zone says nothing about exhibit type.
- [place.area_frac] Region area is not emphasis: the two largest numbers (0.277, 0.271) belong to the two big grids, while every composed figure is tiled into small boxes of 0.024 to 0.13, so area measures how the detector cut the artwork, not how loud the exhibit is.
- [place.section_kind] Nine exhibits land in result sections, eight in model sections, two in sensitivity, one in introduction and one in symbols - the exhibits are used as the evidence for a claim already made in prose, never as the argument itself.

## Templates this paper hands you

**Section → exhibit map (the whole architecture, in order).** One schematic opens
(introduction: the framework, which every later section maps onto), one nomenclature
grid and one taxonomy schematic follow (symbols, model), then a four-exhibit chain
inside the characterisation subsection: raw scatter row → fitted line row → the
20-panel small-multiple strip → the two-header parameter grid. Similarity gets one
heat-map pair, influential factors one 2x2 scatter grid, evaluation three grids in a
row, prediction a schematic and two three-panel rows, targets a bar row and a radar
row, sensitivity a two-panel pair and one function table. Nothing is decorative:
every exhibit answers a sentence the prose has already made.

**Three-dimensional condition: never fires.** Twenty-three printed exhibits, four
data types, twenty items, four states, and not one surface, no 3D bar, no 3D
scatter. The paper reaches for a panel row where a weaker paper reaches for a
pseudo-3D bar to get a third variable in. If a model must show three dimensions,
this paper's answer is a colourbar (Figure 6) or a shared legend across a row
(Figure 11), not a z axis.

**Table type vs data scale.** Two cells wide is a nomenclature grid with twenty
rows. Ten cells wide is a parameter grid with two row-stubs. The rule the paper
follows: transpose so the long axis holds the many things named (items, criteria,
states) and the row axis holds the few things measured (PCC/PRC, beta/alpha/MAPE).
Two-header grids appear exactly when a set must be cut in half and both halves need
their own labels - here ten items twice and eight factors twice. Units never enter a
header: the one table with a unit carries it in the caption, and the one with
formula cells carries them as inline expressions, which is why nothing in this paper
is decimal-aligned.

**Caption template.** `Figure N: <Noun phrase naming the thing, not the finding>` -
8 of 13 figure captions are eight words or fewer, none concludes, none names a
variable, none gives a condition except the two that must (Figure 5's ordering
pointing at Table 3, Table 3's arrow and L/S key). Tables get the same shape with
`Table N:` and one working clause allowed at the end (Table 3's key). Period at the
end only when the line is a clause; otherwise none.

**Context-weld template.** Three sentences in this order: (1) pointer, "The results
are shown in Figure 11" or "As shown in Fig. 13(a), when we change b from 0.1 to
0.9, the predicted curve moves upward"; (2) observation, what is visibly true -
"the predicted values with targets all outstrip those without"; (3) mechanism, why
it is true - "This is because larger c0 generates faster decaying of the effects by
ARMA". Sentences (2) and (3) exist for 10 of 13 figures and are absent exactly where
the artwork is supposed to be self-reading, which is the failure mode to avoid.
