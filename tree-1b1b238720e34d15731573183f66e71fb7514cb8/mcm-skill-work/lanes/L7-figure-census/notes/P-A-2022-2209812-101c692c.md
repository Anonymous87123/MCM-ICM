- Every figure caption sits under its graphic and every table caption over its header row; no exception in 20 figures and 1 measured table [caption.position].
- The family mix is diagrams and curves: line 8, flowchart-or-schematic 6, composite-panel 2, table-as-figure 1, scatter 1, map-or-plan-view 1, photo-or-raster-image 1, table-grid 1 [geometry.family].
- Two rows reach whole-page area, for different reasons: Figure 2 really is a one-page work-structure chart, while Figure 14's measured box is loose and swallows the prose below it, so its solo-page flag describes the box, not the artwork [place.solo_page] [geometry.family].
- Three-dimensional drawing appears once, in Figure 15, as perspective bars over a receding ground plane on the three unmeasured top panels; those three panels have no measured region and are named in the item note [geometry.three_d].
- Multi-panel exhibits are stacked pairs (power over elevation) or a 3x2 course grid; every panel after the first is measured separately and excluded as a fragment, and the layout pass emits several nested duplicate boxes per plot that are excluded as duplicates [geometry.panel_count] [geometry.panel_layout].
- Two captioned tables are recorded as grids: Table 1 (14x2 notations, unruled) and Table 2 (4x2 URL list, booktabs); Tables 3 and 4 carry captions but no region of their own, so they are declared in the record note instead of being recorded as rows [table.rule_style].
- The one table that looks like a table but is captioned as a figure is Figure 9, a boxed grid with full vertical rules and a pictogram stub instead of row labels [table.rule_style] [geometry.family].
- The citing spelling is always the capitalised 'Figure N' or 'Table N', never abbreviated and never lowercased; the appendix-free memo pages carry no numbering at all [mention.style].
- Five lead sentences point directly at the exhibit ('... are shown in Figure 9.', '... are summarized in Table 2 below,', '... in Figure 13 below.'), two name it as the subject ('Figure 12 shows ...', 'Figure 17 shows ...'), two use 'as shown in ... below', and eleven carry no lead at all; six rows answer back with a trail, and that trail always opens on the exhibit ('Based on this picture', 'Thus, Cd = 0.88') rather than restating the caption [mention.lead_pattern] [mention.trail_sentence].
- Captions run 4 to 14 words, name the object and never a conclusion; the only caption that carries a reading condition is Figure 15's '(Up is the north)', which is why its `caption.conditions` is true and every other caption's is false [caption.words] [caption.conditions].
- Two exhibits are uncaptioned and unnumbered on the memo pages, one of which is a verbatim re-use of Figure 20's artwork, so the paper's figure count cannot be recovered from the text layer alone [caption.numbering].
- A composite row bounds one panel, never the whole grid: Figure 15's bbox covers map panel (a) while its panel_count of 6 describes the composite, and the note has to say so or the count contradicts the box [geometry.panel_count].
- Two measured boxes are unclamped and must be declared rather than trusted: Figure 1 runs 55 pt past the bottom trim and Figure 14 starts left of the text column and swallows the prose below the schematic [place.section_path].

## When the paper goes three-dimensional

Once, in Figure 15 (`three_d = perspective-bars`). The condition is visible in the data
shape rather than in the topic: the sensitivity claim needs three axes at once (wind
velocity, wind angle, percentage of affected cyclists), and the paper reaches for a
perspective bar surface instead of a heat map. Everything else, including the two other
sensitivity figures, stays flat. Corollary worth carrying: 3-D arrives with a composite
panel (six panels here), never as a lone chart, and the perspective panels are the ones
the layout pass fails to measure.

- 14 rows x 2 cols: the notation table (Table 1, p5). Unruled: the symbol and gloss cells

- 4 rows x 4 cols: the minimised time cost table (Table 3, p15) and the critical-point

  same reason, and neither has a region of its own: Table 3 sits inside the over-large p15

  cells, which is why a naive group spans the header row only; Tables 3 and 4 have no rules
  at all and never become a region. Four table captions, two recorded grids.

## Caption templates

- Numbered figure: `Figure N: <noun phrase>`, 4 to 14 words, no verb in any of the 18
  numbered rows, so `caption.voice = none` throughout, exactly as in the 2022 A paper at
  #279.
- Numbered table: `Table N: <noun phrase>`, 5 to 7 words. `Table 3: Minimised Time Cost (s)`
  is the only caption in the paper that carries a unit, and it does it in the caption, not
  in a header cell.
- Reading condition: `Figure 15: Wind Effect to Defferent TTCs (Up is the north)` is the
  only caption with a parenthetical that tells the reader how to look at the panel, hence
  the only `caption.conditions = true`.
- Casings are not normalised by the authors: `Figure 7: the Result of Task 1` starts
  lower-case, `Figure 15` misspells "Different", and `[15]` is pasted into two captions
  when the artwork is taken from a reference. Verbatim is kept in all three cases.
- Table captions sit above the grid, figure captions below the lowest panel; Table 2 is the
  only row whose caption is above, at 20 pt from the header band.

## Connector-sentence templates

- Lead, direct pointer (5 rows): `... are shown in Figure 9.` / `... are summarized in
  Table 2 below,` / `... in Figure 13 below.` — a clause lifted out of the running
  sentence, no standalone sentence of the form "This figure shows".
- Lead, numbered subject (2 rows): `Figure 12 shows a course of own design located in ...`
  and `Figure 17 shows a scatter plot with time cost and cost percentage`.
- Lead, as-shown (2 rows): `As shown in Figure 20 below, in a cycling team race, ...` and
  `As presented in figure 21 below, ...` — the second is the paper's only lower-case
  spelling of a figure number.
- No lead (11 rows): the two structure diagrams, Fig 5, Fig 10 and Fig 11 (both reached
  through one shared sentence), Fig 16 whose caption opens the page, both p19 panels whose
  lead is a "are drawn as follows," colon clause, and the two memo exhibits.
- Trail (6 rows): `Based on this picture, we can draw some conclusions,`, `Thus, Cd = 0.88,
  A = 3.9 ft2 ...`, `We equate the propulsion power u(t) to the cyclist's power on the
  pedals`, `So, under normal temperatures ...`, `Figures (18) and (19) demonstrate how
  cyclists are influenced ...`. Every trail picks one number or one mechanism out of the
  graphic; none re-describes the drawing.
- Paired citation is common: `Figure 3 and 4`, `Figure 10 and 11`, `Figures (18) and (19)`,
  and sub-panel letters `(Figure 15a)` / `(Figure 15b)` / `(Figure 15c)`.

## Declared gaps

- Two of the four table captions have no region of their own: Table 3 (p15) is inside a
  background container, Table 4 (p19) has none. Both are named in the record `note` and
  absent from `tables`.
- Three captioned figures have no measured region at all: Figure 3 and Figure 4 (both p6)
  and Figure 6 (p8). Their captions are in the text layer and their artwork is real, so
  they are declared rather than dropped.
- Figure 15 is a six-panel composite bounded by one panel: the row's bbox is map (a) only,
  maps (b) and (c) are `fragment-of-other` exclusions, and the three perspective bar panels
  were never measured. Its `panel_count` therefore describes the composite while the box
  describes one panel - the shape of a geometry field that cannot hold a grid wider than
  the region the extractor emits.
- Figure 18 is split across three regions, none of which bounds it; its row's bbox covers
  the upper half only, and the elevation panel is recorded as `fragment-of-other`.
- Reconciliation: the extractor emits every raster region about four times (266 raw
  entries, 77 distinct boxes here; p19 alone 104 raw / 20 distinct, p14 58 / 14, p8 16 / 4).
  `measured_regions` counts distinct boxes, so `duplicate` on a row always means an exact or
  near-exact copy of another row's bbox, while `fragment-of-other` means a different panel
  or half of the same exhibit. 55 boxes are excluded, 41 of them under those two reasons.
- Two rows carry an unclamped measured box and the note says so: Figure 1 (p3) reaches
  y = 846.8 on a 792 pt page, and Figure 14 (p16) starts at x = -85.6 and swallows the prose
  below the schematic. Each is the only region on its page, so no tighter box exists; a
  consumer that crops by bbox should crop the upper or central band instead.
- Two exhibits are uncaptioned and unnumbered, and p24 is a verbatim re-use of Figure 20's
  artwork, so the count of distinct drawings is 20 while the count of numbered figures is
  18.
- Positive check: every printed caption in the text layer (Figure 1-21, Table 1-4) has a
  matching row or a declared gap, and Figure 4 is the only printed number with no caption
  line of its own - it is cited only as `Figure 3 and 4`.
