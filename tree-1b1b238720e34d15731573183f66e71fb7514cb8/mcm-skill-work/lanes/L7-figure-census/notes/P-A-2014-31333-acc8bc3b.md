# P-A-2014-31333-acc8bc3b — distilled rules

- No exhibit in this paper is numbered. Not one of the eight charts has a printed caption line in the text layer, and no prose sentence cites a figure by number: the only pointer is the section-4.2 lead 'The figures presented on the following pages summarize the data that were collected for each of the four', plus one structural reference on p12. All eight rows therefore record an empty caption, `caption_source` none and numbering none [caption.numbering].
- The chart titles are drawn INSIDE the artwork - 'AVERAGE FLOW RATES OF RIGHT HAND RULE' and the like - in small caps, so they are quoted in the row notes and are not mistaken for captions; the caption fields stay empty rather than carrying an in-image title as if it were a caption [caption.verbatim].
- All eight charts are one construction: grouped vertical bars, twelve simulation-type groups, a grey-to-black ramp of lane-count series keyed outside the frame at the right, a complete frame, horizontal gridlines at every tick and rotated text labels on the x axis with no numeric x scale [geometry.family].
- Four of the eight are flow charts and four are accident charts, and the flow charts do not share a y scale: 0 to 4 on p8, 0 to 4 on p9 and 0 to 3.5 on p10, so only the accident charts (all 0 to 2000, 2500, 2500 and 3000) are even roughly comparable [geometry.printed_numbers].
- The accident plates are mostly empty by nature: accidents only appear in the two accident-prone-heavy groups, so ten of the twelve x slots carry no bar on most accident charts. An empty group is not a missing series and is not counted as an exclusion [geometry.legend].
- Two legends are defective. The p9 accident chart lists five swatches of which two both read 'three lanes' with no 'four lanes' entry, and on the p8 and p9 flow charts no group draws all five series the legend promises; both are recorded as printed [geometry.legend].
- Exactly one plate writes values onto its data - the p11 accident chart, with 211.3, 98.8, 66.2, 97.3, 30.6, 102 and 86.1 beside the small bars and nothing beside the two tall ones - so `data_labels` is true on that row alone [geometry.data_labels].
- The eight pointers are deictic. Seven rows quote the section-4.2 lead sentence one to four pages before their plate, and the p11 accident chart quotes the p12 sentence 'This can be seen in the second figure of Section 4.2.4', which points back a page and by position rather than by number, so it carries `lead_pages` -1 [mention.lead_pages].
- Section paths come from the four rule headings the paper prints - 4.2.1 Right Hand Rule, 4.2.2 No Rules, 4.2.3 No Lane Changes, 4.2.4 Middle Lane Rule - under 4.2 Figures, so each plate sits on the heading that names the rule it charts instead of on a flattened 4.2 path [place.section_path].
