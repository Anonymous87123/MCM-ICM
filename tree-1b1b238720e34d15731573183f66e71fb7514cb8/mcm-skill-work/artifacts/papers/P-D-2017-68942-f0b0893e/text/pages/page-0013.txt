As the number of document-checking oﬃcers increases, both the average wait time and the wait time standard
deviation decrease signiﬁcantly, with the largest decrease occurring from one to two oﬃcers. However, while
there is a signiﬁcant increase of nearly 100% in average throughput from one to two oﬃcers, the increase
from two to three is much smaller. Therefore, when taking cost into account two oﬃcers does seem to be
the optimal number when there is one active PreCheck lane and low traﬃc.
