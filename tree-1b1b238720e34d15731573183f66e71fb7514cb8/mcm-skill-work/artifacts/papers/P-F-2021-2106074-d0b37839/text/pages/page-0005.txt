3 Establish NHHE Evaluation Model

     As one of the most important industries in a country, higher education is responsible for
cultivating talents for the future development of the country. Different countries have different
basic national conditions and have different system of higher education. To help policy makers
provide favorable policy advice, it is urgent to build a model to judge the health of a country's
higher education system. Consequently, we define the NHHE as a measure and establish a model
that can be applied to evaluate the NHHE in any country to quantify this index.
