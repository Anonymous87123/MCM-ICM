4.1   Number of Unique Plant Species
One primary purpose of this research is to investigate the interactions between different species amid
cycles of drought, which are undeniably related [9, 10, 11]. Therefore, we first study how the system
changes depending upon the number of unique species, beginning first with 𝑘 = 1 (i.e., a plant
community with just one type of plant), which will serve both as a control group and validation of the
model’s performance.
