4.2 Solution of the Wear Depth Distribution
                                    号




     Observation reveals that the steps are not uniformly worn but exhibit a concave state, with the
central area being more severely indented than the sides. Considering that people tend to step within a
                       信




certain range when walking on the steps, and taking into account the habits and force distribution of
people's footsteps while going up and down the stairs, theoretically, the steps that have been used for
                      微




a long time would develop two indentations, as each foot lands in a different position, forming one
indentation on each side. In practice, these two indentations are almost identical. Therefore, we
simplify the model and only study one indentation. To simplify the analysis, we only consider the case
of a single person walking, then there is only one center of wear, resulting in the wear distribution of
the steps taking the form of a two-dimensional normal distribution.
