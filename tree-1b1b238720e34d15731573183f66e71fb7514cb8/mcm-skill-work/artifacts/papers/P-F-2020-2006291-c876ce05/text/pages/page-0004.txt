separate steps. The first is to use historical data of sea level rise as the explanatory variables, and
to use historical data of global average annual temperature and time trend as explanatory
variables to perform regression to obtain the regression function of
sea level rise. Based on this, the extent of sea level rise at a certain
average temperature at a future point in time is estimated.
