• Queues operate on the “First In First Out” (FIFO) discipline. This
   principle states that the ﬁrst person to line up in a queue will be the ﬁrst
   person to be served.

• All employees are identical in terms of competency at their job.
   We assume that all TSA employees are equally capable. This isn’t entirely
   realistic because some workers are better at their jobs than others; however,
   this distinction doesn’t serve to provide any further meaningful insights into
   the problem.

• Passenger wait times are an accurate measure of throughput e -
   ciency. Large passenger wait times in queues are the result of ine ciencies
   in the TSA screening process. Passenger throughput (given as passengers
   per unit time) is inversely proportional to time spent in the system by each
   passenger. Thus, maximizing passenger throughput is essentially the same
   as minimizing wait time.
