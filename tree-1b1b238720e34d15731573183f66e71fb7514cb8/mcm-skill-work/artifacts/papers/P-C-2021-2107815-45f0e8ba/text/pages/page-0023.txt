      Asian giant hornets are called the "Murder Hornet" by the media, and its recent appearance in the United
States has attracted national attention. If the wasp’s nest is not destroyed in time, the species invasion of this
species will seriously affect the diversity of local species, cause local economic losses, and even casualties.

curb its spread.
      In this project, we have designed a three-dimensional analysis model. This model divides time and space,

information weight of each dimension. Spatio-temporal corresponds to the propagation model, text corresponds
