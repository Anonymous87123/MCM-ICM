5.2.1 Indicator Establishment

      (1) Light Damage Degree

light to the human body, which is closely linked to the intensity of light, specifically the
brightness of the nighttime sky. Studies have indicated that artificial light-induced damage to
the human body varies across regions, with the degree of damage increasing with rising
brightness until it reaches a peak level. To simplify the calculation, a differential equation for
