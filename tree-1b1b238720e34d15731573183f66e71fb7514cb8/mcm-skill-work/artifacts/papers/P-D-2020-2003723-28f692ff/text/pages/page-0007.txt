4.1.3 Network Properties

There are many parameters to describe the complex network. We selected the

most authoritative three indicators to describe the structural characteristics of the

passing network, and compared the Husky team with its opponents, and presented the

results in the form of column chart. The first graph of Figure4 shows the comparison

of C (clustering coefficient).Clustering coefficient is an indicator of the local

robustness of the network, because when a triangle connecting three players (nodes)

exists, it cannot pass the ball between the two players and can reach another node

through the other two sides of the triangle. Therefore, in football, the clustering

coefficient is used to coordinate the triangular relationship among the three players.
