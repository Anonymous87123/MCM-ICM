6.3 New Zealand

    New Zealand's fragile state index ranks 169th out of 178 countries. FSI categorizes New
Zealand in the list of "sustainable" countries. Sustainable development has been the central focus
for Ministry (The Heritage Foundation, 2018). Access to natural resources, water quality, urban
issues and climate change are major sustainable development issues for New Zealand in the early
years of the 21st century.
