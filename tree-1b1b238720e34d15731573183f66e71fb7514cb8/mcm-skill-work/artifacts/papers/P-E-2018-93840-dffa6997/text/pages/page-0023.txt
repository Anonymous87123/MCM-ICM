Demographic Metrics

    Population density
       Population density (people per sq. km of land area) can be found from The World Bank.
       This measurement helps us to predict the trend of future economic development.

    Natural Resources abundance
       Total natural resources rents (% of GDP) from The World Bank can be used to rep-resent
       natural resources of a country. This also helps us to predict the potential of economic
       development.

    Internally displaced persons
       internal displacement datasets is collected by Internally Displaced Monitoring Centre
       (IDMC). This dataset contains estimate of number of people displaced because of con icts
       or disaster in a country.

    Brain drain
       Brain Drain data is established by Institute for Employment Research over the period of
       1980-2010 (Br•ucker et al., 2013). This data covers the international migration in-
       formation for 20 OECD destination countries by gender, country of origin and level of
       education. Its data contains total number of foreign-born individuals aged 25 years or older.
