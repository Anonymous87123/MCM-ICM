9.3.2   Average Speed




            Figure 31 Average speed in light & heavy traffic (different pexit )



In the previous analysis, the average speed is always consistent with flow rate.
Adding ramps, however, breaks this consistency, especially in the heavy traffic
case. As pexit increases, a large number of vehicles need to exit through the off-
ramp. They will decelerate in advance, which causes low average speed.
