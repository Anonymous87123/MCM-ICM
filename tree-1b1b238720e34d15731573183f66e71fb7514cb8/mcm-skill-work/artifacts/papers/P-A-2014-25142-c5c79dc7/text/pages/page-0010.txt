2.4     Overtaking Model

2.4.1   Overtaking Probability

   The driver will decide wether to overtake another car with the probability Po .
Probability Po depends on the vehicle A and the vehicle B ahead. Let Vmax1 be
the velocity of vehicle A, and Vmax2 be the velocity of vehicle B. The probability
Po satisfies:


                           1 − 0.9 · eVmax 2 −Vmax 1 , if Vmax2 < Vmax1
                       
                Po =
                                     0.1             , if Vmax2 ≥ Vmax1

    It is rational to assume that the larger the difference in velocity, the more like-
ly the vehicle is to accelerate. The probability distribution reflects the tendency
well.


2.4.2   Overtaking Condition

    A driver can’t overtake as he pleases. Overtaking can be sometimes danger-
ous, and a vehicle should to be able to overtake successfully, i.e. able to return
to the former lane, under the Keep right except to pass rule. Thus, restrictions
are made on overtaking.

   Condition of Overtaking:

   • the gap G0 on the target lane is larger than Gs , and
   • the velocity of the vehicle is larger than that of the vehicle ahead.
