7.2 Weaknesses and Possible Improvements
   • Our description of changes in soil moisture is too simple.
     we assume that the absorption rate of plants is proportional to soil moisture and that the
     rate of evaporation of water is also proportional to soil moisture, and these assumptions
     may not be very reasonable. In practice, these relationships may need to be further
     discussed with more experiment or research results.
   • We used a self-created precipitation function to simulate weather change and did not
     use real climate data.
     Our self-created precipitation function cannot simulate the full details of realistic weather
     changes, which may make our model biased. In the practical application of the model,
     historical precipitation data can be found directly.
   • We ignored all factors other except precipitation.
     In reality, plant growth may also be affected by other factors such as temperature and
     sunshine, and we need to carefully consider variations in these factors if we wish to
     further improve the accuracy of our models.
