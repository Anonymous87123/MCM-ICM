Ultraviolet light is the source of energy in this model. We have defined it as a variable, U, only
substituting in a value when necessary to show example outputs from the model. A true
experiential value for U would need to be calculated on site and calibrated to represent the average
Joules of UV light that reaches the Pacific Gyre per year. U is the amount of energy available to
impact the system in J/cm2*yr. UK uses the constant K to define that energy as amount of mass in
grams, cleaved off by the breaking of a total number of Joules of C-C single bonds. The term UK
therefore, has units of g/cm2*year. To solve for a total change in grams over a specific time and
area, we integrate the term with respect to time from zero to t, and then with respect to area, a
double integral in polar coordinates.
