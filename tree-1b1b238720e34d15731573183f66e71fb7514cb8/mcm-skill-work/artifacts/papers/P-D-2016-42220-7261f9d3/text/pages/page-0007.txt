 Content Characteristic (c )

Inherent value of content plays an important role in the information flow. For example,

the funnier or more important message tends to spread more quickly. To evaluate such

abstract idea, we quantify it for two aspects: the value of information content and the quality

of presentation.
      Luckily, the article in Journal of Information Engineering University[4] determines 5

indicators for the value of information content and 5 indicators for the quality of presentation.
