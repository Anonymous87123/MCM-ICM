         To explore the underneath information within these items, we make some aggregations
     to form new items listed in Table 2. Inside, RPES/UPES/RCES/UCES (RPES as an epitome)
     characterizes energy usage distribution over different kinds of sources while RCEES/UCEES
     (RCEES as an epitome) shows that over different sectors, serving as a supplement to items
     with totality property; SRP/SRC represents the popularity of renewable energy use; RCPR/
     TCPR evinces the balance of energy use in a state. All these aggregated items enrich our
     consideration in energy profile characterization.
