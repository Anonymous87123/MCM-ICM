3.3.4 Mean Shortest-path Length (MSP)

For a pair of nodes (i, j) ∈ E, we define the topological distance between them

to be 1/Aij. Then we can calculate the length of shortest path SPi,j between each pair

of players (i, j) by Dijkstra’s algorithm [5]. Note that SPi,j may not equals 1/Aij even

though (i, j) ∈ E, since the topological distances may not satisfy triangle inequality, and

SPi,j < 1/Aij means it is easier to pass between player i and j indirectly than directly.
