3.1.2 Predicting future sea surface temperature

these features do not change significantly over the next fifty years, that is, the eigenvec-
tor ⃗vk does not change. Therefore, the overall prediction of sea surface temperature can
be realized by predicting the subsequent changes of each time vector ⃗ak . We proceed our
