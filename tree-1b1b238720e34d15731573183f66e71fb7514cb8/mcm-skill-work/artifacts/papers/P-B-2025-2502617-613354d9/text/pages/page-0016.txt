                                1.Optimal Number of Tourists: We traverse the number of tourists in the range of 0-10 million,
                            and through the coupled analysis of social, economic, and environmental benefits, we conclude that
                            the total benefits is maximized when the total number of tourists is 1.32 million.
                                2.Global Pareto Optimization: The maximum total benefits T is about 8 million when
                            considering only the changes in the decision variables, at which point the net income (Ct − Cgc )
                            is 576 million dollars and the total performance (E + S − Env, means the direct sum of each
                            benefit, representing the total benefit, used to measure the overall benefit; while weighted T used
                            for optimization) is about 15 million. The optimal solution where the benefit first increases and
                            then decreases with the number of tourists is shown in Figure 9, which is obviously in line with
                            reality.
