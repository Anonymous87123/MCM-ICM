5.3 Model Ⅲ: An Optimization Model for the Minimum Cost of Toll Plaza


     Because the land and road construction are expensive, so when design the toll plaza , we should
also consider the cost factors to ensure the toll plaza traffic conditions safe and efficient.

5.3.1   Modeling Ideas

     Location choice of highway toll plaza should insist on serving the public, with the principle of
economizing on investment, reducing land use and protecting surrounding natural environment, the
location of freeway toll plaza should consider the land acquisition cost, construction cost and
operation cost comprehensively.

5.3.2   Supplementary Assumptions and Justification

     In the same lane, we assume that the same direction of the vehicle traveling at the same speed,
and there is a certain distance between vehicles. This situation constitutes a stable traffic flow. If the
spacing of the following vehicle is too small, then prone to crash; if the spacing is too large, it will
affect the capacity of the road.
