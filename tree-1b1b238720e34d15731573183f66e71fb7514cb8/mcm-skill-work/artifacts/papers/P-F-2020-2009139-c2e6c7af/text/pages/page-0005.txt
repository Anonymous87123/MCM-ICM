4.1.2 Simulation of the distribution of people

    A parameter ρ which means the population density is introduced. The simplest idea is assuming
population are uniform distribution. But it gives an obvious error because people always live in the
cities that the density is far away from uniform distribution. Fortunately, we ﬁnd a group of data
that describing how many people are living in the land under ﬁve meters’ sea level in the database of
World Bank. So we use this data to correct the density. See the distribution in the Figure 2.
