        In short, there is a rate for NSDVs to decelerate under certain circumstances. As shown in Lane
        1 in Fig. 2, for an NSDV A, every turn a random number R between 0 and 1 is given in the
        Following Model, and:

                                          P1,A ⩾ R ⇒ vA = vA − 1, bA = 1

        Besides this effect, if there is another vehicle within vehicle A’s MinSFD, A has to decelerate
        and turn its back light on. That is:

                                     ∆dA ⩽ Dmin (vA ) ⇒ vA = vA − 1, bA = 1
