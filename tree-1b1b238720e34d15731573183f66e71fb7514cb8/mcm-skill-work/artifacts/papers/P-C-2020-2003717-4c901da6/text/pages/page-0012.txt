4.1   Reputation Metric

    The reputation of a product is related to many factors (e.g. the average star rating it receives, the
authoritativeness of its reviews [12][13] and the sales volume). As we assume that the sales volume over a
period of time is in proportion to the number of reviews the product receives. The reputation score a product
earns over a period of time can be seen as the joint contribution of all reviews it receives, which indicating
both the quality and sales volume.
