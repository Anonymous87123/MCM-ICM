7.4 Accuracy Discussion of Classification Model

    Finally, we randomly sampled 30 words, manually labeled their difficulty levels, and used
the K-Means++ model for clustering analysis. Comparing the manually labeled difficulty levels of
sample words with the difficulty levels obtained from the clustering analysis, 28 out of 30 data points
matched, resulting in a 93.33% match rate. This indicates that the difficulty level classification
obtained by the model is consistent with our subjective judgment of word difficulty. Therefore, we
believe that our classification model is relatively accurate. The data for the word samples are
presented in the AppendixA.
