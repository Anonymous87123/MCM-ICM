            The model mainly includes two parts. One is to define index weight and the other is
         to score each state. In the index weight definition part, we use the entropy method and
         in the scoring part, we combine the TOPSIS method and the PROMETHEE method,
         which can not only reflect the difference among clean renewable energy use in different
         states, but can also instruct what states can do to approach the ideal condition. Thus, we
         can evaluate and sort each states’ usage of renewable energy comprehensively.
