5.3.3 Migration Simulation Algorithm

   On the basis of the estimation of ∇u and f , the process of re-identiﬁcation of the ﬁshs
location can be get as follows,
 Algorithm 1: The process of location change of ﬁsh
   Input: ρ(x, y, 0), β, vmax , umin , umax
   Output: ρ(x, y, 50)
   for t = 1 to 50 do
       The random distractor ϵt can be get in the process of identiﬁcation of variance
       The dispersed u(x, y, t) can be predicted based on the model ARIMA(1,1,0) and the
         u(x, y, t − 1)
       The continuous u(x, y, t) can be get based on the linear interposition of value of the
         dispersed u(x, y, t)
       The continuous ∇u can be identiﬁed based on the equation (11)
       The continuous v(x, y, t) can be calculated based on the equation (12)
       The location change of each ﬁsh can be calculated based on equation (7)
       The ρt (x, y) of each ﬁsh can be refreshed based on the equation (8)
   end
