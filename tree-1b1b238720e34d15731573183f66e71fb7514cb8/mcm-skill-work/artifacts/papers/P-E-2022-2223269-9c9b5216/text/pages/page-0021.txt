period, and can provide help for forest managers in formulating harvesting plans. Moreover, the
forest management plan obtained according to the model can achieve the required goals in a
balanced manner.

6.3 Transition Strategy

      We use the model to solve the best management plan for the forest, but the reality is often not
the best. For example, the optimal harvesting cycle for a forest is 10 years longer than the current
cycle, in which case we need to consider how to gradually transition to the optimal harvesting
cycle.
