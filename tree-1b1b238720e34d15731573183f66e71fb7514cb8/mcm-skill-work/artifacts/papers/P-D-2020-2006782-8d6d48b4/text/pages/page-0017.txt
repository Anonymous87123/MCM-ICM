Below we comment on Huskies’ performance in this season.

• The value of Coordination has been low, the team’s cooperation lacking, resulting
   in a large number of Offside and Foul, unable to play a threatening cooperation.

• The number of Flexibility is not high enough. The team’s offensive line is actually
   more simplistic. The tactics of the midﬁeld straight out of the penalty zone cannot
   guarantee the goal rate and attack efﬁciency. The Huskies must choose to start
   from a wide open area.

• The Pressing value shows that the team is too conservative in defense. Most of
   the time it has been active in its own half, staying away from the opponent’s goal,
   it is difﬁcult to pose a threat, which reﬂects the lack of offensive organization
   ability of the team.

In summary, in the change of next season, we should mainly consider the three

indicators of Coordination, Flexibility and Pressing, improve the diversiﬁcation of the

team’s offensive methods, and defensive counterattack with efﬁcient and threatening
