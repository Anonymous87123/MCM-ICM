5.2   Adaptation to Miami tourism

our multi-objective dynamic programming model. While Juneau experiences highly sea-
sonal tourism with a strong focus on natural attractions, Miami attracts a steady influx of
international tourists year-round, drawn by its cultural, urban, and environmental offer-
ings. The differences between the two cities and the corresponding model adjustments
