6 Strategies

6.1 A Balance Between Safety and Costs

   Our new multiple dam system is modeled based on three key factors, Risk Cost,
Power Supply, and Series Safety. Risk Cost fully considers the balance between safety
and costs by acceptable risk ratio Pf . Series Safety further investigates the safety of a
dam group. Thus, our system is well-balanced. What’s more, we also take benefits into
consideration.
