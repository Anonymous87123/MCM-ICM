tourism, and with the change of forest function, the weights of the three indicators will
also change. For example, the tourism weight of ornamental forest accounts for a larger
proportion, and the economic weight of economic forest accounts for a larger propor-
tion, which will make the best management applicable to the forest transit from one
plan to another.
