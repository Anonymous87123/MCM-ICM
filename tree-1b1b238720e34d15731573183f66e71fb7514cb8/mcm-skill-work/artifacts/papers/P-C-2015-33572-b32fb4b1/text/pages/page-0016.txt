   The dynamic processes’ direct and indirect effects on the organization’s productivity are
analyzed below:

    • Direct effects:
       From Figure 8, the productivity of the organization is directly influenced by the clerk’s

       the staff in the early stage has much promotion space so that the ability score of the or-

       productivity. Finally, the number of employees is tending towards stability and the ability
