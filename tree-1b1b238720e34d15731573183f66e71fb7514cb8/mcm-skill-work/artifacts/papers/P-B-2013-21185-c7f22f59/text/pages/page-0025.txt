4.3.7    Conclusion
Spatial Balancing of Water Resources Strategy Model solves problem in the decision-making
process of cross-watershed water transferring, and put forward a realistic water transfer plan.

Although the plan we discussed above is nationwide, we can also apply the model to small
watershed and local water transfer strategy. That is to say, we simply need to refine the geographic
attributes and watershed attributes to solve a more detailed water transfer strategy. So our model is
universal and feasible.

In a word, our model is efficient, feasible, cost-efficient and universal.
