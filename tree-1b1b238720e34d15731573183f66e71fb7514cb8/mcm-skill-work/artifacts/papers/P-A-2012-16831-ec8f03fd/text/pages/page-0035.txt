       Then the leaf mass can be calculated:

         Leaf mass= volume of geometric solids * leaf mass constant
      The leaf mass constant (LMC) can be found in the literature. For example, for
 oak trees the value is approximately 280g/cm3 (). Then we need to calculate the
 volume of the geometric solids using the crown heights (CH) and mean crown
 radiuses (CR).
      If we use a cone to approximate the crown, the leaf mass for an oak tree can be
 calculated as follows:
