4.2 Leaf Size Model
Analysis before modeling
      It’s easy to know that leaf blade area increases when illumination strengthens
since the process of photosynthesis becomes intensive and more nutrition is
produced which will help the growth of leaf. However, the size of leaf cannot be
infinitely large, that is, many limitations lying in environment condition (e.g. the
density of carbon dioxide) and genetic factors may cause the termination of growing
even if illumination intensity keeps strengthening.


Details of the model
