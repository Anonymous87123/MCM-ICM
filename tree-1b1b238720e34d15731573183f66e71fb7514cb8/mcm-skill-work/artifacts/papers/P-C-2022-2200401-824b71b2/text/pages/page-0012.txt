5.2     APGM
With the help of the ARIMA model, we can use the past data to make a simple prediction of the next
day’s price. We can optimize the grid strategy so that it can shift the grid according to the prediction
given by ARIMA. The following picture depicts the effect of grid shift in our model over time.

In our model, the movement of the grid is determined by the weighted sum of the long-term indicator
from the MA and the short-term indicator from the ARIMA model. The amount of movement of the
grid is calculated by the following formula:

              𝑝˜𝑖{𝑡} = 𝑝𝑖 + 𝜔(𝑀 𝐴 {𝑡} (𝑁) − 𝑀 𝐴 {𝑡−1} (𝑁)) + 𝜇( 𝐴𝑅𝐼 𝑀 𝐴 {𝑡+1} − 𝐴𝑅𝐼 𝑀 𝐴 {𝑡} )
The parameters 𝜔 and 𝜇 control the weights of two indicators in the procedure of grid movement.
These two parameters will be adjusted adaptively in the backtesting phase and we initialize 𝜔 to 0.3
and 𝜇 to 0.3 in the first period. Therefore, the final decision was made by the adjusted grid model to
control positions in the gold market and the Bitcoins market.
