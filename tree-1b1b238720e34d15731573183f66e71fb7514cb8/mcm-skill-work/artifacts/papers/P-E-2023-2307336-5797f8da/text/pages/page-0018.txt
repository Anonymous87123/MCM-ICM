    Action 3: Restrict reflection material

    We can choose to use more opaque materials, such as concrete, brick, or wood,
which are less reflective and can help to reduce light pollution. Finally, the use of light
control technologies, such as light shields or directional lighting, can also be employed
to minimize the amount of light that is emitted and reflected from buildings.

    Potential Impacts: In this strategy, Action 1 aims to reduce the angle index in the
risk assessment model. Reasonable restrictions on building height and density can
reduce the H and BD indices. Managing the use of building materials and utilizing
low-reflectance coefficient building materials as much as possible can reduce the Rm
index. However, on the contrary, the reduction of building height and density will lead
to a sharp decrease in the number of residents within the same area, which will bring
pressure to housing. This impact is particularly significant in urban areas.
