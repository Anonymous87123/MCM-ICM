Efficiency
Finally we see that no one shape can dominate over every circumstance. The optimal shape depends on
the parameters given. Overall, the three most efficient shapes under our optimization methods are the
circle, square, and hexagon. Should our goal be to mass produce, we would want to have circular pans be
our product. Otherwise tailoring our product to what the consumer desired would be ideal, as we could
charge more for custom products. As far as producing the optimal shaped pan, it depends on the situation.
However, given the situation we can always find the best pan for it.
