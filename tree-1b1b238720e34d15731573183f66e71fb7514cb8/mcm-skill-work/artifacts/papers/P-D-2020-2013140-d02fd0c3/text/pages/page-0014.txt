3.2.3 Correlation Analysis and Model Test

Here, SPSS is used to analyze the correlation between the actual team performance obtained in

3.2.1 (represented by GD) and the total attributional indicator obtained in 3.2.2 (represented by

TAI). The value of statistical significance between them is 0.024, indicating that there is a

significant positive correlation.
