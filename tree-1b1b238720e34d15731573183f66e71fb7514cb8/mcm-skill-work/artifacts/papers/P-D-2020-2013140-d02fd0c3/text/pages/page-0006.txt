·Back Pass Back Cut (BPBC)

The tactic shown in Figure 3(a) is called Back Pass Back Cut, with two passes and one cut

through. This tactic uses the combination of short passes between player A and player B, with

player B passing the ball back to player A as soon as he receives the pass from player A. At the

same time, player B immediately reverses direction and runs in the opposite direction, so that

player A quickly passes the ball back to player B who is already running forward, thus achieving

the goal of shaking off the defender.
