4.4 Improve the Accident Prevention Ability
     4.4.1 Hierarchical Merge Pattern

     Traditional tollbooth has only one merging point at the end of the transition zone,
increasing the possibility of being overcrowded. But for in our cellular tollbooth, there are
bends in the cellular, keeping the speed of vehicles within a safe range. Therefore, we can
decrease the possibility of these two kinds of accident:
     (1) Traffic accidents caused by excessive speed;
     (2) Traffic congestion caused by overcrowding.
