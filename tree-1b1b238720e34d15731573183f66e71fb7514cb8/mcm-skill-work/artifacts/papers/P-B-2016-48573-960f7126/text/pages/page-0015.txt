   We can see from the figure that the specialization in cleaner indeed has some-
what positive effect on restricting the debris. The peak of the satellite population
occurs in more than 100 years in the future, which is later than that in Model II,
and the maximum is also larger. However, this result is not surprising since the
specialization in cleaner may indeed increase the efficiency so that the overall
debris population grows slower than that with identical cleaners.

   Practically speaking, it is better for us to design machines for special use or
aimed at different types of debris. A combination of different removing methods
may also be the best choice in cleaning space debris.
