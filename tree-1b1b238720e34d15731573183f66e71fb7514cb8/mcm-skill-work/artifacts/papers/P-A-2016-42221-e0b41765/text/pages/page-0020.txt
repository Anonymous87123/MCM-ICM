     From the result, we can find that when the shape of the person is close to reality, the
difference between the final temperature and the initial temperature, the wasting water and the
water inflow per unit time get some increase. According to the mathematical knowledge, in
the same volume, the cylinder’s surface area is bigger than the cuboid’s surface area thus the
area the person contact with water increases which leads to more heat exchange. Hence, more
heat is needed.
