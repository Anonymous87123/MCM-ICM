4.2   Model Buliding
The sea lamprey has a complex life cycle. Juvenile lampreys are hatched in freshwater
rivers, and after metamorphosis and development into adults, they migrate to seawater
to continue to survive, and then migrate back to freshwater rivers to spawn during the
reproduction period.
