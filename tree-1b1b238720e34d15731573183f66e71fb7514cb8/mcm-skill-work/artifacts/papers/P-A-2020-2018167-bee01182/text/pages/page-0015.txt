5.2 Results of cases
To achieve universality, we take 1oo0 independent repeat experiments, then write down the best case,
worst case, and most likely elapsed time as shown in Table 6.
Table 6: Results of cases
time(year)	case
best case worst case most likely elapsed time
fish
herring	35	16	24
mackerel	23	10
We take the time with the most occurrences as the most likely case. The shortest time is the worst
case, and the longest time is the best case. It can be seen that for herring, it is most likely that small
fisheries companies will have nothing to harvest in 24 years and the frequency of occurrence is 31%.
For mackerel, it is most likely that small fisheries companies will lose nothing in 10 years and the
frequency of occurrence is 33%. Therefore, small fisheries companies need to change their economic
strategies.
