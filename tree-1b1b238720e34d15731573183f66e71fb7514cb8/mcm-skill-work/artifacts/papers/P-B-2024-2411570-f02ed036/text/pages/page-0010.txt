3.2.2   Boundary conditions
The initial boundary conditions are the parameter characteristics corresponding to the location
where the submersible is released. The boundary conditions corresponding to the end moment
of the submersible’s motion are the parameter characteristics corresponding to the location of the
submersible’s mooring. It should be noted that there are two possibilities for the location of the
