5.2 Results
     We first give the value of parameters based on others’ studies. Then we get
the calculation results and simulating results via those data.

5.2.1   Determination of Parameters
     After establishing the model, we have to determine the value of some
important parameters.
     As scholar Beum Kim points out, the optimal temperature for bath is
between 41 and 45℃[1]. Meanwhile, according to Shimodozono’s study, 41℃
warm water bath is the perfect choice for individual health [2]. So it is reasonable
for us to focus on 41℃~45℃. Because adding hot water continuously is a steady
process, so the mean temperature of bath water is supposed to be constant. We
value the temperature of inflow and outflow water with the maximum and
minimum temperature respectively.
