7 Discussion and Conclusion
     We develop two models to elaborate the ball-bat collision. It has been shown
that the models’ simulation well match the empirical data and well explain some
phenomena. It seems that some physical issues including the vibration of the bat
and the nonlinear compress behavior of ball have been neglected in our paper.
However it’s not that case. We will validate our concise model in the following
part.
