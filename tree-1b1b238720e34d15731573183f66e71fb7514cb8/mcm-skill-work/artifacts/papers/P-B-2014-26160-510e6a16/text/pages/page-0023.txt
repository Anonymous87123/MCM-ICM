7 Is Time Line Horizon Influential?

that different society and live environment has different effects on coaches.
Meanwhile, evaluation criterion would change with time changing. These reasons all
affect fairness of college coaches’ ranks.

on coaches’ ranks or not. Physical environment, spiritual environment and recognition
of coaches would change with time changing. Based on considering physical and
spiritual environment, we provide new college coaches’ ranks.
