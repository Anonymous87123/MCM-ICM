3.3 Deﬁnition of β—the Review Vector

β is an n-dimensional column vector whose value is strongly associated with reviews and
some other inﬂuencing factors. It comprehensively takes into account of the sentiment inten-
sity of review text, varying lengths of different reviews, helpfulness ratings, the authority of
reviewers (i.e., whether or not he/she is an Amazon Vine Reviewer 1 ) and whether or not the
reviewers are veriﬁed buyers. Therefore, it is made possible to fully exploit the information
within the provided data and effectively integrate textual reviews and other non-numerical
inﬂuencing factors into our data measure.
      We deﬁne it as:

      • A is an n-by-n diagonal matrix used to describe the sentiment polarity of reviews;

      • Φ is an n-dimensional column vector used to quantify the semantic information and
        other inﬂuencing attributes of reviews.

not inﬂuence, modify or edit the reviews. Therefore, we have reason to believe that a review
