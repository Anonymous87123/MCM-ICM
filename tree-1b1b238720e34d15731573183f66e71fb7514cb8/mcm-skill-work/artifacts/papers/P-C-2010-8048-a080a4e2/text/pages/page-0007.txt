 Determine Weights
    We specify the calculation of Debris; impacts can be calculated in the same way.
Understanding the impacts of different types of marine debris is important. Not all
types of debris are equally harmful and not all organisms or regions are equally
vulnerable. After comparing the effect of two criteria in the same layer to the higher
layer, we can construct the conjugated-comparative matrix with Saaty’s Rule. For
example, a 1 3 indicates the difference of the effect on direct data between plastics and
textiles. Let M 1 be the conjugated-comparative matrix of Debris:
                                                          1        1/3        1/ 2
                                         M1               3          1           3
                                                      1/ 2         1/3           1

   After calculating the matrix using the summation method, we obtain the weight
vectors:
                                           1
                                                  (0 .5 3 9, 0 .1 6 4, 0 .2 9 7 )
