4.1.3 Temperature field distribution of the water in the tub

     In this section, we establish a model of the distribution of water temperature in
the tub when injecting hot water again, and solve the model by the PDE tool of
Matlab. However, the solution is difficult to be used in the following models, hence
we use plane fitting in the numerical solution and fortunately. Then we find that the
fitting degree is pretty high. So we use the fitting plane as approximate analytic
solution of temperature field distribution of water. It greatly simplifies the difficulty
of solving the model, at the same time, with good accuracy.
