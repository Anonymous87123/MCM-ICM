    We used a computer program written in Python (code included in Appendix B) to simulate
the change in temperature of the bathtub according to the differential equation over a period of 40
minutes. All units are converted to standard SI units in the simulation. The simulation accounts
for the loss of heat to the environment, as well as the heating of the water due to the person
operating the faucet. In order to achieve this, we set up a loop to run the simulation between
time t = 0 minutes to t = 40 minutes, what we assumed to be the end of the bath. During each
one-minute ”frame” in this interval, we calculated the new temperature of the water using the
previous temperature with the two equations for change in temperature per minute calculated in
the previous section.
