 Ⅶ. Take Root at Home or Develop Further Abroad
   When a portion of fishery assets transfers to another country's territorial waters,
according to the cross-border fishing agreements, there are various limitations ranging
from the amount of fish caught to the number of ships allowed to fishing. Additional
costs such as tariffs will add new economic burdens as well. furthermore, currents and
winds in uncharted waters will exacerbate risks and difficulties to sailing, and
unexpected losses may increase the cost of fishing.
