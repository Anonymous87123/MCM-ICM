4.1.1 Data Acquisition
   We use global sea surface temperature data from 1870 to the present, published by
the Met Office Hadley Center. In the data, the temperatures are stored as degrees C *
100. 100% of sea ice-covered grid boxes are flagged as - 1000, and land squares are set
to - 32768. As shown in the figure below, the data are covering global data, latitude and
longitude in integer for data storage.

4.2 Grey Model for TSS
   In the following discussion, we find out that in 1970 the earth experienced a cold
wave, with global temperatures plummeting. To mitigate the impact of the cold wave
on SST predictions, we will use data from 1980 to 2019 to predict SST for the next 50
years. However, these 40 years of data are not enough to predict temperature trends for
the next 50 years. Given the current situation, we design a grey prediction model to
obtain more reliable data, thus successfully overcoming the shortage of data volume.
