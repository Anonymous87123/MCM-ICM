4.3.2 Factors affecting the living place of fish
  According to previous studies, the spatial distribution of fish is related to the
ocean's SST, bathymetry, and average sea surface salinity (SSS) [1]. The sea depth
data come from the global ocean sea depth map, and the SST data come from ICES
data[5]。
  In the above data set, we can obtain a combination of three environmental
parameters for the two types of fish. We use the average of the period 1980-2018 to
determine the average environmental combination of the geographical location. We
combine the expert method to assign weights to the three factors affecting fish life.
