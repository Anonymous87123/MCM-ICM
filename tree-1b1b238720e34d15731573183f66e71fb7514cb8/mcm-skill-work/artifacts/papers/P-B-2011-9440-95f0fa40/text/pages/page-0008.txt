    Proposition 1: A strong solution is a solution.
    Proposition 2: When R  2 , any solution is a strong solution.
    To find a strong solution is much more difficult than to find a solution, and these two tasks
are equivalent to each other only if R  2 . We will respectively study the cases of R  2
and R  2 , emphasizing on the strong solutions.
