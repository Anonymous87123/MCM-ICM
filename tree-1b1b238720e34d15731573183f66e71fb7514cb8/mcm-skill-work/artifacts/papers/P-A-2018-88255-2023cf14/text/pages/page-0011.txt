            To calculate the maximum number of hops, it’s necessary to estimate the signal attenuation

       surface reflection attenuation.

            Free space is the indefinite space that is filled with homogeneous and dissipationless
       medium, which has the character of isotropy, electrical conductivity σ= 0, relative permittivity
       εr=1, and relative permeability μr=1.
