         7.1 Weakness

               Simplifying Assumptions. Simplified assumptions are adopted for a solvable model, so the result may slightly digress from the ground truth.


               Lack of possible cost. We did not have time to take construction costs of plants into con-sideration and nor did we add into the
               model the constraints of technology and natural resources.

               Local maxima or minima. Our model have considerable amount of parameters. Although it fits good to predict the future, it is
               inevitable that local maxima or minima is reached in predicting some factors.
