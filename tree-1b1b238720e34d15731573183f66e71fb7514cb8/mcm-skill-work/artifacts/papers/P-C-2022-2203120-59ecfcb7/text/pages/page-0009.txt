    In addition, we make the following assumptions:

• The period from September 11, 2016 to November 11, 2016 is the zero cycle, and no
  transaction is conducted as the experience accumulation period. Since November 12, 2016
  is the ﬁrst cycle, the number of cycles is n = 588.

• If the transaction starts from the second cycle, the asset situation in the ﬁrst week is the same
  as that in the zero cycle.

• Since gold is not traded during holidays, when the predicted value of gold price in a certain
  period is empty, it means that gold is not traded in that period.
