7.1    Variation of the Scale of Drug Reports
    We tested our model’s sensitivity to high mi values, which simulates a quickly
increasing epidemic. We doubled the 2015 drug report values, keeping the 2014 drug
report values the same and recalculating the linear fits and mi values. Our model
had an excellent linear regression slope of 1.01. The residual was only slightly higher
at 60186 drug reports. This low sensitivity is likely due to the quadratic weighted
best-fit line, which puts a higher weight on changes closer to the present.
