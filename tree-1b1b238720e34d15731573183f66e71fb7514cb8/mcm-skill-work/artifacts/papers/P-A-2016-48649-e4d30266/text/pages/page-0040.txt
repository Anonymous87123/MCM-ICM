7 Model for Cleansing with a Bubble Bath Additive (CBBA model)

   If the person used a bubble bath additive while initially filling the bathtub to assist in
cleansing, then the heat dissipation through the water and the air will be influenced by the
bubble layer. Thus we expand the CBBA model based on BWP model.
As Graph 27 shows, we modeled the bubble layer as a kind of porous medium that locates
between the water surface and the air.




                          Graph 27: The diagram of the bubble layer

the water and the air through the air can be modeled as a heat conduction process.
