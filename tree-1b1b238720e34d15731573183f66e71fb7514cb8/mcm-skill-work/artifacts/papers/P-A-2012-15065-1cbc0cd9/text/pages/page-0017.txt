5.1 Leaf Mass per Area Ratio (LMA)

Leaf Mass per Area (LMA) is defined as the ratio of leaf mass over its area (g m-2).

In this section, we describe a method to calculate the average LMA for the tree.
Since Leaves on a tree have different thicknesses due to different photosynthesis rate
determined by the sunlight irradiance at the leaf (SARAH J. COOKSON1, 2005), they
tend to have different LMA. Thus, we need to calculate LMA for each leaf based on
their photosynthesis rate and then calculate the weighted average of all the
individual LMAs.
