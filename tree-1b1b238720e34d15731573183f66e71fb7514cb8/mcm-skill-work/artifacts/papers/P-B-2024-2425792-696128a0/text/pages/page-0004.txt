     Once a defective submersible lose its propulsion, it might end up situated on the seafloor or at
a level of neutral buoyancy, where it neither descends nor ascends. However, in the complex and
dynamic deep water environments, the submersible can be drifted away by currents and difference
of seawater density. Once a defective submersible lose its communication to the host ship, it is
hardly possible for the host ship to locate the submersible. Therefore, enhancing the accuracy of
localization before the rescue is the first challenge. Moreover, once the area where the submersible
