3 The Calculation of GGDP

3.1 The Calculation of GGDP

    Green GDP aims to provide a more comprehensive measure of economic performance by accounting
for the negative externalities of economic growth, such as air and water pollution, deforestation, and
depletion of non-renewable resources.

    There is no single "official" calculation method for Green GDP. We choose ANI as the basis of our
approach because it is an expansion of GDP. The part of measuring ecological development is added
to the basis of GDP. However, there are also many forms of the formula of ANI. To avoid controversy,
we revised GGDP into two parts: GDP and environmental destruction cost. [1]
