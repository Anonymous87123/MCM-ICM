                 Figure 7 shows the trends of vehicle quantities in China. The increase of
            EV/HEV is even more obvious than that of France and USA, from 0.11×108/0.11×
            108 to 9.1×108/3.03×108. This indicates a huge EV/HEV market in China and a
            huge demand for electricity power. The control of CV quantity is not as effective as it
            seems in France and USA. It will rise from 0.9×108 to 2.3×108 and drop to 1.6×
            108. The peak time appears in 2035. It is because the quantity of total vehicle will
            enter a rapid increase period at that time and increase of EV/HEV is not able to
            follow that trend. Although the total quantity of CV will ultimately increase, the
            proportion of CV in the entire vehicle will drop significantly, from 90% to 10%.
