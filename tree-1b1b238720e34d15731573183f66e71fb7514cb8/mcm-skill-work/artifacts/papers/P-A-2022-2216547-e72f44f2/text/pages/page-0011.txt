4.2.1   Discretization of the Cycling Courses
    We calibrated the discrete data points (each point contains its latitude, longitude and altitude
information) on the real bike courses on the Google Earth. For the straight roads,the two discrete
data points to form them are farther apart, while for the curves, the discrete data points are closer
together in order to get better precision.
