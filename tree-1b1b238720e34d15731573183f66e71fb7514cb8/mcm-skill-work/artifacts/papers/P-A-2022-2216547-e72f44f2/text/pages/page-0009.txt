4.1.4   Power Curve
    The power curve represents the maximum power that the rider can maintain for a given
length of time. We obtained the power curves for different types of athletes using the following
algorithm.
   Algorithm idea: constant output power P, find the maximum length of time that the athlete
can adhere to this power output.
