6    Sensitivity Analysis

The initial distribution of service time is a specific gaussian distribution with chosen mean
value and variation. Since service time is a substantial factor in judging the qualified work-
ers, the distribution can affect the number of qualified workers. Changing the distribution
coefficient the results may change a lot.
    We choose the σ as the variable to be checked. The σ is originally set to be twice of
the length of level interval ( lj with respect to level j ). Thus the new σs are the same
as the length of level interval and three times of the length of level interval. We run the
simulation in Task 5 and the results are shown as follows:
