3.3.2   Factor of Employee Centrality

Individuals with greater centrality in a communication network are less likely to turnover
due to the information and social support benefits provided by peers in the workplace [15].
     In our model the overall importance of an employee is determined by his centrality in
graph G (cf. Figure 2) and the importance of his department in graph g (cf. Figure
3). Because Closeness Centrality can be regarded as a measure of how long it will take
to spread information from certain node to all other nodes and is widely used in Social
Network Analysis [17], we choose Closeness Centrality as the measurement. And the
importance of his department in graph g is determined by the LeaderRank Value of the
office.
