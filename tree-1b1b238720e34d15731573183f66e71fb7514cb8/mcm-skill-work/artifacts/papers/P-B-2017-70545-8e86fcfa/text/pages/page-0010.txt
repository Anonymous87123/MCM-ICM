variables including time needed by driver to search money or credit vehicled, time needed by
cashier to take related record, etc. According to the central limit theorem, we can assume
that the service time of ith vehicle (th,i ) of the each lane follow normal distribution with
µ = 15 and 2 = 4 independently and identically.
