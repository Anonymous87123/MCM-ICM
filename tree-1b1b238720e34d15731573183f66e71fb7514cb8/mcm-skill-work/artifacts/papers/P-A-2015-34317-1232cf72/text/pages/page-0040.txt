10.2 A No-boundary Scheme

   In our paper, the models of locations and TSP apply in three countries (Sierra
Leone, Liberia and Guinea) respectively. We consider there is no contact among these
Ebola-hit countries and the delivery locations distributed in three countries and every
countries delivery locations only send needed drug to itself.
However, we human beings should unit to eradicate the Ebola in the face of such
serious situation. Therefore, we are better to combine the three countries tightly and
arrange the delivery locations and transportation system without considering the
boundary and political problems.
