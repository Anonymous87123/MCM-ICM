5.3   Subjective Weights Analysis: Analytical Hierarchy Process
      (AHP)

    Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) is a policy decision method which quan-
tizes the subjective factors.[31] It aims at judging the importance degree between
the two indexes and therefore simplifying a complex problem by decomposing
the problem to several hierarchies and factors. Then, by examining the consis-
tency of the pairwise matrix, we can minimize the evaluation deviation deriving
from the subjective factors and thus render a more reasonable and convincing
evaluation scheme systematically.[32]
