Therefore, we need to focus on Lz0. The force analysis is in figure 8. Since the angle
between N and snowboarder has to be small or the snowboarder will lose balance,
the torque of N is comparatively small. So N plays a negligible role in increasing the
snowboarder’s Lz0. What’s more, the gravity is exerted on the mass point and the
exerting point of f2 can be regarded as near the mass point, so the gravity and f2 are
of little help to increase Lz0. Plus, f1 can only make Lz0 smaller. Therefore, Lz0 is the
final angular momentum of the snowboarder’s performance on the half-pipe, which
has a close relationship with the shape of the half-pipe, to be more specific, the
curvature.
