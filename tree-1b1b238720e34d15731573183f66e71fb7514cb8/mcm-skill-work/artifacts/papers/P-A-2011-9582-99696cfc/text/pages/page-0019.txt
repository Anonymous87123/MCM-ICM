Solutions to the problems
Question1:
Determine the shape of a snowboard course (currently known as a “halfpipe”) to
maximize the production of “vertical air” by a skilled snowboarder.
“Vertical air” is the maximum vertical distance above the edge of the half-pipe.


Answer 1:
We have used numerical method to test how “Vertical air” is affected by other
factors: width of the ramp, the gradient angle of the pipe, the path the snowboarder
chooses, etc. Optimizing all factors, we got shape of the cross-section of ramp, which
maximize the production of “Vertical Air”.
