From numerical computation, within about 15 meter (possibly the same value of the
width), higher ramp can results in higher velocity and larger “Vertical Air”. But when
the ramp is higher than 15 meter, the velocity and the “Vertical Air” will decrease
with height. Commonly, the height of ramp is around 3 meter to 6 meter, so we can
accept that it is advisable to build a high ramp in this range.
