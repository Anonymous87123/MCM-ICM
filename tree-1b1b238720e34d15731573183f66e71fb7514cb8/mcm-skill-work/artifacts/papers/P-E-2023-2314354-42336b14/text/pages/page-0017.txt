c) Providing incentives such as tax credits and rebates for businesses and organizations that

use renewable energy technologies

To promote the use of renewable energy in outdoor lighting and other applications, provide incen-

tives such as tax credits and rebates for businesses and organizations that use these technologies. This

can be supplemented by the use of zoning regulations that limit the location and density of outdoor light-

ing in sensitive areas (e.g., nature preserves, parks, and residential areas). These codes can also include

requirements for the use of renewable energy for outdoor lighting. Such lighting codes and standards

should be developed in collaboration with stakeholders in the area, including city or regional government

officials, lighting designers and engineers, and environmental organizations.

d) Conduct an educational campaign

This campaign can raise awareness of the importance of reducing light pollution and promote the

use of energy-efficient and dark-sky friendly lighting technologies. These campaigns can target busi-

nesses, homeowners, and the general public and can be conducted through a variety of media channels,

including television, radio, and social media.

Overall, policies that combine these and other measures can significantly reduce light pollution. The

exact percentage change in value will depend on the specific policies and actions implemented, as well

as the level of compliance and enforcement.
