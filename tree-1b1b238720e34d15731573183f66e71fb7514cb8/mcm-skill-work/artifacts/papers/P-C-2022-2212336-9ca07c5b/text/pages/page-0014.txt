is used to perform selection with reading strings, which are then translated into the corre-
sponding strategies and tested. After testing, the algorithm will give the return of each strat-
egy.

      Step 2: Mating.Its main function is to pair the winners selected by the selection algo-
rithm, which becomes the basis for the next step of reproduction.

      Step 3: Crossover. It is the process of exchanging some of the genes of two paired chro-
mosomes with each other in some way based on the crossover probability, so that we can
form two new individuals.

      Step 4: Mutation. There are three possible forms of variation in this problem: genetic
variation, strategy swapping and strategy variation.
