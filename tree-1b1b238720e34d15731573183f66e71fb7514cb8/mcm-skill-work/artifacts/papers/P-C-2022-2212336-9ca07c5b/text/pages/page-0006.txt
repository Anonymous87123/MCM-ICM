4.2.1 ARIMA Linear Prediction Model
      The ARIMA ( p, d, q) model is known as the Autoregressive Integrated Moving Aver-

age Model, where p is the autoregressive term; d is the number of differences when the

time series is stationary; q is the number of moving average items. This model is a combi-

nation of autoregressive (AR) and moving average (MA), which can transform a non-station-

ary time series into a stationary time series, and then regress the lagged values of the depend-

ent variable, the present and lagged values of the random error term to the model established.
