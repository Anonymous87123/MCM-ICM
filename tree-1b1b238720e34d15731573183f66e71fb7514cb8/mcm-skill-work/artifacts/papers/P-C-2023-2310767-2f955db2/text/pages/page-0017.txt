    The use of GMM to classify words by word feature distribution is based on two
starting points: 1) the Gaussian component of GMM can describe the distribution of
certain word vectors; 2) the linear weighted Gaussian density function can approxi-
mate the probability distribution of arbitrary shapes, and we are uncertain about the
probability distribution of the data, so GMM is chosen to classify words.
