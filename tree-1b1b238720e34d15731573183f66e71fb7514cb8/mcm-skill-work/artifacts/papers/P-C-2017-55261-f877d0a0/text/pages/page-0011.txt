occurs (denoted as A → B). We will first analyze under normal circumstances
with average traffic volume, and then special cases during peak hours.
    Case 1: Non-self-driving or self-driving vehicle merges before a non-self-
driving vehicle.
    This is the original circumstance, where tc is found using (12). The difference
between vehicle A being self-driving or non-self driving is negligible, because it
must still take full cautionary measures, not being able to predict the behavior
of a conventional driver.
