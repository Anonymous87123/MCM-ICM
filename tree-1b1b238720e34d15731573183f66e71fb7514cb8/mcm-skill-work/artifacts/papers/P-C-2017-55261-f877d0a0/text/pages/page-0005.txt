Model Design and Justification
Flow-Density-Mean Speed Relations Model
As stated in the justifications, our model treats traffic flow as a continuous
variable in space and time. The fundamental equation governing the relation
between traffic flow, q (number of cars passing a given point on the highway per
unit time) and traffic density, k (number of cars per unit length of the highway
at a given time) is:
