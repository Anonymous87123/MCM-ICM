To further test the flexibility of our models and best solutions, we decide to try two
new scenarios.



          6.1 Bayesian Adjusted Flight Path with a lost Cessna 172
A Brief Description:

On the opposite end of the spectrum from the Boeing 777, this miniscule craft has a
search area of only 29 by 99 miles and is about 42 times smaller in size, according to
our chart in Section 3.2. In our model, we will adjust the search area to match the
Cessna’s and the probability of detection to one fortieth the 777’s, which gives us
P(D) = 2%​. We will keep the search plane, sensor, and everything else the same,
searching for 24 hours.
