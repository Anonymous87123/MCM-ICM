5. Pharmaceutical Intervention
     If medicine and vaccines can be available to patients and healthy people, the
survival rate of the patients will increase and the situation of affected countries will
improve to a great extent. Therefore, the manufacturing and delivery of medicine
should remain an important factor in combating Ebola. Our tasks are to calculate the
total amount of medicine, design a favorable delivery system and compare the
intervention extent of different manufacturing speed and different level of medicine
efficacy.
