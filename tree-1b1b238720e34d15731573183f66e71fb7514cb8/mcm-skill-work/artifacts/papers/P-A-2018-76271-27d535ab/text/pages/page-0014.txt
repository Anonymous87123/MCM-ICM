         3.   Loss
             To tie our model together, we return to the overall link budget, updated with new
         losses:

                         PRx = PT x + GT x + L free + Labsorption + Lreflection + GRx             (22)
            After each ionosphere and surface bounce, our model reevaluates this equation to deter-
         mine if the link margin is met. If it is met, the signal continues for another bounce. If not,
         we determine that signal to be lost, and record its last known surface location. Overall,
         we have 180 dB in the budget, of which we can lose up to 170 dB to remain over 10 dB.
