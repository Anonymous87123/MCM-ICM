         A.    Problem Summary
              • High Frequency (HF) radio waves, from 3-30MHz, can “hop” between the ionosphere
                and the surface of the Earth, travelling a greater distance than they could along the
                ground. When sufficient signal strength is maintained (>10 dB), this enables long
                distance communication.

              • If the signal reflects off the ocean surface, it may be affected by changing electrical
                and magnetic properties of the waves, as well as size, shape, frequency, and direction.

              • If the signal reflects off the land, it may be affected by terrain variations.
              • Followingthe loss of a multi-hop signal necessitates the modeling of the transmitter,
                propagation between bounces, interaction with the ionosphere, interaction
                with the Earth’s surface, and ftnally the receiver.
