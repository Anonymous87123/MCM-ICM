                                      IV.    Model Development

         A.    Model Construction
             The model is constructed in order to accurately create the path of the bouncing signal.
         This path provides the loss due to free space and ionospheric absorption, which
         are determined per distance. The ground model determines the loss from the terrain
         interaction as well as the new incidence angle for propagation of the next bounce.

         1.   Propagation and Ionosphere Interaction
            The first portion of the model consists of the signal travelling from the ground to the
         ionosphere, refracting, and travelling from the ionosphere back to the ground. Over this
         entire section, we determine the path above and below the ionosphere separately. The
