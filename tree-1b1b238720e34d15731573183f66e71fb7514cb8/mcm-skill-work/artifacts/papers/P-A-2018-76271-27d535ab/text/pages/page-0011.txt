         2.   Ground Interaction
             When the radio wave comes in contact with the earth, we employ a different model to
         predict its new direction and loss. This model is generalized to allow for different types of
         land and water terrains.
             For fixed terrain such as land masses, geological survey data exists that we can use to
         createrealistic shapes. TheUnited States Geological Survey websitecontains downloadable
         data sets for elevation, such as the Space Shuttle Radar Topography Mission data that
