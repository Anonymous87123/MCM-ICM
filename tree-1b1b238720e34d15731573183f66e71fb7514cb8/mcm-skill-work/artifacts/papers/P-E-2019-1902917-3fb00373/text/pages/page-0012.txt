      When measuring the economic cost, we select the average economic cost (AC) of
the industry which the project belongs to as the benchmark to determine the scoring
standard. We evaluate the project’s rank according to the ratio of the project cost to AC.

      Thus, we obtain rank vectors of the five environmental cost indexes and economic
cost index. And the value of total cost can be obtained from inner product of the rank
vectors and combination weight vector.
