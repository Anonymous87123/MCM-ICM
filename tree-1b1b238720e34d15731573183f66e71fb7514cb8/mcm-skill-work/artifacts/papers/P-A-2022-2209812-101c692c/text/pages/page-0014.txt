▶ The Effective Mass
    In Equation (14), for the sake of standardization and ease of calculation, we assume that the
bicycles covered in this article are all the same, where two wheels of the same size, both with a
radius of 0.35 m, weigh 1.2 kg, so that the Iw of each wheel is 0.147 kg · m2 . Thus the effective mass
