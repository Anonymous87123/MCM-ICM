3 Data Preprocessing

    Since we are only allowed to use the datasets ’LBMA-GOLD.csv’ and ’BCHAIN-MKPRU.csv’
provided by COMAP oﬃcial, we need to pre-process the data for both datasets before solving
the problem.

    Notice that there is no missing value in the bitcoin price dataset ’BCHAIN-MKPRU.csv’,
however in the gold price dataset ’LBMA-GOLD.csv’, there are 10 trading days with missing
price data, except for the non-trading days which do not appear in the dataset.

    To facilitate the follow-up process, and to ensure the reliability and reasonableness of the
data, we take the average of the prices of the two trading days before and after each trading day
with missing gold price as the gold price of that day. The trading days with missing gold price
and their corrected gold prices are shown in the table below.
