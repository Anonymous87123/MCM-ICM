4.1.2 Weight of indicators

    (1) Entropy weight method
    In this section, with the evaluation indicators defined above, we further determine
the weights of these eleven indicators, resulting in the combination of primary
indicators. We first use the entropy weight method(EWM) to eliminate the data
incommensurability caused by the inconsistency of data dimensions, based on the
attribute type of the original indicators, we use the standard 0-1 transformation and the
given optimal interval method to do non dimensional and normalization. Therefore, it
is convenient to judge the merits of the evaluation indicators directly from the numerical
value, and facilitate the evaluation of multi-attribute decision-making.
