4. Ecological Services Analysis

    In this section, in order to measure the impact of ecosystem services, we make up
the ecosystem services index ESI. First, 11 three-level indicators are selected from four
aspects primarily, and then they are integrated into ecosystem service index(ESI) by
using entropy weight method (EWM) and coefficient of variation method (CVM). In
addition, hierarchical clustering analysis (HCA) is applied to divide the ecosystem
service intensity of the project into three categories: weak, moderate and strong.
Eventually, we use four projects of varying sizes to verify our evaluation system.
