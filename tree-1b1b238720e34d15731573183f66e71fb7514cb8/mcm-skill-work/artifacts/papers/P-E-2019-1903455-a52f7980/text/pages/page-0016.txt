    As for the Figure.7(b), the project PA is based on the consideration of the cost of
ecosystem services, that is, the external cost. Even if the rate of profit growth at the
beginning is slow, the benefits have been slowly increasing with time. In the predicted
nearly two decades, the benefit does not show a declining trend, but steadily increases.
It can be seen that considering ecosystem services can effectively increase the life cycle
of the project, consolidate the existing status, and delay the arrival of the recession.
