           The question asks us to predict whether or not a country that has never won a medal will
win its first medal at the 2028 Summer Olympics and to predict the probability of winning. The

question is essentially a binary classification problem. The predicted outcomes are categorized

into those that will win and those that will not. Therefore, we build a binary logistic regression

model.
