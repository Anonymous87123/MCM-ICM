                                                                                2 Basic Assumption

                                                                                 Hypothesis 1: Assume that the number of athletes participating in the 2028

                                                                                     Olympics, the number of countries, and the number of events held are the same as
                                                                                     in 2024.

                                                                                     Legitimacy: The data review revealed that the number of countries participating in
                                                                                recent Olympic Games and the number of events held have not changed much. To simplify
                                                                                the calculation of the model, it is assumed that the number remains constant.

                                                                                 Hypothesis 2: The variables are independent of each other and only affect the
                                                                                     results.

                                                                                      Justification: while these variables interact, considering them in the model adds

                                                                                complexity and unpredictability. For extensive analysis, we focus more on measurable and

                                                                                consistent factors
                                                                                ྘  Assumption 3: Excluding the cost of investing in great coaches
                                                                                      ଆ Justification: focuses on the direct impact of coaching quality on athlete performance,

                                                                                      team performance, or overall sport system development.
