4.1 Problem analysis
             微




     In Problem 1, we are required to develop a model for a new agricultural area that includes
various creatures, species interactions, and human factors. To accomplish this, we begin with
a food chain model consisting of only three trophic levels. Building upon this, we expand to a
food web model that incorporates decomposers and soil components. Finally, we take into ac-
count the effects of seasonal agricultural cycles. The final model, a food web model considering
agricultural cycles, achieves the goal.
