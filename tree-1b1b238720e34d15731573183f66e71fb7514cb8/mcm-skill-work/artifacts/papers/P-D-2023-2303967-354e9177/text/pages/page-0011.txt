    In the SDG Network, all goal nodes are interconnected. This feature stands in line with the reality.
For instance, SDG 5 (Gender Equality) is closely linked to SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth),
as empowering women and girls is vital to creating a more inclusive and equitable economy. SDG 16
(Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions) is dependent on the achievement of many other SDGs as it is
difficult to achieve peaceful and just societies without addressing issues such as poverty, inequality, and
lack of access to education and healthcare. Therefore, it is important to view the SDGs as a whole
and to work towards achieving them in an integrated and coordinated manner.

   source node. Usually, edge weight is used as an index of accessibility: the greater the weight, the
