4.2.3 No Lane Changes
No lane changes refers to algorithms that do not allow cars to change lanes at all. There is
no overtaking, all cars in this model have their initial starting lane generated randomly,
and it is as if they entered the free-way, moved to a random lane, and stayed there until
they would decide to leave the freeway. All lane-changing algorithms were removed.
