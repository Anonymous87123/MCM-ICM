10 Sensitivity analysis
To have confidence in our mathematical model we must show its robustness through sensitivity analysis.
We conducted a One-At-a-Time (OAT) sensitivity analysis on the input parameters for the model. We
perturbed each variable throughout their maximum range and then calculating the eigenvalues of the
Jacobian at the non-trivial equilibrium point discussed in 7.2. This provides insight on how each variable
affects the long-term behaviour of the system. See Appendix 14 for code.
    Iterating over each of the parameters individually reveals that adjusting the majority of the parameters
does not change the classification of the equilibrium point. The exceptions are 𝐼𝑡 , 𝑃𝑡 and 𝑚, which require
𝐼𝑡 < 𝑃𝑡 and 𝑚 < 53.77. Breaking these rules causes an unstable equilibrium. The first condition, 𝐼𝑡 < 𝑃𝑡 ,
is part of our assumptions. The restriction on 𝑚 is due to the fact that excessive migrations will cause the
system to go over the logistic limit of growth we had as an assumption.
