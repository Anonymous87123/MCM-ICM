3 Assumptions
In order to construct our model, we made various simplifying assumptions for the Maasai Mara itself, and
for the interactions between each of the processes we modelled in the preservation, as follows:

3.1 Maasai Mara
M-1. Dynamical uniformity: We treat the Maasai Mara in its entirety as one dynamical system, rather than
     multiple systems interacting with each other. In reality, areas like the Mara Triangle (which is even
     administered by a different entity to the rest of the preserve) may present different dynamic patterns.
