4.1 Safety Strategy
    In the actual submersion process of a submersible, in the event of an accident, communication
              信




with the main ship may be lost. Based on the fundamental principles of search and rescue, to
ensure safety, it is advisable to stay in place after losing contact with the main ship. Due to
      微




the diﬃculty of determining the submersible’s position in the deep sea and the main ship being
unaware of the submersible’s status, it is recommended that, after losing contact with the main ship,
the submersible, regardless of whether it still has propulsion, should immediately cease movement
to avoid complicating the search and rescue eﬀorts.
