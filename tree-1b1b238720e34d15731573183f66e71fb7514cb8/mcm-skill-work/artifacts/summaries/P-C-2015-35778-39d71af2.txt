1	As an indispensable part of team science, managing human capital in organizations directly determines the company’s productivity.
2	This paper tries to combine network model with team science.
3	Firstly, build the human capital static network: we use ICM’s 370 positions as nodes, the affiliations and cooperative relationships as edges.
4	For different nodes of position, we define some quantified values that can reflect basic attributes of the position based on proper assumptions.
5	By building the Post Archives Matrix(PAM), we can record the internal attributes of each position.
6	This matrix can be really helpful to simulate the real conditions.
7	For every edge (𝑖𝑖, 𝑗𝑗), the value is 1 if and only the node 𝑖𝑖 and node 𝑗𝑗 have the working relationship.
8	Then we put forward the relationship compactness calculation method, which combines affiliation and cooperative relationship, and this is applied to quantify team performance.
9	Secondly, build the human capital dynamic network model: we regard employees’ demission, promotion and recruitment as the main causes of network evolution and internal churn in the company.
10	Based on the real conditions and some reasonable assumptions, we designed three evolution driving algorithms.
11	For demission algorithm, there are two conditions: if the employee who wants to leave is in low level, he can dismiss directly; if he is in middle level, promotion and recruitment algorithm must be called to fill the position.
12	What’s more, we consider carefully about the declination of working enthusiasm and the increasing in churn rate for surrounding people when there is one employee churn.
13	For promotion algorithm, we determine the promotion conditions for different level to realize the layer-by-layer promotion of internal employees.
14	Besides, promotion is always in priority to recruitment.
15	For recruitment algorithm, we regard the 2/3 of the vacant position as recruitment plan and we build the on-position time vector to represent delaying effects.
16	Thirdly, focusing on the issues that the supervisor needs us to consider, we draw the conclusions: in the next two years, the recruitment and training cost are 26.22σ and 140.15σ; When the churn rate reaches 25% and 35%, working rate is steadily below 80% and bring some negative effects such as the declining of company performance and employees’ enthusiasm; The no-external recruitment policy is beneficial to maintain the company performance and working rate, but we still have to admit that it will cause the declining of enthusiasm of middle-level employees.
17	Finally, we regard friendships, competition and Human Capital as three network layers to describe the connection compactness in the whole team.
18	By using the main statistical indicators (such as the average length of path), we draw the conclusion that multilayer network can reflect internal connections more precisely and comprehensively than human capital network.
