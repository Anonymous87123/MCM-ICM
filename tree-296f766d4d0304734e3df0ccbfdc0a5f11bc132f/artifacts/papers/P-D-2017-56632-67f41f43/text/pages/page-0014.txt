Figure 5 shows the calculated results from our model against the simulated results.
It appears that they follow the same trend on average as the number of passengers
increases from 50 to 4050. As a result, we are more inclined to believe the results
obtained from using our simulation in the proceeding experiments. The simulation
also seems to vary from the theoretical results more drastically in the waiting
time for queue 1. However, looking at the scale on which these waiting times are
plotted, we reason that we see this relatively large variance because the waiting
times for queue 1 are much smaller in magnitude than those for queue 2. The plot
of simulated versus theoretical values for total time spent in the system is omitted
since it is essentially the sum of the above two plots and as a result, follows the
same trend.
