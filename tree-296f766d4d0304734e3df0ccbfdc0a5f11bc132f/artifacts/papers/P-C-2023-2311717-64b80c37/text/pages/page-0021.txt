8 Task 4: Other Interesting Features

      In exploring the statistical properties of the number of reports, we found that the density
of the distribution of the number of reports over 359 days showed a similar pattern to its trend
over time. Then, we fitted the distribution of the number of reports. As shown in Figure 15, a
good result can be achieved by fitting the number of reports using a log-normal distribution.
Referring to Figure 16, it can be found that the distribution obtained from the fit shows a fairly
high similarity to the change in the number of reports over time.
