      As we have state above, the inherent value of the information will influence the

From the result above, we can get the following conclusion:

 The influence in flow of information and public’s final average opinion

From the figure, we can see that the influence of inherent value in the flow of

information and public’s opinion is similar. The bigger the inherent value of information

is, the more the change of public’s opinion and the flow of information.

 Interesting finding: people’s opinions are changed suddenly.

We can find that when att1 arrive the value of 0.5, people’s opinion will suddenly

change a lot, while at first public’s opinion nearly do not change.

The reason this happens is that when att1’s value is bigger than 0.5, the media

nodes will begin to forward the information, and this result in much more people get

the information and then public’s opinion is changed.

This inform us that in the real world, if you want you information have a big

influence, you will have to find some media or important person who have many fans

to help you. So if you want a big influence, go to find a media.
