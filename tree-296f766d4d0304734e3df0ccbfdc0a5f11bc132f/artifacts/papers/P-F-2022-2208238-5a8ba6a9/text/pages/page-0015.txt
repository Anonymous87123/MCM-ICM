      independent country is entitled to a "planetary area" of its own. Due to the different
      conditions of different countries, our team offers three different ways out for the
      remaining countries, based on the premise that there are already countries that have
      conducted asteroid mining efforts.

      ✓ For countries that have certain technology, financial resources or labor force but do
           not have the conditions for space flight, they can choose to conduct joint mining
           with space-capable countries, play their role in the system and participate in the
           distribution.

      ✓ For countries that do not currently have spaceflight capabilities, but may do so in the
           future and decide to mine independently, organizations such as the United Nations
           can "hold" a piece of the planet's surface until the country has the ability to mine
           independently, then they can start mining.

      ✓ For countries in extreme poverty or perennial war, where asteroid mining is not
           planned for a limited time scale, the "planetary area" can be the national property of
