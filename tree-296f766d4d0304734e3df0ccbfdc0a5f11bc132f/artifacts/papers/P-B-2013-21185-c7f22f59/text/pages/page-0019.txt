4.3     Water Transfer Model: Spatial Balancing of Water
    Resources Strategy

4.3.1   Introduction
Our goal in Spatial Balancing of Water Resources Strategy is to solve inter-regional unevenly
distribution of water resources in space. In other words, no matter what kind of season, wet or dry,
if a region suffers from high water supply pressure all the time, we need to consider inter-regional
water transfer.
