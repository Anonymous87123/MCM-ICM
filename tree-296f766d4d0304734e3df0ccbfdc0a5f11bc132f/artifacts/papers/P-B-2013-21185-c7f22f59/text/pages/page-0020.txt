Similarly, China has long history in solving the water movement problem. The Dayu Legend, which
happened five thousand years ago, tells us the story of the first water movement project of ancient
China. At that time, the water transfer project mainly aimed to solve the flood, but nowadays we
build these projects to better meet the needs of human development and ecological conservation.
Over the last decade, the Chinese government not only constructed large-scale water transfer project
like South-to-North water diversion but also built local water transfer project like Dujiangyan Dam.
Some projects are effective, but others remain to improve.

Therefore, in order to efficiently solve spatial problems, we must consider the following
sub-problems:

   Geography and Topography
   Watershed Delineation
