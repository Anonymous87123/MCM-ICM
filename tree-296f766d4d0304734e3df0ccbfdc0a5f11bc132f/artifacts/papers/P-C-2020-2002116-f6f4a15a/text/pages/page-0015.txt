5     Predict Potential Success or Failure
    In the last section, we construct a mapping REPt0 θ : [T0 , T1 ] × {B, M, H} → R+ where [T0 , T1 ]
is the time range of the given review date. In this section, we will employ time series forecasting
method to predict the value of REPt0 θ on extended future domain, namely [T1 , T2 ] × {B, M, H}
for some T2 > T1 in section 5.1. Based on the forecasted REPt0 θ in the future time domain, we
will evaluate the success or failure potential of each product in 5.2. We show the results on the
real data and predicted their potential success or failure in section 5.3.


5.1    Time Series Forecasting for Predicting Future Reputation
