2.2     Basic Assumption

      • Trend of opioid addiction distribution changes over time. When doing prediction, we take time into
        account. The latest tendency will have a higher influence factor.

      • Opioid abuse spread depends on graphical location. We assume that the spread of drug mainly from
        one place to adjacent places and will not across a great distance.

      • When addressing data missing, we regard it as average value.
