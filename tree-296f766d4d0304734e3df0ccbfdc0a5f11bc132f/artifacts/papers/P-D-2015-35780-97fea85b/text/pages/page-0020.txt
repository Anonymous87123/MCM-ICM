It is obvious that the development pattern of a country always changes, and the Linear Time-
invariant Systems can never completely describe the change of SDI though it is capable of
revealing the tendency. Thus, we do stability analysis on our model.

In section 3, sensitivity analysis is used to analyze the effectiveness of control elements bi and
ci. With a similar processing mode,we can use sensitivity analysis to assess the stabilityof our
model. We add disturbance to system parameter αij, and observe how the outputs deviate from
expectations. Here, a OAT/OFAT method[?] is used. Uncertainty Dij is derived from αij with
following equation:

Taking α12 as an example, we set αij = (1 + η)αij, (η = −0.3, −0.25, ..., 0.03) and k =
2014, 2015, ..., 2018, and get a sensitivity analysis result shown in Figure 14.
Obviously, although variation of α12 causes deviation of outputs, the deviation becomes sta-
ble while variation of α12 becomes greater. When change rate of α12 belows 30%, the outputs
change less than 11%. This shows that our model has stability to endure disturbance or calcu-
lating error to a certain extent.
