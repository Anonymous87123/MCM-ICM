5.1 Adding a Perturbation Matrix to State Equation

While a country can accept aid from ICM to improve its sustainability, the country may also be
inﬂuenced by external uncertain factors. Some external factors (e.g. foreign investment, glob-
alization)exert positive effects on sustainability; others (e.g. climate change, natural disasters)
exert negative effects.

In Equation (6), the external effects are described by the external effect matrix BUˆ . BUˆ is
commonly respected as a random matrix; its elements obey a normal distribution where µ = 0
and σ is determined by effect degree. Since elements in row i of BUˆ are in proportion to Fi,
here we let

                                                  BUˆ = Aˆ F
where Aˆ is a perturbation matrix of F; elements αˆij in Aˆ are perturbation increments and
αˆij ∼ N (0, σ). Therefore, the ﬁnal expression of the state equation is obtained as

                  F = (GcAA + GbA + Aˆ )F                                 (10)

increase the difﬁculty of forecasting. When σ = 0.4, the overall increasing tendency of perfor-
