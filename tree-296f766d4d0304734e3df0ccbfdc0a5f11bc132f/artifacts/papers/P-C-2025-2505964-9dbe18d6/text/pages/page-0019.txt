   Elasticity Analysis
   We also calculated "Elasticity," which, under the log-linear assumption, is defined as:
                                  Elasticity = β1 × (NumEvents).
When NumEvents = 0, elasticity is 0; when NumEvents = 10, elasticity is 1.912; and at NumEvents
= 20, elasticity is approximately 3.824. These values are illustrated in Figure 14 (Elasticity of
TotalMedals with respect to NumEvents), showing an approximately linear increasing curve. This
indicates that as the number of events rises into higher ranges, "adding one additional event" yields
a significantly greater relative increase, amplifying its impact on the final medal output.
