   2.PR Curve and Sensitivity
    In Figure 7, the horizontal axis represents Recall (sensitivity). Fold 3 achieves a PR-AUC of
0.926, showing that when Recall is elevated to high levels (e.g., 0.8 or above), Precision remains
in a relatively good range. This is especially advantageous for scenarios where the objective is to
minimize the risk of missing any medal-winning athletes. Conversely, Fold 4 shows a PR-AUC of
only 0.639, indicating that to ensure high Recall, Precision might significantly drop, resulting in
more false positives.
