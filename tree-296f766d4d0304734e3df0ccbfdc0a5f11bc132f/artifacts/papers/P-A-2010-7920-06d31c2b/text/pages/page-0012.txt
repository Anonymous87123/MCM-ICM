7-b. Cork Model Augmentation
        When a bat is corked, several changes take place. First, the mass of the bat decreases at
the free end because the material used to fill the barrel is less dense than the wood of the bat
itself. This causes the weight of the entire bat to decrease, and also means that more of the
weight is located closer to the hands. This translates into a lower moment of inertia. The
combination of a lower moment of inertia and less weight in the bat results in a faster swing,
given that the same amount of force is present.
