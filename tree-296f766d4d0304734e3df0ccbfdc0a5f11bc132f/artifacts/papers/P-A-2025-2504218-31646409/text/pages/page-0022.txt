5.1     Multi-layer structural PDE
    Let the steps have a total of m+1 layers throughout their history of use and renovation: L0 , L1 , . . . , Lm ;
where L0 is the original layer and Lm is the current top layer. Notation:

                  hi (x, y, t) is the cumulative wear depth of the ith layer at moment t.                           (24)

Each layer has an initial thickness δi > 0. When hi < δi , the layer is not yet worn through; if hi reaches
δi , the layer is declared to be worn through at the point (x, y), and the layer below begins to be exposed.

5.1.1    Worn-out equation
      In the absence of renovation disturbances, the ith layer of material is subject to the combined
effects of foot traffic and the environment with the following wear equation:

                                ∂hi                              
                                    (x, y, t) = κi N (t) ϕi (x, y) + Fenv (x, y, t)                                 (25)
                                ∂t
where κi is the wear coefficient of the material in the layer, N (t) is the volume of foot traffic, ϕi (x, y)
denotes the tread distribution (which may be related to the same layer i or may be unchanged), and
Fenv is an additional factor such as environmental weathering.
      However, this PDE only takes effect when "layer i is uppermost and exposed"; if there is another
layer i + 1 above it that is being covered, layer i no longer accumulates wear.
