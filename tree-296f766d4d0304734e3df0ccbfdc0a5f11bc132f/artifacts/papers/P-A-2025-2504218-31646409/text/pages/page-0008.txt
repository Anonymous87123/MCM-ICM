                      3.1.1                                 Generalized PWE

                      (depending on factors such as pedestrian flow rate and footstep distribution) and environmental fac-
                      tors (such as rainfall, weathering, or particle impact)[4]. Here, we simplify the step to a lateral width
                      interval [0, W ], with the wear depth h distributed along the coordinate x. No variation or diffusion
                      coupling occurs in the longitudinal direction y. Thus, at any given moment, the relationship between
                      the horizontal position on the step surface and the amount of wear in the depth direction can be written
