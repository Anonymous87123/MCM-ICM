8.3    Probability of Willing to Change Lane

We consider the case that a driver might choose not to change lane even if all the
other conditions are satisfied. Obviously, the data are hard to get and it may suf-
fer from a severe change during a short period of time. We assume probability
of willing to change to the left lane and right lane are 0.5 and 0.7 respectively in
our model. Therefore, we change the probabilities by up to 15% proportionally.
The maximum deviation is 7.05%, which indicates a good robustness.
