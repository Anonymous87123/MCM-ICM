4.2 Construction of Word Attributes

    To explore whether any attributes of words affect the percentage of people report-
ing scores in the difficult mode, we first looked for attributes of a number of represen-
tative words. The following are the definitions of the attributes.

    Number of syllables

    The number of syllables reflects the number of constituent elements of a word. In
this paper, we choose the method of counting syllables to measure word length. A syl-
lable is the smallest unit that an individual can produce in a single breath and usually
contains a vowel or vowels plus one or more consonants.

    Word Class

    A word class is a group of words that display the same formal properties. The data
set given in the question contains seven main word classes: nouns, verbs, adjectives,
adverbs, pronouns, conjunctions, and prepositions. Since pronouns, conjunctions and
prepositions account for a small percentage, we categorize them as other.

    Vocabulary usage index

    Vocabulary usage index. Generally speaking, the more words are used in daily life,
the easier it is for players to answer the puzzle, and conversely the rare words will
increase the difficulty for players to pass the game.
