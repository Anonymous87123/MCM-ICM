4.4 Model IV: Light Pollution Treatment Application Model

      This section chooses two sites from among four different types of sites and forecasts the
data for each of their primary metrics in order to show the validity of Model III. The predicted
data are used to determine the optimal short-term, interim, and long-term light pollution
management strategies for the two locations as well as to analyze and discuss how the
management strategies impact the risk levels of light pollution at the two sites.
