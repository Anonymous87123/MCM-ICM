4.3 Rural charging station location model

      The rural areas have low density of population, which results in low charging needs.
Therefore, we can ignore the waiting time. If the mileage of one charge is larger than the
distance between two adjacent charging stations, the charging network can be regarded
to cover the entire rural area.
