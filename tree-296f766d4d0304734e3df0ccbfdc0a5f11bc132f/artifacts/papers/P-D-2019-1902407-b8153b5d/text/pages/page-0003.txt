                          Ⅱ. General Assumption

⚫ In the event of a disaster, people in the building can be informed of the
     dangers in a timely and simultaneous manner and immediately begin
     evacuation operations.

⚫ The evacuation emergency personnel can start the evacuation operation in
     time, and can obtain the current evacuation status of the current population
     through electronic devices.

⚫ The evacuated masses listened to the command of evacuation emergency
     personnel.
