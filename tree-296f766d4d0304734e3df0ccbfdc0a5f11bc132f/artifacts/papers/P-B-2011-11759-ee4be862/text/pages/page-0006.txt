3.2     Continuous Tone-Coded Squelch System

Continuous Tone-Coded Squelch Systems (CTCSS), often called Private Lines (PL), further
mitigates interference by associating a subaudible tone with signals being received/transmit-
ted by the repeater. In order to communicate through a private line repeater, users must
also broadcast this tone. This allows users in a densely populated area to communicate on
the same channel with minimal interference. Private line repeaters are not necessarily
closed [2] as these CTCSS tones are often published. Since it is our intention to increase the
number of available channels, CTCSS tones will be common knowledge to all users.
