6.2 Feature Engineering

    To identify countries that have never won medals, we process the table summerOly_athletes.csv
to identify athletes who have won medals, then filter the countries they represented in the com-
petition. From the list of all countries, we exclude those that have won at least one medal.

    The core principal here is to simplify the process by counting only unique countries, so
duplicates are considered only once.
