The outbreak of EDPs may drag natives’ wage and jeopardize countries’ high-end
industry. While our policies previous assume a similar industry structure benefits
EDPs, we know the cost is natives’ welfare. This is a situation that quite serious. If
natives think EDPs occupy their jobs, the society will be instable.
Based on this point, we reconsider the factor of employment in our polices. Since the
cost of offering a job is too high, we recommended arrange them in a more flexible
way. Country could encourage do some jobs that natives have not tried before, or just
offer them some special occupations.
