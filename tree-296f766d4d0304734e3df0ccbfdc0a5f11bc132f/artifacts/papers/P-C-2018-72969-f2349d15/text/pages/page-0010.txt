          We further apply our model into the remaining energy profile time series and present
     the regression parameters of AZ in Table 3 and the corresponding regression curve in Fig. 5
     (the placing order is the same as Table 3). As we can see, β reflects the general trend of the
     evolution, α represents the temporal fluctuation of measurements while σ2 shows the noise
     error. Items (RC, UC, etc.) have α σ2, which means that fluctuation takes the predominant
     part while random noise can be neglected; For those where α is close to σ2 (UCES, SRC, etc.),
     it is not reasonable to omit the impact of random noises.
