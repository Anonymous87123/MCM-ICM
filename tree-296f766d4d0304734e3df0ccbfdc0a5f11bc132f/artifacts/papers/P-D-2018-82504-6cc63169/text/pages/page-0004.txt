2 Preparation of the Models

2.1 Assumptions

1. (Shortest-path) Each vehicle go to the station with the shortest distance to
   get charged.

2. (Range-anxiety threshold) An electric vehicle driver will only seek to recharge
   his or her vehicle if its state of charge (Soc) falls below 10%

3. Only public charging stations are considered in this essay.
