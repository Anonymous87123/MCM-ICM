4.2 Repeater’s Capacity
     In this subsection, we will calculate the maximal number of users that can be simultaneously
accommodated in a repeater, namely the capacity C.
     Ignoring the background noise and the interference, namely we assume that signals from one
repeater will not affect others. Considering the transmitter frequency in a repeater is an exact value
rather than in a broad band, we come up with a method to calculate the capacity through the gain.
