lion cruise passengers, generating $375 million in revenue. However, it also encountered issues
such as glacier retreat, strained infrastructure, and social discontent. A multi - objective opti-

      Key Results
      Optimal Solutions: Model results suggest 12,000 daily tourists, an $80 - per - person tour-

the model. More tourists initially increase revenue but also heighten environmental stress and
lower satisfaction. A higher tourism tax raises government income but may drive away tourists.
      Effects of Measures
      ·Tourist Number Limitation: Limiting tourists (12,000 daily) reduces environmental and
infrastructure stress, ensuring a quality tourism experience.
      ·Tax and Fee Adjustment: Raising taxes and fees can fund environmental and infrastruc-

efforts preserve Juneau's beauty.

      ·Policy - making: Regulate tourist numbers according to capacity, factoring in seasons
