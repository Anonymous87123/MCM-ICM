7. Model Extension
7.1. Establishment of factors pool
    The mathematical model above has provided a relatively reasonable ranking result.
Hence, we can classify sports coaches according to the factors by pushing this model
further to other sports. Considering that the factors in each sport can be varied, but the
principle and basic model are the same, we hereby establish a factors pool, which
includes all the factors across sports when ranking the college coaches. In this way,
a coach in some sport can be ranked by the factors belong to the sport. After screening
out the factors, we apply the formal mathematical model to ranking the coaches.
    The factors pool is demonstrated in Fig 11. The sports of basketball, football and
baseball are considered.
