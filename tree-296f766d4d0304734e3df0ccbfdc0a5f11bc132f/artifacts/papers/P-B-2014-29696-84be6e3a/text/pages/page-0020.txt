5.2.2. Coaching difficulty optimization
    After analysis, we believe that there is a significant impact on the factor of
winning rate caused by different coaching time line horizon. In early times, the teams
that participated NCAA games are less, and hereby, they got a greater chance of
winning, compared with those today. However, with the progressive development of
NCAA games, teams participate in are much more. As a result, the pressure is higher
to not only players but also coaches and the opportunity to win is harder to earn.
    We carried on the statistics of the active coaches in NCAA from 1942 to 2013.
