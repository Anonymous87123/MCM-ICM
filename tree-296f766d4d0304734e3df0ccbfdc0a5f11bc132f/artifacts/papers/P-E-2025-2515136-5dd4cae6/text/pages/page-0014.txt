5.2 Maturity Assessment of Ecosystems

    In the context of ecosystem development, a gradual transition is observed, accompanied by
alterations in species richness, biomass (total bioenergy), P/R ratio, energy utilization, and food
web complexity.

    In order to assess the stability and maturity changes of the previously mentioned wheat field
ecosystem (transformed by forested areas), the maturity index of the ecosystem, ΩEcoMat, was de-
fined and the meaning of the indicators in it was explained as follows,
