4.3     The Effects of the Severity of Drought
An increase in the degree and duration of drought can negatively affect plant communities. The
severity of drought can be simulated by adjusting the drought coefficient dr and drought duration
td in Plant Community Evolution Model. When the drought becomes more severe, dr and td obey
the following normal distribution:
