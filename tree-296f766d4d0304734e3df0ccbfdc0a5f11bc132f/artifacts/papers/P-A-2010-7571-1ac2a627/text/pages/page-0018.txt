4. The behavior of a “corked” bat
In history, players using a corked bat perform impressively and even hit home runs
with it. People get curious about the property of the corked bat and it has become a
controversial issue. Which indeed has better performance, the corked bat or
uncorked one?

In this section, we augment our model to solve this problem and further discussion is
given based on model analysis. We analyze the influence the cork length lays on a
bat’s performance and we conclude why the corked bat is banned.
