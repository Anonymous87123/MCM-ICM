5.4. Why aluminum bat is banned?
Though the aluminum bat has much superiority over a wooden one, it’s usually
banned in a game. The reason is analyzed as follows:

   The exit speed gets to be as high as 4365cm/s when an aluminum bat is used.
    We assume the ball weighs 145g and the contact time is 0.1s. It suggests that a
    impact force of 63.3N is applied on the hands of a batter, threatening the safety
    of the batter.

   The over high-speed batted ball is also a threat to the safety for the other
    athletes in a baseball game, which runs counter to the spirit of sports.
