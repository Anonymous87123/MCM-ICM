5.2.2 fLetter:Letter Frequency

    Each letter has its own usage frequency. When Wordle players use words with higher letter
frequencies for guessing, they may have a higher chance of hitting the letters in the answer and thus
receive more clues, reducing the number of tries.

    Letter frequency,fLetter is obtained by adding up the usage frequency of each letter in the words.
