    In traditional K-Means algorithm, the initialization of cluster centers is usually randomly
selected from k sample points. However, this random selection method is prone to local optima,
leading to poor clustering results. The probability selection process introduced by K-Means++
algorithm can make the cluster centers more dispersed, making it easier to find the global optimal
solution, and thereby improving the quality of clustering results.
