6. Model Analysis

6.1. Agent-Based Model

Model Strengths and Insights The ABM allowed us to evaluate global scenarios of dragon population
expansion without being overly specific about the ecological niche that the dragons will adopt.

         – Our model shows that the most likely scenario is that dragons will congregate in the most suitable
           habitat for them.
         – Despite this grouping, dragons are likely to wreak havoc on the environment no matter how long they
           occupy a region due to their rapid migration and destructive habits.
