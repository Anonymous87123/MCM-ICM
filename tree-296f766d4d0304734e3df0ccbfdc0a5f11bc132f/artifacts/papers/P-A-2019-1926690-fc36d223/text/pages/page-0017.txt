    While changes in emax don’t have drastic effects on the dragon’s growth (other than slight drops in
    final weight) or vegetation cover, we do observe an interesting effect on the deer population. For emax
    values at or above 0.3, the deer population eventually dies out, albeit at slower rates for lower values,
    but for emax = 0.2 the deer population increases exponentially, even though the dragon doesn’t seem
    to be too adversely affected. This result is hopeful, as it demonstrates that the deer herd can endure if
    a dragon is bad at hunting and that the dragon isn’t too adversely affected by its poor hunting skills.
