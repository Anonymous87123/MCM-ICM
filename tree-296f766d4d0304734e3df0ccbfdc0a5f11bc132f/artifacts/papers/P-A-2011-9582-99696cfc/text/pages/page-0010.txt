B. Rotation
“Vertical air” is not the only judging criteria of a snowboarder’s performance. The
total degree of rotation and the level of difficulty are also essential. Hence, it is
necessary to discuss the half-pipe shape’s influence on rotation.

Step 1. Analyzing the Problem
As soon as the snowboarder flies into the air, if we neglect the air drag, his angular
momentum cannot be changed, so his rotation only depends on the snowboarder
himself. By changing his own posture, the snowboarder can do twist, flip or whatever
he wants. Thus, since we are discussing the influence of the shape of the half-pipe,
we only need to consider the initial angular momentum of the fly. To maximize the
twist, we have to maximize the initial angular momentum.

The angular momentum can be disintegrated into three directions----x, y, and z. Let’s
represent them with Lx0, Ly0, and Lz0. The shape of the half-pipe can only affect Lz0,
while Lx0 and Ly0 are determined by the snowboarder, with the help of the friction of
the snow.
