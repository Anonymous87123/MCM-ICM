Conclusion 1:
Wider and higher ramp, larger gradient angle, g, chosen by skilled snowboarder all
contribute to “Vertical Air”.

While the width and height of the course is fixed, the shape of the course is close
to an ellipse.

Sometimes, a short ‘Vert’ and ‘Flat’ is also preferred.
