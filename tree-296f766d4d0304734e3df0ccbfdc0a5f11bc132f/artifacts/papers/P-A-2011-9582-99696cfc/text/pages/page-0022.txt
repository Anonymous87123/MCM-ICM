   𝑧𝑡 (𝑙𝑜𝑐𝑎𝑡𝑖𝑜𝑛 𝑜𝑓𝑡𝑎𝑟𝑡 𝑝𝑜𝑖𝑛𝑡 𝑜𝑓 𝐹𝑙𝑦)
    What path the snowboarder takes, which is defined by Zt in model, plays
    significant part for “Vertical Air”. As previous work *14+ suggests, steeper path
    provides the player higher speed, larger chance to fly farther down the pipe, but
    fewer tricks in a run. Shallower path means cutting straight across and straight
    up the wall, but provides less momentum and lower speed.
