9 Modle Evaluation and Further Discussion

9.1 Strengths

    • Novelty. We used concepts such as information entropy to model the initial distribution
       and transfer probabilities of Markov chains, proposed two game strategies and successfully
       estimated the proportion of use of the two strategies in historical data, and used time series
       prediction methods on them, eventually combining them to obtain the results.

    • Interpretability. We use ARIMA and GPR models for time series forecasting, K-Prototypes
       methods for cluster analysis and Markov chains for modelling wordle games, all of which
       have a strong interpretability or deep statistical basis. The innovative part of the model also
       has a convincing structure.

9.2 Weaknesses

    • Missing other potentially useful features. The model we built only uses part of the features
       of words, and there may be some unexplored features that can improve the model.

    • Certain degree of arbitrariness in model designs. We adopt an arbitrary mindset when
       designing part of our models.
