From the above results, we find that the baseline strategy and fixed upper and lower bound periodic
grid models perform very well. However, both strategies are extremely dependent on Bitcoin’s uptrend
and insightful upper and lower bounds respectively. The baseline strategy is highly dependent on the
ratio of the price on the beginning day and the price on the end day. The traditional grid strategy is
too conservative and therefore cannot adapt to unilateral market conditions. In addition, the upper and
lower bounds of the traditional grid strategy also seriously affect the final payoff. The following Figure
