4      Vanilla Grid Strategy
4.1     The fundamental of Grid Strategy
Vanilla Grid Strategy, which is known as the Forex grid trading strategy[1], is a technique that seeks to
make a profit on the natural movement of the market by positioning buy stop orders and sell stop orders.
This is performed on a predefined market distance with a preset size of take-profit and no stop-loss.
The grid is divided by a sequence of assessments of current prices, from high to low, expressing
expectations for long-term future prices.
