3.2.1    Building The Network of Personnel Relationship

Now that there are a grant total of N (N ⩽ 370) employees in ICM, which is corresponding
to the N node in graph G, and we use vi (1 ⩽ i ⩽ N ) to denote the ith node in G. In order
to simplify the problem,we make a natural assumption that any pair of nodes vi and in G
is connected if and only if the three following conditions are satisfied:

   • vi and vj are in the same Branch or division;

   • vi and vj are in the same level;

   • vi and vj are respectively leaders of two office whose superior department are the
     same.
