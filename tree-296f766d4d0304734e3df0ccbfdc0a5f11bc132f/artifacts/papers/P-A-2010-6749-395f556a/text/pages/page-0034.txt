 Prediction and Comparison of behavior for wood and metal bats
    As Figure 24 demonstrates, such a special metal bat will have the same
controllability as the wood bat concerned. However, this special metal bat will
have a faster 𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵 due to the contribution of trampoline effect. In a word, our

 General cases
     From the special case, we can conclude that in common situation, metal bats
will possess both better BBS and controllability. The reason is that a better metal
bat can be manufactured through the following steps on the basis of the special bat
design:
1) Reduce 𝐽𝐽𝑐𝑐𝑐𝑐 and 𝑀𝑀 by remove a certain amount of metal, thus increase the
controllability.
2) Streamline the inner cavity to enhance the strength of the bat.
     After the two steps, the newly-made bat still has strong trampoline effect
while its controllability is raised.
