2 Assumption
1. No consideration for the failure of the bat. Our model is developed under the
   precondition that both the uncorked and corked bat are working well.
2. The loss of energy caused by bending vibration is neglected (except for
   Bending Vibration Part).
3. The uncorked bat is uniform in density and is symmetric to the parallel axis.
4. The stuffing object of the corked bat has the same perpendicular axis as the
   corked bat.
