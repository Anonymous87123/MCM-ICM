    In general, our prediction models receive two limitations: the accuracy of the prediction and the
generalization of the model. For other prediction models such as LSTM, it is possible to get good results
on the given dataset with the tuning of hyperparameters. However, the same model and hyperparameters
may perform poorly on another similar dataset, i.e., the problem of poor generalizability. In our model,
on the one hand, prophet is based on a strong statistical theory and the model has a strong interpretability,
which is more easily accepted by the market. On the other hand, the model has better generalization
ability and can get a good result for most of the data, saving the time and effort of tuning.
