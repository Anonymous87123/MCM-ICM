model called X-Prophet, which combines the advantages of Prophet and XGBoost. What’ more, based
on the unstable price of bitcoin, we proposed a novel sliding window mechanism. Using the prediction
model, we can predict the price trend with excellent generalization ability and interpretability.

bitcoin conform to 5-day SMA Principle of price patterns. In order to better adapt to the price trends
of gold and bitcoin, we decided to combine the price prediction with 5-Day SMA Principle to make
decisions on buying and selling gold and bitcoin. The results of the improved model are based solely
on price data prior to the current day, i.e., the price forecast for the next N days is based entirely on past
data. Evidence shows that our decision model is able to achieve optimal results when the forecasting
model performs absolutely well.
