and GGDP obtained by 5.3 on the forecast results, we re-predicted the future climate conditions

emissions reductions that resulted from the transition immediately slowed climate deterioration,

and by 2075, the climate score began to recover. From an ecological point of view, this means

that the amount of carbon dioxide and waste digestion and renewable energy each year already

exceeds the amount of human emissions and consumption of natural resources each year. Con-

sidering the climate situation, our change has been more than worth it, and it has had amazing
