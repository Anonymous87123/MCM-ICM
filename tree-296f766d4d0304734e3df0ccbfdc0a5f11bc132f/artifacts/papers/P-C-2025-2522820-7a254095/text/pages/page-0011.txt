ັ ğM 3.5 Projections for Countries Yet to Earn Medals
 ‫ ݼ‬Our model accounted for countries that generally don’t medal through clustering and a specific binary

regression model to determine if those countries do medal in a certain year or not. Our model predicted these
values as stated previously in the paper as

       ྐPredicted Number of Medals = (1 − P (Yij = 0)) · yˆij

Here yˆij is the number of medals they would earn if they medalled at all, and it is multiplied by the probability of

ັ earning a medal. We looked at the list of countries that have never medalled in Olympic history and compared it
to our predictions for 2028. We assigned odds to these estimates as the probability these countries medalled at

all from our regression (the odds are P (Yij = 1)). We found that two countries, Angola and Saar, are predicted
to win 1 medal in the 2028 Olympics and have never won a medal previously, with odds 32.54% and 40.08%

respectively.
