4.3 Floating Exchange Rate Institution

4.3.1 Balance of International Payments

    The ﬂoating exchange rate institution can realize the external equilibrium
spontaneously through the free ﬂuctuation of currency.

    Situation I: ofﬁcial settlement surplus
    If there is a surplus in balance of international payments, domestic currency
will appreciate and digital currency will depreciate. For individuals in virtual
countries, they will reduce imports. Therefore, the country’s exports are reduced,
FE curve moves to the left, and the IS curve is panned to the left (or below).
Ultimately, it will achieve internal and external equilibrium and hence the digital
currency will depreciate.
