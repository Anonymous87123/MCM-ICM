5.2 Test for Robustness

    In this subsection, we consider the robustness of our model. To test the ro-
bustness, we apply the model to other countries to see whether the model also
work well as the real cases analysis’ result in 4.4.

    For ﬁxed exchange rate system, we take Thailand as example. According to
the Figure 11, Thailand should pursue a tight ﬁscal policy and an expansionary
monetary policy and will ﬁnally achieve internal and external equilibrium. For
ﬂoating exchange rate system, we take Mexico as example.According to the Fig-
ure 11, Mexico should implement a tight monetary policy and eventually achieve
internal and external equilibrium, which attests to the effectiveness of our model.

    Additionally, using the anova test in R, we can easily get that these models
are signiﬁcant enough to explain the real cases.
