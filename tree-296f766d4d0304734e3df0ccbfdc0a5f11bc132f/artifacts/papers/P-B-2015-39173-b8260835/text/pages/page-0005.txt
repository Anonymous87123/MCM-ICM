         The probable distances from the point of lost contact to the point

     accounted for a single dimension, we can easily extend it to the left
     and to the right, indicating a plane’s maneuver off course. The next
     portion of our model accounts for the possibility of veering off the
     intended linear path.
         Veering away from the IFP is considered by assuming the segment
