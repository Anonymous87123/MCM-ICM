     When the remaining Rem is very small and μ is close to 0, the resources allocated to Mexico are also
close to 0. When Rem is greater than or equal to twice America's monthly demand Req, μ is almost 1,
giving Mexico the amount of the United States in a month, i.e., giving Mexico the amount of the United
States in a month at most. (The calculation period is one month). When Rem is exactly equal to Req,
μ=0.5, giving the United States half of what it needs in a month.
