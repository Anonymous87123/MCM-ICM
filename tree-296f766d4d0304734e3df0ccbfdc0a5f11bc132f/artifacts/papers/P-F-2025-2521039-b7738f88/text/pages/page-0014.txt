                    In the results analysis, the key coefficient β3 is used to evaluate the effectiveness
                of national policies on the distribution of cybercrime. If β3 > 0 and is significant, it
                indicates that the policy has a positive effect on reducing the target count of cyber-
                crime. Conversely, if β3 < 0 or is not significant, it suggests that the policy has limited
                effectiveness or may even have negative impacts.
                   The meaning of β3 varies depending on the indicator and requires logical adjust-
                ments. For instance, the β3 of target count represents the net effect of the policy in
                reducing the target count, where a negative value is preferable. Similarly, the β3 of
                success rate reflects the policy’s impact on reducing the success rate, with negative
                values being more favorable. On the other hand, the β3 of thwart rate, reporting rate,
                and prosecution rate reflects the policy’s effectiveness in improving these rates, where
                positive values are preferred.
