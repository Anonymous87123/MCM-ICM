3 Basic Model Analysis

3.1 Measure of the Return about Currency Stability R1

Our hypothesis assumes that the total number of digital currencies is fixed, but the need of the currency is
constantly increasing. Finally, there must be deflation in the system. We try to set the value of the currency
in terms of the level of productivity to determine the degree of the deflation. Finally, we want to use
currency stability to measure the return of the digital currency system.
