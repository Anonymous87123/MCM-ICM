      4.1.2                                 Analysis of Economic Costs
          The components of economic costs are more complex than economic revenues. On the one
      hand, the government of Juneau needs to invest a certain amount of fixed expenditures on public
      services every year, such as household garbage removal and sewage disposal, etc. On the other
      hand, the surge in the number of tourists has led to accelerated destruction of the infrastructure in
      Juneau, and the government has to invest more revenues in the construction and maintenance of the
      infrastructure. At the same time, the cruise ship traffic brings a large amount of carbon emissions,
      the government of Juneau needs to solve these challenges.
        It is important to note that the economic costs are not strictly linear with the number of tourists.
