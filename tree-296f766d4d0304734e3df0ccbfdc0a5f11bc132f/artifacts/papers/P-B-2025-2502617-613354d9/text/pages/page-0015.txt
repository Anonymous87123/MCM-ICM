  4.4.2                                    Global Pareto Optimization in the Game between Scenic Spots
     Economic, social and environmental benefits usually form a competitive relationship. This
  competition is typically influenced by multiple factors (e.g., the number of tourists and tax rates),
  and in the absence of coordinated mechanisms, natural game-theoretic interactions often lead to
  outcomes that deviate from Pareto Optimization, with strategic autonomy tending to converge
  toward suboptimal equilibria.
      Specifically, by solving the Multi-Objective Programming Model using the SLSQP method,
  under the above constraints, we can obtain a Pareto optimal solution where the benefits are most
  reasonably allocated, and there is no room for improvement in the total benefit, which is the results
  of the Multi-Objective Programming Model.
