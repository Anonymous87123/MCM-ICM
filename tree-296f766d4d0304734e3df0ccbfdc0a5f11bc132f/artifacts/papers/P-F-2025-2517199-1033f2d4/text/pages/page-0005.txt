Stage II High Risk Region Location
Using the cybercrime records in the VCDB and other indicators reflecting regional
cybercrime risk levels, we developed a plausible risk assessment model and created a
visualization system to represent global risk levels.

Stage III Policy Evaluation
Based on the collected cybercrime records, we identified apparent crime trends in
specific countries. We compiled the cybersecurity policies implemented by these
countries and categorized them into three categories based on their characteristics.
