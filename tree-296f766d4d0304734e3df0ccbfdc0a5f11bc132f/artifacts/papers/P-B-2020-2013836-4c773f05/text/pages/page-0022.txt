Strengths

able to simulate the complex system such as sandcastle, but the cellular automata
can simulate the spatiotemporal evolution of the complex system. After the sta-
tionary cellular automata runs for a certain time, the cellular space will tend to a
fixed state that does not change with time, this represents an ideal 3-dimension
prototype.
