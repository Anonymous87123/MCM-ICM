7.3 Strategy III: Compensation Strategy Based on Intensity Factors

In addition to directly regulating the use of light, we can also compensate for the harm

caused by light pollution through governmental macro-regulation, for example, by taxing

enterprises and factories that produce light pollution and using the tax revenue for

infrastructure development as well as the development of technology and new materials

which is related to light pollution reduction to reduce the impact of light pollution on health,

biodiversity.

As described in Section 7.2, we specify intensity factors with values {1,2,3,4,5} to

represent the factors of the five strategy implementation intensity levels {weak, weak,

moderate, strong, strong}.
