5. Management Plans Balancing The Values of The Forest

    This task constructs a multi-objective optimization model that is used to synthesize
various values of the forest and develop an optimal forest management plan. Also, the scope
of application of the decision model is explored, as well as the scenarios of forests that are not
subject to deforestation. It also investigates the determination of transition points of
management plans for different forests.
