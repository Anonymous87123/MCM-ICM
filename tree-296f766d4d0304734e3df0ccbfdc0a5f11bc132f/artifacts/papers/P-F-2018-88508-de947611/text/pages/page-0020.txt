6 Testing the model

6.1 Examination Using the Real Data

The data we used in our model consists of parts of the data in literatures and simulation data imitated by
computer. Although the model considers many factors and is also optimized, the results obtained may have
partial deviations. Therefore, we use the real data in Acquisti's article[1] to test the effect of our model.
Taking the varieties in the tendencies to share personal information among different age groups as an
example, the results show that the difference between the two is between 0 and 0.17. It indicates that our
model is similar to the real situation and it reflects the correctness and rationality of our model, that is to say,
the model is credible.
