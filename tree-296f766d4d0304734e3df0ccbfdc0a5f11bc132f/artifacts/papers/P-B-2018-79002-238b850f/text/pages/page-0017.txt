     Whenever establishing a new office in a new location, the company will have a wider range of consumer groups,
     and thus more revenue. But at the same time, building a new office also costs a lot. According to the general
     Benefit = Revenue − Cost equation, we need to clarify the source of company’s revenue R and cost C.
     Since English is a necessary language, we used the number of the most popular language speakers except
     English in that region, to judge the company's profitability when we set up our Cost-Benefit Analysis model.
     Here we give some Assumptions as follows:
