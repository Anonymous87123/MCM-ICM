4.6    Sensitivity Analysis
    We set several parameters according to the keep-right-except-to-pass rule, in ad-
dition to predict the movement of cars in the simulation. However, we lack the offi-
cial data of these parameters, therefore, we should carefully examine the simulation
model’s sensitivity by changing in our setting. We change parameters respectively
and results are shown in following tables. With the changes of the parameters ,our
model criteria only have limited changes, therefore, our simulation model is robust
under reasonable conditions.
