4.1    Model Overview
According to the physiological activities and ranges of the sea lamprey, the life cycle of
the sea lamprey is divided into three stages: the juvenile stage of metamorphosis in the
river, the adult stage of survival by migrating to seawater, and the reproductive stage of
migrating to the river. We focus on the effects of sea lamprey sex ratio, two migrations
and biotic interactions on the abundance of different species in the community.
