4.2.1   Migratory Sub-model
There are two important migrations in the life cycle of the sea lampreys. The first occurs
from March to April each year, during the northern hemisphere spring, when large num-
bers of adult-bodied lampreys of varying ages (mostly 5-7 years old) migrate upstream
from the ocean to freshwater rivers for subsequent mating and spawning [8]. The second
time is in May to June each year, when most of the larvae undergo a long metamorphosis
into adults (mostly 3-5 years old) and migrate toward the ocean for more food[9].
