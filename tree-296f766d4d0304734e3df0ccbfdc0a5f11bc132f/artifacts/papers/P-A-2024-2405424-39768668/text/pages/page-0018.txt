at around 1 × 106 due to this ability. On the other hand, when the sex ratio is constant,
the minimum population size of the lamprey is about 2 × 106 , which indicates that
the ability to change the sex ratio improves the population’s ability to resist external
disturbances at the expense of reducing the population size.

limited population size for the species.
