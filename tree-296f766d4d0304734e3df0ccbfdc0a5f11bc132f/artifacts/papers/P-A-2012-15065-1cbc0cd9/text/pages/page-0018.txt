leaves. By observing the venation network of a given leaf area, one can also find a
surprisingly correlated relationship between three venation functional traits and LMA
(Benjamin Blonder, 2011). We follow the modeling process of the derivation of LMA
with respect to the following functional traits:
   Density σ : total path length of veins in the area of interest (ROI) divided by

   Distance d : the mean diameter of the largest circular masks that fit in each
    closed loop
