2.2.2.1 Child Branch Formation

Given the length and location of a parent branch, we want to find the direction and
length of the child branch. At this point, the Child branches generated will subject to
the following:

a) Number of child branches at the branching point N is given by
                                  N=[𝜃 ∗ 𝜎(𝑘)],
where
  𝑘: A species-wise constant coefficient which is related to the mean of the
   number of child branches for the species.
  θ: Uncertainty compensator which incorporates the genetic uncertainties and
   environmental uncertainties.
  𝜎: A function which maps the species of a tree to the mean number of child
   branches generated at this branching point.
