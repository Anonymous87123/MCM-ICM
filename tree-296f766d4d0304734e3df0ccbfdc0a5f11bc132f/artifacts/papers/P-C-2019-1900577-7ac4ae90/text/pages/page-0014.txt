5.7   Adjustments
   To take into account these correlated socio-economic factors into our model, we
turn to a multivariate approach to find the current trend of drug reports with respect
to time. Instead of using a weighted linear regression to find the value of mi , we
perform a polynomial regression fit on the total drug reports with respect to time
and our most correlated socio-economic factors. Equation 6 indicates the form of this
equation fit for county i with socio-economic factors x1 through xn in year y.

                Total Drug Reportsi = m1 y + m2 x1 + · · · + mn+1 xn               (6)
