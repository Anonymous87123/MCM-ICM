  4.5 The productivity and cost
     As we all know, the task of HR is maximize the output and minimize the cost. Maximizing the
profit is the most important principle of all profit organization. Then, we discuss the productivity
and cost.
  4.5.1 Productivity
     In this paper, we assume the output is related to the rate of on-the-job and the
organizational structure. In the provided data we know ICM usually has only 85% of its 370
positions filled at any time. So we assume that under this situation the organization can operate
effectively and make maximum output.
