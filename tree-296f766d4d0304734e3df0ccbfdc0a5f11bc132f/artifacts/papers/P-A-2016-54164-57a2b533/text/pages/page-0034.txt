5.3   Observations
We at first expected to discover a consistently increasing or decreasing trend between volume and
optimal flow rate, and surface area and optimal flow rate. However, as we increment surface area
under constant volume, we note that the optimal flow rate decreases, but then increases again,
following no set pattern.
