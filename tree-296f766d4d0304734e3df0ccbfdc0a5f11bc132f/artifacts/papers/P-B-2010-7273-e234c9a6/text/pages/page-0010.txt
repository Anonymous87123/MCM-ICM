5       Case Studies
In this section we apply the models discussed earlier to two notable cases, namely
the Yorkshire Ripper (Peter Sutcliffe) and the Atlanta Child Murderer (Wayne
Williams).

5.1     Yorkshire Ripper
The Yorkshire Ripper murders were a series of murders which occurred between
1975 and 1981 in which 13 women were murdered and 7 attacked in the Manch-
ester and Leeds area of England. Most of the women killed were prostitutes.
Peter Sutcliffe was convicted of the murders and attacks. According to [3], the
bodies did not appear to have been moved after the murders. We treated attacks
and murders identically in our analysis.
