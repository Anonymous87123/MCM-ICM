7.2 case 2: China Baowu Steel Group
       Steel, as a necessary material in many diﬀerent area from military use to
       the structure of buildings, has played an important role since the Worlds
       Industrial Revolution. Steel industry thus has been seen as the key to
       economic prosperity. Large steel companies could generate employment,
       export earnings and tax revenues. However other than these social bene-
       ﬁts, steel industry also has a signiﬁcant impact to environment.

    The process of making steel starts from the production of iron. Iron is made from
iron ore going through reduction reaction with carbon monoxide under extreme high
temperature. The carbon monoxide using here is made from heating carbon with
oxygen. The side products of this reaction contribute to major air emission in the
process of making iron which are carbon dioxide, sulﬁde and nitride. Methods for
manufacturing steel from iron involve basic oxygen steelmaking(BOS) and electric arc
furnace(EAF). Like the process of making iron, both of these two methods require
extremely high temperature, thus costing signiﬁcant amount of energy. To provide
this high requirement of energy, burning coal is usually involved to generate heat.
Also, signiﬁcant amount of water is involved in the cooling process.

    Thus, due to the huge production of steel and various side product involved and
the own characteristics of the production process, steel industry has brought the
environmental problems to the table. The costs of ecosystems services must not be
ignored.
