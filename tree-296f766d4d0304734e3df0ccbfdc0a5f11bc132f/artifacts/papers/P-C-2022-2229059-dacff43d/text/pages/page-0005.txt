1.3 Assumptions

We make several assumptions in this model to aid in our modeling.

1.3.1 Non-Zero Assumption

Assumption: We assume that the price of the assets will not fall to zero, and stay at zero.

Justification: Gold is very stable, and is often used as a hedge against unfavorable mar-
ket conditions. It is safe to assume that the price of this commodity will not reach zero.
Bitcoin is a foundation for cryptocurrency. There would have to be a full scale melt-
down of all crypto infrastructure, either resulting in faulty blockchain usage (not enough
miners) or 0 demand. Both of these are extremely unlikely.

1.3.2 No Outside Investment

Assumption: We assume that we will not be able to leverage our investments and use
margin in our model.

Justification: Day trading in bitcoin is already a high risk investment, and using margin
makes the risk untenable.
