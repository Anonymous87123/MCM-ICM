6.2 Estimated from data
For 𝑘, which denotes the carrying capacity of the prey in the Maasai Mara ecosystem, we looked at the
stable population of wildebeest in the Serengeti-Maasai Mara region and then divided by the proportion of
the area of the Maasai Mara relative to the whole region[19].
    𝜂, which quantifies the sensitivity of the carrying capacity to environmental degradation, was obtained
by observing the decline of the population of the wildebeest from 1980 to 2000 [4]. We arrived at a value
of a 1% decrease per year.
    To obtain a value for 𝑚 0 , we obtained the value from data provided by National Geographic [13].
