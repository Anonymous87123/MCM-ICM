2. Coeﬃcient of Variation Method

The coeﬃcient of variation method is also a method of objective weighting. Among the indicators,
the data changes under some indicators have large diﬀerences, and the data changes under some
indicators have small diﬀerences. The data that has a greater impact on the subject should be the
data with large changes, and the corresponding indicators are of higher importance and should be
given higher weights. The formula for calculating the coeﬃcient of variation is

                        cvi    =  SDi
                                   xi

Where SDi is the standard deviation of a set of data, and x¯i is the mean of a set of data. Then the
weight calculated by the coeﬃcient of variation method is

                        Wcvj =    cvi

                                  n    cvi
                                  i=1

Taking the sustainable development index as an example, we get that its coeﬃcient of variation is
0.3102, and its weight is 0.031.

3. Combination Weight Calculation

Sometimes, the index weight determined by the entropy weight method has the defect of equal-
ization. In order to avoid this problem, we adopt the method of combining entropy weight and
coeﬃcient of variation to obtain the index weight. Assuming that the preference coeﬃcient of the
weight is λ, the combined weight is

                                          Wj = λWj + (1 − λ) Wcvj

                                                     λ ∈ (0, 1)
