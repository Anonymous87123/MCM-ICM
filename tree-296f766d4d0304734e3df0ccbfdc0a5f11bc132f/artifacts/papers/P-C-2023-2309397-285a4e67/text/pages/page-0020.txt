After specifying the indicators used to express the difficulty level of words, we chose a
GMM model to cluster these indicator values to classify the difficulty level of words.
Compared with the traditional K-means clustering, GMM clustering belongs to the
hybrid clustering, which uses M’ distance to measure the constraint relationship
between different variables, and can be considered as a better clustering method.
