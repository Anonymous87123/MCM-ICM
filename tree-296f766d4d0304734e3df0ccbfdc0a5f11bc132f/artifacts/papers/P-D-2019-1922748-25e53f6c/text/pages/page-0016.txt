6.4 Adaptive analysis

According to the topic requirements, museum managers hope to obtain an adaptive model
that can be used to address a wide range of considerations and various types of potential
threats. Let’s take a ﬁre accident in a room as an example to show that our model is
adaptable. Assuming that room 8 is on ﬁre, it should meet such requirements: First, only
leaving is allowed for room 8. Secondly, people in room 8 have to be evacuated as soon as
possible. According to our aforementioned algorithm, the above two requirements can be
achieved by blocking the entry channel of the node 8 which is shown in Figure9 (c), (d) and
lead people evacuate to neighboring rooms just like Figure9 (a), (b).
