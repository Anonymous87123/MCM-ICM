  • Probability Distance Method: The probability distance method
    takes into account a location’s distance from each particular previous
    crime location. In this model, a probable next crime location would
    be relatively close to multiple previous crimes. Thus, we have

    where f is a distance decay function defined in one of the following
    ways:

      – Linear Distance Decay: The probability of the next crime
        location decreases linearly away from a particular previous crime
        location, given by f (d) = α − βd, where α, β are decay constants.
      – Exponential Distance Decay: The probability of the next
        crime location decreases exponentially away from a particular
        previous crime location, given by f (d) = e−γd , where γ is a decay
        constant.
