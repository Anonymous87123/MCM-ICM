to appropriate values and adjusted them to see how the model performs with different
parameters. Eventually, we determined the model parameters to the following values
and used the model to solve the results.
   It is worth noting that our model is highly adaptive and does not apply only to
the Great Lakes region. When the model is migrated to other regions, local data are
needed to support it.

canceled the sex ratio adjustment mechanism, i.e., the probability of larval transform-

unchanged, simulated the population evolution, and observed the pattern of the evo-
