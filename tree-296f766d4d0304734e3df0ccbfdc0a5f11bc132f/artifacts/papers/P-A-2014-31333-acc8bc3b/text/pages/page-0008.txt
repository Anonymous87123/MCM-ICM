4.2.1 Right Hand Rule
The Right Hand Rule Algorithms represent the standard convention of driving on the
right, and moving left to overtake a car in front. All of the cars start in the right most lane,
as if all the cars have all just entered the freeway. The cars then disperse to overtake
slower cars but always try to move back into a spot in the right most lane possible.
