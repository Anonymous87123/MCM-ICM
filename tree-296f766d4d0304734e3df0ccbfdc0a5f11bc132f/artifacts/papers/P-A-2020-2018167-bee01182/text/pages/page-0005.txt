4.1 Signal filtering processing
According to Met Office Hadley Centre observations datasets [3], we get the sea surface temperature
(SST) from 1970 to 2019. For each year, there are 12 data for each month. The SST data are the
temperature data of the grid that divides the globe into grids using longitude and latitude as integer
lines. The intersection of the latitude and longitude lines with integer values of latitude and longitude
is taken as the center of the grid, 0.5 degrees up and down, and 0.5 degrees left and right are used
as grids. We take the average sea surface temperature of a grid for 12 months as the sea surface
temperature of that year. Based on the latitude and longitude of Scotland, we analyze the SST of
300 grids ranging from -17° to 3° longitude and 48.5° to 63° north latitude. We number the 300 grid
centers from left to right, top to bottom, and the partial annual mean SST of part of the grid was
shown in Table 2.
