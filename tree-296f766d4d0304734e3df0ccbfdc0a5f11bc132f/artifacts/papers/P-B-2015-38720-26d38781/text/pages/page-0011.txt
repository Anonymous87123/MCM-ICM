      2.5 History of Oceanic Search Theory
The branch of applied mathematics known as “Search Theory” originated during
World War II as a method for locating enemy ships, and was quickly adapted for
                                               [12]​
rescuing airmen that crash-landed in the ocean​    . These historical searchers had an
advantage over our situation due to abundance of manpower and the frequency of
                                                                                   [12]​
military radio contact; they generally knew the exact moment the plane crashed​        .
This information allowed the searchers to look radially outward from the point of lost
contact and quickly perform their rescue.
