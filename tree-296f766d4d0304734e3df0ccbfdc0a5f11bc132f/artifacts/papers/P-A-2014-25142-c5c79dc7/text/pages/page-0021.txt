7     Driving on the Left

    We’ve discussed the right-hand traffic and now, let’s consider the left-hand
traffic. The situation is exactly mirror symmetrical to that with right-hand traffic.
So we require the vehicles used in this model to be right-hand drive and make
a switch of the left and the right in our former model. Then the situation of
driving on the left is simulated.
