C. Commuters
      Most commuters live and work within the city, so the city's transportation layout
affects their commute time. Good internal accessibility in the city shortens commute
times, improves work-life efficiency, reduces commuter fatigue, and enhances overall
satisfaction with both work and life.

D.Transit passengers
      Travelers with intercity needs, such as those connecting flights or buses, or on
cross-city road trips, are impacted by the accessibility between the urban fringes.
Poor accessibility can lead to missed connections and higher costs, while good
accessibility ensures a smooth journey and improves the overall impression of the
city.

E.Visitors
     Tourists often visit multiple attractions in a single day, and the accessibility
be-tween these attractions affects their experience and time management. Good
connec-tivity allows tourists to move between attractions more easily, visit more sites,
and have a higher level of satisfaction. On the other hand, poor connectivity may lead
them to skip some attractions, reducing the overall experience. We quantify the
bene-fits for tourists based on the accessibility between these attractions.
