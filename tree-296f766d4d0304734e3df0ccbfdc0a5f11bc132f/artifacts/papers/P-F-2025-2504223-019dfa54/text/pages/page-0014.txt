6.1.5 Placebo test

    After solving the model and obtaining preliminary results, in order to exclude the
impact of non-policy factors on cybercrime and avoid subjective changes in the re-
search subjects due to advance knowledge of the policy implementation signal, which
may lead to errors in the "policy effect", the following placebo test is conducted:

                                                Figure 7: Placebo test

    The kernel density distribution of the placebo test results is shown in the ﬁgure. It
can be seen that the p value distribution is close to the normal distribution, and the
regression result is not signiﬁcant, indicating that the impact of the implementation
of legal policies on cybercrime governance on the attacking country is not accidental,
which further veriﬁes the robustness of the model.
