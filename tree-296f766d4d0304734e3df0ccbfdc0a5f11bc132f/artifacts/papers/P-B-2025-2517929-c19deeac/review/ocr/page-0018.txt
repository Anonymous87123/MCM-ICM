6 Sensitivity Analysis
For each optimal solution, we used the local perturbation method to detect variable sen-
sitivity. We varied one decision variable within the perturbation range of (-10%, +10%) while
keeping other decision variables constant and observed the impact on the objective function.
The final results were presented in a heat map, where the color intensity or temperature varia-
tion indicates the magnitude of the correlation coefficient. The closer the absolute value is to
1, the stronger the correlation.
