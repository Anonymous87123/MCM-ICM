queuing vehicles from one to four, the toll plaza capacity increased significantly; when it from four to
eight, the toll plaza capacity increased slowly; when it from eight to ten, the toll plaza capacity
increased further slowly. In view of this regulation of toll plaza capacity changes, the service level of
