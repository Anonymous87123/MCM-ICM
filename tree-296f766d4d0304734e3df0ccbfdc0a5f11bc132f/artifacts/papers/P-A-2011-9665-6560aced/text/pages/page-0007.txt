3.3.2 The Analysis of Snowboarder’s Movement


   As shown in Figure 2, when an athlete starts from point O to a random extreme
point A , there only exists the gravity and friction which are in work in the whole
process.
   According to the law of conservation of energy, we can get
                                     W p  W f  mg  h,
                                      
                                       h  h  hx ,

   where Wp denotes the work done by the snowboarder on the flat and it is used for
acceleration, W f denotes the work done by friction.
   Obviously, in order to maximize the “vertical air”, we should maximize W p and
minimize W f , respectively. Note that the resistant function of friction for the
snowboard is much smaller than the work done by the snowboarder, the achievement
of maximizing the “vertical air” can approximately be obtained only by maximizing
Wp .
