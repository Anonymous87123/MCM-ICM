From the figure, it can be seen that the model is rather sensitive to lowering the patience, yet the effect is
less drastic as the patience rises. This is due to the fact that if the maximum patience is lower, then cars are
much more likely to try to leave their lane, but that after a threshold, the number of times that a car has to
brake to desire to switch lanes is not reached by most of the cars at any point while they travel through the

6.2    Sensitivity with Respect to Number of Self Driving Cars
As self-driving cars continue to captivate the imagination of the nation, it would be worthwhile to know
what effect they might have on the traffic encountered in toll plazas. An initial sweep was done ranging
from 0% to 25% on the symmetric six lane nine toll layout with a medium traffic level in order to gauge the
impact.
