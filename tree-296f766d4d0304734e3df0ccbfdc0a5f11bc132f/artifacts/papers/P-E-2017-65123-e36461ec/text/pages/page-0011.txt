 3.4.2 Determination of First-Class Index
      Based on the indicator system and weight method aforementioned, a comprehensive

index is proposed to measure the success degree of smart growth (SDSG) of a city. The
indicator system is used to provide the essential second-class indicators, while the weight
method is adopted to evaluate the weights w of the SGSD. Let SGSD denote the success
degree of smart growth. The function of SGSD can be represented by

                     SDSG  w1 * IOL  w2 * SED  w3 *TQ  w4 *CD  w5 * EDL

      where wi is the weight of i th second-class indicator. The specific weights are as
follows.
