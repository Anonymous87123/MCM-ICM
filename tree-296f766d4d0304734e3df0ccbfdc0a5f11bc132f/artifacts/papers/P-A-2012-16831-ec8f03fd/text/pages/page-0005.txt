    Where
    D( X , Y ) represents the distance between X and Y,

    Centroid is the centroid of leaf.
    Cci is the point on the concave part of the margin.

    Cv j is the point on the convex part of the margin.

    N    is the total number of Cci .

    M    is the total number of Cv j .

     NR can be used to determine the degree of serration of the margin, the smaller
NR is, the sharper the serration is. Thus NR=1 means the leaf have smooth margin
and NR=0 occures when the leaf is analogical to a maple leaf.
