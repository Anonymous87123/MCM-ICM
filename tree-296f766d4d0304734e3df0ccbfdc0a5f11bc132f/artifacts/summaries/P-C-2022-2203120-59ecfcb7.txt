1	It is of great signiﬁcance for traders to buy and sell volatile assets reasonably in market transactions.
2	Traders choose diﬀerent trading strategies and get diﬀerent beneﬁts.
3	This paper studies the best trading strategies for diﬀerent traders based on the daily prices of gold and bitcoin in the past.
4	For problem 1, the original data is preprocessed ﬁrstly, including ﬁlling the missing value by the daily price of gold in the past, and standardize all the original data.
5	Following the processed data, the ARIMA model is used to establish the price prediction model.
6	Correlation coeﬃceents between the prediction results and the price of gold and bitcoin are 0.8592 and 0.9304 respectively.
7	The eﬀectiveness of our model is denoted.
8	Thirdly, a dynamic programming model with largest total assets and risk as the regular termis is established, with the constraints hit rate and transaction volume limit.
9	Lastly, the optimal daily trading strategy is solved with dynamic programming method.
10	Final proﬁt is $ 132089.1078 on September 10, 2021.
11	For problem 2, we prove our strategy is the best judged from the generalization ability, robustness and beneﬁt of the model.
12	By adjusting the variable parameters in the model, the strategies suitable for three traders are formed, which are insurance type, common type and radical type.
13	The solution method of problem 1 is used to obtain the changes of each asset and the total earnings of the three trading strategies.
14	Furthermore, the variable parameters in the model are disturbed, and the total assets change within a certain range, indicating that the model has strong anti-interference ability.
15	Finally, the assets obtained by the three types of trading strategies are $14312.6827, $50040.5474 and $132089.1078 respectively.
16	It can be seen that our model yield can resist inﬂation greatly.
17	For problem 3, ﬁrstly, the sensitivity is deﬁned as the change rate of transaction frequency.
18	By changing the transaction cost of gold and bitcoin, it is calculated that the sensitivity of gold transaction cost is 0.0422 and that of bitcoin transaction cost is 0.0658.
19	It can be seen from the comparison that the transaction strategy is more sensitive to the transaction cost of bitcoin.
20	Secondly, the impact of transaction cost on transaction frequency, asset return and asset risk are analyzed.
21	We ﬁnd that when the transaction cost increases, the transaction frequency, asset return and asset risk are decreases correspondingly.
22	For problem 4, we summarize the models and strategies established in the ﬁrst three questions, and introduce to traders in the form of memorandum how to simply use the trading strategy to trade and the beneﬁts brought by diﬀerent trading strategies.
