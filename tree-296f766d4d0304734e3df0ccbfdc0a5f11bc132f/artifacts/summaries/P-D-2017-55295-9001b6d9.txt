1	In order to improve the performance of airport security check while preserving the same security standards, we model and reﬁne this process.
2	We run extensive simulations to analyze performance of our model.
3	Speciﬁcally, For task (a), we model the airport security process with a queueing network.
4	We get the parameters of the queueing network model by ﬁtting the data in the provided data sheet to different distributions.
5	We validate our model by simulating it with the real world parameter combination and comparing its output against data collected from airport passengers.
6	We identify three bottlenecks in our model: lack of sufﬁcient ID check points; exclusive assignment of screening check points to passengers; the presence of suspicious passengers.
7	For task (b), we propose three routing algorithms to optimize the throughput and variance of the airport: greedy algorithm, backpressure algorithm, and drift-plus-penalty algorithm.
8	The best one of these algorithms achieves high throughput that is close to optimal and relatively low variance in waiting time.
9	Besides, our proposed routing al- gorithm manages to retain passenger satisfaction while scheduling.
10	We also explore the optimal ratio of different kinds of check points and test the robustness of modiﬁed mod- els.
11	For task (c), we analyze the impact of different cultures and traveler types and run simulation to see the reaction of our model.
12	The cultures we analyze are collectivist cul- tures, individualist cultures.
13	The types of travelers we analyze are family-based travelers and tech-based travelers.
14	We also propose accommodations to these special situations.
15	For task (d), we propose both globally applicable and locally applicable policies to airport security managers.
16	Our policies are based on our analysis of different cultures and types of travelers as well as our analysis of our routing algorithms.
