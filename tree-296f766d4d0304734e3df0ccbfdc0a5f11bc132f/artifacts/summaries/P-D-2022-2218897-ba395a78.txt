1	The concept of big data has gained more and more attention these years with the development of information technology.
2	Data can be friends if we treat it well.
3	In the era of data science, it is turning point determining companies to take the lead or be an underdog.
4	This paper focuses on the measurement and also the improvement of the D&A system of the International Cargo Movement cooperation.
5	In Requirement 1, we ﬁrst deﬁne several sub-indicators respectively for people, technology and process indicator for measuring the maturity level of the D&A system from a thorough and comprehensive perspective.
6	In terms of people, we measure the talent of D&A system .
7	Each indicator is quantiﬁed by an expression or a method we provide.
8	Then, we utilize mapping function to transform each indicator into a intuitive maturity score.
9	Finally, we measure the maturity quantitatively by multi- layer fuzzy comprehensive evaluation, where we use analytic hierarchy process to determine weights of indicators.
10	In Requirement 2, we construct an indicator relation network to demonstrate the connection among people, technology, and process, and we use Pagerank algorithm to determine signiﬁcant indicator in the network as the crucial point which should be paid more attention for improvement.
11	Furthermore, we give suggestions for op- timization respectively in terms of people, technology, and process.
12	Suggestions are provided according each sub-indicator, and are aimed at improving the maturity in each sub-indicator.
13	In Requirement 3, in order to propose effectiveness measuring protocols, we pri- marily establish two models.
14	We ﬁrst construct an M/M∞ data transmission queueing system based on queueing theory, where data input ﬂow is regarded as a Poisson process.
15	According to the data transmission queueing system model, the stationary distribution of data volume, and the relation between it and the data input rate as well as data processing rate of the D&A system are obtained.
16	Besides, we construct a data network model where we use the network average degree and the network diameter to measure the data cascade.
17	Eventually, we suggest protocols including ﬁve rules to evaluate the effectiveness, regarding the data processing rate, capacity, data cascade level and cascade speed of the D&A system.
18	In Requirement 4, we ﬁrst apply our maturity evaluation model in Requirement 1 to a data set generated randomly.
19	Besides, we extend our model to be more general which can be applied to different companies D&A system.
20	Besides, we estimate how costumers use the maturity metrics beneﬁt the ICM Corporation by the replicator dy- namic equation.
21	Additionally, we give some advice to help the ICM Corporation for the higher beneﬁt from the metrics.
22	Finally, we analyze the strengths and weaknesses of the model from different as- pects.
23	We also write an one-page letter for customers to outline our model and boost their conﬁdence in the D&A system of ICM corporation.
