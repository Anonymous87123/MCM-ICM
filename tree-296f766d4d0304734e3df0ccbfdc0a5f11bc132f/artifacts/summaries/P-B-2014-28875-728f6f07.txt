1	Our goal is a model that finding out the “best all time college coach”.
2	This is crucial to the promotion of the college coaching.
3	Evaluation methods must reflect the practical training level and ability scientific, accurate, comprehensive and objectively.
4	In our paper, to guarantee the fair and equitable of the selection results, comprehensively considered coaches coaching skills, experience, team management and his/her team performance and other aspects of teaching.
5	Determine the metrics such as Games, Winning percentage, Championships, etc. for assessment.
6	Then build a fuzzy comprehensive evaluation model based on improved analytic hierarchy process named IAHP-FCE model to find out The Best All Time College Coach for Sports Illustrated.
7	First of all, we applied the analytic hierarchy process (AHP) and fuzzy comprehensive evaluation method (FCE).
8	Standardized the metrics data we collected by eliminate the effects of dimension.
9	And calculate the relative membership degrees among the metrics.
10	Then according to the fuzzy evaluation matrix constructed by the single metrics relative membership degree, built the modification of judgment matrix in the AHP.
11	Secondly, calculated the weights of each metrics based on the reciprocity of judgment matrix.
12	And carry on the consistency check using the single target linear programming to calculate the consistency index coefficient by Lingo7.0.
13	Thirdly, build a fuzzy comprehensive evaluation model based on Analytic Hierarchy Process named AHP-FCE modelⅠ.
14	Using modelⅠwe can obtain the composite score of each coach.
15	Take NCAA Basketball to test the modelⅠ.
16	Finally, we got the Top 5.
17	Compare with the Authority Top 5 coaches, the accuracy of modelⅠ is 80%.
18	To achieve our goal, we need get a higher Accuracy, so we decided to improve the model by introduced the Classification thought to decompose the metrics into 2 levels.
19	Based on the modelⅠ and combined the new Classification, we built a simplified calculation and higher accuracy modelⅡ named IAHP-FCE modelⅡ.
20	Then also take NCAA Basketball to rank the Top 5, followed by Mike Krzyzewski、Dean Smith、 John Wooden、Bob Knight and Roy Williams.
21	Contrast with the Authority Ranking, the modelⅡ is correct almost 100%.
22	So modelⅡ got a great improvement.
