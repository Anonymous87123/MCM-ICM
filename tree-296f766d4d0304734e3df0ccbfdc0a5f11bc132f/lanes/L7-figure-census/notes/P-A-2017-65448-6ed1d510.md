# P-A-2017-65448-6ed1d510 — distilled rules

- Every caption sits below its art, is a bare noun phrase (voice none, no condition, no conclusion), and carries no unit and no variable of its own in all 35 exhibits; the only unit-bearing text in an exhibit is the table header [caption.position] [caption.voice] [table.units_in_header]
- Four seasonal rainfall panels share one axis-title pair and one misspelling 'Rainall/mm', and their legends run Oct-Dec, Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep, so the printed seasons are southern-hemisphere while the month labels are northern [geometry.legend]
- Two captions on one print line cannot be assigned by caption alone; bind a panel to its caption by x-position, left panel first, and check the panel order against the exhibit order on the page [place.page_order]
- Figures 3-6 and 7-8 are never cited individually: the body points at the whole run with a hyphenated range 'Figure 2-6' or 'Figure 7-8', which is the only spelling that exists in the text layer [mention.style]
- A table floated above the next section heading still belongs to the section it serves (Table 3 on p20 belongs to 5.4.2, not to the 5.4.3 heading printed under it); take the section from the printed heading order [place.section_path]
- A period form is not a caption: 'Figure 20.' at the end of a sentence on p21 is a reference, and only the colon form on p22 owns art, so numbering collisions must be resolved on the raster [caption.numbering]
- Figure 13, Figure 14 and Tables 5-12 are never named in the body, so their mentions record count 0 with an empty style list and no first page rather than a guessed lead sentence [mention.count] [mention.first_page]
- The three sensitivity tables repeat one layout - an empty stub header, the per-cent sign inside the cells, one stub rule - and each carries one degenerate row that reproduces the perturbation vector instead of a response [table.header_rows] [table.rule_style]
- Every table in this paper is open-sided three-rule: a rule above the header, under it and below the last row, at most one stub vertical and never a side rule; Table 11 drops even the stub rule [table.rule_style]
- A caption can gain characters from the text layer as well as lose them: 'Table 3: Types of each dam 5.' picks up the next heading's number, while 'Distrcts' is genuinely printed, so check the raster before 'fixing' a typo [caption.verbatim]
- One measured region can hold two exhibits (Table 11 over Table 12 on p28) and one exhibit can hold no region (Table 10 on p27), so region count is not exhibit count and the difference belongs in the note [place.doc_order]
- The strategy diagrams are single-panel vector flowcharts with no legend, no axes and no numbers: their semantics live in the diamond text, which the caption never summarises [geometry.family] [geometry.legend]
