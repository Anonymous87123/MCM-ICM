# P-A-2016-42221-e0b41765 — distilled rules

- Caption polarity is set by exhibit class in this paper and never by the individual plate: every figure caption is printed below its art, every table caption above its rules, and the one notation table has no caption at all [caption.position].
- Two plates printed side by side under a single caption are two rows with panel_count 1 each, not one row with panel_count 2: the caption is shared but the plates are separate measured regions, and a census that merges them leaves a region unaccounted [geometry.panel_count].
- In a two-column exhibit pair the two captions are welded into one interleaved text run, so neither caption is a substring of the page and both have to be typed from the crop; the same interleaving is what forces caption_source typed-from-crop on eight exhibits here [caption.verbatim].
- Split the ANSYS output into two families by device, not by software: a flat rectangular slice with cartesian ticks, axis titles and a letter 'T' over the frame is heatmap-or-contour, while a black-field perspective solid with only a colourbar and a metadata block is surface-or-3D [geometry.family].
- The same author's field renders print no axis numbers at all, only the SMN/SMX block and the colourbar ticks, so printed_numbers is true while data_labels stays false - the two flags describe the art, not the metadata block [geometry.printed_numbers].
- Shape and field are printed as a matched pair in the sensitivity sections (Figure 11 with 12, Figure 15 with 16): the wireframe of the changed object carries no colourbar and the field render that follows carries one, so colorbar is a property of the pair member and not of the section [geometry.colorbar].
- The 'best strategy to add water' table is printed six times from Table 4 to Table 9 on one identical six-column symbol-over-value grid that differs only in the five numbers, so each printing is its own row and the row/column counts are what carry the information across printings [table.rows].
- Units are run into the value cells in every table of this paper - 15J/(Ksm), 0.358m3, 319.5K - and never placed in the header band, so units_in_header is false throughout [table.units_in_header].
- Because the unit suffix rides on the number, the numeric columns share neither a decimal point nor a right edge; decimal_aligned is false in all ten tables even where a column is wholly numeric [table.decimal_aligned].
- The notation table on page 5 prints no 'Table N' caption: it is titled by the printed heading 3.2 Symbols directly above it, so the heading goes into section_path and caption_source stays none [place.section_path].
- A wide vector block of table height can be misread as an equation fragment by the extractor - the notation table carries 105 paths, area 0.316 and a fragment flag - so the area and the aspect ratio are checked against the crop before a region is refused [place.area_frac].
- The summary sheet names most of the second half of the paper in grouped lists - '(Table 6,7,8 ,Figure 13,14,16)' - so first_page is 1 for fourteen exhibits while the grouped pointer is one mention each, not one per number printed inside the brackets [mention.first_page].
- A figure placed on an earlier page than the sentence that names it yields a negative lead_pages; that is the honest encoding (exhibit page minus first mention page) and not an error to be repaired by moving first_page forward [mention.lead_pages].
- The paper misnumbers its own plates: the prose introduces the 2-D contour pair as 'shown in Figure 3', while the caption printed over the pair on the next page reads Figure 4 and Figure 3 is the geometry scheme two pages earlier. The uncaptioned pair is recorded with caption_source none and count 0, and the defect is recorded here instead of resolved by guessing [caption.numbering].

### 五个判断

1. **同一题注下的左右两片各成一行**。Figure 4 只有一行题注横跨两片，但两片是两块独立测量区域；合成 `panel_count=2` 一行会让另一个区域无处记账。按 [geometry.panel_count] 分两行，`panel_count` 均为 1。
2. **ANSYS 输出按器件分族，不按软件分**。平面切片带笛卡尔刻度与 "T" 字 → `heatmap-or-contour`（p12 两片 + Figure 4 两片）；黑底透视图只带色标与元数据块 → `surface-or-3D`（Figure 5/6/11/12/15/16/19）。见 [geometry.family]、[geometry.colorbar]。
3. **编号缺陷按原样记录，不替作者改正**。p12 正文写 "shown in Figure 3"，而 Figure 3 是两页前的示意图；p12 那两片无任何印刷题注，p13 那片题的却是 Figure 4。故 p12 两片记 `caption_source=none`、`count=0`，Figure 4 也记 `count=0`（无一句指向它），缺陷写成规则 [caption.numbering]。
4. **单位一律在数值格内**。Tables 1–9 全部把单位（100J、15J/(Ksm)、0.358m³）写在数值后，表头是裸符号，故 `units_in_header=false`、`decimal_aligned=false`（单位后缀破坏小数点与右缘对齐），即使整列全为数字。见 [table.units_in_header]、[table.decimal_aligned]。
5. **p1 r1 拒绝为表头格式元素**。裁图证实是摘要页 "Team Control Number / For office use only" 表格与印刷队号 42221（71 条矢量路径、0.165 页面积），属竞赛表单抬头，非展品。
