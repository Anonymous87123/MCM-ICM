- Five of the numbered grids have their caption on a different page from the fragment that carries their header - Table 5.2, 6.2, 6.4, 6.7 and 7.2 all break between the last data row and the caption - so the row is the TOPMOST fragment (the one holding the header) and the caption is quoted from the page that prints it [caption.position]
