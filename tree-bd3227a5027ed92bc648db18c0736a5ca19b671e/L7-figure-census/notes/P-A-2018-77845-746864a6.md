# P-A-2018-77845-746864a6 — distilled rules

- Caption side never varies and it inverts the usual table convention: all 18 figure captions and all 7 table captions print BELOW their artwork, so 25 of 25 exhibits are below-cased and the paper has no above-case to compare against [caption.position].
- One numbering vocabulary runs the whole body - 'Figure N:' for 17 numbers and 'Table N:' for 6 - a colon with no period anywhere, and the only exhibits outside it are the two appendix reprints, which print no number at all [caption.numbering].
- Captions are labels, not sentences: the 17 numbered figure captions run 8 to 41 words with a median of 18, and the four longest (Figures 5, 7, 11 and 14) are long only because they enumerate panels or parameter values rather than because they argue [caption.words].
- Only three captions name a plotted symbol, and one of them names two: the 'change of s over grazing angle' of Figure 8, the 'change of s over frequency and Sh' of Figure 9 and the '... power with Sh' of Figure 13 - so everywhere else the caption names the exhibit's subject and leaves the plotted quantity to the axis title [caption.variable_count].

- No caption in the paper concludes anything: none of the 25 states a result, a comparison or a direction of change, and the four that look like conclusions (the 'A show of the change of ...' family) stop at naming the two quantities being plotted [caption.concludes].
- Every one of the seven grids is booktabs - a top rule, one rule under the header band and a bottom rule, with no vertical rule at any cell boundary and no box - which makes this the cleanest table set in the batch after three papers of fully ruled grids [table.rule_style].
