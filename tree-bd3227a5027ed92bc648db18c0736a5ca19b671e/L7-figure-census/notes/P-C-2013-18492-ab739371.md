# P-C-2013-18492-ab739371 — distilled rules

- This paper labels every exhibit by section number - 'Figure 3-1', 'Figure 3.1.2-1', 'Table 3.1.2-1', 'Fig. 3.3.5-1', 'Figure 4.3-2' - and the `NUMBERINGS` enum knows only the flat 'Figure N' families, so every record carries numbering `none` and the printed label survives inside the quoted caption [caption.numbering].
- Nine of the twelve numbered exhibits are cited in prose by the same section-numbered label ('as shown in Fig. 3-1', 'shown in table 3.1.4-1', 'shown in Fig. 4.3-2'), which is why the mention blocks carry the literal spellings 'Fig. 3-1', 'table 3.1.4-1' and so on rather than a normalized form [mention.style].
- Five measured regions are a second box around a chart that was already measured: the p16 bar chart and the three p17/p18 situation charts each open an outer region (caption and frame) and an inner one (plot area), because the page paints an empty frame image over live vector artwork. The inner box is excluded `fragment-of-other` and the exhibit is recorded once and `panel_count` stays 1 on all four [geometry.panel_count].
- Three tables print a single internal vertical rule after the stub column with no outer verticals, and separate their rows by alternating grey fill rather than by rules, so `rule_style` is `vertical-rules` and `zebra` is true on all three [table.rule_style].
- The p7 table's index column prints bands as ranges ('0~50', '51~100') and the p9 table prints two cells as formulas ('Vn*3.0', 'Vn*0.6'), so neither table has a column that is numeric end to end [table.numeric_cols].

- The p16 chart is the lane's only extruded bar chart: `three_d` is `perspective-bars` and the bars themselves are one embedded raster inside a vector frame [geometry.three_d].
- The p14 lattice is a bare 5x5 cell grid with six cells filled, no axes and no key, so it is recorded `heatmap-or-contour` on the strength of the cell matrix alone [geometry.family].
- Nine measured regions are not exhibits: two prose bands on p7, three bands on p19 (two prose and one display equation) and the four duplicate chart boxes. `check.py` counts them as excluded, so the arithmetic stays 9 + 3 + 9 = 21, and the excluded bands are the smallest regions on their pages [place.area_frac].
- Every heading in the paper is numbered (3.1.2, 3.3.5, 4.3), so `section_path` quotes the printed heading and `section_numbered` is true throughout; p14 needs two routes because its two figures close one section and open the next [place.section_path].
