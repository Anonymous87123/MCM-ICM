4. Player’s position assumption. The manager doesn't put players in positions they're not

good at. For example, a striker will not be assigned to the position of defender, and a defender will not be assigned to the position of striker. Players' position and ability characteristics are relatively fixed in modern soccer, and we do not consider the situation where the manager does not arrange the formation according to the players' good position.
