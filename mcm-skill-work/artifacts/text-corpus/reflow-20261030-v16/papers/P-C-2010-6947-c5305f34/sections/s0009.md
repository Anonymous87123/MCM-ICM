Recommendations

Due to the extensively use of plastic in industries, just forbid the production of the plastic to abate the pollution is unrealistic. To improve the marine environment, we recommend: z Reduce the production of plastic products which will decompose into fragments or thin plastic films, such as hard plastic and plastic bags, as far as the basic demand of people can be met. z Modify the design of products or package to reduce the use of plastic. z Make plastic more durable so that it will be reused to reduce the total demand for plastic. z Make policy that banning all the promotion for plastic products. z Substitute away the toxic constituents in plastic products. z Moderately increase the tax for purchasing plastic products.

z For the area that is seriously polluted, clean up the debris with an efficient and economic method. For example, work in night to reduce the damage to the local ecosystem because the plankton abundance during the day is higher than that at night. z In future research on marine plastic pollution, much more importance should be attached to the abundance of fragments and thin plastic films. The changes of them should be monitored and used to adjust their standards. And the policies can be adjusted according to the risk standards. z Increase the funding on research of plastic degrade. z Improve the reuse of the plastic products. z Establish a comprehensive and accuracy marine pollution database for further study.
