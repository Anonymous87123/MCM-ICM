8.3 Conclusion

Changes in the number of lampreys populations are very sensitive to their reproduction coeffi- cients and sex ratios, and if it is necessary to control the number of their populations, it is possible to control the changes in the number of their populations by controlling their migratory spawn- ing, and also by changing the amount of environmental resources to control the sex ratios of their populations, thus controlling the reproduction of their populations. The effect of parasites on the population of lampreys is related to the degree of damage caused by the parasite population, and the introduction of parasites of lampreys can be considered to inhibit the growth of the population of lampreys when certain lampreys organisms are invaded.
