3.1.2 Lampreys Ecosystem

The larvae of lampreys mainly prey on zooplankton, while the mature bodies of lampreys par- asitize fish. After considering artificial fishing we divide the ecosystem where lampreys are found into three parts, and their relationships include competition, parasitism, and so on.
