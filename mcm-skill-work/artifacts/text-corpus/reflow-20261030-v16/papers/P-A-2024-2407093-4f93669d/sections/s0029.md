What we can get from Figure 11 is that:

• In Figure 11a, after suffering disturbances, the lamprey population significantly de- creases but shortly recovers to pre-disturbance levels, indicating that lamprey pop- ulations with a constant sex differentiation ratio have a certain ability to resist dis- turbances.

• In Figure 11b, the population also significantly decreases after disturbances and recovers quickly, but the post-recovery population dynamics slightly differ from the pre-disturbance state.This phenomenon shows that lamprey populations with a variable sex differentiation ratio also possess resilience to disturbances.

• The advantage of variable sex differentiation ratio populations is their lower devia- tion from normal conditions and faster recovery, suggesting greater stability under disturbance. Small-scale fishing of lampreys for consumption purposes can enable the sustainable survival of lamprey populations. This model will be further refined and quantitatively analyzed for its disturbance resistance in Section 5.3.

• The resilience could be a disadvantage when human interventions aim to control invasive lamprey populations for ecosystem balance, as their stability makes them challenging to manage, potentially negatively impacting local fisheries and lake economies.
