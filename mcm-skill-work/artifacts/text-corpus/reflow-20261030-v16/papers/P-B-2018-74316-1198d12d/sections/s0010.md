2.1.2 Assumptions

(1) The time distribution of some native speakers is determined by the pattern of the population distribution. The factors besides population changing are neglected. (2) The time distribution of non-native speakers is determined by the serial correlation, population migration and those factors, including international business relations, increased global tourism, the use of electronic communication and social media, and the use of technology to assist in quick and easy language translation. (3) There are some factors that have the first co-integration relationship with the non-native speakers.
