3.5.2 Historical Analysis

Based on the above analysis of data in diﬀerent years, we come to the following conclusions:

In 2000, the coeﬃcient of variation of asset is 1.2437, and the coeﬃcient of variation of achievement is 0.5766;

In 2020, the coeﬃcient of variation of asset is 1.5332, and the coeﬃcient of variation of achievement is 0.6912.

From the data, we can ﬁnd that from 2000 to 2020, the coeﬃcient of variation(CV) of achievement index and asset index has increased, indicating that the degree of dispersion has become larger, the gap between the rich and the poor has intensiﬁed, and global inequity has become more serious.

According to the World Bank’s World Development Report 2020, the global gap between the rich and the poor is widening. Climate change, scientiﬁc and technological development, population change, informatization and other factors will exacerbate global inequity. This also veriﬁes the credibility of our model.
