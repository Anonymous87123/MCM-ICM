5.1.4 Inverse Problem Solving

Based on observational data, such as step profile measurements hobs(x, y) or multi-time-point laser scans, we define a set of unknown parameters Θ = {R, (tr1, δ1, κ1), . . . , (trR, δR, κR)}, where R repre- sents the number of renovations. These parameters are used in the previously established multi-layer PDE model with free boundary conditions, forming the basis for solving the optimization problem. By inputting them into Model 2, the problem can be solved.

The multi-layer model provides a more accurate and realistic representation of the wear and ren- ovation processes, effectively capturing the dynamic interactions between layers.
