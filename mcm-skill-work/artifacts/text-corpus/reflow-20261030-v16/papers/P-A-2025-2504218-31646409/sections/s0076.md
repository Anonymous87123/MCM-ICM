5.1.3 Penetration conditions

Each layer behaves as follows:

( hi(x, y, t) < δi ⇒The layer remains intact and can still be stepped on, unless covered by a layer above it.

If a layer Lj overlaps above Li, then Li will not accumulate any further wear during this time. Once Lj is completely worn out or removed, Li will once again become the "top layer" and continue to accumulate wear.
