2. Differential Wear and Front/Rear 2/3 Partitioning:

Using a single “entire-foot” contact profile cannot capture the marked discrepancy between “front-foot dominance upstairs, rear-foot dominance downstairs,” leading to inaccuracies in sim- ulating wear on the front vs. rear edges of a stair. To better represent these biomechanical differ- ences, we employ binary images of the front 2/3 mapped to ϕup(x, y) (upstairs) and the rear 2/3 mapped to ϕdown(x, y) (downstairs), each corresponding to its primary load-bearing zone.
