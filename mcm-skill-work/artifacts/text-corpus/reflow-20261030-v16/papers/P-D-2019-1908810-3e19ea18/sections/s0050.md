8.2 Leave-One-Out Analysis

We also saw in our sensitivity analysis that it is desirable for an emergency exit plan to be robust to obstructions. We have a means of quantifying robustness in our simulation by removing connections and determining evacuation time. The increase in evacuation time (∆T) measures how critical that connection is to evacuation and how robust the evacuation plan is to emergencies with obstructions. Evacuation plans could be altered to reduce this ∆T for connections deemed most vulnerable or likely to be attacked.
