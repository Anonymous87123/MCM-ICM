Algorithm 4 Assign Channels

Require: RepeaterAdjacencies, the adjacency matrix for our repeater net- work. Ensure: Channels contains a list of the channel assignments of each re- peater such that each repeater is of the proper channel to communicate with its neighbor closest to the hub of the network.

Find the repeater with the highest closeness centrality in the network.

This is our hub repeater. Assign it channel 1.

Let CurrentChannel = 1.

Let ConnectedRepeaters contain all of the repeaters connected to the hub.

while At least one repeater does not have a channel do Let NextConnectedRepeaters be an empty array for all Repeaters in ConnectedRepeaters do Assign the repeater to channel CurrentChannel + 1.

Add all of the repeaters connected to the repeater that do not have channels to NextConnectedRepeaters end for Increment CurrentChannel Let ConnectedRepeaters equal NextConnectedRepeaters end while
