4.2.2 Snow Friction and Collision

Now we take the collision into consideration. Here we ﬁx the landing point x0 = 0.5. By re-conducting the calculation before, we get Fig. 8. It is shown that for each b, Et has a peak value in the interval [x0, w], and the peak shifts to the right when b becomes larger. On the other hand, for each a in Fig. 8, Et ever increases as b gets larger. In this case, our conclusion is that the longer b is, the smaller the energy loss there will be, but the value for a is inside the interval [x0, w] and is determined by related parameters.
