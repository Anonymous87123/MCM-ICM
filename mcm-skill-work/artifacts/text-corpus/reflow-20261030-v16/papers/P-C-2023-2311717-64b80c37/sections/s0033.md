6.1.3 Bagging-based prediction model with integrated BP neural network

校苑数模 In the training of the BP neural network, we randomly selected the training set according to 85%. Therefore, we decided to adopt the Bagging algorithm to integrate multiple BP neural networks. Then, the 校苑数模 final results are derived through a hard voting mechanism, which has the effect of reducing the generalization error of the prediction results.

In the integration algorithm, we obtain different sub-training sets by randomly sam- pling 85% of the overall data times, and each time the percentage of sampling is also 85%. Then, neural networks are obtained based on these sub-training sets trained with the same 校苑数模 parameters. Next, these neural networks are used to predict the distribution of word guesses. All neural networks output their respective predictions directly and the final prediction is ob- 模 tained by a minority-majority voting mechanism [8].
