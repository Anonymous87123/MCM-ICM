9.2 Further discussion

①Although the solutions in Part 1, to some extent, are not realistic, the method limiting the orbits and distributing the campsite sets for each type of trip is valuable and could be adjusted to be used in practical applications. ②Using the model in Part 2, the government could do some practical research and questionnaire surveys in order to find the more accurate probability, describing the behavior of tourists’ choice of their own route, than the classical probability. ③In Part 3, we assume that all available trips provided to tourists share the same probability, which may be not realistic. However, the government and the river agency could also do some relevant research to find out which types of trips the tourists more likely to prefer, and use the results in the model in Part 3.
