2. General Assumption

●Assumption I: The distance between any vehicle gathering point and charging station is the straight line distance between two points. ●Assumption II: Electric vehicles in class j are evenly distributed, but different classes of areas have different densities of distribution. Reason: From the national level, even though the density distribution of vehicles in urban, suburban and rural areas is different, they can be regarded as uniform compared to the entire land area ●Assumption III: No matter how many electric piles are set up in a charging station, it will not affect the charging of electric vehicle and the electricity consumption of the surrounding residents
