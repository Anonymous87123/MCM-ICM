6.2.2 Electricity Market Integration

As the compact mainly focuses on renewable and clean energy, we don’t con- sider other kinds of energy. Renewable energy source has four major compo- nents: biomass, hydropower, wind power and solar thermal. The four states don’t have much differences in biomass and hydropower has already reached its market saturation. However, the four states differ signifantly in wind power and solar thermal for the reason of different altitude and geology. As a result, the four states have different radiation intensity, wind speed and so on, which means they have different potential ability wind and solar energy. Thus, we only pay attention to the cooperation on the last two type of energy.

After integration, states can buy energy from others with lower price while states who provide energy earn money when building more plants.
