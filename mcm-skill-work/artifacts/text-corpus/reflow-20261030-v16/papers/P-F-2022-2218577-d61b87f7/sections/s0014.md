3.1.2 Energy Equity

Limited resources cannot satisfy the inﬁnite desires of human beings, which is a view generally agreed by economists, and the same is true for energy. Energy equity is reﬂected in two aspects, one is the intergenerational energy equity, that is, the issue of energy sustainability; the other is the international energy equity, that is, the distribution of energy in the world.
