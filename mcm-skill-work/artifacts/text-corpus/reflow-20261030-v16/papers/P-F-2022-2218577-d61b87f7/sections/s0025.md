2. Coeﬃcient of Variation Method

The coeﬃcient of variation method is also a method of objective weighting. Among the indicators, the data changes under some indicators have large diﬀerences, and the data changes under some indicators have small diﬀerences. The data that has a greater impact on the subject should be the data with large changes, and the corresponding indicators are of higher importance and should be given higher weights. The formula for calculating the coeﬃcient of variation is

cvi = SDi xi

Where SDi is the standard deviation of a set of data, and ¯xi is the mean of a set of data. Then the weight calculated by the coeﬃcient of variation method is

cvi Wcvj = Pn i=1 cvi

Taking the sustainable development index as an example, we get that its coeﬃcient of variation is 0.3102, and its weight is 0.031.
