8.1 Strengths

Our model achieves a good simulation of the process of single-hop and multi-hop propagation for high-frequency radio waves.

Our model takes into account the effects of calm seas and turbulent seas as well as different terrains.

The model gives a scheme for the ship to be able to receive the signal continuously, and analyzes the length of the time the ship can remain receiving the signal.

The model can be widely applied to the design of radio wave propagation path and the decision of frequency.
