Strengths

The biggest strength that our model has is its high eﬃciency of 98.8%. This is very good since the best possible eﬃciency is around 99%, due to the fact that no boat can reach the end of the river on the ﬁrst day, leaving some unused campsites on the ﬁrst few and last few days. Our model also gives the smallest amount of crossovers for a randomly selected group of trips. The reason that this is the case is because our priority scheduling prevents a boat from overtaking another unless the boat has a lower number of days left on its journey. To maintain a wide variety of trip lengths, this situation is inevitable.
