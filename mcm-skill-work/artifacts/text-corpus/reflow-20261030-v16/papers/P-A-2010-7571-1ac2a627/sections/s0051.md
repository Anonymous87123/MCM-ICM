 The Sweet Zone of an aluminum bat is [43.95，52.77], with a span of 8.82cm.

 The Sweet Zone of a wooden bat is [46.09，53.87], with a span of 7.78cm.

 Clearly, the span of Sweet Zone for an aluminum bat is 1.1cm longer than that for a wooden bat, which means that an aluminum bat is easier to control since it provides higher probability for a batter to obtain an expected exit velocity.
