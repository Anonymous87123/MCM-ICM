2.5.2 Assumptions

⚫ One of the three states of culture happens every year: boom, stable, recession ⚫ The state that happens in the next year depends on current state and education, percent change in GDP growth rate, inundated land, and Tourism

This means P(𝑋𝑡+1|𝑋𝑡, 𝑂𝑡ℎⅇ𝑟𝑡, 𝑋𝑡−1,𝑂𝑡ℎⅇ𝑟𝑡−1, 𝑋𝑡−2 … . ) = P(𝑋𝑡+1|𝑋𝑡, 𝑂𝑡ℎⅇ𝑟𝑡)

⚫ IL(inundated land) happens at each term, while the ED(education), DE(development), BU(buffer) in the future are random variables and obey the same Gaussian distribution. ⚫ Every variable generate a possibility based on different information. ⚫ In a very long time, a culture will ultimately disappear.
