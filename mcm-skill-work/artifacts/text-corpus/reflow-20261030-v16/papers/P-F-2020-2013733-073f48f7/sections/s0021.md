2.5.1 Basic Idea

Development(div2 GDP) Since cultures are products of human Climate(drown land) interactions, we develop our model in this Interaction(Tourism) part with the core ideal, “people”. We use education to measure the internal motivation of a culture, adopt the change in

GDP growth rate to describe the tendency of the culture; then we use the inundated area each years divided by the remaining land to illustrate direct climate pressure. At last, we use the tourism GDP (%) as an index of culture interaction in our modeling. As the future is unpredictable, we set the whole process as a completely random one.
