From the previous analysis, we take advantage of GM and BP-ANN to establish a forecasting model. 
In details, we select the Accumulated Generating Operation (AGO) and Inverse Accumulated 
Generating Operation(IAGO) from GM, and use BP-ANN to solve out the parameters and make the 
final prediction.

so in 2020 EVs will have been in large scale use.
