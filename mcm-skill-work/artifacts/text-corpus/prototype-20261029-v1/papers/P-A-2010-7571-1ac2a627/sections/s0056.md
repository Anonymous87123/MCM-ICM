Basic conclusions we get:

 The location of Sweet Spot stays stable with the change of t.

 The exit velocity decreases with the increase of t.

 Hence, producer of aluminum bats should try to make the wall thinner in order

to raise the exit speed for the bat. However, there is a lower limit of the wall thickness since too thin the wall is will make the material easily bent and the coefficient of resistant will turn to be very low.
