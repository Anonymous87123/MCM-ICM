5.1 Fishing Area

According to Jansen T's (2012) [2] study, fishing boats can catch quantity of fish within 90 km of the edge of the continental shelf, while at 120 km there are few fishing boats. As the distance is too far away, the fishing boats have problems such as fish preservation, safety and the longest voyage distance. Therefore, it is set in this paper that the fishermen fish within 120km from the mainland. The fishing area around Scotland is shown in the figure below.

As mentioned above, we have the number of different kinds of fish that can be caught in different regions. We can approximately consider the number of fish in the center as the maximum number of fish that can be caught, and the number of fish around it is positively correlated with the total number of fish, which also decreases at the rate of normal distribution. As long as the shoal of fish is within the range of the fisherman's range, the total is the number of fish the fisherman can catch.
