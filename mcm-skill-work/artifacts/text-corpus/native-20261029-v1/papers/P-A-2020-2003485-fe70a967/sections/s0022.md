7.2 Friendly Exchanges and Cooperation in Fishing

According to previous analysis, herring schools will stay in Icelandic territorial waters for 29 years, and mackerel schools will remain in Norwegian territorial waters for 11 years, which is

undoubtedly a huge blow to the Scottish fishery. According to international easements, active easement is the obligation of a country to allow other countries to engage in certain activities, such as fishing, on its own territory. So for the Scottish economy, the Scottish government should actively negotiate with Norway and Iceland to develop a common fishing policy.
