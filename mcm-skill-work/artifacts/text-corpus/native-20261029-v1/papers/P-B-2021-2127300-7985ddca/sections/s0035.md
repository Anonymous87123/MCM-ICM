7.2 Weakness

 In problem 2, we ignored the change of the fire between areas and expand by proportion regarlessly. Thus the model is still an approximate on a large scale. This has doomed to limit the applications of it.  In the prediction the future fire event, we only used fire data in 2019-2020 for regression prediction, so data quantity is too little. Besides, There are many factors affecting fire, such as artificial fire fighting measures, etc., which reduce the accuracy of fire prediction.
