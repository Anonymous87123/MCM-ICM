We use data from 2009-2013 to forecast the comprehensive performances on environmental, social, and economic aspects for different countries based on the model introduced in section 2.3. An analytical-least square method and function ODE45 are used to determine the coef- ﬁcients in our forecast model. countries which have a low performance but is improving, and countries which maintain a low performance now and in future. A 20-year prediction of three sample countries are shown in Figures 3-4.
