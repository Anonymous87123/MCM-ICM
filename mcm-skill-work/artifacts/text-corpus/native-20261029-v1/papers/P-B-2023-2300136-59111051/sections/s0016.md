2) Supplementary assumptions and justifications

⚫ When considering wildlife numbers and biodiversity, we use the number/diversity of

mammals as an indicator for all wildlife.

Data on large mammals are more accessible as they are listed in various rangeland reports based on aerial surveillance. The exact number and species of reptiles/amphibians, birds and smaller mammals are

difficult to obtain. We believe that the richness in mammal numbers/species can indicate the health of a bio-ecosystem.
