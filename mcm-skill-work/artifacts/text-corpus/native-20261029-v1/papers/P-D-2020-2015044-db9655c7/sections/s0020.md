Conclusions of the Problem

We analyze the teamworks of Huskies soccer team based on passing networks. The

passing networks are constructed from pass events during the games, which enable us

to analyze the team behavior qualitatively and quantitatively. We propose a perfor-

mance indicator to measure the success of teamworks from three aspects: structural,

configurational and spatial-temporal. Based on the performance scores, we give advice
