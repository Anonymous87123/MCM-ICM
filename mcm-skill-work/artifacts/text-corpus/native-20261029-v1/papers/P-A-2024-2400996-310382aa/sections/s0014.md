Assumptions and Justiﬁcations

• Assumption 1: The time for adult ﬁsh to migrate, lay eggs, die, and hatch into larval is negligible. Justiﬁcation: Our model calculates in years, so ﬁner time scales will not be con- sidered. The only time we primarily consider is the time it takes for a larval to grow into an adult, which will be counted directly as a yearling after spawning.

• Assumption 2: Sea lampreys die immediately after spawning.

Justiﬁcation: Based on our review of the literature [7], lampreys die after spawn- ing in a short time, so we neglect the time after them ﬁnish spawning.
