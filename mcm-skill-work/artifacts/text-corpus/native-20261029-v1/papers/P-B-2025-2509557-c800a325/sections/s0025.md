The following Figure 7 highlights the dynamic feedback loops within our model by revealing the relationships among tourist volume, infrastructure level, environmental degradation, resident satisfaction, tax revenue, and tax allocation. The model emphasizes the importance of achieving a balance between economic growth, environmental protection, and social well-being. For in- stance, higher tax revenues can support investments in infrastructure and environmental protection, thereby enhancing resident satisfaction and maintaining the attractiveness of tourist destinations. Conversely, uncontrolled tourist volume may exacerbate environmental degradation, threatening long-term sustainability. This framework provides a quantitative basis and policy guidance for achieving equilibrium in tourism development.
