3.7 Scenario Simulation

• Policy Independence:Different policies (e.g., increasing environmental investment, opti- mizing traffic management) are independent of each other, and their effects can be superim- posed directly.

• Short-term and Long-term Effects:Policies may cause system fluctuations in the short term but can achieve a new steady state in the long term.
