According to the position model established in the previous section, when the submersible loses contact with the main ship, it may sink to the seaﬂoor, remain at certain neutral buoyancy points, or rise to the sea surface depending on diﬀerent failure scenarios. This presents varied requirements for search and rescue operations. We deﬁne regions with depths below 3000 meters as deep-water zones and those above 3000 meters as shallow-water zones. To address all possible situations, we have researched equipment designed for search and rescue in surface, shallow-water, and deep-water areas.
