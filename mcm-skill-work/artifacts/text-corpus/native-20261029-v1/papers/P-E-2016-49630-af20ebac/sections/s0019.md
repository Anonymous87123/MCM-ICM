Strengths

1. Our metric for the ability to manage water scarcity, TSM, and its two components, PSM and ESM are easily calculable and encompass nearly all drivers. Besides, the data re- quired in TSM model is available in the database of international organizations. Thus it can be easily applied to identify water scarcity level for most countries.

2. We apply Grey Relational Analysis to determine the weights in TSM model, which avoids the interference of human factors. Thus the model is objective and convincing.

3. In the factor forecasting, both the Grey Forecasting Model and Regression Analysis we introduce have little error, implying a good performance of our prediction.
