1.1 Defining the Problem

● How should trips of varying length and propulsion be scheduled to maximize the number trips possible over a six month season? ● How many new groups can start a river trip on any given day? ● What is the carrying capacity of the river?

○ We define carrying capacity as the maximum number of groups which can be sent down the river during its six month season.
