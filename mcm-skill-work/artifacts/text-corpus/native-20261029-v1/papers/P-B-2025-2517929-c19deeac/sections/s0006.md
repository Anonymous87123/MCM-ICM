2 Assumptions and Justifications

⚫ Assumption 1: The structure of the tourism market and the spending preferences of tourists remain relatively stable. Justification: Based on the empirical dimension, we assume that the consumer market and preferences are stable. Avoiding the instability of the model due to frequent changes in market structure and consumption preferences facilitates the construction and analysis of the model.
