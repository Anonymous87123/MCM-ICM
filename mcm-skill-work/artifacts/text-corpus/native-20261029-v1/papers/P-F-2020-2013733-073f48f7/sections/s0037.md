3.5.1 Basic idea

After selecting the immigration area, we will calculate all costs and find countries to pay for it. And we will build a model to analyze how much each area will pay. Our charging model assumes that before the necessary immigration, people on the island are relatively free to move to other similar areas. For those similar areas, the additional social security and other burdens brought by immigrants have the nearly same amount with the economic contributions they bring. Thus the only direct economic cost is just new houses and the compensation for those who choose to be paid in cash.(It will be further discussed later.) But our world needs to pay more. In order to preserve culture, it is helpful to establish a database of pictures and videos, and even museums if necessary. Besides, cultural activities and festivals may need to be held regularly.
