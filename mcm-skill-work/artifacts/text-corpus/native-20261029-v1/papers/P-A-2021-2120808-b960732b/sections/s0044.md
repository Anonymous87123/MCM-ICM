1.2 Fungi’s Role in Ecosystem

For a long-term sustainable ecosystem, there must be producers, consumers, and decomposers. Producers are creatures including green plants and autotrophic microorganisms (for example, thiobacil- lus, nitrifying bacteria, cyanobacteria and so on), providing materials and energies for various organ- isms in the ecosystem.
