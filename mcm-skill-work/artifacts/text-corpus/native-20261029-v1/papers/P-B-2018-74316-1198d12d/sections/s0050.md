1. Languages spread in 2 ways, the variety of the number of native speakers and the non-native

speakers. In the short-term, the distribution of the former is mainly reflected in the temporal sequence correlation. The temporal sequence of most L1 speakers of most languages is a non- stationary unit root process, which increases as the local population increases except Russian. The temporal distribution of the latter is related to all kinds of influences and factors. We think that economic, social, welfare and cultural factors have a positive impact on the number of non-native speakers and the total number of speakers.
