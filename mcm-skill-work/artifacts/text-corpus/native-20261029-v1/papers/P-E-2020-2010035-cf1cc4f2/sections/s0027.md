In this section, we devise a model PWH (Production-Waste-Harm) to simulate the

process of annual Production, usage and consequent Waste, and the Harm, or pol- lution done to the environment by plastics. In this model, we distinguish 2 kinds of plastics: single-use or disposable plastics and other plastics.

In terms of programming, we adopt the Dynamic Programming instead of recur- sive prediction. This way, calculation efﬁciency and accuracy of the model can be largely improved while solving the optimization problems.

Input data collected and used in the PWH model include annual plastic production, recycle rate, incinerate rate, discarded rate and different plastic’s life-time information from [1] and [5].
