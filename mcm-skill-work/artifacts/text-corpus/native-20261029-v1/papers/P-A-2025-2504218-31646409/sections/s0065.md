Weaknesses

• The combined use of multiple sub-models and Bayesian priors requires managing many param- eters, which can be computationally demanding.

• Accurate 3D stair-surface data are essential. Sparse or low-quality measurements limit the preci- sion of the PDE and probabilistic models.

• Imposing a second-order gradient penalty enforces global smoothness but might overlook sud- den wear anomalies or abrupt material changes.
