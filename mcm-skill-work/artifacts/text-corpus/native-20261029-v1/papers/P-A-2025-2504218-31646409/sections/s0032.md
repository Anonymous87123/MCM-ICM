Foot Partition-based Travel Direction Model (FPTDM)

The proposed FPTDM relies on the following observation: when walking upstairs, the primary load-bearing and contact area of the foot is in the front two-thirds, whereas when walking downstairs, the main load-bearing region shifts to the rear two-thirds. By extracting the contact patterns from these two binary images—one representing the “front two-thirds” of the foot and the other the “rear two-thirds”—and incorporating flow rates of people going upstairs and downstairs, we can formulate a partial differential equation that describes staircase wear.
