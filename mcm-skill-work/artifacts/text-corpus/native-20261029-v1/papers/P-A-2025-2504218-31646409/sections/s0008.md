2. Assumption: The steps are assumed to be ideal horizontal planes, and any surface inclination

caused by depressions in the stairs can be neglected. Justification: In the context of archaeology, long-term foot traffic, varying walking patterns, and environmental erosion contribute to increased surface complexity, unnecessarily complicating the analysis. Therefore, the idealized horizontal plane assumption simplifies the problem.
