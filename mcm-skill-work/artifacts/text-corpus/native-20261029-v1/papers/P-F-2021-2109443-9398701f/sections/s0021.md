We introduce the Education Power (EP) as the overall description of a country’s higher education level.

The entropy weight method (EWM) is commonly used as a weighting method that measures value dispersion in decision-making. It assumes that the greater the degree of disper- sion, the greater the degree of differentiation, and more information can be derived.
