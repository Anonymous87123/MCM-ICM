4. We assume that the health status of a country's higher education system can be

comprehensively and scientifically reflected through limited and reasonably selected in- dicators.

Justification: The health of a country's higher education system is determined by many factors. Because of the interaction between these factors, some factors can be eliminated with- out affecting the evaluation effect, and finally a limited number of factors that can fully reflect the health status can be screened out.
