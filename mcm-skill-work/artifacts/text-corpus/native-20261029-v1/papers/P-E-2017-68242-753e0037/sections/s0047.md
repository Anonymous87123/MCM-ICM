8.2 Weakness

 Data deviation: data we collect is from multiple websites, the difference in statistical

standards may result in bias conclusions. What’s more, the lack of data for more indicators may lead to the errors in evaluation system.  Lack of reference cities: the number of the cities we use as reference standard may be

small, which may be not able to sufficiently support our analysis.  Subjectivity: a lot of subjective methods are in the model, and some indicators are born in

terms of our own experience and intuition. Some may be not credible.
