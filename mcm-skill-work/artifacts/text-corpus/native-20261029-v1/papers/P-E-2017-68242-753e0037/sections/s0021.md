After browsing web about environmental topics[6] in EPA, we realize that environmental

sustainability contains key words such as air, chemicals, climate change, greener living, land, waste and cleanup, water, as well as many other topics. We classify these topics into three categories. The first is environmental quality, the second is utility of land, and the last one is multiple transportation. We show this relationship in figure 5.
