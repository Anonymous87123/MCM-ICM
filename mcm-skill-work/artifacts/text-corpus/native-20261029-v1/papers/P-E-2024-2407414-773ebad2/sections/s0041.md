Sensitivity Analysis

Assuming that the initial capital of insurance companies remains constant and market conditions remain unchanged, we aim to calculate the probability of ruin by considering the impact of extreme weather events. To ensure the accuracy and rigor of our data and results, we introduce a perturbation of 20% to the initial capital of insurance companies (s) and the compensation ratio of insurance companies (αd). This allows us to observe the effect of parameter changes on the results by comparing the modified model with the original one after solving for 40 sets of probability of ruin. Additionally, we introduce noise to our indicators to assess the model’s robustness against random fluctuations.
