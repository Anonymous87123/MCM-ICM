Organic Agriculture Impacts Evaluation (OAIE) Model

The adoption of environmentally sustainable practices is an inevitable consequence of the pur- suit of sustainable development. In this section, a comprehensive fuzzy integrated evaluation sys- tem of the impacts of organic agriculture is established. This system is based on real data collected on organic and conventional agriculture, selecting multi-level indicators. Building upon the find- ings of prior studies, this analysis will assess the broader ecosystem impacts of organic agriculture.
