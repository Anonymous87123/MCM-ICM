2. Penalty for future damage caused by present actions. One reason that so much

harm has been done to the environment is that, when people make decisions, they only consider the immediate beneﬁt and/or costs of their decision. When a person uses a single-use water battle, they do not consider the environmental damage that will result from their action. However, through our seabird population model, we are able to quantify the future harm that will be caused by current plastic consumption. By providing a concrete prediction of future costs, governments and people can make better informed decisions about their plastic consumption
