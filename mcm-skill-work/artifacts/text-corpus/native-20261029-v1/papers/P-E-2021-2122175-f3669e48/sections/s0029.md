4.3.2 coefficient of variation method

The basic idea of CVM is: in the multi-indicator comprehensive evaluation, if an indicator has a large degree of variation in the observed values of all the evaluated objects, indicates that it is difficult for the indicator to reach the average level when executed by the evaluation com- pany, which would make it possible to clearly distinguish the competence of each evaluation object in the specific area, then the weights of indicators should be given greater. On the con- trary, a smaller weight should be given.
