Energy is a very intuitive indicator to respond to the risk of light pollution. With the economic de- velopment and urbanization promotion, leisure areas and commercial concentrated areas mainly to en- hance urban night scenery are increasing year by year, which to some extent leads to the proliferation of artificial light at night and causes serious light pollution [4].Power consumption is a major secondary indicator, and in addition, through the policy of light pollution reduction around the world, we know that reducing the brightness of indoor and outdoor lights and limiting commercial areas LED lighting levels are the main strategy at present [4][5], so in this paper, Power consumption and area average light inten- sity are selected as two secondary indicators of energy index.
