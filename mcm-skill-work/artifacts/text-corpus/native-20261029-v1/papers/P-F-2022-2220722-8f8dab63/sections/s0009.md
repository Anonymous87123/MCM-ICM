2. General Assumption and explanation

To simplify our problems, we make the following assumptions:  Assumption 1: Each country could be regarded as a macro unit in analyzing the global equity and job distribution of asteroid mining. - Due to the limited resources and technology in several developing countries, some may not be able to take part in the process of asteroid mining. To simplify the actual situation, we assume that each country could be regarded as a unit in our models. 
