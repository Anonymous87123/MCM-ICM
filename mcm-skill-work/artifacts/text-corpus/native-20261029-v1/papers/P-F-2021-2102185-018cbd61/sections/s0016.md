General Assumption

1. Assume that the number of higher education institutions (HEIs) in a country is relatively stable. Generally speaking, the period for preparation of setting up a new university is long, so the number of universities will not ﬂuctuate too much in a short term;

2. It is assumed that there is a uniﬁed standard for deﬁning the government’s education expenditure among countries. Due to the diﬀerence of education policies, it is inevitable that diﬀerent countries will adopt diﬀerent standards when calculating the government’s higher education investment. Therefore, in order to make the selected factors describe the model more accurately, it is necessary to unify the standards of diﬀerent countries;
