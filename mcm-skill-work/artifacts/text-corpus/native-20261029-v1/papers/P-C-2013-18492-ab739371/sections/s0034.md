5.2 Weakness

 Our model is sound, creative and exciting; however, we do not have the necessary data to verify our model although we have discussed the data requirement. Hence, the correctness of our model remains to be verified. Besides, we use the resource amount of an area before industrial evolution as the upper bound of the quantized resource value, which is not a very accurate measurement.  Since the measurement of critical nodes and links are numeric values, we can only get a rank list of critical nodes and links respectively without a clear criterion whether a node or link is critical or not. Also, in order to obtain a joint nodal health measurement considering different importance of three environmental factors, we use analytical hierarchy process, which introduces subjective impacts into the model.
