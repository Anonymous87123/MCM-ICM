Assumptions: The national development directions set by countries after adopting the

evaluation criteria of GGDP will be planned strictly according to the new criteria.

▷Justification: The criteria for evaluating the health of a country’s economy are cen- tral to a country’s development planning, and changes in the criteria will affect the direction of the country’s development.

Assumptions: The world will not experience any more sudden global events such as large-scale regional conflicts, wars or economic crises within the time horizon main- tained by the vision.

▷Justification: The presence of war and economic crisis will perturb the results of our predictions and deviate from the objectives and directions of our study.
