4.1.1 Plantation Source

Planting is one of the main components of agriculture. It is an agricultural production department that cultivates various crops and obtains plant products. The crops cultivated by the planting industry include the production of food crops, cash crops, vegetable crops, fodder crops, pastures, flowers and other plants. Here, considering most of the developing countries in the world, such as China, India, and countries in Southeast Asia, the main source of food for the people is the food crops in the planting industry.
