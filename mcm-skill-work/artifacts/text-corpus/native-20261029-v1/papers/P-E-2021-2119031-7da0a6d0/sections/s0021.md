5.2.1 Calculation of The Total Cost of The Carrier

The transporter will have a certain freight cost when transporting across different areas. It should include transportation fuel costs and labor costs. We assume that the fuel cost is propor- tional to the transportation distance, and should also be proportional to the weight of the goods transported. The labor cost is directly proportional to the transportation time. We ignore possi- ble traffic jams and traffic accidents on the transportation road, and believe that the transporta- tion time is also proportional to the transportation distance.
