3. Arrivals are uniformly random.

According to the given data, the arrival times of travelers do not follow any clear pattern within short time periods (Figure 3). Thus, the best way to model arrivals is to generate travelers with a constant probability. The probability itself may vary between regular and Pre-Check arrivals, as well as with total traﬃc volumes.
