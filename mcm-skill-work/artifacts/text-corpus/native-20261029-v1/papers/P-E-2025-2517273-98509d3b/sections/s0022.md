The soil organic matter dynamics equation (dO/dt) represents the belowground processes, incorporating organic matter input, plant contributions, and decomposition rates. This equation is particularly significant as it connects aboveground and belowground ecosystem components through explicit mathematical relationships. The inclusion of microbial interactions reflects the important role of soil microorganisms in organic matter cycling.
