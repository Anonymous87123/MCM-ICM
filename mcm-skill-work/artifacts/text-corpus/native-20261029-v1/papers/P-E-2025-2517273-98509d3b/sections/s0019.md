The plant biomass dynamics equation (dP/dt) incorporates a modified logistic growth function where the carrying capacity Kp(S,M) is dependent on both soil organic matter and microbial populations. This dynamic carrying capacity reflects the ecosystem's adaptive nature and represents how soil conditions directly influence plant growth potential. The equation also accounts for plant-insect interactions through predation terms and includes positive feedback from soil and microbial communities.
