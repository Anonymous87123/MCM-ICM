The soil microbial activity equation (dM/dt) describes microbial population dynamics with a growth rate dependent on soil organic matter availability. The carrying capacity Km(S) is a function of soil organic matter, representing the substrate-dependent nature of microbial growth. This component completes the soil- microbe-plant feedback loop essential for ecosystem functioning.
