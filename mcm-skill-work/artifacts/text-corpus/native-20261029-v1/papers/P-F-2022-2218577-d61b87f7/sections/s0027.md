3. Combination Weight Calculation

Sometimes, the index weight determined by the entropy weight method has the defect of equal- ization. In order to avoid this problem, we adopt the method of combining entropy weight and coeﬃcient of variation to obtain the index weight. Assuming that the preference coeﬃcient of the weight is λ, the combined weight is

Wj = λWj + (1 −λ) Wcvj c

λ ∈(0, 1)
