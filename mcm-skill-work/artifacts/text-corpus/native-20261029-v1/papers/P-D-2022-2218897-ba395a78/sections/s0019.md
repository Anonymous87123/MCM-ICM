7. Whether consider a combination of hiring, contracting and training? This issues

needs to be generally considered in combination with income, turnover indicator, pro- ﬁciency indicator and ability indicator. In detail,

• Conditions of low turnover rate, abundant income, low proﬁciency or ability, consider a combination of hiring and training.

• Conditions of high turnover rate, insufﬁcient income, low proﬁciency consider a combination of contracting and training.

• Conditions of middle-level turnover rate, middle-level income, low ability, middle- level proﬁciency, consider a combination of hiring, contracting and training.
