The Beneﬁt to the Seaport from the Customers’ Use of the Metric 6.2

By adding the indicators based on the customers’ type, the proposed maturity eval- uation model can be easily extended and be applied to different kinds of costumers,

like the trucking company and the ship company. When the customers use the same metric as the seaport, the corporation efﬁciency will be improved, which brings both the costumers and the seaport considerable utility. However, whether to use this met- ric only depends on the costumers. As is known to all, changing or building up a new metric is a high cost work. If costumers’ utility cannot make up for the expenses, they won’t use this metric. Let’s denote the utility and the cost as α and β separately. Then, the costumers who use this metric get the payoff α −β, and those who do not use this metric get 0. We deﬁne the beneﬁt of the ICM Corporation from the metric as R = αx because the mutual corporation often brings the same utility, where x is the proportion of the costumers that use the metric. According to the replicator dynamic equation, we get the differential equation

= x(1 −x)(α −β).

Apparently, if α < β, the cost is not make up. Therefore, the costumers prefer not to use the metric. But if α > β, the costumers may use this metric. With this equation, we can measure how the beneﬁt of the ICM Corporation R changes with the utility α and the cost β. Fig. 10 shows the change of ICM Corporation utility as time passes, where α = 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 and β = 1.0, 1.5, 2.0. Obviously, when α > β, the beneﬁt goes higher than any situation when α < β. However, with α > β, the beneﬁt increases faster with a low cost β. Therefore, according to our model, once the costumers’ utility is higher than the cost, the ICM Corporation obtains a high beneﬁt. Additionally, a fewer cost brings an even higher beneﬁt when the costumers’ utility is ﬁxed.
