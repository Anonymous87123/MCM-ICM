4. Where should we look for D&A talent? Recruitment is generally divided into

school recruitment and social recruitment. Where hiring managers should recruit can be determined by proﬁciency and ability indicator. Employees hired by social recruit- ment are usually full of experience, who can improve the proﬁciency indicator. Em- ployees hired by school recruitment are usually well-educated and advanced, who can improve the ability indicator.
