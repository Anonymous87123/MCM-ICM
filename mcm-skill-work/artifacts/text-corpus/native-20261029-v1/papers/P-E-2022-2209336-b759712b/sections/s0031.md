There are many factors aﬀecting R, involving ever-growing ﬁelds. In line with data from 1990 to 2020 , we selected three representative factors the annual amount of deforestation, the latitude of forest and GDP of the country to simulate the value of R and their standardized values are XD, XL andXG respectively.

We can get the equations about the explanatory variables XD, XL, XG and the explained variable r by Multi Factor Line Regression Method.
