3.3.2 The Multi-Layer Networks

Depending on the six periods stated above, today’s social information network consists of five layers of network which include newspaper network, telegraph network, television network, the Internet and the mobile web. Here is the schematic diagram of the total social network:

The schematic diagram of the multi-layer communication networks. Since every layer has similar topology, we just show three layers in the schematic diagram. The bottom layer is the network before 1870, in which information can only flow between person nodes. The second layer is the network based on newspaper and telegraph, in which information can flow between person nodes and media nodes. And the third network is the network based on television.
