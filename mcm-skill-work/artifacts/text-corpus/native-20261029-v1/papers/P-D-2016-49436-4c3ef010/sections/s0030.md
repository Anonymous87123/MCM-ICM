6.2 Simulation

We follow two steps to simulate this piece of information’s flow.  Step 1: Initialization First, we initialize our networks. With the data we found, we can estimate the network’s parameters. The BBC Tech is the only media node in the network. Then we consider the 770 thousand followers as the total person nodes and the 200 thousand users as the nodes that directly connect to the BBC Tech. The 440 thousand views can be the number of nodes that receive this news. Because of the limited computing resource, we scale down the real network on the scale of 1/10000. Thus we have 1 media node whose degree is 20 and 77 person nodes. The person nodes’ degrees obey a normal distribution, and the person nodes are randomly connected.
