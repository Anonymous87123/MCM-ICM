9.1 More people agree at first, more people agree at last

In real world, people’s initial opinion will influence the flow speed of information. For example, if people all agree with the information, they may be more likely to forward it because they are more likely to like it. Also, the flow of information may also change public’s opinion. The analysis of initial public average opinion From the result above, we can get the following conclusions:  The influence in flow of information and public’s final average opinion

(1) If more people agree with the information at first, more people will agree with

the information at last. (2) The initial opinion will not influence the percentage of nodes receiving the

information. It is not accorded with the fact a lot, but it is not a big problem.  Interesting finding: there are some people you cannot change. From the figure, we can see that with the increasing of initial public average

opinion, less people is changed. And it means that with there are some people you cannot change their opinion even many people agree with the information.

The practical significance of this finding is: in the real world, there are people who have a poor connection with the information network and this make them cannot receive the information outside, which will make their opinion difficult to change.
