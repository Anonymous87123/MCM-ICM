2. Medium Risk Strategy States falling under this category have moderate population sizes and

economic scales, or they experience a moderate frequency of extreme weather events. Texas, for example, with its significant population and economic strength, faces moderate risks, such as occasional hurricanes. Similarly, Nebraska, with a moderate population and economic size, also experiences moderate weather risks, making both states suitable for the Medium Risk strategy.
