2. Spatial Heterogeneity in Extreme Weather Events: There is a significant spatial heterogeneity

in the impact of extreme weather events on insurance payouts across different geographical loca- tions. This heterogeneity can be effectively captured by the Multi-Scale Geographically Weighted Regression (MGWR) model, leading to more accurate insurance pricing and risk assessment.
