• Gender

Agriculture was developed by women, so gender is also one of the inﬂuencing fac- tors of the food system. Women produce half of the world’s food and make the most important contribution to food security (Jatinder Bajaj,2001). However, the women-centered food system is gradually transforming into a capitalist patriarchal enterprise. Peoples neglect of womens contribution to the food system has prevented womens role in the food system from being brought into play. This inequity also aﬀects food. The system has an impact. Therefore, it is necessary to establish a fair, sustainable and healthy food system based on women’s food and agricultural heritage, and give full play to the role of women.
