9.2 Weaknesses

 The standard for selecting factors is rigorous to some degree. In terms of se-

lection of best all-time college men’s football coaches, we choose Division I coaches with more than 200 wins. However, some famous coaches, although fail to achieve 200 wins, should have been granted the qualification to be selected as best all-time coaches.  The collection of the numbers of Coach Awards is subjective. Some awards

should not have been included.  The analysis of time line horizon is imperfect. For example, it is difficult for us

to set a correction factor for coaches who started their careers before 1950s.
