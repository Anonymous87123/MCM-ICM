4.2 Multi-Trophic Food Web

The multi-trophic food web we constructed includes primary producers, primary con- sumers, secondary consumers, tertiary consumers, and decomposers, while incorporating the dynamic effects of herbicides, insecticides, and fertilizers. Primary producers form the foundation, supplying energy that flows upward through trophic levels, while decomposers recycle nutrients by breaking down organic matter.
