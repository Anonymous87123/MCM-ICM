7. Future Improvements

●If we have more time, we will first consider the differences in the number of charging piles in the same type of charging stations. Different charging piles represent different service capabilities, so that the area that they can cover will change. Our model will be more realistic. ●When designing model 1, taking into account the possible waiting time for the electric vehicles to charge in the charging station. At the same time consider the impact of some human factors on the model. ●Find more reliable data to fit the future growth model of electric vehicles and reduce errors.
