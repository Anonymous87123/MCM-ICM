Annex 1 Service level classification based on average queue length

Service level Standards

Toll stations almost no queuing vehicles, so vehicles are free to choose the toll lane to Level One

receive service directly.

There are a small number of queuing vehicles at the toll stations, but the queuing length Level Two

is short and some vehicles will have to wait for some time before they can receive services.

Toll stations are a lot of queuing vehicles, queuing length is longer, almost all vehicles Level Three

need to wait for a longer time to accept the service.

A long queue is formed within the toll station, and all vehicles must wait for a long time Level Four

before receive service.

Annex 2 Service level classification based on average delay time

Service level Standards

For conventional tollbooths, the vehicle does not have to queue up at the toll road; for Level One

automatic tollbooths, the queuing time of the vehicle is shorter.

For regular tollbooths, the queuing time of vehicles is longer; for automatic tollbooths, Level Two

the delay time is longer, but shorter than the regular tollbooth.

Level Three The average number of vehicles queued in front of tollbooths is large.

Traffic demand is greater than the tollbooths capacity, the vehicles queued before the Level Four

tollbooths is of a long team.
