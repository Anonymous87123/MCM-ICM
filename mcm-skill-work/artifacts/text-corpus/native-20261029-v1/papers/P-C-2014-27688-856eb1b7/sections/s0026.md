8. Further Discussion

We simplify some problems because of the limited time, and in the nest step we plan to so some further work:  Considering the relationship between the frequency of citation and time of duration, we can divide the time periods into several parts. And then we count the cited times and compare them in different periods.  Furthermore, we can do some research in the distribution and trend of the frequency of citation to decrease the influence of citation time while valuing the papers.  When discussing the importance of a paper, we can also take its author(s) into account. Similarly, when discussing the importance of an author, we can also take his(her) published paper into account.  In this paper, we only consider the single vertex’s influence. Obviously, we neglect the influence of a group in the network. Hence, we plan to consider the influence of clustering phenomenon.
