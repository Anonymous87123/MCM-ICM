II. Den Searching After gold mining, the dragon may search for a den if it is currently homeless and

has at least 50 nuggets. A searching dragon has a 50% probability of ﬁnding a den on each timestep (day). Once a region has no more available dens, a new dragon will need to migrate if it wants to settle permanently.
