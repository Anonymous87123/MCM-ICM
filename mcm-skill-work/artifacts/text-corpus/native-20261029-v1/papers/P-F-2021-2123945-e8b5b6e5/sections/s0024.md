4.2 Weight Calculation

Among 12 indices of the three dimensions, it is obvious that different factors have different impacts on their dimensions, which also play distinct roles in our ﬁnal results. Therefore, we apply Group Decision Making Method (GDM) to calculate. However, the subjectivity of the method is unavoidable, for which we will apply Entropy Weight Method (EWM) to assist the weight conﬁrmation process. The weight integrated the subjectivity and objectivity will be determined for each dimension.
