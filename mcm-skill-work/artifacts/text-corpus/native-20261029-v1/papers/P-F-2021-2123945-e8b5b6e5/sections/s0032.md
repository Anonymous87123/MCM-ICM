In the CST (Curved-surface Target) model, the two elasticity indicators, Em and

Eθ, play a critical role in the determination of the ﬁnal curved-surface. Meanwhile, the conﬁrmations of Em and Eθ are tightly related to two groups parameters, (α1, β1, γ1) and (α2, β2, γ2). Therefore we slightly change one group parameters while controlling the other group to discuss the sensitivity of our analysis.

First we maintain the value of (α2, β2, γ2) and provide tiny changes for (α1, β1, γ1) for three times, shown in Table 4. Exchange the position similarly, we can conduct data as Table 5.
