5.4 The Effect of Fluctuant Frequency of Drought

The drought weather conditions usually come in the form of less rainfall amount each time and lower raining frequency. In order to simulate the impact of various frequency and occurrence of droughts on the plant community, it is a good idea to accordingly alter the time distribution and water amount of each rainfall. When the drought occurs, the rainfall will have less probability to have the same frequency (There is even likely to be no rainfall in the whole year!). This requires a new random variable to control the raining times. Besides, the water amount of each rainfall will also be lower and suffer from more fluctuations, indicating a .
