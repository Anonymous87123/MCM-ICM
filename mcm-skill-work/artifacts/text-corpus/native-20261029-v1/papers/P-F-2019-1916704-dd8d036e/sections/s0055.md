Model 3: Future Effects−Spatial Autoregressive Model

6

In this section, we predict the effect of the introduction of digital currency on the future develop- ment of the world’s long-term overall economic system, including the effect on the banking industry, the effect of global economic trends, and the effect of trends in inter-state relations. Since the economic effect is globally linked and related to its historical development level, the key to our prediction model is to solve the effect of the "contagious" economic linkage between countries and the effect of the pre- vious economic level on the future. We applied a spatial autoregressive model (SAR) to predict future developments.
