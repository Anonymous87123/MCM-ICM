4.1 Reorganizing index - Establish DS and RS system

Generally speaking, risk assessment needs to consider the advantages and disadvantages, supply and demand relationship and other factors. Metric of the risk of light pollution should be based on two perspectives:

a) Damage of the excessive use of artificial light, including adverse effects on society ,

people's lives and destruction to the ecological environment. b) The requirement of artificial light for human normal work and life. This is mainly re-

flected in industrial production, urban construction and other production activities. By considering the relationship between the secondary index and the risk of light pollu- tion, we reclassify the secondary index and sort out the two evaluation subsystems of DS (ar- tificial light damage system) and RS (artificial light requirement system).
