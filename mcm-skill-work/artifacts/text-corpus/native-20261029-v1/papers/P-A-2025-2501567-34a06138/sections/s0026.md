Parameter Calibration and Damage Calculation 4.9.2

To apply the Chaboche model to step wear analysis, we assume the step material is con- crete. The material parameters are calibrated based on uniaxial cyclic tests:

• Elastic parameters: Young’s modulus E = 30 GPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.2.

• Plastic parameters: Initial yield stress σy = 20 MPa.

• Backstress components: Three components are used, with parameters C1 = 1000 MPa, γ1 = 100, C2 = 500 MPa, γ2 = 50, C3 = 200 MPa, and γ3 = 10.

• Damage parameters: S = 0.1 MPa−1, s = 2, and m = 1.
