      The Markov Chain model can also be regarded as a directed network model: each

3.5.2 Transition Probability and Stable Distribution

The transition probability matrix can be estimated from the data:

                Pij  ≈  The  number of passes from player i to player  j.
                              The number of passes from player i

The estimated transition probability matrix is shown in Figure 5. From this figure,
we find several dyadic configurations: (D10, D3), (M10, D7), (M7, M1), (M8, D7) and
so on. We also calculate the stable distribution π, in which πi characterizes the prob-
ability that player i holds the ball as time evolves to infinity.
