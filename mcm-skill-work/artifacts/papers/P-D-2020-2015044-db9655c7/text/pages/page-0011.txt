4 Performance Indicators for Teamwork

      In this section, we propose a performance indicator for teamwork based on three
aspects: structural, configurational and spatial-temporal. The general framework is
illustrated in Figure 6.

4.1 Structural Indicators (SI)

      Structural indicators have been discussed in Section 3.3. We calculate the metrics
λ1m, λˆ 2m, CEm, MSPm for each match, where m denotes the ID of the match. Then we
normalize the indicators to make them comparable,

                λ1m  ←     λ1m − minl λ1l       ,  λˆ 2m  ←     λˆ 2m  −      minl λˆ 2l  λˆ 2l  ,
                        maxl λ1l − minl λ1l                  maxl      λˆ 2l  − minl

CEm             ←    CEm − minl CEl          ,     MSPm   ←    maxl MSPl      − MSPm                .
                   maxl CEl − minl CEl                       maxl MSPl −      minl MSPl

After normalization, every indicator ranges in [0, 1]. And higher value corresponds to

better performance. We sum these scores to obtain the structural indicator

                        SIm = λ1m + λˆ 2m + CEm + MSPm.
