      Solution 5.2.1: Bayesian Adjusted Flight Paths
A Brief Description:

This solution uses Bayesian Search Theory to predict the optimal flight path. The
solution is quite like the spiral striped model used for the purely normal model, but
with different locations for each pass depending on where the new peak of the
probability distribution lies.
