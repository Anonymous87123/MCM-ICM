      Solution 5.1.2: Bounded Random Flight Path
A Brief Description:

Expanding from the completely random search solution, we decided to improve it
with some “guidance”. We added the boundaries of our calculated search area to the
solution in order to keep the search plane in the general vicinity of the crashed plane.
