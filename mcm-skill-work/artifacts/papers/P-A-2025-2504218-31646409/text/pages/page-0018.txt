Step A: PSO for Global Optimization
   1. Initialization: Randomly generate particles Θj and their velocities vj within the prior bounds, for
      j = 1, . . . , P .
   2. Iteration: For each particle at generation k,

      record Θ∗ ≡ Θ(gbest) .
