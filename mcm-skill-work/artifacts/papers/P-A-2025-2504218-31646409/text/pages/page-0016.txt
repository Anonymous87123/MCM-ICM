4     Model II: Wear Parameter Inversion
     In the context of a non-diffusion linear wear equation, we aim to invert multiple parameters and
quantify uncertainties. Given that the measured data are typically noisy and that the stair wear profile
often requires smooth transitions, this work integrates prior distributions and second-order gradient
smoothing regularization into the Bayesian inference. Subsequently, it employs an improved strategy
with a heuristic-based preliminary search to accelerate convergence to the global optimum.
