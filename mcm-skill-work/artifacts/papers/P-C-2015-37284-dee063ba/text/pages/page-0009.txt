  4.4 Organizational churn in dynamic process
     In every month, we know every individual’s loyalty, which may be adjusted by the regulation
of position or the churn of connected people. According to the loyalty, we can simulate the
organizational churn.
     There are two reasons may lead to people’s churn.
     (1) People want to churn for their own reasons, which are not influenced by connecting
people. Loyalty will identify this situation.
     (2) People want to churn because of connecting people’s influence. For this reason, we take
both loyalty and intimacy into account.
