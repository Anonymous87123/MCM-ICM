6.3.1   Determination of Parameters
     One of our goals is to keep the temperature even throughout the bathtub.
So we firstly have to determine the value of corresponding parameters such as
the initial temperature of inflow and outflow.
     According to the data we use in the first sub-model, the temperature in the
bathtub ranges between 41 to 45 ℃ . In this sub-model, we still set the
temperature of inflow water to be tin 445 ℃ . In order to give the user
comfortable experience, we set the change of the mean temperature less than
1 ℃ .thus the lowest mean temperature tf 1 =42 ℃ .While the highest mean
temperature tf 2 =43 ℃. The expected mean temperature turns out to be ta
442.5℃. In that way, the maximum change of the mean temperature is only
0.5℃.

model. We first focus on the different temperatures during the heating process.
