6.2     Standby Model

6.2.1    Process Analysis
      After the heating process is finished, the mean temperature of bath water
is tf 2 . In standby process, it is assumed to be put in a space of which the
temperature is t and transfer heat into the space. Finally, the temperature
decreases to a specific temperature tf1 and the next iteration begins. During
the standby process, the mean temperature of bath water is expressed by tf
which is a function of time.
