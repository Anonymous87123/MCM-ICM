ଆ 1.2 Our Work
    To explore the role and influence of “momentum” in tennis matches, we selected different
indicators from game data for quantification and analysis. In the first question, we constructed a
PCA-TOPSIS model and selected 18 indicators to evaluate player performance and determine their

࿐ weights. In the second question, we conducted a white noise test on the model results, proving

that changes in game momentum are not random and refuting the coach’s viewpoint. In the third
question, we further constructed a GM(1, 1) gray prediction model to forecast turning points in the

ඔ game and determined the factor with the highest correlation coefficient with momentum shifts. In

the fourth question, we compared the model with real game data to test its accuracy and extended its

ğ ls application to other matches, making it more widely applicable. Lastly, we test the PCA-TOPSIS
