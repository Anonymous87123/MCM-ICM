3.1     Tourist Demand Model

3.1.1    Base Tourist Demand Equation

    The tourist demand model estimates the number of visitors to Juneau over time. The
model accounts for economic conditions, taxation policies, and external demand shocks.
The number of tourists, denoted as T (t) in year t, is influenced by the tourism growth
rate g(t), the tax rate τ (t) applied to tourism revenue, and random demand shocks δ(t)
that reflect external influences. Furthermore, the model includes the demand elasticity
parameter γ, which captures the negative impact of taxation on tourism growth.
