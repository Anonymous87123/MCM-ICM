3.3.2    Government Intervention

   To control the negative effects of tourism on the environment, a fraction of govern-
ment revenue from tourism taxation is reinvested into environmental conservation. The
improved environmental index Esub (t), after applying government intervention. M(t) is
the government expenditures, and α is the sensitivity of environment to the additional
government expenditures. The environmental index is modeled as follows:

                                  Esub (t) = E(t) + α · M(t)                           (12)
