                                                                     min-max scaling, which transforms data values into a comparable range of [0, 1]. Then, in

                                                                     ing method is applied. Entropy measures the degree of variation in an indicator across all
                                                                     years. Once entropy values are calculated, the weights for each indicator are assigned.
