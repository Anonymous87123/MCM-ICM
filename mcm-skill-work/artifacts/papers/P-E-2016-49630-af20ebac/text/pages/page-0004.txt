3 Total Scarcity Metric Model

In this section, we construct a metric incorporating a country’s ability to provide improved
water to meet the needs, named Total Scarcity Metric (TSM). It measures the ability to manage
water scarcity for a country. A larger TSM implies a weaker ability for a country to handle
the balance between improved water supply and demand, and a harder work for it to solve
its water problem.
