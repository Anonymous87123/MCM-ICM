5.5.1 The Strategy for Regular Road Sections in Japan
    Based on our segmentation of the track, in the remaining 62 regular road segments, ni = 20, nj =
25, nk = 17. Because 1 − cos 2◦ < 7 × 10−4 , cos 2◦ − cos 4◦ < 2 × 10−3 , Therefore, we apply the
weighted simpliﬁcation method in 5.4 to the slopes whose angle satisﬁes θ < 2◦ and 2◦ < θ < 4◦
respectively. The set of road segment numbers that meet the conditions is T U2 , T D2 , T U4 , T D4 .
