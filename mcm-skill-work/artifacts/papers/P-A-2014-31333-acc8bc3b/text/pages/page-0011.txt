4.2.4 Middle Lane Rule
The Algorithms from Middle Lane Rule start all of the cars in the middle most lane of the
freeway, and they disperse out to overtake one another. The overtaking mechanism is
similar to “No Rules” in that they can over take on the left or on the right, but the cars
always try to find an open spot in the middle lane of the freeway.
