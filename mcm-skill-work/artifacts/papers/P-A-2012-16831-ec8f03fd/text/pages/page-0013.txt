3.1 Building the Model
    We mainly discuss the shoot with two layers of leaves following, because of the
most significant impact is given by the neighboring layer above. That means we only
need to consider 4 blade, 2 on upper layer, and 2 on down layer.
    To give the criteria of overlapping, effective area ratio (EAR) is defined by the
formula below.
                                              SU
                                     EAR 
                                             Stotal

     Where SU is the total leaf area that is not shaded, namely the effective area,

and Stotal is the total leaf area.

      Under this definition, our solution should be able to calculate the shaded area
of the two leaves in down layer. That means, we should get the relation between the
shade profile in upper layer and the profile of two leaves in down layer.
