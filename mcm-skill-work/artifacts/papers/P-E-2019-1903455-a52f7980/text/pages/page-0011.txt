5. Ecosystem Service Valuation Model

    In the process of economic development, the initial construction projects mainly
rely on extensive economic growth mode of increasing investment and material input,
and the contradiction between economic development and resource environment is very
sharp. While individually these activities may seem inconsequential to the total ability of
the biosphere’s functioning potential, cumulatively they are directly impacting the
biodiversity and causing environmental degradation.

development projects of varying sizes based on the cost-benefit analysis method(CBA),
