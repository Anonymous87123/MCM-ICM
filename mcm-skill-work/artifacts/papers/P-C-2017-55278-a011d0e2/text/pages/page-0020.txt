5     Sensitivity Analysis

    We analyze the sensitivity of some parameters in our model. We run simulations
on a 250-cell long high way segment with 5 lanes, and no dedicated lane. The daily
traffic count is 100, 000. With this setting, we analyze how the parameters in our model
influences our conclusion. The results show that our model is quite robust.
