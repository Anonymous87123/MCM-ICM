6.3 Guidance for Emergency Water Flow Situations

   Emergency water flow situation(i.e. flooding and prolonged low water conditions)
can generate catastrophe. Flood water may wash away any cities downstream and cause
considerable loss of life. Extreme low water conditions could destory many fields of a
country. To ensure the safety of our new dam system, we will provide the ZRA managers
with specific guidance.
   The aim of this guidance is prevent an uncontrolled release of water from reservoirs.
    For flood control, our objective is to maintain as much control over flows entering
the system above a critical flood-prone reach as possible. [8]


Method Filling the upper reserviors first and emptying the lower reservoirs first.
    An illustration of this method has been studied [9]. Flood storage at the reservoir
close to upstream provides greater flood controlability than the lower reservoirs.
   However, an exception is that if the lower reservoir’s outflow capacity is restricted,
then it is better to fill the lower reservoir first to increase head on the outlet. In this way
we can increase the release capacity from the entire sistem to the downstream channel
capacity. This exception is prepared for some extremely serious floods.
   For prolonged low water conditions, our objective is to maintain the river runoff.
