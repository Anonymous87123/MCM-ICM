 ྘ We use data prior to 2024 as the training set and 2024 as the test set. The k-fold cross-

validation method is used, taking k to be 10. The training set is divided equally into 10 equal

remaining 9 subsets as the training set. The random forest model is trained on the training set
and the model performance is evaluated on the validation set and the evaluation metrics are
recorded. Finally, the average evaluation metrics of all the subsets are calculated, giving a

࿐ comprehensive estimate of the model performance, as a way to assess the stability and
