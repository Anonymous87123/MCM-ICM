     Based on the information consulted, fishing boats generally less than 12m in length are
classified as small fishing boats, and 12-24m are classified as medium-sized fishing boats.
According to the Scottish Sea fisheries report, we set the hull length L is 24m, power P is
440kw, and displacement  is 70t [8] . We get vvessel  18.1 km / s . Set t fresh to 6 hours, then
the boat can travel Dmax  108.6km . At this speed, the medium-sized fishing boat can travel up
to 108.6km after catching the fish. Beyond this voyage, the fish will be spoiled because the
preservation period has passed. At the capture point, the area of action of the fishing boat is
about 74066km2.
