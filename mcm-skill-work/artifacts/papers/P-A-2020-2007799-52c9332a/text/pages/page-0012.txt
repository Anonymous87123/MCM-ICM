     Conclusion In the best case, elapsed time is 33 years, after that all fishery companies in
each port except Shetland will not be able to capture because they are too far away. In the worst
case, the elapsed time is approximately 21 years. One interesting fact is that the small fishing

herring habitat have moved to Shetland, allowing the company to harvest until 2070. Despite
