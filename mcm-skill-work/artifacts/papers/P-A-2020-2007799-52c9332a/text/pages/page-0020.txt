7.2 Further Discussion
    If we add more influential factors to the cellular automaton, such as ocean depth, ocean
current factors, coastline, etc., we can more accurately fit the migration of biological habitats
and some other behaviors.

     In fact, some of the models we have a wider range of uses. Cellular automata models can
arbitrarily add multiple factors, set complex behavior patterns to simulate real environments, or
extend to land to simulate the evolution of terrestrial biological communities. Our value
appraisal model can evaluate any task that fits the purchase of equipment and relocation.
