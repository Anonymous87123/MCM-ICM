            In these equations, I(t)，S(t)，J(t) and R(t) respectively represent, at time t, the
       density of the unknown, the spreader, the super-spreader and the immune. The density

       of four types of users meets the condition of normalization: I t  S t   J t   R t   1.

       When  0,   0 , this model will turn into classical SIR model. With the evolution
       of the system, when the number of spreaders is zero, the process stops and spread of
       network ends. And the network will only contain those who have not yet received any
       information and the immune who are transformed from the spreader and super-spreader.
