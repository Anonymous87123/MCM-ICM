4 Model Preparation
4.1 Data Processing
    We selected one of the typical ancient architectural steps, the steps inside the Leaning Tower of
Pisa in Italy.
    To extract more accurate images, we used computer vision technology to process the images.
⚫ Step 1: Image processing
    The original images were first transformed into grayscale images, then the images were denoised
using Gaussian filtering.
