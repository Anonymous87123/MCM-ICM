     Both herring and mackerel live in shallow water. The temperature suitable for herring is
about 8.8 ℃ ~ 9.8 ℃, and that for mackerel is about 8.86 ~ 9.6℃[16]. From the updated
waters, looking for a location that matches the living temperature of herring and mackerel is
the direction in which these two fish move. According to data from the Scottish Marine
Information Authority, in 2016 herrings near Scotland were mainly distributed around (-1.3,
60) and (-7.4, 58), and mackerel were mainly distributed around (-5, 58.5) and (0, 58.5) .
