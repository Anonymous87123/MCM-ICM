              Assumption                  2

We assume that effects of external factors on our model obey the Gaussian
Distribution.

We made this assumption based on reality, because the kinds of external effects considered in
our study (e.g. climate change, international aids) seldom have abnormal strong impacts on the
original model. Under this assumption, we can easily quantify the external effects in our study.
