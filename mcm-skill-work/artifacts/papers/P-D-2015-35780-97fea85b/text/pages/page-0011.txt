3.4 Results

3.4.1 Country Rankings

We acquired original data from World Bank Database[20]. The data cover all 17 indicators in
our SDI system. Ten sample countries are selected from 5 different continents and covers both
developed countries and developing countries. Based on our model in Section 2.2, sustainability
scores of sample countries in year 2013 are calculated. The rankings are shown in Table 3. The
lower the score is, the more sustainable a country is; hence the United States ranks the highest
