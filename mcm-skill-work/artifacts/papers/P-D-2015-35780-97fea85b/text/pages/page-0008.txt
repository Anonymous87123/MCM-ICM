3.2.3 Development Index and Coordination Index

Sustainable development is a concept which requires both development and coordination. To
be coordinated requires a country to improve itself not only in one aspect but in all aspects at the
same time. In our model, we use a Development Index (DI) to depict a overall development
status and a Coordination Index (CI) to depict the consistency and coordination of develop-
ment among environmental (en), social (so), and economic (ec) aspects. Both index are based
on comprehensive performance on aspects (en, so, ec). It is only when DI and CI are both
high can we consider a country to be a sustainable one.
