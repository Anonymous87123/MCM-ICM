5.2 Impact of Transaction Costs on Strategy

    In question 2, we divide the strategy model into insurance type, common type and radical type.
When exploring the impact of cost on strategy, we should also consider these three aspects. Let’s
take the common type as an example to explore the impact of various changes of α on the strategy.
We mainly explore through transaction frequency, asset return and asset risk, in which asset
risk is characterized by the variance of return. Speciﬁcally, it is investigated through the changes
of the following four costs.

a. αg fell 0.1% each time and αb remained unchanged.
b. αg remained unchanged and αb fell 0.2% each time.
c. αg rise 1% each time and αb remained unchanged.
d. αg remained unchanged and αb rise 2% each time.

    Figure 14 shows the change of strategy in case a. It can be seen from the ﬁgure that a small
change in the cost of gold will not have an impact on traders’ decisions.
