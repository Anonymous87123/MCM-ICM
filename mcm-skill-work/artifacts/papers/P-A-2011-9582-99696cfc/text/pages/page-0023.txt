Our simulation coincide with this study and shows players’ skills (choose best path)
can help them to optmize “Vertical Air”.

The larger zt is, the steeper the path is and more momentum before the fly. But if the
path is too steep (zt>5 meter) the player will fly more down the pipe instead of
higher above the pipe. Shallower path (zt is smaller than 5 meter) could provide
larger “Vertical Air”.
