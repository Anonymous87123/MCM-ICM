6.3   Results Interpretation
    The nonlinear relationships in the plots indicate that GDP has a significant positive
impact on the number of cybercrime targets, while ICT Goods Export and Population
are negatively correlated with target counts. This relationship arises because the log-
arithmic squared term of GDP in the model holds high representational significance,
causing the coefficients of other variables to take negative values to balance the over-
all data. These findings highlight the complex roles that economic scale, technological
exports, and population distribution play in the distribution of cybercrime.
