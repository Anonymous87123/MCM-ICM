4.1.1 Data overview
     We currently have a common set of socio-economic factors collected for the counties
of these five states during each of the years 2010-2016 from the U.S. Census Bureau.
Except for the three special identification attributes of “GEO.id”, “GEO.id2” and
“GEO.display-label”, the remaining attributes include relationship, marital status,
grandparents, educational attainment, ancestors, etc. in each year’s socio-economic
factors data set. And each attribute has four values (Estimate、Estimate Margin of Error、
Percent、Percent Margin of Error). The overall data frame is shown in Figure 10.
