6.6     Conclusions

Based on the results, we can conclude that:


   • The number of lanes is not an influential factor under any circumstances.

   • The maximum speed limit plays a significant role in light traffic while it is
     of no importance in heavy traffic.

   • The minimum speed limit plays a significant role in heavy traffic while it
     is of no importance in light traffic.

   • Signaling behavior reduce flow rate and average while enhancing safety.
