 1.2    Our Approach
 We propose a model framework as shown in Figure 1, consisting of two major components, the
 cellular automata (CA) model and the Naïve Bayes Model, with several processes that prepare the
 given dataset into inputs for the main model. Cellula automata simulate the dynamics of AGH colony,
 whereas the Naïve Bayes Inference combine three quantities: the output distribution of cellula automata,
