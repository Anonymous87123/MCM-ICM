7.2 Stop Loss on Capital

In gold trading, it is important to set a stop loss. Its main purpose is to help investors

manage their investment risk. If an account is losing money, it can be sold to lock in the loss

and prevent further declines.

Stop loss levels are used to avoid large price fluctuations by setting a profit and loss

point in advance. A capital stop is usually set at a maximum loss that the investor can afford

and once the strategy has been implemented, once the loss on a single trade reaches that

value, a sell will be made, regardless of expectations.
