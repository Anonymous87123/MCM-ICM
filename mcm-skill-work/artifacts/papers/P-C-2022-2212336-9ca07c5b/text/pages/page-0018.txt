8 Transaction Cost Sensitivity Analysis

Transaction costs are one of the key indicators that affect a strategy's yield. Many strate-

gies have good rates of return under ideal conditions, but may have lower rates of return once

transaction costs change. If trading outcomes are more sensitive to transaction costs, it is an

indication that the strategy may not be realistic in the face of changing market conditions.

To see the impact of transaction costs on trading outcomes, we replaced the transaction

costs with the following four scenarios:
