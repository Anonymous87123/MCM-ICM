focus on the more elementary axiomatic approaches.
    Axiomatic Approaches: Given n models that we wish to aggregate,
let Pk (Si,j ) denote the probability that a particular sector contains the next
crime as predicted by the k th model. Let Wk denote the aggregation weight
of the k th model, given such that all Wk ’s sum to one.

   • Linear Opinion Pool: This model is a linear combination of two or
     more probability distribution functions produced by individual models.
