    To maintain a balance between the three variables when maximizing utility, we categorize our
intervention strategies into two components: tax revenue allocation and policies. Each component
                         信




features a finite set of typical and representative solutions. By iterating through these solutions, we
obtain a finite set of potential strategy combinations.
                        微




    Given the current values of H Juneau , N Juneau , and T Juneau , each combination, under the given con-
straints, produces new H Juneau , N Juneau , and T Juneau values along with corresponding scores. We then
select the combination with the highest score to serve as the current intervention strategy, and update
H Juneau , N Juneau , and T Juneau accordingly to these new values.
