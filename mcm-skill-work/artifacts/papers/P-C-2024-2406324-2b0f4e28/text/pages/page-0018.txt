6 Sensitivity Analysis

tables. While our neural network model has achieved very good performance on existing datasets,
considering unexpected factors in real-world scenarios, it’s very likely that there may be inaccuracies
in the data collected before the game. After data processing and cleaning, the number of eligible
data points may be limited and may not reach our expected optimal number of around ten data points.
