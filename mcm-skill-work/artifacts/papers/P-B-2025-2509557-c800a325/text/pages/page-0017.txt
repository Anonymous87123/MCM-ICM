7    Sensitivity Analysis
      This section systematically analyzes the mechanisms by which key policy parameters influ-
ence sustainable tourism development indicators using heatmap visualization. Based on steady-
state simulation results of the dynamic model, the focus lies on exploring the sensitivity responses
of tourist volume, resident satisfaction, and environmental degradation to regulatory variables such
