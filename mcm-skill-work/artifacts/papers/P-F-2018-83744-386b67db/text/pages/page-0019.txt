3.3 Generational difference in PI system

In this section we consider the variation of risk perception by age and how this would aﬀect our pricing
system. We deﬁne risk-to-beneﬁt ratio of PI and data privacy as rw1 . Because the issue of how age diﬀerence
will inﬂuence risk perception is hotly discussed, we will mainly focus on the generation eﬀect on r1 instead
of the beneﬁt w. So the risk-to-beneﬁt ratio is only aﬀected by the change of risk.
We emphasize the meaning of r1 here and provide further explanations. r1, the risk of data disclosure,
is the expected loss derived by objective, rational and detailed calculation. So we assume the value of r1
here is independent of peoples subjective perception. To describe the perception by various groups of indi-
viduals, we introduce an index, µ, to stand for the degree of subjective and psychological risk perception.
The total subjective risk value of individuals become r1′ = µ · r1.
The higher value of µ indicates the larger risk perception. When µ = 1, it indicates that the individual
can accurately perceive the expectation loss of PI disclosure and correspondingly, µ > 1 indicates that the
individual overvalues the risk.
