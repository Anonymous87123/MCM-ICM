where f (t) is the amount of food the dragon is able to eat in a year, and m(t) is the dragon’s yearly caloric
requirement. It is known in biology that an organism’s metabolic requirements can be well-approximated
using a power law with weight, so we assume that the dragon’s metabolism m(t) has the form

                                                     m(t) = aw(t)b ,

where m(t) is in units of calories per year and a and b are parameters. Nagy, Girard, and Brown [10] found
these parameters to be a = 139065 and b = 0.889 for reptiles with metabolism in calories/year and weight
in kg.
