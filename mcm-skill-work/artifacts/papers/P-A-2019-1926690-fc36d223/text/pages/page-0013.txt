5.2. Regional Differential Equation Model We performed a regional differential equation simulation
using our initial parameter set to understand the dynamics of such an ecosystem and gain information on
energy requirements. Additionally, we performed a range of parameter studies in order to understand how
a dragon responds to different environmental conditions and growth rates. All simulations were performed
using forward Euler’s Method with a time step of one month, unless otherwise noted.
