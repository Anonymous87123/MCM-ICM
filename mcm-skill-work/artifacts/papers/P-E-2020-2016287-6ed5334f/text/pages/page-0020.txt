6.2.1 Impacts on Human Life

         Based on Section 6.1, the factors can be divided into 2 categories. From the aspect of
plastic waste production, the financial investment to alternative industry( x3 ) will lead to the
decrease of the price of substitutes( x2 ). Meanwhile, the price of single-use plastic products
will rise due to the carrier bag charge( x8 ) and the production tax on the production( x6 )
of plastic. Therefore, according to Q-P curve in Fig.6, the consumption of single-use products
will decline, also due to the government’s advocation on environmental protection( x5 ). It is
estimated that by that time, most people will use paper or cloth bags instead of plastic ones.

6.2.2 Impacts on the Environment

      In analogy to analysis in Section 7.2.1, from the aspect of plastic waste production, if these
policies such as ban, tax, alternatives (factors x1 x9 ) are effective, the amount of production
will decrease by 55%. From the aspect of plastic waste management, the financial investment
in recycling( x10 ) will increase the percentage of recycling, which lead to 47% decline of toxic
exhaust. What’s more, with the rise of incineration plant( x11 ), the percentage of depositing the
waste in the ocean will decline to 12%, which is good for marine life.

6.2.3 Impacts on the Plastic Industry

    Although the government's policies on enterprises are not the same in the short term, they
will all lead to a reduction in the production of disposable plastics in the long run. In addition,
with the increase of peoples' environmental awareness and government ’s supervision, the
production of disposable plastics will be gradually reduced, and many companies may face
bankruptcy and transformation.
