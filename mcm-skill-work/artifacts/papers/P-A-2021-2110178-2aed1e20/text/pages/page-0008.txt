 4.2 The Estimation of Kd : Competitiveness Index
 In the above description of the enzyme inactivation process, the enzyme inactivation coeﬃcient Kd
 is introduced. A large amount of experimental data shows that in the environment where various
 fungi coexist, their interaction has a signiﬁcant impact on the enzyme inactivation rate. Therefore,
 we redeﬁne the enzyme inactivation coeﬃcient as the competitiveness index of fungi.
