4.3     Strategies Verification

All the strategies above are aimed to decrease influence from opioid drug on these groups. In our model,
we consider the factor as fixed that a specific group is affected by opioid drug. An equivalent way to verify
our strategy is to decrease the percentage of corresponding group. In reality, we can’t actually "decrease"
its percentage, but the "decrease" here serves the same purpose as lower the influence factor for specific
group.
