6.5 Model Fitting and Prediction

    The function P (t, X) aims to model the trend in first-medal-winning probability for countries
and then predict this probability for a certain country based on its characteristics in a certain
year. Therefore, the data we used for fitting is the feature vector X = [Xa(t), Xp(t), Xe(t)]
of each country up to and including the year of its first medal win, namely the process of the
country stays in Olympic Village for newcomers, rather than including the data after a country
wins its first medal. So we marked countries that had already won medals in each Games and
conduct a survival analysis, treating Year as duration time and winning as the event. After
excluding the data of countries with prior wins, we fitted a model and successfully forecast the
likelihood of first-time winning for various countries in a given year such as 2028.
