7. Solution to Problem 3

7.1 Overview of the problem
    Due to the ongoing drought and high temperatures, water resources in Lake Powell and
Lake Mead are strained to meet the water and electricity needs of residents and industry. In
order to protect the production and livelihood of the states as much as possible, additional
measures are needed to meet the demand.The following are specific programs.
