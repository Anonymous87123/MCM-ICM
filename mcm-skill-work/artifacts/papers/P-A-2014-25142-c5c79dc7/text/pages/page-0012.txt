When Gr ≥ Gs, we assume the danger is small enough to neglect, so the danger
coefficient is set to 0. When Gr < Gs , we use the difference of Gs and Gr to
calculate the danger coefficient. A higher danger coefficient indicates the driving
state of the vehicle to be more dangerous.

   The danger coefficients of vehicle-following are similar in various road and
rule conditions, so we consider only the danger coefficients of overtaking in
further discussion.
