4. Summary of Our Model
      Our model starts from studying the aerobic and anaerobic respiration of the rider and de-
termines the relationship between energy expenditure and power output. The model depicts the
rider's energy profile, which is expressed externally as a power curve. For any given track, we
simulate the relationship between the rider state and various resistances on the track. When
combining the above models, we use dynamic programming to obtain the relationship between
optimal output power and track position, which has great reference value for the rider. The best
results are achieved as long as the rider performs for the given power output. In order to reduce
the difficulty of the players' execution, we provide a relatively loose reference time, which can
get close to the best results.
