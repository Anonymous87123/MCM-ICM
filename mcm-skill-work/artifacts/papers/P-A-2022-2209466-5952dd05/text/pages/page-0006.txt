3 Definitions and Notations
3.1 Definitions
     Power-to-weight ratio: The ratio of the rider's output power to his own body weight. The
higher the value, the stronger the climbing ability.
     Functional Threshold Power: The maximum average power a rider can achieve during
an hour of full and steady riding. It is an important indicator of a rider's aerobic capacity. Also
known as lactate threshold, it is usually determined by testing.
     Critical Power: The maximum average power a rider can get during a given period of
time while riding with full force and stability.
     Sharp turn: The turning angle is greater than or equal to 90 degrees within 50 meters.
