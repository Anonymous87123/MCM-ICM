3.3.1 Effects of Species Reproduction on Niche Width
In this paper, the increase in the niche width of each species due to reproduction is influenced by the
current niche width, environmental capacity, and interspecific competition. The larger the current
niche width of a species or the environmental capacity, the more it reproduces, and the more it
increases its niche width. On the contrary, the greater the competition between species, the less
it breeds and the less they increase its niche width. Therefore, the expression of bi (t) is obtained
