    In summary, the niche model suggests that each species in a biological community has a
specific niche and interacts with each other through competition and mutualism. Protecting fragile

5     Sensitivity Analysis
5.1    Sensitivity to Unpredictable Factors
When establishing the differential equation for the population niche width, we added white noise
to simulate the effect of unpredictable factors on community succession. The variance of white
noise needs to be within a certain range. If the variance is too small, it cannot simulate the effect
of unpredictable factors. If the variance is too large, it will affect the correctness of the community
succession process.
