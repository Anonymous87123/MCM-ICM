4.3 The Perspective of Entire Nation

Unlike individual and group pricing models, nation pricing model is more complex. The national pricing
model should take into account market factors, policy factors (such as price regulation, specific data
protection) and cultural constraints.

      Because the factors that need to be considered at the national level are too complex, it is very difficult
to portray the national pricing model in a comprehensive way[14]. Therefore, this article slightly simplifies
the national pricing model, supposing the country has an ideal market conditions, the ideal macro-control,
the ideal policy role. We analyze from the perspective of the market, the policy and the culture. Finally, the
three perspectives are integrated to obtain the national pricing model.
