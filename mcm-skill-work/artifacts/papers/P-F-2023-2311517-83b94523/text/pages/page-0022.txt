8 Sensitivity Analysis

     We use the world's energy consumption in different years in models that predict future
development in different countries. However, in the real situation of calculation, data from
different sources often fluctuate due to artificial error and unpredictable factors, which may
affect the results to some extent. Therefore, in order to test whether our model still has stability
when external conditions are disturbed, we use sensitivity analysis to evaluate our model.

     In order to simulate data fluctuations of different amplitude, we added 3%, 5% and 10%
disturbances to the data of Energy Consumption(EC), which were brought into the prediction
model for calculation and compared with the original situation without disturbance. The results
are as follows:

                     Figure 16: Relationship between different degrees of capacity reduction and expected GDP

     It can be seen that despite certain fluctuations in the data, our model still gives similar
results from a qualitative point of view, that is, economic sacrifice is made in the short term
due to the pursuit of GGDP, but at some point in the future, it will recover to the level without
reform, and achieve better growth at a faster speed later. Moreover, we find that the greater the
sacrifice in the short term, the sooner the recovery will be achieved and the faster the future
economic growth will be, which is precisely the nature of the policy.
