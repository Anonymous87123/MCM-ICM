         4 Radio propagation on sea surface model

         4.1 Introduction to HF propagation

         4.1.1    Way of HF propagation

         HF propagation is the radio propagation of radio waves in the range of 3 to 30MHz(corre-
         sponding wave length 100 to 10m). HF communication has the advantages of long
         communication distance, strong persistence and simple equipment,meanwhile, it has
         the disadvantages of channel congestion, time-varying, dispersion characteristics, and
         so on. Moreover, it is easy to be influenced by other external factors and is unstable. HF
         propagation can propagate along the ground as the ground-wave, and it can also prop-
