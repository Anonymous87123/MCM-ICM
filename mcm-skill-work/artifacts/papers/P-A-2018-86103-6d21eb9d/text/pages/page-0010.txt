         4.3 Transmission loss of HF radio waves Lb

         During the transmission of radio waves, there are many factors that can affect the trans-
         mission loss. We divide the basic transmission loss(Lb) during the whole propagation
         process into four parts: free space transmission loss Lbf , ionospheric absorption loss La
         , ground reflection loss Lg, and additional loss YP . If all losses are expressed in deci-
         bel(dB), the basic transmission loss of sky-wave propagation is as follows:

                                         Lb = Lbf + Li + Lg + YP                               (5)
