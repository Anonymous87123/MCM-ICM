   In summary, combining the graphs of the optimization effect of the governance strategy at

sites 2 and 28 and the optimization effect table, we give the following conclusions.

   1.For site 2, Strategy 1 has relatively good treatment effects in the short-term, interim and

medium and long term, respectively, so we suggest to implement strategy 1 in the short term,

strategy 2 in the medium term and strategy 3 in the long term to ensure that light pollution can be

effectively treated in different periods in the future.
