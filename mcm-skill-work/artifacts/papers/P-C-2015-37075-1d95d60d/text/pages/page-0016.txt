5.6        Comparing among Strategies

Now we aim to improve the current situation by adopting some alternative strategies.
Currently, when deciding which one to promote among all qualified candidates, HR
manager will choose the one with the longest working time (Experience). We consider
two other ways discussed before: selection by centrality within the information network
(Centrality) and selection by likelihood to leave (Likelihood). We still use closeness cen-
trality defined in previous sections.
