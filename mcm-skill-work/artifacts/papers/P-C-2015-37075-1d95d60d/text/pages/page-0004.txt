3.1     Constructing Human Capital Network

First, we merge the table and graph given in the problem by assigning levels of positions
to entries based on several reasonable assumptions:

      • Every senior/junior manager has a clerk in his office for administrative tasks.
      • The level of position of a staff member tend to be higher if his office is closer to the
        CEO in the organizational graph.
      • The level of position of a manager cannot be lower than someone whose office
        belongs to a lower tier in the organization graph.

    We begin to build the human capital network in ICM. Define V (G) = {v1 , v2 , ...v370 }
as the set of all positions. Each node denotes one position. Define E(G) as the set of
edges in the network. (vi , vj ) ∈ E(G) if at least one of the following holds:

      • i and j are in the same office. Here one entry in the organization graph is consid-
        ered as an office, whether it consists of two divisions or only four staff members.
