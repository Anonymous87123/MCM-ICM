4 TASK 2: Evaluation of Successful Teamwork

4.1 Identiﬁcation of Performance Indicators

    To further evaluate whether the teamwork is success, we should select some rel-
evant indicators. This paper simpliﬁes the performance indicators into two aspects:
Static and Dynamic. The Static performance indicators mainly contain: Coordination
among players, Distribution of contributions. And the Dynamic performance indicators
involve: tempo, ﬂexibility, Pressing.

4.1.1 Static Layer

    • Coordination among players

    In terms of coordination among players, we use cluster coefﬁcient C to quantify it.
One player’s cluster coefﬁcient means his connectivity with other teammates. The
team’s average cluster coefﬁcient embodies the level of coordination in the team. There-
fore, the higher average cluster coefﬁcient C is, the stronger coordination γ is.
