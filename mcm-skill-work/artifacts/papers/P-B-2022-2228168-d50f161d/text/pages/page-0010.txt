volume originally expressed by integration, which degenerates into the product of the
difference between water level change and the average surface area of the reservoir.
     At the same time, the change of reservoir water volume remains conserved. The
change of reservoir water quantity is equal to the amount of water flowing into the
current reservoir and the increase of water replenished by rainfall in time T minus the
amount of evaporation from the reservoir and the reduction of water supply withdrawn
from the reservoir in time T.
