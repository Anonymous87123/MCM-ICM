4.1.3 Calculation of Weights

    Determination of the weights is essential to evaluate the different contributions of the indicators.
Consequently, two weighting models are adopted to calculate the weight vector.

    The traditional AHP model uses Group Decision Method(GDM) to determine the weight of each
indicator. This method requires experts to give the comparison matrix of all main factors. GDM has
its rationality, but it is very subjective.
