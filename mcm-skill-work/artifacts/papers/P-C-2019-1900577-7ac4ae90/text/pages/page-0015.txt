Figure 6: County drug report data for 2015 versus non-adjusted model prediction
based off 2014

    With the adjusted model, as shown in Figure 7, we examine how the addition of
the socio-economic factor influenced our predictions. After using the adjusted model
to predict 2015 drug report spread based off of 2014 data, the slope of the best-fit line
stayed only slightly increased (to .9288 from .9276). But, the sum of the residuals with
the adjusted model was 41631, which is a 12.2% decrease from the 47408 drug reports
in the baseline, and a 3.9% decrease from the 45539 drug reports in the original model
