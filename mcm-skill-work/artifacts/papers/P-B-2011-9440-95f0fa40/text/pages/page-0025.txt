     We next prove that the solution shown in Table 5 with 11 repeaters is the optimal solution,
namely 10 repeaters with radius no more than 15.28miles cannot cover a circle with radius
40miles.
     Lemma 1 [21]: Let r(n) be the maximum radius of a circular disc that can be covered by n
closed unit circles, then r(n)=1+2cos(2π/(n−1)) for n=8, n=9, and n=10.
     Proposition 3: To cover a circular area with radius 40, the least number of circles with radius
15.28 is 11.
     Proof. Table 5 has already shown a possible solution, and thus we only need to prove that to
cover with 10 circles is not possible. According to Lemma 1, r(10)=1+2cos(2/9π)=2.532, which is
smaller than the ratio 40/15.28=2.618, and thus the coverage with 10 circles is not possible.
