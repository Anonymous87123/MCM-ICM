3.3     Distribution of Space Debris
3.3.1      General Rule of Space Debris Distribution

     Using the DAS, we can figure out how space debris distribution varies with altitude.
     Figure 5 indicates the number (per year) of debris impacts, of each impactor size we expected.
Numbers of debris impacts are given on a logarithmic (base 10) scale. Results are a function of
altitude [6].
     In this plot, we supposed the launch date is on 2016.01.01 and the duration lasts one year. It’s
apparent that the smaller debris size is, the more debris are. Also, the number of debris varies with the
height, and, particularly, the height of 700-1000 kilometers and 1400-1600 kilometers are two peaks
indicating the hardest-hit areas.
