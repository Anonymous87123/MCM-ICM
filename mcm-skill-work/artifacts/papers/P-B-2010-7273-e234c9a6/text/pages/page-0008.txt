4.3     Application to Rossmo’s Model
In [9], Rossmo presents the model for estimating the the residence of a criminal
based on the location of their offenses. The model makes use of the idea of a
buffer zone. The buffer zone is an area surrounding a criminals residence in
which a criminal avoids committing crimes. The idea behind the buffer zone
is that criminals will attempt to balance the energy they need to expend in
order to go long distances away from their residence to commit crimes, and
the increased risk of committing crimes near to where they live. There is a
