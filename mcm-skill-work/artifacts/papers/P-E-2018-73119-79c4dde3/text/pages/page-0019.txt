public service. In the following years the government took active measures of
disaster recov-ery and reconstruction. It is not until 2010 that the country recovered
from the hit of Cyclone Sidr in 2007.

In June, 2012, rainstorm gave rise to flood and landslide, caught deaths and destruc-
