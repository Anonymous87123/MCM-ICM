The collision in 𝐶𝐶𝑀𝑀𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵 reference frame can be divided into the following four
procedures:
v)    The ball and bat (with spring respectively) approach each other;
vi)   The two springs contact and compress until the velocity (in 𝐶𝐶𝑀𝑀𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵𝐵
      reference frame) of bat and ball turn to be zero;
vii) The ball and bat are accelerated by the springs respectively. In procedure ii
      and iii, the loss of energy happens through the effect between bat (ball) and
      spring.
viii) The two springs is no longer at contact, and the ball and bat separate each
      other.

    By definition of the coefficient of restitution (the ratio of the differences in
velocities before and after the collision), we obtain the equivalent BBCOR (with
trampoline effect and material effect inside) in a reference of home frame

                                    𝑣𝑣𝑖𝑖 − 𝑉𝑉𝑖𝑖
