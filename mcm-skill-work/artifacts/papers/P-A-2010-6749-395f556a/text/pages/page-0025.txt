 The Effect of Mass ( 𝑴𝑴) on Maximum BBS
    Similar to the effect of MOI, the effect of Mass on BBS cannot be determined
only by qualitative analysis. We make the quantitative analysis in the same
procedure as MOI, only replacing 𝜔𝜔𝑖𝑖 with 𝑉𝑉𝑖𝑖 , and 𝐽𝐽 with 𝑀𝑀.
From Figure 17, we easily obtain the similar result that the increase of 𝑀𝑀
(limited in possible field of variation) leads to higher maximum BBS. What’s more,
Figure 17 also tells us that the increase speed of maximum BBS is getting slow
with the increase of mass.

    Especially, we draw the swinging bat speed 𝑉𝑉𝑖𝑖 and maximum BBS 𝑣𝑣𝑖𝑖 with
respect to mass 𝑀𝑀 in the same figure, as Figure 18 shows. Comparing with
Figure 4 which is drawn with experimental data [Bahill and Freitas, 1995], our
data obtained from calculation of our model exactly match that experimental data,
which proves the correctness of our model.
