4.4 Fitting the Model Using Bayesian Inference and MCMC

    In this problem, we utilized Bayesian inference in conjunction with Markov Chain Monte
Carlo(MCMC) methods to fit the model and derive its posterior distribution. Bayesian inference
allows for the integration of prior knowledge or beliefs regarding the parameters, which can sub-
sequently be updated in light of the observed data. The application of MCMC techniques enables
efficient sampling from the posterior distribution, particularly in scenarios where direct computation
