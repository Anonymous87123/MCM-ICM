                           Figure 12: Correlations Among Average Speed, the Percentage of SDVs and the Traffic Flow

    As we can find in Fig. 12, all data points fit to a continuous smooth surface, showing that the
higher the percentage of SDVs is, the faster all vehicles run and the larger the traffic flow is, the lower
the average speed is. These two features are consistent with common sense. Another interesting
feature is that the decreasing trend of speed related to the traffic flow is stongly influenced by the
percentage of SDVs. Some typical curves of that are shown in Fig. 13. Also, we can find that there is
a maximum traffic flow for each percentage of SDVs, which is shown in detail in Fig. 14.
