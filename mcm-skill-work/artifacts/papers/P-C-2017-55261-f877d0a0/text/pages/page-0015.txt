Model Testing and Error/Sensitivity Analysis
In order to apply our model to the given data, we replicated the curve for
weekday traffic volume in Figure 1 and normalized it to obtain a distribution
function, which was scaled by the ”Average Daily Traffic Count” statistic given
in the problem for each point in space on each highway (use of the same distri-
bution for all points in space discussed in the Assumptions and Justifications
section). This provided estimates for traffic flow at all points (s, t). We used a
30 second time interval, and for space intervals we used the distances between
consecutive mile markers. Those intervals are roughly characteristic of average
car speeds on the highways, since many markers were spaced less than one mile
apart.
