Lane Change Model
Another main aspect in traffic flow is the interactions among vehicles as they
change their lanes. A change of lane occurs when ramp vehicles merge into
a highway stream, highway vehicles leave for minor roads, and when vehicles
change into a different lane of the route to prepare to turn or leave the road.
