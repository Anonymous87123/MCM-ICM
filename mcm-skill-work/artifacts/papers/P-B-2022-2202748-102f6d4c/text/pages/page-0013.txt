          14       P ← P which is calculated based on the volume

      The parameters to be input are: the initial water level heights P and M of the two reservoirs, and

      The parameters in the model are: calculated waterway transport loss α, calculated circuit

     Solution and output of the model: According to Model One, the reservoir water consumption
