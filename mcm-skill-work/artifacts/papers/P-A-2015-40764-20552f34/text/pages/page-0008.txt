3.1.3   I0 , H0 , P0

The initial population found in Section 3.1.2 entirely consists of the three states of the general model,
I0 , H0 , and P0 . To determine the initial population of each of the states, we consider WHO data for
the number of Ebola cases from November 12 2014. This data is given in Table 6.

        Table 6: WHO Ebola data for November 12 2014 (World Health Organisation 2015a)

                            Country           Total Cases      Total Deaths
                            Guinea               1,878             1,142
                            Liberia              6,822             2,836
                            Sierra Leone         5,368             1,169
                            Overall              14,068            5,147


To estimate I0 , we assume that all reported cases that have not resulted in death are still infected, and
therefore
                                   I0 = Total Cases − Total Deaths.                                     (7)
Although this neglects the possibility that a reported case has recovered from the virus, which would
lead to overestimating I0 , we accept the assumption. This is because the total number of infections
is low relative to the total population, the WHO acknowledge that their statistics underestimate the
Ebola epidemic (World Health Organisation 2014b), and that it is difficult to otherwise predict current
infections from the data. Based on this assumption, we also have
                                                 H0 = 0,                                               (8)
and
                                      P0 = Initial Population − I0 .                                   (9)
This gives the following values for the initial states.

                               Table 7: Initial states for the Ebola model

                                   Country             I0        P0
                                   Guinea             736    12,343,043
                                   Liberia           3,986    4,585,810
                                   Sierra Leone      4,199    6,362,992
                                   Overall           8,921   23,291,845
