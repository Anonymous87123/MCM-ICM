As what we can see from the figure above, the types and number of drones ob-
tained by greedy algorithm are: 121 drones of type B, 54 drones of type D, and
3 drones of type H. We find that the result is rational and acceptable due to fol-
lowing aspects:

   • The type D drones have the smallest volume and a not bad max payload
     capability. They have the largest ratio of max payload capability to the
     volume.

   • The type B drones have the furthest flight distance, which means that the
     area they can reconnoiter is the largest. Hence, type B drones have the best
     ability of reconnaissance when we don’t consider the speed of each type of
     drones.

   • What’s more, type B drones are the drones that have the highest speed;
     thus, they can connect each cargo container to allow even greater flexibility
     (from cargo container A to B for recharging).
