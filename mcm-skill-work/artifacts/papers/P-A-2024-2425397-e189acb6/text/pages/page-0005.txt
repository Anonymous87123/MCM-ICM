3 The Lampreys Ecosystem Model

3.1     Model Preparation: Age Grouping of the Lampreys Population

3.1.1    Age Profile of Lampreys and its Subgroups

    According to the data collection [1], the lifespan of lampreys is 7 years, the first 4 years are
larvae without sex difference, and they feed on plankton; in the 5th year, they start to change their
morphology, and there are male and female, and the sex ratio is mainly affected by the food supply.
Therefore, the amount of food during the larval period of lampreys can affect the sex ratio during
the morphological change and thus the population size of lampreys, and the influence relationship
is shown in Figure 3.
