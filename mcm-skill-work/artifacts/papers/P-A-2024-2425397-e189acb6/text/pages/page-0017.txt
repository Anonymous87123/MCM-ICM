    According to the population biology of lampreys, lampreys reproduce once and then die. We
simulate that a certain generation of lampreys was subjected to artificial interventions or environ-
mental influences that resulted in too low a survival rate of spawning, halving the number of lam-

of lampreys is too low for spawning due to artificial intervention or environmental impacts, the

ulation and leads to the decline of the population size, which puts the population in an existential
crisis.
