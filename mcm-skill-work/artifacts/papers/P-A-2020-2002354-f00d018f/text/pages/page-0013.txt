5.1 Fishing Area
  According to Jansen T's (2012) [2] study, fishing boats can catch quantity of fish
within 90 km of the edge of the continental shelf, while at 120 km there are few fishing
boats. As the distance is too far away, the fishing boats have problems such as fish
preservation, safety and the longest voyage distance. Therefore, it is set in this paper
that the fishermen fish within 120km from the mainland. The fishing area around
