4.2     Interspecific Relationship
   In a specific ecosystem, lampreys interact with other species and the abiotic environ-
ment. For instance, lampreys, as an invasive species, have had a significant impact on the
Great Lakes ecosystem. Next, we will discuss the mathematical models of lampreys as
prey, parasites, and hosts, and briefly analyze their relationship with the abiotic environ-
ment.
