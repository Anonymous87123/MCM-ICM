4.1 Random Sample Of 10000 People

To study the demographics of the potential Population Zero members, we extract data from PUMS database,
which contains personal data of 1618489 individuals, and carry out data mining. Regarding the limitation
of computational power, analyzing the whole data set is inconvenient and unnecessary. Instead, we ﬁrst
generate a sample of 10000 immigrants totally randomly using SAS, SQL Server. We suppose that every
individual has an equal opportunity to be selected as a member of Population Zero.
