5.6.2 Migration System

New immigrants from Earth have their original attribute, and they may contribute to the diversity of the
population on Mars. Meanwhile, they may also increase the burden on the welfare system.

    Marriage relationship may be established between a new immigrant and a member of Population Zero
or a Mars-born adult. The marriage linkages are built based on the same rules as stated above. New
immigrants are allowed to get married to a Mars resident right after they arrive on Mars, no time lag is
considered.
