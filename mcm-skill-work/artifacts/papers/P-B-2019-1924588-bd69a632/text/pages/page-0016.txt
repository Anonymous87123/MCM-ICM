4.3.3. Establishment of Optimization Model

     Determine the place where containers are put into operation, and optimize the objective
of investigating the road network as comprehensively as possible. In the road grid model, if
the container is placed at this location, we will take that location as the center of the circle.
The area that the UAV can detect is a circular area with a radius of half of the maximum flight
distance of the UAV. The larger the number of traversed grids included in the investigation
area, the wider the range of roads that the UAV can detect. The constraints of the optimization
model are that the distance between each container and its corresponding hospital is less
than half of the maximum flight distance of the UAV.
