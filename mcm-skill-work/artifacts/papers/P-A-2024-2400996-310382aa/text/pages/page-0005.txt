2 Model Preparation

2.1   Assumptions and Justiﬁcations
   • Assumption 1: The time for adult ﬁsh to migrate, lay eggs, die, and hatch into
     larval is negligible.
     Justiﬁcation: Our model calculates in years, so ﬁner time scales will not be con-
     sidered. The only time we primarily consider is the time it takes for a larval to
     grow into an adult, which will be counted directly as a yearling after spawning.
