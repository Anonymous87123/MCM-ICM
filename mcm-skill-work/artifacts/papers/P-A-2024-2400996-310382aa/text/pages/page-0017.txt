4.2.1    Lamprey sex ratio and ecosystem stability

    As we noted above, lamprey larvae grow at different rates under different food
conditions, and the growth rate of larvae determines their probability of differentiat-

different from those on other populations. Here, we still discuss larval and adults sep-
arately, and use the Ecosystem stability assessment model developed above to analyze
