4.1     Simulation of the Lamprey Population Iteration Model

4.1.1   Algorithm of the Model

    Our model performs calculations in a differential form, with the results of each
calculation derived from the previous one, to simulate iterations of the lamprey popu-
lation based on the input parameters and to investigate whether it is able to maintain
stability over time.
