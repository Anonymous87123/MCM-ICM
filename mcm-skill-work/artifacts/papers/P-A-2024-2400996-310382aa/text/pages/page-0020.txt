4.2.3   Detailed Impacts on Other Species

the effects of this mechanism on different species in the ecosystem. We analyzed the
effects of this mechanism on ﬁsh parasites and lamprey parasitic species of the same
status as lampreys. Keeping other parameters constant, the ecosystem was simulated
and the changes in population density of the above species with and without the sex
