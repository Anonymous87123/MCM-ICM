Program 3 Frequency Allocation Algorithm

1 repeater list is the list containing the locations of repeaters
1 r is the effective radius of a repeater’s signal
1 frequency set is a list of allowable, non-overlapping frequencies
1
1 Function greedy frequency allocator
1
1 Let G be an edgeless undirected graph with one vertex per repeater in repeater list
1 Let freqmap be a mapping from repeaters into frequencies
1
1 for each repeater pair (i, j) with i 6= j:
1 1 if dist(i, j) ≤ 25 r:
1 1 1 Add an edge in G connecting the vertices representing i and j
1
1 /*Obtain a coloring of G, represented as a vertex → integer map*/
1 coloring = greedy color(G)
1
1 For each vertex v in G:
1 1 freqmap[v ] = frequency set[coloring[v]]
1
1 return freqmap
