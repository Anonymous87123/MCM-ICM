    What’s more, as the staff comes and goes, the structure of the multiplex network has been
changed. The new nodes are put into the networks while the long gone nodes are deleted.
Thus, the connections between nodes become changeable. As time goes by, the total number of
clerk resigning their positions is increased, causing great damage to the Friendship Network.
What’s worse, the dissatisfaction for not being promoted has pass on through the Information
Flow Network resulting in the growth of bad atmosphere in the organization. So once a leader
is moved to another department or quit his job, the demission index rises to the threshold and
the demission event is triggered. After a gradual decline of the demission index, a dynamic
balance is gained between the number of recruitment and the amount of resign. Therefore, the
sum of personnels remains stable and the factors of the networks are dynamically balanced.
Meanwhile, the structure of the multiplex network also becomes stable which means the allo-
cation of each layer in the organization is steady.
