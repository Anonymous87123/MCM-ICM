   This illustrates the following results for doubling the effectiveness parameter:

   • There is a noticeable increase of cumulative probability over the course of a 20 day
     search.

   • The greater effectiveness parameter does not fully double the cumulative probability
     of the search with lower efficiency.

    These results are consistent with what would be expected. The Law of Diminishing
Returns states that if only one factor is increased continually, the return rate will decrease
over time due to the incremental increase of this factor[8]. As shown above in Equation 29,
q decreases non-linearly with an increase of z or λ. This means that the longer the path (or
the longer the search duration), the less probability of success will be accumulated on any
given day of searching.
