Result Analysis:
     According to the results of the above classification discussion, when the species type is mutually
beneficial species, the stability of the plant community can be greatly improved. When the species
type is competitive, the plant community can remain basically stable. When drought-intolerant species
exist, the plant community can not maintain stable.
