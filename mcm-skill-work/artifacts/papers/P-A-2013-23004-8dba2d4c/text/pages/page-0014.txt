     For any point that lays inside the rectangle, it must comply the following conditions:
    1. The least distance between this point and the edges of the rectangle must not be less than the
       radius of its inscribed circle;
    2. For hexagon, the least distance between this point and the edges of the rectangle must not be
       less than the radius of its circumcircle;
     For regular hexagon, because of that the distance between dots along x axis and along y axis are
different, we need to rotate the rack 90 degree when one analysis is finished and do the analysis again,
and then pick the optimal arrangement.
