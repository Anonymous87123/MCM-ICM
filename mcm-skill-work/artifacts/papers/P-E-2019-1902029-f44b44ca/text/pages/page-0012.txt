3.5.3 Evaluation of ecological service value

In 3.4.1, the value of ecological services in different provinces is quite different,
mainly caused by the large difference in land area between different provinces.
In order to more objectively evaluate the service capacity of an ecosystem, This
paper proposes two ways to eliminate the impact of total area on ecological value
assessment.

1 Dividing the value of the ecological services in each province by the area of the
       province gives the value of the ecological services per unit area.

                ESCi = ESVi/Ai    (8)

2 Dividing the value of the ecological services in each province by the province’s
       GDP, the value of the ecological services per unit of GDP per province is
       obtained.

                ESCi = ESVi/GDPi  (9)
