2.2    Hypothesis
    • We treat the snowboarder as a mass point with mass m, ignoring body twists
      of snowboarder and other geometrical properties.

    • The mass point moves right on the snowboard course rather than traveling
      along the trajectory of the original gravity center of a snowboarder above the
      snow surface.
