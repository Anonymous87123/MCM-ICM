4     Application in 2D Case

4.1     Quarter-Circle Type
    From a circle series expansion point of view, we can use a circle to simulate a
curve as the first-order approximation. Thus in this issue we firstly assume the trail
consists of a vertical wall, a horizontal path and a quarter circle. This is simple
but can provide us much information about the interactions among the relevant
factors. In this case, no energy is added to the snowboarder, therefore the player
may not reach the other edge of the course. So we only study process in (x0 , w),
where w is the midpoint of the path. Our goal here is to analyze how the related
factors, µ, hi , x0 and w, influence the energy loss.
