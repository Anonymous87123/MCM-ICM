3.5 Charging Model

3.5.1 Basic idea

After selecting the immigration area, we will calculate all costs and find countries to
pay for it. And we will build a model to analyze how much each area will pay.
Our charging model assumes that before the necessary immigration, people on the
island are relatively free to move to other similar areas. For those similar areas, the
additional social security and other burdens brought by immigrants have the nearly
same amount with the economic contributions they bring. Thus the only direct
economic cost is just new houses and the compensation for those who choose to be
paid in cash.(It will be further discussed later.)
