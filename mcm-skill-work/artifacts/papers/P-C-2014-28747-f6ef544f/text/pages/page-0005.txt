4     The influence of researchers
    Before modeling, to avoid ambiguity, we will define the two confusing concept.

    • The importance degree of a node : the node’s influence in the network. It mea-
      sures a node’s ability to communicate with neighbour nodes and to build the con-
      nection among different nodes in the whole network.

    • The influence degree of a node: Due to the connection with other nodes, the
      node was affected by other nodes and therefore it possesses its own comprehensive
      strength, namely the influence.
