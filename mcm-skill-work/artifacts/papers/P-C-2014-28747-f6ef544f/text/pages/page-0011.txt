4.3    Result analysis: some interesting data

    We already know, the researchers’ importance index and influence index are two d-
ifferent evaluation criterions of measuring the level of scientific researchers. The former
reflects the researcher’s ability to contribute to the connection in the network by contact-
ing other researchers, while the latter shows the researcher is affected by herself/himself
and her/his partners and can change these factors into her/his overall influence.
    Comparing the rank result of the two methods, we find that the change of over 80%
researchers’ ranking is within 75. In view of their difference of emphasis, it is acceptable.
   However, analyzing the remaining data, we find some interesting phenomenon,we
show it in Table 3
