5.2 Reputation Mapping

Considering predicted reputations after Aug. 2015 may vary a lot in values, we might not effec-
tively ﬁnd an interval of convergence, which makes it hard to evaluate a product’s successful-
ness. Therefore, we seek to ﬁnd a mapping approach to map our non-converging reputation
predictions so that mapped reputations ﬂuctuate within an distinct interval of convergence,
while the reputation values before and after mapping should indicate the same trend. We
