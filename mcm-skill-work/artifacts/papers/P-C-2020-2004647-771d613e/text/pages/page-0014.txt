    Hence, we know when two time series {x1 , · · · , xi , · · · , xT } and { y1 , · · · , yi , · · · , yT } have the
exact same trend, then we have τK (X, Y ) → 1; when the two series have the exact opposite

τK (X, Y ) → 0. That is to say, |τK (X, Y )| ≈ 1 suggests trends of the two series are highly
correlated while |τK (X, Y )| ≈ 0 suggests they are not correlated at all.
