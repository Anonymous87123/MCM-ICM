4.3.2 Kendall’s Tau Method

To evaluate the trend similarity between reputation and sales, we adopt Kendall’s Tau Method
[9] to study the correlation between trends of reputation and trends of sales.
     The procedure of Kendall’s Tau Method is as follows: suppose {x1 , · · · , xi , · · · , xT } and
{ y1 , · · · , yi , · · · , yT } are two randomly generated time series where i = 1, 2, · · · , T , we per-
form sorting operation on both series respectively by the values of xi and yi . Then, we ob-
tain two sorted time series—say, {x(1) , x(2) , · · · , x(T ) } (termed the order statistics of the time
series {x1 , · · · , xi , · · · , xT }) and {y(1) , y(2) , · · · , y(T ) } (termed the order statistics of the time series
{y1 , · · · , yi , · · · , yT }) —where x(1) ≤ x(2) ≤ · · · ≤ x(T ) and y(1) ≤ y(2) ≤ · · · ≤ y(T ) .
