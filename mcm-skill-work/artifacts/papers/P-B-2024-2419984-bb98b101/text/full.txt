    After losing contact with the main ship, a suitable positioning model, search equipment, and
search strategy for the submersible can signiﬁcantly reduce rescue time and increase the success
rate of the rescue mission. In this paper, based on diﬀerent malfunction scenarios of the sub-

mersible, a positioning model and search strategy for the submersible’s movement have been es-
tablished, providing some suggestion for maritime rescue.

                                      ：

                                                                  els
are plotted. The primary uncertainties in the model include the submersible’s initial state, ocean
currents, and fault conditions. Therefore, the submersible should be equipped with sensors such as
                                     号

                                                                od
