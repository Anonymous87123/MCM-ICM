7.1 case 1: Desert greening in Kubuqi desert, China
       Kubuqi Desert, positions at Inner Mongolias, spans around 18,600 sq km
       of golden sand. Centuries of grazing had denuded the land of all vegeta-
       tion, and the regions 740,000 people were wallowing in isolated poverty. In
       1988, the Chinese ﬁrm Elion Resources Group partnered with local peo-
       ple and the Beijing government to combat desertiﬁcation. Almost three
       decades later, one third of Kubuqi has been greened. Special plants have
       been grown to grip the shifting sands and to prevent the dunes encroach-
       ing on farms and villages. (Campbell and BaoTou, 2017)
