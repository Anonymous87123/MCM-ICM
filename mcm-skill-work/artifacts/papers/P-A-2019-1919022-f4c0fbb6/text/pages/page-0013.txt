4.4   Human being’ Restoration to the Ecosystem

    The introduction of dragons may cause the destruction to the existing ecosystem, so it is
necessary for human beings to help reconstruct the environment. In this part, we quanta-
tively calculate the impact factory µ of human’s conservation.
    Human beings reconstruct the environment by repairing the habitat that dragons have
destroyed. However, human beings cannot completely repair the destruction the dragons
make. We set human beings’ impact factor as µ. Besides, considering the observation, anal-
ysis and action of human beings have certain hysteresis, we set the time difference between
dragons’ destruction and human beings’ reconstruction as . So the ratio of destroyed habi-
