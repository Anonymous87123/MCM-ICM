   Desert
    Figures. 8, 9, 10 show the influence in desert. In this region, the area required is 10 times
bigger than that in the temperate deciduous forests. In Figures.8 and 10 , we find that only
the top populations are hugely affected by the dragons while the weak population are not
seriously affected. In Figure. 9, we find that in the early 2000 years, the status of adjacent
populations are interchanged. And as the temperate deciduous forest, the extinction proba-
bility of populations is smaller in larger area.
