3.1.2    Action and Rest

    Animals will adapt their metabolic rates q to different motion states, so will the dragons
do. In Equation (1), the adaption is reflected in the characteristic constant q0 . The motion
state of a dragon can be divided into two types: rest state and action state. The rest state
includes sleeping and relaxing while the action state includes flying, preying and so on.
Dragons consume much more energies in the action state than in the rest state.
    By this classification, we calculate the characteristic constant q0r for the rest state and q0a
for the action state. According to the Assumption. 1, dragons’ motion mechanism is similar
to birds and bats, so we use the metabolic rate of different kinds of birds and bats to fit the q0 .
Parts of the data are present in the Table. 2, whose third and forth columns are the metabolic
rate per gram q̂ [5], and the complete data are present in the Appendix A:
