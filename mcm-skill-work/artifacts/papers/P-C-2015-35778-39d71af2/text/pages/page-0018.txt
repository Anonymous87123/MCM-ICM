3    Multilayer Network Model
3.1 The multilayer network for ICM
The single layer network can only include one aspect of a problem. However, the team performance is
not only influenced by working relationship but many relationships in common life. The Human Capital
network is a one-layer network reflecting the relationship between higher level and lower level. We add
the friendship network and the competition network to the human capital network. Thus we get a multi-
layer network.

The multilayer network is based on the Networks office in ICM, which consists of two divisions of 7
people. The reason why not based
