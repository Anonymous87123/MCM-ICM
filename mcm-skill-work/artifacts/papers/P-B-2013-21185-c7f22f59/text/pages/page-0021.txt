               Water Movement Principle
We should follow principles below to work out an efficient water movement strategy.

   We use inner-regional water transfer when water resource supply is complementary within a
    region

   We use inter-regional water transfer when most water resource supply within a region is too big
    or too small

   We only transfer water from high terrace to low terrace or in the same level of terrace
