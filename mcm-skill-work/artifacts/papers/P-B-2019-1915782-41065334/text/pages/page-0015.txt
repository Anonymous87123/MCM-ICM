6.2.5   The Algorithm of the Location Model
   Based on the previous analysis, all road information on the map was extract-
ed, and the scale of the map and the position of each location in the map were
determined through the Google map[5]. Under the condition that the constraint
conditions are satisfied, all the points on the road in the map are traversal, and
the corresponding objective function is calculated, and then the optimization re-
sults under the current preset parameters are selected.
