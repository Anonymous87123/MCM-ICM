6.3 Future Plans

    Our present PBQ model has ﬁxed total peak passenger ﬂow, in the future, we try
to use the passenger generating time to decide the duration of peak period, MPL can
study the linkage condition and time node at a ﬁxed peak time, make more objective
evaluations on its performance.

    Make the occurrence probability of the cutting in line be related to the congestion
level, in the real condition, more crowded the airport is, more cut-in-line phenomenon
will take place.
