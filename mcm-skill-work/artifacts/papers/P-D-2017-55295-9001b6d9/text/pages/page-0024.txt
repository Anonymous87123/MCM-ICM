7.4 Future Application of Models

    Our proposed model can also be generalized into ﬁelds like:

   1. communication systems: our scheduling algorithm can be used to schedule the
       transportation of data packets in order to improve throughput and reduce vari-
       ance.

   2. logistics networks: our models correctly reﬂect the queueing process and trans-
       portation process of logistics networks. The optimization approaches mentioned
       in our model can also be applied in this ﬁeld.

   3. queueing hardware: in hardware systems, several queueing devices may need to
       compete to use the bus to send their data to destinations. The scheduling algorithm
       in our model can be used to optimize the throughput and variance of such systems.
