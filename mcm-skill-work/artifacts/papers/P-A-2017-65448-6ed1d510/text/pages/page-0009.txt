5.1.3   Boundary Condition and Initial Condition

river should be given. Boundary conditions refer to the current velocity and river depth at the head-
stream, which can be measured in reality. Initial condition refer to the current velocity and river
depth anywhere along the river when t = 0, which can also be provided by hydrological stations.
