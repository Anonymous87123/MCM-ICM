6.2 The strategy to add water

    To solve the multi-objective optimization model in order to propose the strategy to add
water, we use the Genetic Algorithm and address the problem in four steps:
(1) At first step, we select countless values from the range of the temperature and the range
    of the volume of the hot water added in per unit time we set in equation (20)
(2) At second step, using the objective function min 1 2  2 Tz  3Vp to filter the
    selected values, and then get the values that satisfied the objective function
(3) At third step, based on the values we picked out, using the objective function to filter.
(4) At fourth step, repeat the third step until find out the best strategy.
