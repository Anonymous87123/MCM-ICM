1.2195e-7 Mt of plastic per capita per year, which is above x¯HI = 1.08e-7. We know solely
from this information that the United States will have to reduce yearly plastic consumption
per person with βHI,US < 1 since x˜HI < x¯HI . Thus, xUS > x˜HI for any policy.
