4.2.1. Mass of the bat
Mostly, producers drill a cylinder about 2.54cm in diameter, and 25.4 deep and fulfill
the hole with rubber or cork, which reduces the mass of the bat by 42.5g.[9]

Since a 25.4cm long cork can reduce the mass by 42.5g, the mass will be reduced by
1.67 x when the length of the bat equals x and the diameter is kept as 2.54cm.
