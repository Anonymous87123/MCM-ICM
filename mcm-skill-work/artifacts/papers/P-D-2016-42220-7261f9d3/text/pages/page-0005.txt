3.2.1 Index Extraction
      In order to describe the flow of information specifically, we choose Twitter as an

example. Based on the AsIC Model, to improve the accuracy of our model, we take the
inherent value of information, the media effect and personal subjective emotions into
consideration and give five indexes to analyze the information flow: Communication Media
Effect, Sender Characteristic, Receiver Characteristic, Content Characteristic, Relation
between Sender and Receiver. Suppose that node u as a sender, node v as a receiver.
