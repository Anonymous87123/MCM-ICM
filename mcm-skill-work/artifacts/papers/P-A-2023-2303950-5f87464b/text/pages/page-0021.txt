⚫ Modifications
     Despite all these advantages of natural grass communities, they are still vulnerable to
severe drought conditions. And modifications can be made to improve the viability of plant
communities. Specific strategies are as follows:
(1) Introduce new species. We found that species such as shrub can bring facilitation to other
    species and add more stability to the ecosystem, which is consistent with the literature.
    Therefore, some suitable species can be introduced.
(2) Build irrigation facilities. Some irrigation facilities, such as the Karez in Iran, can gather
    and preserve the water from the rainfall with less evaporation. So, increasing the soil water
    availability through artificial facilities is also worth consideration.
