  3.4.2   Interactions Among Different fungi
      In model (22), we summarize the internal interaction among different types of fungi as the
     信




  competition among different types of fungi. We put forward a definition (Definition 3). This
  definition considers the effect of another species on a type of fungi mainly based on the exten-
    微




  sion rate. A percentage factor is used to reduce the original extension rate. This competitive
  relationship is relative, so it will affect the whole process of decomposition and growth.
      From the perspective of the short run, according to Fig 3 and Fig. 4, the additional consider-
  ation of fungal competition does not have much impact. This is because in the initial stage, the
  remaining amount of wood, that is, nutrients, is sufficient. Even if the growth rate of fungi is
  limited, their growth process takes place in a similar exponential form. Therefore, in the short
  term, the impact of competition is not obvious.
