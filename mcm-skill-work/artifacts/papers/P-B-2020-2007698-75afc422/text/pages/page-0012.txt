4       The Optimal Sand-to-Water Mixture Proportion

4.1     Model Preparation

4.1.1    The Principle of Model

   We do experiment with different water-sand mixture proportion m j . After each ex-
periment, we record the time (t j ) it takes to make the sandy base disappear completely,
and write down the data points(m j , t j ). By fitting function curve of these points, we
obtain the best fitting curve. Then we can calculate the optimum proportion of sand-
to-water.
