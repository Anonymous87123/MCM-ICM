6.2.2 Grey Correlation Evaluation Model

     Because of the relationship between every criterion and total evaluation is
sophisticated and interacting, we develop Grey Correlation Evaluation Model to
analyze and evaluate general capability of college coaches. Grey Correlation
Evaluation Method is branch of Grey Theory, the method are usually used to evaluate
interacting factors.
