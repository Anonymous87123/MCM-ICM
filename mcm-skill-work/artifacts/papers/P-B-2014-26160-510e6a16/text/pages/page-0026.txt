8.2 Sensitivity of Fuzzy Comprehensive Method
      The sensitivity analysis is crucial to stability and rank of the reliability evaluation
model. Herein, we analyze the sensitivity of evaluation criterion weights. Briefly , to
show it’s sensitivity，we change the minimum and maximum weight ratio to get the
different score of different coach.
      We choose basketball coaches’ data as an example.
      First, we adjust the maximum weight of the evaluation make it increase 0.02, get
the score of the coaches.
      Then, we adjust the maximum weight of the evaluation make it decrease 0.02,
get the score of the coaches.
