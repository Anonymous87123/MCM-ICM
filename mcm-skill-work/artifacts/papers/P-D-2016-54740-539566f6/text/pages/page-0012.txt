4 Simulations

The parameters mentioned above were placed in a simulation with a few additional modiﬁcations.
As mentioned in Section 3.3, we categorize news as falling into one of three functions: politics,
entertainment, and consumption. The nodes follow the proportional topical distribution of each
time era. We only allow data to originate from one source and the nodes themselves can only hold
one string at a time until another piece of data overrides it. We run the simulations on two networks
representing the cultures of the 1880 and 2015. The diﬀerences include the topical proportions,
and spread layouts. These correspond to the media categories and sources of the times. Finally,
the simulation allows for a random generation of information input (order, type, relevance) or a
controlled news input. A few simulations are represented below.
