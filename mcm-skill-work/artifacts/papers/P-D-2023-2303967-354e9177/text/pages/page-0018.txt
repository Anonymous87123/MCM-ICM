5.2 Addition of the Node: Goal Inclusion

    In today’s digital age, Access to Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs) should
also be proposed to UN for the inclusion [9]. Though its slightly mentioned in SDG 9, it is playing
an more and more critical role. It varies from groups to groups, such as developed and developing
countries, rural and urban areas, and different socioeconomic classes. The lack of access to ICTs can
limit individuals and communities’ opportunities for education, health, and economic growth, and block
the SDGs development.

    To illustrate how a new SDG impacts the Dynamic SDG Network, the method is similar to that in
Section 3. The selected indicators are

   1. proportion of the population that uses the Internet

   2. mobile broadband subscriptions per 100 inhabitants

   3. proportion of individuals using the Internet who have basic digital skills

    After adding the SDG18: Access to Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs), the result
SDG network is visualized as Figure 15.
