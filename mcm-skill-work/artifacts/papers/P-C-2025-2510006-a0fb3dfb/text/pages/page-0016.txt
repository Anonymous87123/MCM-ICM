and gold medal count for Russia are zero. In the ﬁgures below, we can see that among the
major Olympic medal-winning countries, the United States is most likely(95%conﬁdence) to
improve (in both total medals and gold medals), except for Russia. This is due to the home
advantage for the host country, including, but not limited to, familiar venues, cheers from
local audiences, accustomed accommodation and food, and no time zone adjustment. There
is no signiﬁcant decrease in the total medal count for the United States; however, China is
expected to show the most notable decline in gold medals among these ten countries. This
is mainly due to the cancellation of weightlifting, China’s most successful event (where it
won 5 gold medals at the 2024 Olympic Games).
