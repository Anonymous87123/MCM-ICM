countries will focus on balance of payments when formulating foreign trade strategies. The share

balance the relationship between capital flow and trade surplus (deficit). Assume that a country has

a large trade deficit and there is no investment opportunity in the country. To attract foreign funds,

the state will lose the power to issue general currency. Therefore, consciously adjusting the balance

of payments will be an important goal of the central banks of all countries.
