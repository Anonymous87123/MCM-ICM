We fit the trade situation of the United States from 1994 to 2016 and get the corresponding fitting

function to forecast the total trade volume of the United States in the next ten years. If there is a

digital currency acceptable to the whole world at this time, the United States will trade in both the

flat currency market and the digital currency market. The initial ratio is set randomly, and the annual

trading ratio in the two currency markets is determined by the trading ratio in the previous year and

the change of exchange rate.
