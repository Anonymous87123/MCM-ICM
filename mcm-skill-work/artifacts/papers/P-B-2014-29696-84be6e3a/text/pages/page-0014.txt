4.3. Model formulation
4.3.1. Vector definition of the “idealistic coach” and ranking
    In this topic, there are 12 factors that make impacts on the ranking of coaches,
after data analysis. Accordingly, standardized vector‟s dimension is eleven.
    Considering the large amount of the data, we classified all the coaches firstly.
Then we ranked the coaches from the first group, who are the best coaches of all
groups. In this case, we used Euclidean distance-based clustering analysis to
classification.
