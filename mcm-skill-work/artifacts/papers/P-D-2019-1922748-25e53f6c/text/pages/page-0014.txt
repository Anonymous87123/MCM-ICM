6.3 Three types of bottleneck problems

There are many bottlenecks in the actual evacuation of the building crowd. We divide them
into three categories: (1) bottlenecks due to channel supply and demand imbalances (2)
bottlenecks due to uneven distribution of exports (3) single room export bottlenecks.
