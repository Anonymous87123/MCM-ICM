5.1 Network Representation

    Before analyzing the impact of the Francis Scott Key Bridge, we model Baltimore’s
transportation network G as a 4-tuple: G = (V, E, A, W), where V = {vi}ni=1 is a set
of nodes through edges_all.csv, E ⊆ V × V directed edges (road segments) through
edges_all.csv, A : E → Rd denotes Edge attribute function, W : V → Rk denotes Node
weighting function.

    The transportation network comprises two fundamental topological elements: in-
tersections (nodes) and road segments (edges), each characterized by distinct geomet-
ric and functional attributes.

Node Structure Each intersection node vi ∈ V is formally defined as a quadruple
encapsulating its essential properties:
