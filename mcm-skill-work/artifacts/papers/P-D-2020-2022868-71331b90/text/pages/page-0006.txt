1. Passing Network Centroid

   The centroid of a passing network for a team is calculated as the average start position
   of all passes. In particular, the x-coordinate of a passing network centroid indicates the
   average position of the passing network along the length of the ﬁeld and is a metric of how
   aggressively a team is playing as a whole.

2. Dispersion of Players around Centroid

   The dispersion of players around the centroid represents how spread out a passing network
   is with respect to its average position. It is calculated as:
