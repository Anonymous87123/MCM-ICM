and the classification standard of tree age class and age group, the forest life is divided
into five stages, Young Forest (<41), Middle Age Forest (41-80), Near Mature Forest
(81-100), Mature Forest (101-140), Overripe Tree (>141). With the increase of tree age,
biomass increases, and its average productivity decreases [6].
