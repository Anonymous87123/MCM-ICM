Basic Assumptions
         1.     Electric cars mainly refer to hybrid electric cars.

          2.    a1 , a2 , b1 , b2 are constants, because they are determined by the specifications
     of the cars themselves instead of exterior factors.

         3.     a0 , b0 will change according to exterior factors.
