is, for any given spatial coordinate in the habitat, the color of the location represents the biomass of
the plant at that given location at the specified point in time. Figure 4e represents a graph of the total
plant biomass in over the 1000 week period. Note how the periods of sudden decline correspond to the
periods of drought shown in Figure 2.
