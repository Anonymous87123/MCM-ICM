          Figure 10: Dynamics of the two-plant system with more frequent drought cycles.

    Figure 10 shows that more frequent droughts, unlike more severe droughts, do not ever threaten
the overall existence of the plant community. Indeed, Figure 10c and 10d show that the plants are still
considerably alive after periods of drought, while Figure 10g illustrates that the differences between
drought and non-drought periods are not as extreme. This suggests that generally, more frequent but
less severe droughts are preferable to their alternative.
