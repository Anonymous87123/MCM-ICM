no plants remaining, though the clustered shapes of the plants remain. However, the plants are able
to make a quick recovery from these periods of drought, as they have not completely gone extinct, as
shown in Figures 9e-9h. Figure 9i highlights the drastic peaks and troughs that occur as a result of the
severe droughts, with the total biomass of the system nearly falling to zero at certain points.
