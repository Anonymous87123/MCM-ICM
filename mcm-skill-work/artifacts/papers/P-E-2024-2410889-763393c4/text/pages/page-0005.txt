are "economic beings". That is, insurance companies are most concerned with proﬁt,
and when the proﬁt is positive, insurance companies are willing to underwrite policies

in a region. In order to calculate the proﬁt, we ﬁrst consider how to evaluate the risk

of a policy in a region, which is set as RO in this model. Then we develop a premium

pricing strategy based on RO. To obtain greater income as well as to reduce risk, we
