   Since the main exit of the Louvre is on the 2nd floor, it takes the longest time to
evacuate from the 2nd floor. Although there are entrances and exits on both sides of the

Louvre, the exit of the Lions Gate is not connected to other rooms on the ground floor;
the exit of Richelieu will give priority to people on the north side. Therefore, if people
on the 2nd floor choose to go out, it will cause more big congestion. Therefore the best
way to exit the 2nd floor is to return to the main exit of the Napoleon Hall.
