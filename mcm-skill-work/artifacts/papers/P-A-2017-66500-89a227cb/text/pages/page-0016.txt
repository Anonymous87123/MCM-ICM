8.2. Strategy for Emergency Water Flow Situations
     Zambezi river basin suffers from natural disasters frequently, the major of them are long-
term drought and outburst flood. In order to deal with these emergencies, we should make
further adjustments to our system.
8.2.1. Long-term Drought
     During the long-term drought period, the discharge of the whole river is generally reduced.
Our adjustments are made to satisfy the need that the water supply in the downstream is
