   Host-fish
   Existing studies have demonstrated that [13], parasitism may reduce host density and
induce extinction of the host population. Let the biomass of host-fish be H = H(x, y, t),
and divide the hosts into those that are parasitized, Hi = Hi (x, y, t), and those that are
not, Hu = Hu (x, y, t), then the change in the population should be expressed as follows:
Hi = Hu (x, y, t), and Hu = Hu (x, y, t). Then the change in the biomass of host-fish should
be expressed as:
