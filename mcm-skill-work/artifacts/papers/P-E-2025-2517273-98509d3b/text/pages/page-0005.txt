                                               Agricultural Decline Phase Model

                                                                       For the decline phase of agricultural conversion, we develop a detailed model focusing on soil

                                                                       phosphorus, ryegrass, aphids, and ladybugs. The aphid-ladybug interaction is modeled using a Holling

                                                                       Type III functional response to reflect the density-dependent chemical defense mechanisms of aphids.

                                                                       At high aphid densities, the production of alarm pheromones and defensive compounds reduces

                                                                       predation efficiency, creating a nonlinear predation response that stabilizes the system. The model also

                                                                      incorporates time-dependent chemical control through herbicide and insecticide applications, simulating
                                                                       external interventions and their impacts on ecosystem dynamics.
