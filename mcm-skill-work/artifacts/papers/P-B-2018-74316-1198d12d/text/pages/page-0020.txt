      5.3 Resource-saving Suggestions – MINE Model
      Except for the original assumptions, one more assumption is added:
      Global communication is between New York, Tokyo, Hong Kong and London, four major economic
      centers.
      Based on the above model, we have set up eight international offices. We are considering reducing some
      of our new offices to reduce costs. New offices are expected to spread around the world as far as possible
      so as to improve coverage, because the coverage radius of offices that are too close from each other will
      easily overlap, resulting in a waste of resources. We identify overly dense offices by the Maximum
      Information-based Nonparametric Exploration proposed by M Filosi (2014).
