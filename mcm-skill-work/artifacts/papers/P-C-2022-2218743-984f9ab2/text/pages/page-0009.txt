    The Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC) is an information criterion function that can be
used to determine the optimal order of a model, and is constructed based on a Likelihood
function. According to the historical data, we calculate the BIC value of the model under
diﬀerent orders through the computer programming loop, and ﬁnd the order p and q that make
the BIC the smallest, that is, the optimal order of the model. After determining the optimal
order, we perform parameter estimation, and then calculate the predicted prices Gt and Bt of
gold and bitcoin on day t.

3. Residual test:

    In order to determine the validity of the model, a residual test is also required, in which a
white noise veriﬁcation is needed for the residual sequence {aˆt}. If the residuals are randomly
and normally distributed and have no autocorrelation, it means that the residual sequence
approximates a white noise sequence, indicating that the model ﬁtting eﬀect is great. We use
the Ljung-Box statistic Q(m) to test the proximity to a white noise:

linear dependence of the data.
