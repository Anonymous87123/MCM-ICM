   In summary, we can conclude the following,
    • The model advocates utilizing as much power as possible at the start of a race, regardless of
the kind of athlete. However, after a certain distance, aim to maintain the power at critical power.
This implies that putting in a respectable all-out effort at the start of the race will have little
effect on the backstretch.
    • As we can see, for all power profiles, the model suggests that cyclists ride uphill with a
particular level of power then ride downhill with as little power as possible while maintaining pace.
Therefore, in the actual race, cyclists should make reasonable use of riding downhill to rest and
have some physical preparation for the upcoming ride uphill.
