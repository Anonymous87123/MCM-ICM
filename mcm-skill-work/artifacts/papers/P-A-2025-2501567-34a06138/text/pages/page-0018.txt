 Algorithm 1: Stair Wear Monte Carlo Simulation
     Input: T : Total simulation time
     l, w: The length and width of the stair
     α, H, g: Material wear coefficient, hardness and correction
     N : Number of people per simulation interval
     µw , σw : Mean body weight and standard deviation of body weight
     µn , σn : Mean number of people using the stairs simultaneously and standard
     deviation
     pup : Probability of going upstairs
     Output: wear: Stair wear
 1 wear ← [l, w];
 2 for time ← 0 to T do
 3     for person ← 1 to N do
 4         weight ← NormalDistribution(µw , σw );
 5         parallel ← NormalDistribution(µn , σn );
 6         if Random() < pup then
 7             direction ← UP;
 8         else
 9             direction ← DOWN;
10         end
11         normal_f orce ← GRF(weight, direction);
12         sliding_distance ← L(weight, direction);
13         h ← normal_f orce · sliding_distance
            (x, y) ← PositionGenerator(direction, parallel);
14         wear[x, y] ← wear[x, y] + α × Hh × g;
