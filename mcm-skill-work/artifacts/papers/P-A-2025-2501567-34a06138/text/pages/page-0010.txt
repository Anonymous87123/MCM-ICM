4.3     Walking Frequency Model
4.3.1   Frequency Estimation
In real-world scenarios, wear volume is directly related to the wear depth on the stair-
case surface. The wear depth provides crucial information about how much material has
been removed due to usage. In analyzing the frequency, we simplified the process by esti-
mating the frequency based on the overall wear level rather than using complex physical
models for detailed calculations.
