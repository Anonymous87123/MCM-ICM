8.2 Weakness

   • Inaccuracy
     When considering the value of parameters, we obtain them through a lot of differ-
     ent research papers which may influence the result of our model.

   • Simplifying assumptions
     To simplify the model, we make a few assumptions which may affect the result of
     our model.

   • Lack of data
     For the lack of data, we have to simulate the models abstracted from the reality to
     obtain the data we need which may be different from the real data.
