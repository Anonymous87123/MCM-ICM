3.2.1 Relationship Between Water and Environmental Capacity

    Above, we designed the models to describe the plant growth under the environment con-
straints, and the interactions between species. However, interactions between plants and the
larger environment also have an important role on the competition. According to Assump-
tion 2, under the drought, soil moisture is the most important factor determines whether a
plant will live or die.
    Assuming that W is the soil moisture, and bestWi represents the optimum soil moisture of
the ith plant. Then We use Envi to denote the environmental capacity of the ith plant affected
by soil moisture.
   Envi has several important features:
   • when W equals to bestWi , Envi will equal to Env;
   • when there is a huge difference between W and bestWi , which means |W − bestWi | →
     +∞, Envi will approaches to zero.
