5.2 Task 5 : Impacts from Habitat Reduction and Environmental Pollution
    In this section, we will study the effects of habitat reduction and pollution. The total
land available for plant to survive has been squeezing by the logging and consequently and
caused a decrease in the environmental capacity of the community. In addition, due to the
excessive emission, some lands are polluted, which makes it difﬁcult for plants to survive. So
the actual land available for plants in the community will be reduced, which will also lead
to the reduction of environmental capacity. Thus, the consequences of habitat reduction and
pollution can be attributed to the reduction of environmental capacity.
    Therefore, we will gradually reduce the environmental capacity of the plant community
and observe the changes in plant biomass during this process. We set the environmental
capacity as a step function to simulate the gradual habitat reduction or environmental pollu-
