1	Because of serious global pollution problems and energy shortages, the study of the environmental and economic effects of developing electric vehicles has become increasingly important.
2	In this paper, we focus on and provide comprehensive solutions to the following issues:  How does tax rate influence the numbers of Conventional Vehicles (CV) and Electric Vehicles (EV)?  What impacts will the replacement of CVs by EVs on oil stock?  What is the upper-limit proportion of EVs under the condition of the present power generation ability?
3	A Static Supply-Demand Model is set up to answer these questions.
4	According to the influence of tax rate on supply-demand relationship, we analyze the change in the demand of CVs over tax rate.
5	Then, we estimate how long the oil will be consumed up by PHEVs using the supply-price relationship.
6	Finally, we provide an estimation of the upper-limit proportion of EVs under the condition of the present power generation ability.  What is the relationship between the change in the numbers of CVs and EVs?  How do factors such as government control, corporation decision influence the change in the numbers of CVs and EVs?
7	We successfully develop a Competing Ordinary Differential Equation Model to solve these problems.
8	We first build up the equations using linear approximation, and then we fit the real data using least-squares principle and get an ordinary differential equation.
9	With the help of the previous Static Supply-Demand Model, we discuss the real meanings of the fitted variables and the interaction between CVs and EVs.  How does the development of EVs influence the environment?  Whether the development of EVs can save fossil fuels?  Whether different types of EVs have the same benefits to environment, energy, or society?
10	To answer these questions, we build up a linear programming model and set minimum environmental cost as the destination.
11	We find the impact of popularizing EVs on the environment.
12	We also demonstrate the advantages and disadvantages of three types of EVs.
13	In the end, we give recommendations for the government on its role to support and guide the development of EVs.
