1	Day Trading in Bitcoin and Gold Everyday, market traders buy and sell financial assets with a goal to maximize their total return.
2	Two popular investment asset choices are gold and bitcoin.
3	Bitcoin is an extremely volatile asset and does not have a strong fundamental base like other assets on the market.
4	However, it is able to be traded every day.
5	In contrast, gold is stable, often used as a hedge against downward movements in other sectors, but it can only be traded Monday through Friday – days when the market is open.
6	Our team has been asked by a trader to develop a model, using only past asset prices to date, that determines daily trading actions, in order to maximize profit.
7	For each purchase or sale of bitcoin and gold, a cost is applied to the transaction.
8	We have built a model that calculates the optimal selling and buying times using past values that take into account transaction fees and balances the ideal amount of gold and bitcoin traded.
9	It uses an instantaneous logic model to analyze current market features and determine the most favorable trading action.
10	The model runs from 9/11/2016 to 9/10/2021 and only uses the data given before the time slot it is currently in.
11	Although the data is not continuous, it serves our purpose well as our model uses a macro scale prediction process to recommend trades.
12	We utilize two different day trading theories and combine them into a weighted model.
13	Using common day trading theories we implement logic to guide our model’s deci- sion making.
14	Reversion to the mean and momentum theory contribute heavily and are the drivers of the model that choose whether a trade should be executed.
15	We add, critically, a normalizing weight factor W.
16	This weight factor cancels out noise that could prompt the model to choose a less favorable trade.
17	On top of the model for standard market con- ditions, we add logic that is activated in extreme market conditions and takes precedence over the standard model.
18	The main advantage of our approach is that the model utilizes a well-made conglom- eration of strategies allowing us to avoid common day trading failures.
19	The model han- dles extreme market conditions, and can choose between different trading options; im- portant things when trading a volatile medium like bitcoin.
20	Limitations of our approach are a fixed logic system.
21	It is not sensitive to small changes that might be capitalized on by different strategies, and chooses to wait for macroscopic trends in the market.
22	Key Words: Momentum Trading, Technical Analysis, Reversion to Mean, Noise Can- cellation, Normalization, Time Aggregation
