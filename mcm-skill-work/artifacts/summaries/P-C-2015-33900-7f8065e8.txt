1	In this paper, we build a dynamic network model and perform various simulations to study the behavior of Human Capital Network (HCN) under disturbance such as churns.
2	We detail the structure of HCN factors and continuing effects of turnover and dy- namics of HCN utilizing given information and run simulations to examine the contagion of turnover and analyze the sustainability of HCN.
3	We interpret and present our results along with robustness analysis and strengths and weaknesses of our model.
4	For Task 1, we build two networks (Personnel Relationship Network and Organiza- tional Network), which together form our Human Capital Network.
5	We extract important information from problem material and make assumptions when necessary to deduce the detailed structure and assign employees with the offices.
6	We propose an evaluation function of assignments and use Genetic Algorithm to find the optimal assignment.
7	The assign- ment provides us with new information about employee relationship(co-office relationship etc.), together with the supervisory relationship form the edges in HCN and complete our network building.
8	For Task 2, we first detail the turnover in terms of factors and impacts and evaluate the cost and productivity of HCN.
9	We then dig into the dynamics in terms of turnover process, promotion process and recruitment process using probabilistic method and discrete time system simulation.
10	The results show that organizational churn has both direct and indirect effects on the organization’s productivity.
11	The direct effect is because it causes loss of employee and the indirect is because it will make other employee more likely to turnover.
12	For Task 3, we utilize our dynamic network model with our discrete time simulating system to calculate the budget.
13	Our result is a budget requirement of 19.14σ for recruiting and 10.87σ for training in the next 2 years, adding up to 30.01σ.
14	For Task 4, we also run simulations and results show that ICM can’t sustain its 80% full status.
15	For Task 5, our results show that promotion increases the number of high-level em- ployee and improve the quality of staff because only those qualified can be promoted.
16	Thus promotion increases the productivity.
17	We also make further discussions on Multi-layer Networks and HR issues which com- plete Task 6, 7 and other requirements that the problem material brought forth.
