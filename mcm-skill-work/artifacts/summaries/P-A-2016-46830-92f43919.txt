1	Keep It Warm Summary Do you usually get annoyed with the cooling of water during a relaxing bath?
2	In this paper, several models are proposed to solve the problem.
3	Our efforts in building these models followed the path below.
4	Firstly, a universal thermodynamic model(UTM) of bath water is established by constructing two initial sub-equations named the Heat Differential Equation(HDE) and the Temperature Distribution(TD) respectively.
5	Based on Fourier's law, the Stefan-Boltzmann law and the first law of thermodynamics, HDE is established, which also takes heat conduction, heat radiation and evaporation of water into account.
6	Moreover, we get the numerical solution of TD by using the PDE tool of MATLAB, and fit its temperature distribution surface by the plane equation, whose goodness of fit is 0.931.
7	The fitting equation is regarded as the approximate analytical solution.
8	Secondly, taking convection caused by human movement and comfort of water temperature into account, we developed an optimization control model for the bath strategy (OCM) based on UTM, which aims at the smallest water injection, the best temperature stability & the highest comfort level.
9	We use the model(OCM) to optimize the two following strategies： i.
10	Injecting hot water constantly at a certain flow; ii.
11	Injecting hot water when temperature declines to Tmin1 and stopping injecting when temperature increase to Tmin 2 .
12	In strategy I, the optimal conditions are that the flow is 0.007L  s 1 and the temperature of water injection is 311.1K.
13	The optimization proves efficient after extensive discussion; In strategy II, the upper and lower critical temperature and the temperature of water injection are 311.7K ，311.7K & 313.1K respectively.
14	It means that the hot water injection is a continuous process.
15	Thirdly, we modify UTM with the actual situation of bubble water to analyze the influence of bubble water towards the optimization, concluding that the heat diss ipating power of bubble water is 45.85% of that of normal hot water at 312K.
16	By OCM, we get the optimal conditions as the flow is 0.004L  s 1 and the temperature of water injection is 311.9K , which shows that the efficiency of bubble water exceed greatly that of normal hot water.
17	Finally, we made error analysis of the factors like external environment etc. and did stability analysis of the geometric parameters of the bathtub & the parameters of human body.
18	The sensitivities of the factors prove the models to be feasible and applicable.
