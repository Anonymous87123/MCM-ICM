1	In 2018, the number of people in the Louvre was 10.8 million, creating a new high.
2	At the same time, in the event of an emergency, such a large number of tourists will increase the difficulty of evacuation and raise the risk.
3	Therefore, our goal is to design the Louvre's emergency evacuation plan to evacuate visitors from the museum and estimate the evacuation time, while also allowing emergency personnel to enter the building as quickly as possible, as well as considering other factors and potential threats.
4	We make the Visitor Emergency Evacuation(VEE) model to design the evacuation plan.
5	The first model of the emergency evacuation plan is based on an improved ant colony optimization algorithm.
6	The model transforms the entire Louvre into a topological map and influences the selection probability by setting the pheromone concentration threshold to determine the optimal evacuation route for visitors to leave from each region.
7	We also established an evacuation time model and found that the shortest evacuation time for the largest number of visitors in the library was 417.2 s.
8	This result indicates that our evacuation plan is reliable.
9	The third model uses the improved Dijkstra algorithm.
10	The model considers the entire Louvre as a time-varying topology map to determine the route of emergency personnel to the emergency area as quickly as possible.
11	By assuming an emergency in the 101 area of the -1F and adding a new exit near the B3 stairs of the 0F, the optimal path for the emergency personnel to reach the area and the shortest time of 130s are obtained.
12	This result shows that our model is reliable.
13	We also discussed other factors that have an impact on evacuation time, such as whether to open new exports, the impact of tourist diversity, the consequences of potential threats, and so on.
14	And we propose policy and procedural recommendations for emergency management of the Louvre .
15	Additionally, we discuss how we can adapt and implement our models for other large, crowded structures.
16	Then we performed sensitivity analysis on two important parameters: the number of people passing through the stairs and the speed.
17	The results show that the changes of these two parameters will have a huge impact on the shortest evacuation time.
18	Key Words: Emergency evacuation plan, Ant colony optimization algorithm, Time model, Dijkstra algorithm, Other factors
