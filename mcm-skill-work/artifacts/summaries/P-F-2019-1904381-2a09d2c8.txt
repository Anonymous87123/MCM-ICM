1	Digital Currency System is Coming Summary In recent years, digital currency has developed rapidly and become an in- dispensable part of the modern economic system.
2	We construct a brand-new dynamic macroeconomic operation system, taking digital currency into account.
3	Our model is based on three perspectives: individual choice, national economy, international economy.
4	We ﬁrst consider the individual behavior choice deci- sion, which is because the aggregate demand of all micro-individuals selections determine the domestic economic operation state, describing as the product mar- ket equilibrium and money market equilibrium.
5	We use models to analyze three short-term economic as well as a long-term economic performance objective, un- der different exchange rate systems, economic shocks and policies.
6	We ﬁrst build a virtual country whose central bank issues the new digital currency.
7	The impact of digital currency on the current banking system and macroeconomic system is determined by the economic interaction with a par- ticular country in the real world.
8	We discuss two different exchange rate systems that a country will choose.
9	Using theoretical analysis and R, we ﬁnd that either systems would achieve in- ternal and external equilibrium and realize economic objects if proper policies are implemented when digital currency is introduced.
10	The innovation of our model is that we introduce the parameters of interna- tional capital ﬂows (ICF) to the model.
11	We analyze the impact of ICF on economy of the country both theoretically and empirically.
12	It is inspiring that we ﬁnd that decentralized digital currency can not only run the economy more efﬁciently by removing barriers to currency ﬂows, but also enhance the world’s welfare by the ultra-high liquidity of digital currency.
13	To ensure the new banking and the macroeconomic systems involving digital currency can operate with stability and effectiveness we propose all countries accepting digital currencies co-create a United Nations-afﬁliated organization– the World Digital Money Bank (WDCB) and develop a series of regulations.
