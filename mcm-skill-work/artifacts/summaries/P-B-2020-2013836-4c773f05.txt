1	Analytical Shape Design For The Most Durable Sandcastle Foundation Summary Sandcastle building, a recreational activity popular among beach goers, offer people with opportunities to create their exclusive sand-made works and making a sandcastle foundations is typically the first step of simple or complicated sandcastles.
2	However, sandcastle foundations are always threatened by external destructive power.
3	Since that not all sandcastles preform the same in dangerous surroundings, people may wonder what should be done to create a more durable sandcastle foundation and whether there is a desired shape of it.
4	In this paper, a cellular automata based mathematical model is established to find the answer.
5	Firstly, we derived models for tides and sea waves by analyzing the movement of seawater.
6	Accordingly, the internal force analysis and external force analysis were carried out, the former includes viscous force model and collapse model, the latter is comprised of impact force and lift.
7	Next, we demonstrated the process of force analysis after dividing the relative position of sand cells into different cases.
8	Furthermore, sand-to-water proportion influences the stability of sandcastle foundation by changing the value of viscous force, which is the basis for identifying the optimal sand-to-water proportion.
9	Afterwards we extend our model with consideration of rain, whose impact force is much more larger than that of seawater.
10	Likewise, sand-to-water mixture proportion also changes when surface contacts water.
11	In computer simulation, we generated initial sandcastle foundations on their 2-D embryos with different gradients.
12	Through iteration algorithm, a series of best 3-dimensional geometric shapes are found, featured with shell-shaped cross section.
13	The ideal shape can be divided into three parts.
14	Part A buffers the incoming sea water and decelerates the water without being broken.
15	Part B cuts off the seawater flow directly, while it could further mitigate the seawater.
16	Part C is the back bulk whose construction is steady, containing most of the sand.
17	In parallel, we figured out the optimal sand-to-water mixture proportion, which is 2.63, or 2.70 in the rain.
18	In the end, we listed practical strategies to make the sandcastle foundation last longer: the adjustment of building time, adding supporting structures and improving building materials.
19	Finally, we finished sensitivity analysis and summarized the strengths and weaknesses of the model.
