1	With the development of global urbanization, urban planning has been a hot spot of most concern.
2	Since the traditional urban sprawl plan has been gradually unable to meet the development needs of the city, smart growth is proposed in 1990’s.
3	In this paper, a series of methods are developed to help to implement smart growth theories into city de- sign.
4	In task 1, based on the gathered abundant data about smart growth of city, 25 indi- cators are selected by principal component analysis.
5	Then we construct a new three-level indicator system, where the first level has 1 indicator, the second level has 5 indicators and the third level has 25 indicators to evaluate the success degree of the smart growth.
6	Moreover, we use the entropy weight method (EWM) to get weight vector of the indica- tors in the third level and the group decision making method (GDM) to get the weight vector of indicators in the second-class.
7	Meanwhile, the standard of the success of the smart growth is obtained through K-means algorithm.
8	A metric system which include the intensity of land, social equity degree, traffic quality, coordination degree and economic development level, is eventually built to measure the success of smart growth of a city.
9	In task2, Pittsburgh and Ningguo are selected to research their current growth plan.
10	Through the metric proposed in Task 1, the strengths and weaknesses of the current growth plan of selected cities is measured.
11	In task3, our smart growth plan is developed based on the index value of the cities.
12	Then the support vector machine (SVM) and weighted moving average method(WMAM) are adopted to predict the change of indicators.
13	The combined prediction model is subse- quently adopted to minimize the prediction error.
14	Eventually, by comparing the indicator between current city plan and our smart growth plan, we can see the success of our smart growth plan.
15	In task4, the single effect of the individual initiatives is evaluated by our metric to rank the potential.
16	The result shows that the most potential to the success degree of the smart growth is the initiative of the economic development in Ningguo, while in Pitts- burgh is the initiative of improving land intensive rate.
17	In task5, since the population structure between the two cites is different, two dif- ferent population growth models(PGM) are adopted to predict the change of the popula- tion.
18	The growth rate calculated by PGM demonstrates that our plan can adjust adaptively to satisfied the increasing need of population.
19	Finally, we analyze the strengths and weaknesses of the methods we proposed in this paper.
20	The research can also satisfy the need of the ICM and make reasonable growth theories into city design.
