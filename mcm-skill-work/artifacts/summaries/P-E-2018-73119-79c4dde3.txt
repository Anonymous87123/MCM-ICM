1	In the past two decades, regional instability caused by some fragile state(s) has become more and more concerned by the whole world.
2	Among the various factors, climate changes usually lead to high frequency of extreme weather events, which significantly exacerbate the fragility of a certain state.
3	How to mitigate the impact of climate changes and prevent a state from becoming a fragile one has been widely recognized as a very important and urgent issue.
4	As a response, in order to predict a state’s fragility accurately, we develop a nov-el model called PSA (Pressure Sensitivity Adaptability), which is significantly ex-tended from the well-known PSR (Pressure State Response) model.
5	Specifically, our model contains three dimensions whose weights are obtained by combining AHP (Analytic Hierarchy Process) and EWM (Entropy Weight Method).
6	Notice that FI (Fragile Index) is a weighted sum of the three dimensions denoting the fragility of a certain state.
7	In our empirical studies, we verify the effectiveness of our model via the ground truth of the public fragile state index.
8	Meanwhile, we use a regression analysis method to get functional relationship between non- climatic indicators and climatic indicators.
9	We choose DRC (Democratic Republic of the Congo) and BD (Bangladesh) in our case studies.
10	Specifically, we use the values of the indicators of a decade as the input data fed to our model, and obtain the FI curve.
11	For DRC, the FI curve without the effect of climate changes is modeled by keep the values of the corresponding climate indicators constant.
12	For BD, we use GM (1,1) to draw the prediction curve of FI and find that the time that BD is likely to become a stable state is around 2076.
13	Furthermore, we study six major intervention policies of BD for climate changes.
14	We find that natural disasters in BD are serious and suggest investing more fiscal expenditure in intervention2 (intervention2: Comprehensive Disaster Managemen-t).
15	It is estimated that this expenditure will increase by 35% every 5 years.
16	We can then get other policies’ fiscal expenditures through GM (1, 1), and finally get the total expenditure in 5 years, 10 years and even 20 years.
17	We select Asia, Europe and Africa so as to study the portability of our proposed model.
18	For continents, the fragile indexes of several representative countries are selected and averaged in order to estimate the continent fragile index.
19	The result-s are very promising, which clearly showcases the effectiveness of our model on continents.
20	Finally, we conduct a sensitivity analysis in order to gain some deep under- standing of our model, and conclude the report via discussing the strengths and weaknesses of our proposed model.
