1	Summary Based on the existing Finite Element Method (FEM) technique, we consider two models to analyze the temperature distribution in the bathtub, including without and with a person in it.
2	Then, we build two inflow models, namely, the continuous inflow model and the discontinuous inflow model.
3	At last, we employ Fuzzy Comprehensive Evaluation (FCE) to evaluate these two inflow models in order to find an optimal solution.
4	First, regarding the model without a person in the bathtub, we calculate the heat dissipation in the water-to-air heat convection and build a hot-water tube model for calculating the heat addition.
5	What’s more, to find the temperature distribution in the bathtub, we simulate such model on ANSYS, which is a large-scale common finite element software.
6	Second, we consider the model with a person in the bathtub from two aspects, namely, the person in the still and the moving states.
7	Specific to the moving state of person, we find that there exist two ways of dissipating heat, including the water-to-air heat convection and the water-to-bathtub heat conduction.
8	Furthermore, we exploit MATLAB to find the temperature curve in natural cooling process. .
9	Third, based on the analysis of above two conditions, we propose to simulate on the continuous and discontinuous inflow models of the hot water.
10	Besides, to find an optimal strategy, we evaluate these two models using fuzzy comprehensive evaluation (FCE), where the variables include the amount of needed water, the comfortable degree of user, the operation complexity and the operative difficulty index.
11	Fourth, to analyze the sensitivity of two models with and without person, we extensively change the velocity and the temperature of the hot inflow, the air temperature, the surface area, volume and material of the bathtub, and the volume of the person body.
12	At last, we further study the model for cleansing with a bubble bath additive.
13	In this case, we vary the condition of the bubble to observe this model’s performance.
