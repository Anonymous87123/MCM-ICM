1	Owing to the report put forward by the Institution of Risk Management of South Africa, the Kariba dam badly needs maintenance.
2	The three options Zambezi River Authority provided might address the problem.
3	Our modeling group make assessments to the three options and provides feasible plans to replace the former Kariba dam with several smaller dams.
4	The requirements are satisfied in this paper.
5	With regard to requirement 1, we list factors of costs and benefits in advance.
6	Then, we categorize potential cost into two parts, direct cost and indirect cost.
7	With relative profiles, the two categories are quantized.
8	However, the benefits of the three options can hardly be quantified.
9	So we take advantage of fuzzy synthetic evaluation method and work out the relative levels of benefits in the three options.
10	Finally, the return on investment (ROI) is calculated.
11	Based on that, we can safely draw conclusion that option 1 is more economically efficient in a short period.
12	But in the long term, option 3 appears to have more potential profits.
13	When it comes to requirement 2, we establish the water balance equation on both reservoirs and channels at the basis of analysis on water transference.
14	Afterwards, we divide the water management capacities into 3 categorizes, the ability against flood, the ability for water supply and ability for electricity generation.
15	Based on analysis to water management, the multi-dam system of versatile functions is proposed innovatively.
16	Considering the actual factors in selecting dam sites, we screen out 29 possible locations.
17	After then, the number and placement of the new dams is determined using optimization model.
18	In order to lower the cost, we set our goal as the minimum number of dams.
19	Along with the restraints of water balance equation and greater water management capacity, we complete optimization.
20	As is shown in our results, 14 dams should be built as alternative to Kariba dam.
21	Finally, error analysis is attached to our model.
22	For the sake that the new multi-dam system can function well under various circumstance, we set our system in three conditions, normal water circles, long-term drought and outburst flood.
23	Particle swarm optimization (PSO) is adopted repeatedly to optimize the control of water discharge.
24	Compare the result to the actual adjustments made by Kariba dam, we can conclude that the new multi-dam system can provide greater level of protection and water management options.
25	Apart form this, we make thorough evaluations towards optimization scheme and distil general strategy in each condition.
26	At last, we apply sensitivity analysis to conditions under long-term drought and outburst flood.
27	The regulation limits are given under extreme conditions.
28	Last but not least, we take extreme disasters into consideration and use Monte Carlo method to make assessments.
29	The result suggests the new system have a better resistance against disasters.
30	Also, we list the overall strengths and weaknesses of our model as well as the further work.
