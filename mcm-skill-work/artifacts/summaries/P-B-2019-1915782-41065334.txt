1	In 2017, the hurricane disaster brought many casualties and substantial economic and property losses to Puerto Rico.
2	We help the NGO HELP, Inc. design a mobile disaster response system that uses drones to transport medical packages to selected areas and to take video reconnaissance of roads in disaster areas.
3	Containers are used to load the system to the disaster area.
4	For the loading problem, the configuration problem of packages in containers is simplified into the combinatorial optimization problem of three-dimensional packing and the optimal configuration problem of medical packages and shipping containers.
5	In this paper, the goal is the maximum space utilization rate of the ISO container, and the constraints in the loading process are as follows: in order to maintain the stability of the contents, each about to be loaded item must be fully supported; In order to facilitate loading and unloading, each item can be entirely separated by vertical or horizontal plane; The ratio of the 3 medical packages is certain.
6	We establish a single objective programming model and use the improved 3D-RSO algorithm to solve the model.
7	Aiming at the location problem, this paper establishes a two-objective programming model aiming at achieving the shortest transportation time and the longest total reconnaissance road to determine the number and location of ISO containers.
8	By analysis and calculation, it is found that under the condition of meeting the daily demand of five hospitals, at least 3 containers are needed to supply medical packages.
9	For the medical supply delivery and video reconnaissance, the specific quantity of drones is determined firstly, and then the loading model is used to determine the assembly scheme of the ISO container.
10	Then the route and schedule of the drone transport medical package are calculated by traversal calculation of all roads.
11	Finally, the transportation plan and the length of roads that can be detected are given.
12	The optimal geographical location distribution (in latitude and longitude coordinates) of the ISO container is calculated as follows: container 1 at (18.3805,-66.5109), container 2 at (18.3490,-66.2830), and container 3 at (18.2191,-65.8241).And we give the detailed daily transportation plan.
13	The daily medical supply delivery can be completed in 38.7124min.
14	The total length of the road that can be detected is 722.38km.
15	The daily video reconnaissance can be completed in 12h.
16	It is calculated that the specific assembly scheme of ISO container is that container 1 contains 53 drones B, 1 Tether H, 700 MED1, 350 MED2, and 350 MED3, and the space utilization rate is 89.10%.
17	Container 2 contains 54 drones B, 1 Tether H, 684 MED1, 342 MED2, and 342 MED3, and the space utilization rate is 89.48%.
18	Container 3 contains 53 drones B, 1 Tether H, 809 MED1, 0 MED2, and 539 MED3, and the space utilization rate is 91.45%.
19	Finally, the sensitivity analysis and extension of the model are given.
20	Keyword: Drone; The location problem; The loading problem; Emergency rescue
