1	An Educational Donation Mechanism Based On Data Insight Summary Recent years, Big Data has become increasingly popular and the guidance of big data is required in many fields, including charitable field.
2	In our paper, we construct a new ROI evaluation system for charitable organization using data mining methods to process data, and succeed in determining an optimal investment strategy for Goodgrant Foundation.
3	First, we operate on the data.
4	We do data screening according to the integrity and re- dundancy of the information, deleting data with information less than the threshold, and merging different attributes using linear fitting and PCA.
5	For the reserved attributes and schools, we do data imputation to fill the missing data based on K-means Clustering.
6	Then we normalize all the data to make them comparable in the following analysis.
7	Second, we construct a ROI evaluation criteria, which is a ratio of output and input mul- tiplying an adjustment coefficient, named “urgency”.
8	The ratio reflects the benefits in related to the cost, while urgency reflects the demand for money which is an important factor should be considered by charitable organization.
9	We use PCA to select attributes, letting salary, education quality and some others to represent output, tuition to represent input and Federal loan, debt and some others to represent urgency.
10	Then, AHP is used to measure the importance between different factors and allocate weights.
11	Third, we put forward two kinds of model, basic model for one year and time series model for five year.
12	Seeing the ROI as benefits from investment, we introduce the fluctuation of output as “risk”, imitating the concept of Modern Portfolio Theory in the financial sector to solve the problems.
13	In the basic model, we develop a Mixed Integer Linear Pro- gramming Algorithm and succeed in finding 14 schools for the investment.
14	Further, we consider the time factor and improve the model into a time series model, using MILP and Grey Prediction to determine the long-term investment strategy. 16 schools are chosen with different time duration and different amounts of money.
15	Finally, we make sensitivity analysis for our model, changing the amount of schools, the restriction for money, whether allocating the money equally or not and so on to analyze the different results of the output and to find the better parameters for ideal results.
16	To sum up, our model is a feasible and reasonable model with technical and data support.
17	Because of the subjectivity, this model can be used flexibly after data training.
