1	As society’s networks evolve, information becomes a new kind of property because of its great power to change public opinion.
2	In our paper, we study the flow of information, the evolution of communication networks and the information’s influence on publics.
3	At the beginning, we describe the topologies of two types of communications which are Mass Communication and Interpersonal Communication.
4	By combining these two types of communications, we construct the single-layer communication network which represents a certain communication technique.
5	Then we study the six periods classified by the techniques and construct the multi-layer information networks based on the single-layer network.
6	Then we define the three attributes of information which are extremeness, entertainment and newness.
7	By using the concept of Indifference Curve, we determine the inherent value of information based on its attributes.
8	And we find the news according to the Pareto Principle.
9	Combining the multi-layer information networks and the inherent value of information, we construct the dynamic information flow model.
10	This model is used to describe how information flows within our society’s networks.
11	In order to validate our models, we find data of a piece of news of BBC Tech and simulate the information flow in today’s society.
12	We find that the real amount of received nodes are 44 and our model’s result is 43.85.
13	Also, the standard error of received nodes’ amount is 4.63.
14	So, the predict capacity and the reliability of our model is great.
15	We define the capacity as the user percentage, and the relations as the relative strength of networks.
16	By introducing the Life-Cycle Theory, we build a prediction model to predict the relations and capacities of networks in 2050.
17	We find that there will be 7 communication techniques at that time and the strongest network is a rising network whose capacity is 0.9611.
18	Later, we modify our information flow model by appending the opinion of people.
19	Then we use this model to describe the mechanism of the change of public opinion based on Threshold Effect.
20	Then we analyze the influence of four main parameters in our model and get three main conclusions: (1) Stubborn minorities. (2) Powerful media. (3) More homogeneous more efficient.
21	Finally, we give the strengths and weaknesses of our models.
