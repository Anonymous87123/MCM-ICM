1	Dragons and Ecosystem: 1 + n Meta-population Model Abstract Dragons are the virtual creatures in the fictional television series Game of Thrones.
2	It is interesting and meaningful to consider what would happen if the three dragons were living today.
3	The purpose of this problem is to simulate the competition game when new species are introduced into the ecosystem.
4	And the result of the simulation can be used for reference in the realistic situations.
5	Our paper propose the 1 + n Meta-population Model, where 1 refers to the Growth Model Based on Energy Flow focus on the dragon’s growth and n refers to the Improved n-population Meta-populaiton Model focus on the ecosystem.
6	We first form the Growth Model Based on Energy Flow to represent the growth process of dragons in the food chain.
7	We calculate the energy expenditure using the Kleiber’s Law and find the relationship between the energy intake and dragon’s mass based on the energy flow in the food chain.
8	Referencing the animals similar to dragons, we set the upper bound of dragon’s mass as 1.17 × 103 kg and time-varying growth rates.
9	Then Improved n-population Meta-populaiton Model is generated to quantatively es- timate the dragons’ influence on the populations in the ecosystem.
10	Based on the Tilman n-population Meta-population Model, we form the initial ecosystem including n kinds of populations and then introduce dragons into it.
11	We regard dragons as the interference in the ecosystem.
12	According to the amount and the abundance of living beings in the ecosystem, we can analyze the influence of dragons.
13	Besides, we further take human beings’ recon- struction of environment into consideration, offering guidance for human beings.
14	After that, we apply our models to the different kinds of environments including temper- ate deciduous forest, desert and ice sheet.
15	By calculating, we know that an adult dragon per should take in 2.4 × 1011 J energy per month and the region must be larger than 1.8 × 107 m2 to support three dragons.
16	We then analyze the ecological response when dragons are introduced in the ecosystem, which conforms to strong population extinction mechanism and Odd-even Symmetry Law.
17	In addition, we conduct sensitivity analysis about the area of the habitat and the time-lag.
18	Finally, we discuss strengths and weaknesses of our models.
