3	We develop a model to determine an optimal investment strategy to improve the performance of undergraduate students in the US.
4	Our model has three parts: In the first part, we collect data about the focus of other foundations’ investment by subjects and locations.
5	We consider the charitable identity of the Goodgrant as well.
6	Then we set out to decide our focus, which is to invest more on those schools with more minority races, lower educational performance, higher debt ratio and so on.
7	In this part, we also classify the data into two groups, one for school selecting, and another for ROI determining.
8	In the second part, as a data extraction, we build a efficient and intuitive model to rank the candidate schools in accordance with the correlation of our focus, using the PCA method.
9	After that, the top 50 schools are selected as our target schools.
10	In the third part，we make a key assumption that the social utility of a school has logarithmic relationship with the earnings of graduated students and the graduation rate.
11	More over, we create a parameter k to denote the marginal rate of substitution (MRS) between the two factors above.
12	After that, we come to define the ROI function of each target school as the incremental utility.
13	We further discuss how to devise the best strategy with several methods.
14	At last, we choose the improved PSO algorithm based on augmented Lagrange function.

16	Then we offer a recommending list by the cumulative ROI in five years.
17	What’s more, our model is broad enough to accommodate any non-­‐linear constraint optimization problem.
