3	An Evaluation Model of State Fragility related to Climate Change Based on the PSR Model and Entropy Weight Method Climate change is likely to influence the fragile situation of a state.
4	Thereby, we propose an evaluation model in order to derive a quantitative expression of state fragility as well as the relationship between it and climate change.
5	First, theP-S-R (Pressure-State-Response) Model is applied in our model to offer a distinct framework of the relationship between fragility and related indicators.
6	The model follows a domain-theme-feature structure.
7	Climate change is involved through direct indicators and indirect impact on social and economic environment.
8	Thereafter, we derive the equation of fragility by Entropy Weight Method.
9	Furthermore, we apply the Discriminant Analysis to obtain the value range of fragile, vulnerable and stable states.
10	The corresponding results are0.4991-1, 0.2196-0.4991, and 0-0.2196.
11	Second, we determine the fragility in Sudan from 2012 to 2016, which shows a fluctuation in the limited fragile range.
12	Meanwhile, it is observed that climate change mainly influences on the food security in Sudan through Gray Relational Analysis.
13	Then by establishing a new framework without climatic indicators directly, we acquire the fragility without climate change, and the result is that the fragility declines.
14	Third, for Egypt, our model shows that the fragile situation isgenerally deteriorating from 2012 to 2016 but still regarded as vulnerable.
15	We obtain that climate change mainly impacts on health condition in Egypt.
16	Then the tipping point of fragility is determined as 0.4991 (value of fragility) in this section.
17	By Line Regression Method, we then predict that Egypt would become a fragile state in 2028.
18	Fourth, we propose a series of interventions based on the model, focusing on climate control and improving the coping capability of a country respectively.
19	The total cost of interventions is calculated as 1,120,070,0000 dollars, and the results show that the fragile situation of Egypt would be 8.92% better after implementing interventions.
20	Lastly, we illustrate the comprehensive modifications of model for better applicable to different sizes of state, mostly concentrated on the indicators we choose. key words: PSR Model, Entropy Weight Method, Discriminant Analysis
