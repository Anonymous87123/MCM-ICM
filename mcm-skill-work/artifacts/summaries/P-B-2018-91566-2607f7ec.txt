1	Forecasting the Language Distribution Summary Language shift is a common and complicated phenomenon since ancient times, which has far-reaching influence on the development of the human civilization.
2	The language shift is influenced by many factors.
3	We study the geographically distributed language shift model and provide solutions based on language shift and economic benefits to the locations of new international offices for a service company.
4	To get the geographical language transformation model, we start off by creating a simple model, and then make it more sophisticated.
5	The Worldwide Language Shift Model reflects the situation when two or more languages communicate.
6	The model takes into account factors such as government support, tourism, international business relations and technological progress.
7	The Domestic Language Shift Model is the application of the worldwide language shift model in country.
8	We adjust some parameters to make this model fit the domestic situation better.
9	The Geographical Language Shift Model is based on the domestic language shift model.
10	This model contains the impact of migration on language shift.
11	We collect rich and effective data, fit the unknown parameters and get the geographical distribution of various languages in the future.
12	Through the sensitivity analysis, we prove the stability and error tolerance of the model.
13	By the model implementation, we found that the geographical language distribution remains basically stable in the next 50 years.
14	To address the locations of international offices, we take into account the predicted ge- ographical language distribution and the economic need.
15	In our opinion, choosing the location of an international office should make the company more profitable.
16	To this end, we establish a model of the impact of geographical language distribution on the company’s effectiveness.
17	We identify the six cities most suitable for hosting interna- tional offices in both short and long terms are: Paris, Tokyo, Milan, Toronto, Bombay, Brussels.
18	We also put forward our own ideas about the company’s long-term plan.
19	In a word, we predict the language development for the next 50 years according to the geographical distribution language shift model, and provide effective reference for the locations of international offices.
