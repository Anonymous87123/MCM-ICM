1	Nowadays teamwork is playing an increasingly important role in all walks of life, especially team sports.
2	Team success is much more than the sum of the abilities of individual players.
3	Rather, it is based on many other factors that involve how well the teammates play together.
4	The purpose of this paper is to create a model that captures performance indicators that reﬂect successful teamwork, and generalize the model to other domains.
5	In TASK 1, we establish a ball passing network based on the passing events, in which each player is a node and each pass constitutes a link between players.
6	We deﬁne several indicators helping us know about the network on the whole.
7	Then we utilize K-means Algorithm to classify dyadic and triadic conﬁgurations in the network, which help to identify the network patterns.
8	Moreover, we explore the network with other scales like time and region to further study the network.
9	In TASK 2, we ﬁrst identify performance indicators that reﬂect successful team- work, such as coordination, distribution, tempo, ﬂexibility and pressing.
10	Then an Eval- uation model based on Adversarial Regression is built to score the teamwork performance of both our team and opponent in one match.
11	To make scores of both teams satisfy the ﬁnal result of each game, we regulate the weights of ﬁve indicators, and record every possible solution.
12	Furthermore, we choose the weight with LowestError as the regression equation.
13	Finally, we notice that only by adjusting the strategy based on the opponents’ strategy, trying to make the total score higher or repress the score of opponents, can the Huskies more likely to win.
14	In TASK 3, we analyze the formations and strategies Huskies adopt, along with the indicators and ﬁnal results.
15	Considering the actual situation of the Huskies, the most effective strategy is Defensive counterattack.
16	Then comparing Huskies with Typical representative of this strategy (Italy National Team), we get to know that the huge gap with the strong teams is mainly reﬂected in Coordination, Flexibility and Pressing.
17	Based on the conclusion we draw, we offer the coach some advice on ﬂexible formation, core players and coherence that help them succeed in the next season.
18	In TASK 4, we generalize our model to more teamwork, not limited to team sports.
19	With reference to the indicators we identify for football match, we come up with the complex set of factors that make some groups perform better.
20	In addition, other aspects of teamwork are taken into account to organize more effective teams.
21	Finally, we conduct a sensitivity analysis in order to gain some deep understanding of our model, and verify the robustness of the model in many cases.
22	Additionally, we analyze the strengths and weaknesses of our model.
