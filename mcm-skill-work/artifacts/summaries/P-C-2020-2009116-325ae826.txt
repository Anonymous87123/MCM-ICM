1	Amazon offers customers the opportunity to score and rate.
2	Other customers can use these reviews to help them make purchasing decisions.
3	Based on information such as customer reviews, companies can make decisions that increase product appeal.
4	If the company masters the future development trend of the product, it can provide a basis for business decisions.
5	As for task I, in order to accurately capture the effective information in the data set, a series of data processing methods to process the missing values, abnormal values and repeated observations in the original data set, and uses the combination of qualitative and quantitative methods to screen the unrelated variables.
6	Besides, for making a preliminary understanding of the product market satisfaction, descriptive statistics is applied.
7	And then this paper employs Lexicon-based approach to analyze the text data, and establishes an emotion score evaluation system, which can quickly capture customer satisfaction from each comment.
8	The task II is divided into three subsections depending on the relevance of the issues.
9	In subsection 1, a three-level evaluation system for star rating has been established.
10	After using correlation test to judge the impact of comments and ratings on stars, a comprehensive evaluation system including voting information, comment content and customer identity was established by principal component analysis.
11	On this basis, by referring to the Amazon platform crude oil star algorithm, the time span index is introduced to update the original three-level system, which makes the system more comprehensive.
12	For subsection 2, a time series model is introduced to study the influence of time on comment orientation.
13	First, we visualize the time series to get the emotional trend chart of products and the time trend chart of other variables.
14	Through image analysis, the trend of emotional score of different products is grasped, and the time trend of star rating, cumulative number of comments and cumulative number of likes of three products are obtained.
15	Next, on the basis of the time series model, we introduce the difference autocorrelation moving average model (ARIMA), which can accurately predict the star rating situation and comment emotion trend in September and October 2015.
16	Finally, we discuss the influence of extreme reviews on other variables and the interaction between extreme reviews and extreme stars.
17	In the study of the impact of the follow-up comments on the number and emotional direction of extreme stars, because the data does not conform to the characteristics of normal distribution, the non parametric test becomes the main method of this paper.
18	The influence of extreme emotion words in the comments on the follow-up stars is analyzed in two directions: positive and negative.
19	In summary, the empirical analysis and models about three productions developed in this paper are also a powerful tool for other electronic business platform, showing an excellent performance.
