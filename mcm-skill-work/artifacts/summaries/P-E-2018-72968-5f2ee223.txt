1	SPEC: A Climate-based Fragility Model Summary Effects of Climate Change have aroused growing attention worldwide.
2	The potential damages of Climate Change threaten most regions in the world, especially those fragile states.
3	A fragile state cannot meet its people’s demands, and is quite vulnerable to those climate shocks.
4	As a result, how to measure the fragility of countries counts.
5	Our SPEC Model provides a quantitative analysis of fragility degree for most countries in the world.
6	It considers multiple aspects, including security, politics, economics and Cli- mate Change.
7	We use 20 individual indicators to measure each aspects.
8	Considering the impact of time, we apply Latest-determine Method and Weighted Average Method to do data weight- ing for different indicators.
9	Moreover, in Weighted Average Method, we use the exponential weighting pattern to have realistic time-relating weights to better measure the indicators in a long period.
10	We divide effects of Climate Change into two parts: General impacts and Extreme im- pacts.
11	The general part refers to those indirect effects of Climate Change.
12	We use four indi-cators to represent the influences of rising sea level, decreasing arable lands, deteriorating ecological environment and restrained water source.
13	The extreme part of Climate Change illustrates the potential damage of extreme weath- ers resulting from Climate Change.
14	We apply Self-regulatory Factor to predict countries’ ability to maintain their current condition facing extreme weathers.
15	We use three indicators to further measure the self-regulatory factor.
16	Moreover, self-regulatory factor also relates to the Tipping Point of a country, and we put forward the Tipping Model.
17	We apply Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to determine the weights of indicators.
18	We refer to different databases such as Worldbank.
19	We simulate our model to 178 countries in the world, and we work out the self-regulatory factor and SPEC index of each country.
20	We apply the SPEC model to Yemen, one of the most fragile country and India, a country with ordinary fragility.
21	We predict the total cost of state driven intervention of India.
22	In order to make our model more applicable, we use Re-weighting Method to modify our model.
23	In this way, we find SPEC works well in "smaller" or "larger" states.
24	Finally, we do sensitivity analysis to the SPEC Model and discuss strengths and weak- nesses.
