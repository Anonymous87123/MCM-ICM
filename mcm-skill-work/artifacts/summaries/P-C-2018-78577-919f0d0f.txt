1	Sustainable Energy Assessment Summary Energy is one of the four pillars of modern social development the most basic material basis, and a prerequisite for human civilization.
2	In United States, many aspects of energy policy are decentralized to the state level.
3	In this paper, we are asked by these states to inform their development of a set of goals for their interstate energy compact.
4	First, we preprocess the data, including the processing of missing values and the normalization of the data.
5	In the process of missing value processing, we deal with the indicators lacking a small amount of data by Cubic Spline Interpolation, the indicators lacking less than a half of data by curve fitting, and the indicators missing the vast majority of data by replacing is with the average values of other cities.
6	After that, we use the ARIMA model to predict each state's indicators.
7	It is estimated that by 2025, the overall score of each state will reach CA: 0.2578, AZ: 0.1966, NM: 0.2545, TX: 0.1518.
8	And by 2050, each state’s score will reach CA: 0.3481, AZ: 0.3334, NM: 0.3232, TX: 0.3132.
9	Finally, we set goals of the four states to be raising the proportion of electric generated by renewable energy source to 34% by 2025 and 59% by 2050.
10	We use the proportion of electric generated by renewable energy source to indicate the renewable energy usage and use our model to predict the proportion.
11	To achieve such goals, we recommend the following three actions for the four states: integrating electric market, establishing a fund and apply tax and subsidy policy.
