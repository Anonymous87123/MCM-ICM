3	A New Space Debris Collisions Insurance Model With the rapid increase of human aerospace activities, the population of space debris has become a universal concern.
4	We successfully find a commercial opportunity under the background of serious space debris population, and that is the space debris collision insurance service.
5	The basic idea of this service is that if any debris collision cause damage to the spacecraft during the insurance duration, we will pay for the claims.
6	Meanwhile, in order to reduce the risks of collision and losing money, we will carry out Active Debris Removal (ADR) measures by renting existing ADR technologies.
7	The structure of whole service is demonstrated in fig.3.
8	To calculate the profit of our proposed service, we should know how much our income (premium) is and how much our cost (ADR cost and claims) is.
9	The premium and claims of this service is determined by the premium rate, which is closely related to the effective collision probability.
10	Therefore, we build an analysis model based on Orbital Debris Engineering Model（ORDEM 2000）to anticipate the distribution and flux of space debris.
11	By improving the method introduced by European Space Agency, and using it on the ORDEM 2000 data, we deduce the effective collision probability from the flux.
12	Furthermore, to connect the effective collision probability with the premium rate, we establish a new insurance model which takes the special features of space debris into consideration.
13	The ADR cost is related to the two-steps strategy of ADR.
14	Firstly, by analyzing the expression of effective collision probability, we set a size limit for the debris to be removed.
15	Secondly, based on fuzzy comprehensive evaluation (FCE) method, we build a model to design the ADR strategy for removing the debris with certain range of size.
16	We simulate the whole insurance service via computer.
17	The result indicates that our insurance service has a great profitability and business competitive.
18	Considering that the debris distribution data from ORDEM varies with time and those data determined both premium rate and ADR strategy, our model is a strongly time-dependent one.
19	We choose premium rate, insurance duration and fuzzy evaluation factor for sensitivity analysis, which show that our model is very robust and can deal with some important “What if?” scenarios.
