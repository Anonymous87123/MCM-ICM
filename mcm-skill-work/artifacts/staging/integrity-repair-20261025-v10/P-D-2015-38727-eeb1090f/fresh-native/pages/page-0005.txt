Social Stability
The social Stability index measures a few factors correlated with social unrest. Social
instability may indicate areas that should be coupled with sustainable development for
maximum humanitarian benefits, or populations with special development needs. The
Social stability index may also help identify social factors that can cause resistance to
sustainable development initiatives.
