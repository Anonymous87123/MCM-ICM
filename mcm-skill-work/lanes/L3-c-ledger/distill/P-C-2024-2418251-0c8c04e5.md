## 5. Reusable writing action

**Turn the construct into a signed ledger, and publish the exchange rate of every entry, so a reader can score a match by hand.** Instead of fitting momentum, the chapter declares four events that earn or lose "potential points" and states each magnitude in the same breath as the definition:

- `deﬁne that for each service game, the athlete earns potential points, which are quantiﬁed as 2 points, meaning Ms=2.` (block 109)
- `We deﬁne that for every three consecutive points scored, a potential point for consecutive scoring is earned, quantiﬁed as 2 points, meaning Mc=2.` (block 112)
- `When an athlete achieves a break in a game, the potential point earned is characterized by 5 points, meaning Mb=5.` (block 116)
- `each occurrence of an unforced error reduces the potential points by 0.5, meaning Mu= -0.5.` (block 119)

and then closes the account in one line: `Mi = Mb + Ms + Mc + Mu (11)` (block 121), with the accumulation over a set as (12) and the reset rule stated as a limit (`there is a break at the end of each set where the coach provides guidance to the athlete ... the athlete's total momentum points are reset to zero`).

The action is to make the definition **arithmetic a reader can reproduce**: name the events, give each a signed magnitude at the moment of definition, add them, and state where the sum starts over. It is the cheapest possible answer to "how do you measure something unobservable", and it buys the paper its later moves - because the output is a number per game, it can be fed to PCA (S8), to an SVM (S9) and to ARIMA (S11) without any further justification.

**What it costs.** The exchange rates are asserted, not fitted or cited: nothing says why a break is worth two and a half serves, and the one rate with a visible derivation (`every three consecutive points` → `2 points`) is the only place the ratio is even argued. The sign convention is not stated as a convention, so `Mu= -0.5` has to be discovered from the prose. And the ledger's unit changes without notice - `Mi` is per game, `M` is per set, Tables 6 and 7 report a per-game `Momentum`, and Figures 11 and 12 plot `momentum` and `accumulated momentum` on the same axes - so a reader cannot tell which quantity the `R2 value of 0.90142` in block 147 is scored against.
