## 5. Reusable writing action

Replace a pointwise match error with a distributional one: define the metric as the mean proportion of
neighbours sharing the class, summed over classes against the real data, and then explain it again in
plain English one paragraph later so the abstract expression and the intuitive one are both on the page.

> "In plain English: for each class, for all counties in that class, our spatial autocorrelation measure
> is the mean proportion of neighbors with the same class. Then we take the sum over the classes of the
> difference between the simulated and real spatial autocorrelation measures."
