## 5. Reusable writing action

Announce the truncation inside the table's own sentence, so a five-brand table is never read as the whole
ranking:

> "In our sales strategy, we will recommend the first-class brands with better response in the sales
> market of sunshine company. Limited to space, only 5 first-class brands of each product are listed in
> the text."

The sentence does two jobs at once: it fixes the table's meaning (five brands, ranked, top class only)
and it converts a space cut into a stated sampling rule, which is why the chapter can report ten grades
while showing five brands per product and still be read as a complete classification.
