quoted:

- "Here we use four factors to measure Output : Salary After Graduation, Retention Rate, Repayment Ability and Education Enhance Rate. But why these four factors?"
- "In order to get the overall evaluation index, we need to compute the weight of the four factors. In the following, analytical hierarchy process, abbreviated to AHP, will be applied to judge importance of the four factors."
