## 5. Reusable writing action

Read the class imbalance as a statement about the instrument, then recycle the minority-free data as a
different measurement. Unit 26 takes one number - "Since only 0.3% sightings are positive" - and converts it
into an assumption about the reporter rather than about the hornet: "it is reasonable to assume the public do
not possess knowledge to differentiate AGH from other similar bees; their frequent negative reports of AGH
sightings more directly reflect their anxiety of an AGH invasion rather than the actual presence of any AGH."
The next paragraph cashes the assumption in - "in the regions where AGH population is insignificant, the number
of reports correlates strongly with the amount of people concerned about the AGH invasion issue" - and from
there the thousands of unusable negatives become a population census, which is the only route to the Gaussian
`(2)`, because as unit 23 says, "human population data is also not provided" and "we design a novel model to
estimate human population distribution P based on accessible statistics."

The move is reusable because it turns a data defect into a measured quantity with a stated validity condition:
the negative class is admissible as a population proxy only where the positive class is absent, and the paper
states that condition before it uses the class. The order is what makes it copyable - defect, inference about
the reporter, condition of validity, new use, in four sentences, with the 0.3% left as the single number the
whole chain hangs on. What the paper does not do is test the condition: no cell's fitted population is ever
checked against a known figure, and the fit is validated only by naming Vancouver, Seattle, Portland, Spokane
and Kennewick (unit 31). The reusable half is the inference; the missing half is the check that would keep it
honest.
