## 5. Reusable writing action

Give every measure the same normalised shape - a helpfulness-weighted sum divided by its own total -
so three different quantities become comparable numbers in (0,1), and then reuse the first measure
inside the later model instead of re-deriving it. The move is announced once and cashed twice:

> "3. Assign a weight of 𝐻𝑁𝑗+1 to time j, and we obtain that the weighted ratio for rating i can be
> formulated as"

and, two sections later, inside the reputation metric:

> "𝑤𝑟𝑟𝑖 is the weighted rate radio calculated in problem 2(a), which contains the information of
> helpful votes."

The same shape is what makes the standard deviation's collapse visible: once every measure is
Σ(weight) × something / Σ(weight), a numerator that carries ss_i where it needs ss_j shows up as an
invariant inside the sum rather than as a subtle index slip.
