## 5. Reusable writing action

**Falsify the predecessor by naming the two regions where it fails, then convert each failure into one added compartment and mint the new model in the same paragraph.**

The move, in block 109, in the paper's own words:

> `However, this model failed to satisfy both the sharp decrease after peaking in February and mild decrease in the second half of the year. After all, while many people might gradually abandon the game, others might maintain their fondness for Wordle. Modelling all players having the same player leaving rate 𝛽might not work well. Therefore, we improved the model by dividing all players into loyal players who has lower tendency to get tired with Wordle and keep sending Wordle tweets, and general players who follow the trend and leave with the trend, Which is named T2PL(Target-2-players-Lost) model.`

Why it works, and the order it works in. (1) `failed to satisfy both A and B` localises the failure in time rather than in principle - February's peak and the second half's slope - so the reader can check it against the figure they have just seen. (2) `After all, ... others might maintain their fondness` supplies the mechanism the old model lacks, so the new compartment is motivated before it is named. (3) The single sentence `Modelling all players having the same player leaving rate 𝛽might not work well` names the exact assumption being dropped. (4) Only then does the paper split one pool into two (`loyal`, `general`), and the split is a direct image of the sentence before it. (5) The name `T2PL(Target-2-players-Lost)` arrives *after* the reader already knows what it is - the acronym is a label for a thing already described, not a promise.

The same paragraph then repeats the move at smaller scale: no sooner is T2PL named than a second deficiency is patched in (`In case there were players who wants to try Wordle again after leaving for a while, an additional variable 𝜃is used to describe the lost-return rate`), so the model that gets printed as equation (6) is two repairs past the one that was first proposed.

What is missing from the move, and worth copying differently: the failure is asserted by eyeballing (`failed to satisfy both ... and ...`) with no residual, no statistic, and no number, and the repaired model is then scored by the same eyeball (`The model is proved to excellently capture the tendency of reported Wordle result in 2022` in 116). A chapter that uses this structure should attach one number to each side - the misfit before and the fit after - because the contrast is already structured to receive them.
