## 5. Reusable writing action

**Define every parameter as the flow it moves, in the same four-part slot, until the ODE system is just the list of those sentences rearranged.** The chapter runs one template four times without variation: `Parameter` + symbol + parenthetical gloss + `representing the rate at which ...` + the flow sentence `the number of <who> ... in unit time at moment t is <expression>`. Verbatim, blocks 97-99:

> "Parameter 𝛾(Boredom Coeﬀicient) representing the rate at which people who have been attracted to do TP get bored with TP, the number of people who have been TPers becoming the Quitted in unit time at moment t is 𝛾× 𝐶(𝑡)."

and blocks 94-96 for β, ending "the number of people attracted and participating in unit time at moment t is 𝛽×𝑃(𝑡)×(𝐶(𝑡)+𝐿(𝑡))/𝑁"; blocks 100-102 for λ, "the number of TPers at the moment of t who are converted into loyal TPers per unit time is 𝜆× 𝐶(𝑡)"; blocks 103-105 for φ, "the number of loyal TPers who will no longer do TP in unit time at moment t is 𝜙× 𝐿(𝑡)".

Why the action works, and what to copy exactly: each slot ends on a rate with units of people per unit time, so the parameter is never left as an abstract coefficient - it is already one term of one equation. The four ODEs then print as a transcription, `d𝑄(t)/d𝑡 = 𝛾× 𝐶(𝑡)` and `d𝐿(t)/d𝑡 = 𝜆× 𝐶(𝑡) − 𝜙× 𝐿(𝑡)`, with each right-hand side the sum or difference of flow sentences the reader has already met one paragraph earlier. The transferable move is the *order*: gloss first, rate second, equation third, and never the reverse - because a symbol introduced after its equation forces the reader to hold an undefined quantity across the display.

Two boundaries of the action, both visible on the page: the template states magnitudes and never the conservation constraint, so nothing in the four slots tells the reader that `dP/dt` losing exactly what `dC/dt` gains is what makes the model a closed system - that has to be added. And the template is not applied to the initial condition: the four starting values enter only as `the initial parameters obtained by the genetic algorithm are shown in the following table`, so the one quantity the fit actually needs is the one the slot discipline skips.
