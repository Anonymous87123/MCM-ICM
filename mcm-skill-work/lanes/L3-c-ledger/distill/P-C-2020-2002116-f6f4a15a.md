## 5. Reusable writing action

Define the vector encoding and then *earn* the word "equivalent" by stating exactly the two
properties the downstream model consumes - the argmax recovers the rating and the components sum to
one - so a conversion that could have been arbitrary is licensed in two lines. The chapter's own move:

> "The mapping VEC is one-to-one, hence we claim the converted form is equivalent to the star-rating.
> Moreover, by our definition, we can find: i) s = argmaxi{veci s}, ii) Σ_{i=1}^{5} veci s = 1."

Note what this action costs when the sign slips: the same paragraph's gloss ("the 4-star rate has the
highest probability to be rated 4 stars") is what exposes the printed `+` in (1), so the invariant is
doing real verification work rather than decoration - state the invariant, then check the display
against it.
