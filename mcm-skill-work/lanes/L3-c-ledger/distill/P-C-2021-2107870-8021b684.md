## 5. Reusable writing action

Declare the priority order before the parameter sweep, then cite the order when the sweep is read. Units
27-30 state three objectives in order - "Maximizing accuracy as measured by TP + TN", "Minimizing FP,
because these assignments as positive or negative would be fed into the CNN as their "true" labels", and
"Minimizing variance in assigned clusters for the images estimated (by our eyeball measure) to be positive" -
and only then does unit 31 print the nineteen-row k sweep. The choice sentence is the payoff: "From the
table, we can see that k = 17 yields the optimal values according to our priorities, so we choose such a k."
The move is reusable because it makes a lexicographic choice auditable: with the order written down, a
reader can check that k = 17 is the only zero-FP row whose variance is the smallest of the tied set, and can
see that k = 2 (accuracy 0.6429, variance 0.5345) was excluded by its two false positives alone. The paper
then forfeits the gain in the next paragraph, where the probabilities computed from the same table use a
denominator of 12 instead of 5 - the order was declared, the arithmetic after it was not kept.
