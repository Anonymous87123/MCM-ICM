## 5. Reusable writing action

Fix the number of equations in a simultaneous system by counting the interactions among the elements
you model, and say so in the same sentence, so the system's size is derived rather than asserted.

> "We introduce three (3) equations into the model, since there are interactions between each two of the three elements, economic development, energy consumption, and environmental pollution."
