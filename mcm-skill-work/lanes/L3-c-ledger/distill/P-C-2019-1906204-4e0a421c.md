## 5. Reusable writing action

Number the modelling assumptions as parenthetical Factors in the same sentence that announces them,
then refer to them by number in every later formula, so a similarity built from three unrelated signals
needs no re-argument where it is used.

> "In our model, we carefully take three main factors into account. (1) Trend over time (2) Geographical factor (3) Drug identification size"

The cost is visible in the same chapter: those parenthetical numerals are the only numbers in
parentheses on the page, so they read as equation numbers, and a text-layer scan for "(1)", "(2)",
"(3)" finds eight hits that are labels, not equations.
