## 5. Reusable writing action

Argue the model choice by disqualifying the alternatives on exactly the property the chosen model supplies, and
let the rejection vocabulary become the requirement sentence. Unit 4 does ARIMA: "ARIMA is the most commonly
used time series forecasting model. However, ARIMA requires the timing data to be stationary, or stable after
differencing. And ARIMA can only capture linear relationships by nature, but not nonlinear relationships."
Unit 5 does LSTM: "relies on the quantity of data and are prone to overfitting ... LSTM requires high demand on
computational resources and may take longer training time ... LSTM lacks interpretability and is difficult to
convince the market." Unit 6 then collects the survivors into one sentence: "Therefore, a more interpretable and
efficient time series prediction model is required for this task." Unit 3 has already introduced Prophet against
those two words - "a simple, easy-to-explain periodic structure that is well compatible with and explains event
nodes such as holidays".

What makes the move reusable is that each rejection names a mechanism, not a preference: ARIMA needs
stationarity and only sees linear structure, LSTM needs volume and is opaque. A reader can therefore check the
claim, and the chosen model's paragraph is answerable to a list the reader has just been handed. The chapter
even states its own caveat inside the choice paragraph - "the original Prophet is prone to over-fitting problems
at some specific time points" - which is what makes the later X-Prophet section look earned rather than ad hoc.

The failure is worth copying down too. The requirement sentence promises interpretability and efficiency, and
the chapter never measures either: no comparison is run, no baseline is fitted, and the improvement that is
supposed to answer the over-fitting caveat is filed in the next chapter, so this one ends on the heading
`4.1.3 Improvement Prophet - X-Prophet` and no content. A selection argument that names its criteria should
either test them or say which section will.
