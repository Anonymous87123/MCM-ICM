## 5. Reusable writing action

**Action: when the model needs a quantity the data cannot supply, print the refusal and the substitution as one two-sentence move, then promote the substituted symbol with an equality - `λ(t) = Ĝ(t)` - so every later equation can use the unobservable name without re-arguing it.**

The chapter's non-homogeneous Poisson process needs an intensity `λ(t)`. Nothing in the dataset measures arrivals per unit time, so the chapter says so in three steps inside two blocks:

> where the meaning of λ(t) is the mean value mX(t) = E[X(t)] of the number of reports on the day t. However, the mean value function cannot be derived from the available statistics. Therefore, we retreat and use the trend prediction result Ĝ(t) from the previous Gaussian regression to approximate the mean function of the number of reports mX(t) instead, such that λ(t) = Ĝ(t).

Why it works: the three moves are separable and each one is one sentence - the definition, the admission (`cannot be derived from the available statistics`), and the substitution, which uses the frame `Therefore, we retreat and use ... instead, such that` and ends by writing the equality the rest of the paper will live on. After that sentence, equations (4), (5) and (6) can all be written in terms of `λ(t)` with no footnote, and the reader knows exactly which earlier object it is. Compare the alternative this paper avoids: presenting the Poisson model first and then quietly using the fitted trend as its intensity, which reads as an error rather than a modelling choice. The admission is the value - `we retreat` is the honest word, and it costs one clause.

**The caveat, and the reason to copy the move rather than the paper:** the substitution is made unconditionally and then immediately broken. Two blocks later the correction multiplies it by a function of the observation, `λ̇k(t) = λ(t) · f(k)` with `f(k) = l · k + m` and `k` the number of reports, so the intensity now depends on the count it is supposed to generate - the same variable appears on both sides of the model. The writing move survives; the equality it promoted does not. Copy the two-sentence refusal-and-substitution, then check that whatever you substitute into does not later turn round and depend on the output.
