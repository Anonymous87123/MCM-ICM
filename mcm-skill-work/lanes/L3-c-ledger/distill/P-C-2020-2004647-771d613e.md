## 5. Reusable writing action

Build the composite measure by substitution, then repair its degenerate case with an indicator and
re-derive the same formula instead of redefining it. The chapter announces the substitution once -
"By substituting Equation (6), (7), (8), (9) and (10) into Equation (5), we obtain:" - then discovers
that the composite collapses to zero for neutral reviews, and rather than patching the definition it
adds ε_j and runs the substitution again:

> "By adding this correction term to our measure (Equation (11)), we have:"

The re-derivation keeps every symbol of (11) in place, so a reader can diff (13) against (11) and see
exactly one term appear. That is the reusable move: when a composite measure degenerates, extend the
composition and repeat the same algebra, so the repair costs one added factor rather than a rewrite.
