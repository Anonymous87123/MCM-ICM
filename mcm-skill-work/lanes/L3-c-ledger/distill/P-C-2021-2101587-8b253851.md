## 5. Reusable writing action

Change the label set when the labels cannot be learned, then project the answer back onto the asked
question. Unit 50 states the scarcity and the substitution in one sentence: "Since there are too few
pictures of the Asian giant hornet in the given ﬁles, it is more reasonable to train the algorithm to
determine the type of insect illustrated in a photo instead of judging whether a photo depicts the Asian
giant hornet." The chapter then trains on thirteen species, and at unit 64 converts back: "If we only
consider whether the insect in a picture is the Asian giant hornet, the model's accuracy increases to a
much more acceptable value, as is shown below in Table 6". Three beats, no apology: the data cannot
support the asked label, so the model is aimed at a coarser label that the data can support, and the
coarse prediction is folded back into the asked question at the reporting step.
