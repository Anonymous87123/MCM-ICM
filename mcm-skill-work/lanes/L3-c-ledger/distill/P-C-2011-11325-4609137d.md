## 5. Reusable writing action

State the model cannot be calibrated, then say what it can still do - the caveat is spent on a use, not
on an apology:

> "The limited amounts of data available on sales and demand of alternative fuel vehicles, as well as the
> unpredictability of future costs of oil and electricity make reliable calibration of the model
> extremely challenging. The model can therefore be best used for running various scenarios and comparing
> outcomes."

The move converts a missing calibration into a declared experimental design: every later paragraph then
scales one input (fuel price, infrastructure, battery technology) and reads the model's response, so the
absent fit never has to be argued again.
