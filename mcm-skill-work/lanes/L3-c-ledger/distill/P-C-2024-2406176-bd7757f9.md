## 5. Reusable writing action

**Build the null model by collapsing one parameter of your own model.** The chapter needs a control that says "this would happen without momentum", and instead of specifying a second model it names a single parameter value that turns the momentum model off - so the rival is provably nested inside the model under test, and the test becomes a comparison of two settings of one equation rather than two competing stories.

> Setting it to a constant 1 makes ˆ𝑝𝑠(𝑆𝑚) = 𝑝0, effectively disregarding in-match performance. Consequently, momentum, which is influenced by the flow of points within the match, ceases to be a factor as it no longer affects the win probability of subsequent points.

That sentence is written where the weight is introduced, as a remark on its range, and it does three jobs at once: it proves the extreme value is the no-momentum case, it gives a reason why the weight has to be strictly below 1, and it plants the null that unit 7 later runs as an actual test:

> To assess the role of momentum in tennis, we apply the Constant Win Probability (CWP) model by setting 𝜂(𝑆𝑚) = 1 in the Real-Time Win Probability (RTWP) model, thereby disregarding all in-match factors including momentum. We then consider the outcome of the CWP model as our observed value.

The second step is the part most papers miss: the collapsed model is not just described, it is made the *reference* against which residuals are computed, so "is momentum real" becomes "are the residuals of the no-momentum model non-normal" - a question with a published test attached.

Copy the shape when a task requires showing that a mechanism matters: introduce the mechanism with a mixing weight bounded in (0, 1), state in one sentence what the endpoint value means physically (`setting it to a constant 1 ... ceases to be a factor`), and later instantiate that endpoint as the rival model (`by setting <parameter> = 1 in the <full> model, thereby disregarding ...`), using its output as the observed value in a residual test. Two properties follow free of charge: the nesting is exact, so no one can object that the comparison is unfair, and the test's null hypothesis is the mechanism's absence, which is what a reader will actually doubt.

What the chapter does not do is finish the move. The residuals test itself - Figure 7, Algorithm 2, Table 3 with `D = 0.49958267649880644` for Carlos - is executed in `results` blocks it does not own, and no sentence in the chapter returns to the CWP construction to say what the rejection means for the weight `𝜂(𝑆𝑚)` that justified the whole model.
