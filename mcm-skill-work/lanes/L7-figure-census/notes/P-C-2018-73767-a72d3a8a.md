# P-C-2018-73767-a72d3a8a — distilled rules

- [caption.position] All 19 figures and 9 of the 10 recorded grids are captioned BELOW their artwork; the single exception is Table 16, captioned above. The side is a house habit here, not a choice made per exhibit kind - this paper captions tables the way it captions figures.
- [caption.numbering] Every recorded caption is the colon form 'Figure N:' or 'Table N:' with the colon attached to the number and no period after it; the caption body is then a lower-case noun phrase that does not repeat the word Figure or Table.
- [caption.words] Captions stay at 2 to 8 words except the three appendix variable-change grids (12 words each), which grow only because they must name both the quantity and its two measures.
- [caption.voice] Twenty-nine of twenty-nine captions are verbless noun phrases: not one states a conclusion, and not one carries a unit or a condition - the conditions live in the table note (Table 5) or in the cells (Tables 14, 16, 18), never in the caption.
- [table.rule_style] Eight of the ten grids are closed boxes with full-height verticals at every column boundary; Table 2 is the one plain frame with side rules and no internal vertical; Table 8 is the only booktabs grid - horizontals only.
- [table.zebra] Exactly one grid shades alternate rows (Table 9, the two-scenario prediction block), and it is also the widest. Every other grid tracks rows with the stub column or with rules.
- [table.merged_cells] Six grids merge cells and every merge is a header merge: a spanner (TFE / CRE, Scenario 1 / Scenario 2, Data from 1960 to 2009, The predicting error) or a stub that spans the three rows of an index block (Table 6).
- [table.header_rows] Five grids carry two header rows and all five are two-block constructions - a group spanner over column names (Tables 5, 9, 10, 14, 16); the other five carry one.
- [table.decimal_aligned] Only Table 9 aligns decimals. Every other grid centres its numbers, which is why Table 14's GDP rate of change prints as a bare 6.736912662 among percentages.
- [table.units_in_header] No grid puts a unit in its header. The three appendix grids put the unit inside each cell ('1116104.735(Billion Btu)'), and no other grid carries a unit at all.
- [table.width] Eight of the ten grids run 0.83 to 1.01 of the 424 pt measure and two overhang it slightly; there is no half-width insert and no column-width grid in the paper, however few columns a claim needs.
- [table.diagonal_header] No grid has a diagonal header cell, and no grid continues across a page break - the one split candidate is Table 3's plate, which is a key rather than a table.
- [table.note_sentence] Exactly one grid carries a printed note under its caption, and it is the significance key for Table 5's stars; the appendix grids instead bury their unit and their date range inside the cells and the spanner.
- [geometry.family] Section 4 and the appendix plates are a fixed quartet per state - a 2x2 line row, a grouped bar, a pie and a variable-change grid - so family is chosen by the state chapter template, not per finding: 6 line exhibits, 4 bar, 4 pies, 2 scatter, 1 map, 2 schematics.
- [geometry.panel_count] The 2x2 panel row is the paper's standard exhibit (5 of 19 figures) and always holds one time series per state or per quantity; every other figure is single-panel, and the only two-up row is the Figure 9 / Figure 10 pair.
- [geometry.legend] Legends are placed where the reader needs them and omitted where a table two pages back decodes the letters: 8 inside the frame, 7 absent, 4 outside-right on the pies - and the two bar charts that print no key are the same chart as the one bar chart that prints one.
- [geometry.three_d] Nothing in the paper is three-dimensional; every one of the 35 regions was cropped and read, and no axis, projection or depth cue appears in any of them.
- [geometry.colorbar] No exhibit carries a colourbar. Colour is used only to separate groups and sectors, never to encode a value, so no ramp is decoded anywhere in this paper.
- [mention.first_page] Only 7 of the 29 exhibits are ever cited by number, and they are the mid-paper results (the elasticity pair, the resource map, the two scenarios, the regression grid, the index grid, the prediction grid). Every appendix exhibit and every chart in section 4 stands uncited.
- [mention.lead_pattern] The pointer is either a flat results sentence ('The results are shown in table 9.', 'The calculation results are shown in figure 4.') or 'as is shown in figure N'; the number is spelled 'Figure' capitalised except three times, where the paper writes 'figure 4', 'figure 9' and 'table 7' in lower case.
- [mention.lead_pages] Citation lands on the exhibit's own page in six of the seven cited cases; the exception is Figure 5, pointed at from the foot of page 12 and printed at the top of page 13.
- [mention.read_sentences] A cited exhibit gets a reading sentence and an uncited one gets none - the appendix plates and the four California charts are presented as evidence with no sentence telling the reader what to see.
- [place.section_kind] Twelve of the 29 exhibits sit in the appendix, 9 in 'other' sections, 4 in model sections, 2 in the reliability test, 1 in results and 1 in the symbols list - the body carries fewer exhibits than the plates do.
- [place.solo_page] No exhibit owns a page: even the two figure-only pages (9 and 28) hold two exhibits each, and every grid shares its page with prose.
- [place.page_zone] Exhibits sit where the section needs them - 11 in the top third of a page, 10 in the middle, 8 at the bottom - so page zone does not separate figures from tables here.
- [place.area_frac] Area is an artefact of how the artwork was cut: the pies and bars tile into 0.10-0.16 boxes, the 2x2 rows into 0.23, and the largest single number (0.555) is the prose strip on page 17 that is not an exhibit at all.

## Templates this paper hands you

**Which section takes which exhibit.** The body is organised as a funnel and the
exhibits follow it rather than decorate it: a symbols table (2.2), then a variable
map (4), then the state chapter - a 2x2 dual-axis line row and a grouped bar for the
raw series, a pie for the 2009 constitution, a key plate and a change grid as the
apparatus around them. Model sections take the apparatus, not the data: 5.1 gets a
two-panel line pair for one coefficient, 5.2 takes the map of what the coefficient is
about, 5.3.1 takes an index tree plus its formula grid, 5.4.2 a pseudocode flow.
Result and test sections take grids only (Tables 5, 9, 10). The appendix reprints
the whole state chapter per state instead of summarising it, which is why 12 of 29
exhibits live there.

**Three-dimensional condition: never fires.** No exhibit in 35 measured regions is
3-D, and no colourbar appears anywhere - colour separates groups and sectors only.
The one encoding that carries value instead of identity is height on a dual y axis,
and both axes are printed on every chart that has them.

**Table type vs data scale.** Two columns is a glossary and prints as a closed box
with a rule on every row (Table 1) or as a plain frame with no row rules at all
(Table 2); the difference is whether the reader must track a row across, which only
happens when the second column is a mnemonic. Three columns is a change grid with a
date spanner. Five to ten columns is a results block and always gets two header rows
plus a group spanner. Units never enter a header - they enter the cells (appendix
grids) or the note line (Table 5's star key), which is why only the one prediction
grid bothers to align decimals.

**Caption template.** `Figure N: <lower-case noun phrase naming the object>` and
`Table N: <lower-case noun phrase naming the object>`, 2-8 words, colon attached to
the number, no unit, no condition, no conclusion, and the same side for both kinds -
below. The one caption printed above its table (Table 16) is a typo in the template,
not a variant of it.

**Context-weld template.** One pointer sentence of the flat results type - "The
calculation results are shown in figure 4." or "The results are shown in table 9." -
followed, for the cited exhibits only, by an observation clause and then a mechanism
clause inside the same paragraph ("From the figure, we can intuitively know that ...
the dependency ... is on the rise ... but the intensity ... differs"). The uncited
exhibits get no weld at all: 22 of 29 are left to be read cold, and in the appendix
all of them are. Copy the weld, not the omission.
