## Templates this paper hands you

## What this paper is

2019 ICM F, team 1916375: a fuzzy-comprehensive evaluation of a proposed global digital currency
system - three return measures, two cost measures, an AHP weighting and a net-income criterion -
then country choices by size, a trilemma argument, and a dynamic model of the banking industry.
Twenty-three pages, fourteen of which carry art, twenty-two measured regions.

**All twenty-two are accounted**: 12 figure rows for 11 numbered figures (Figure 11 is a
two-panel composite and takes two rows), 4 grids (the three numbered tables plus the uncaptioned
notation roster on p6), and 6 refusals. The refusals are the summary sheet's blue banner on p2,
three nested detections around the framed prose callout that sits beside Figure 2 on p6, and two
blocks of body text the layout pass measured on p7 and p8.

- 1.2 "Restatement of the Problem" takes **Figure 1**, the roadmap mind map, at the top of p5,
  with nothing said about it.
- 2.1 "Assumptions" takes **Figure 2**, the game matrix, hung between the assumption it argues
  and the sentence that states its consequence.
- 2.2 "Terms, Definitions and Symbols" takes the **p6 notation roster** - uncaptioned, unnumbered,
  starting straight under the heading.
- 3.3.1 and 3.3.2 take **Figures 3 and 4**: one construction, printed twice, short run then long
  run, eight lines apart on p9.
- 3.4, 3.5 and 3.6 take **Tables 1, 2 and 3** - one grid per subsection, each opening its page.
- 4.1.1 and 4.1.2 take **Figures 6 and 7**: the large-country and small-country loanable-funds
  schematic, one question asked twice.
- 4.2 takes **Figure 5**, the trilemma triangle, immediately after the sentence that states its
  conclusion.
- 4.2.2 takes **Figures 8 and 9**: the floating-rate pair and the fixed-rate schematic, one page
  apart.
- 5.4 "Conclusion" takes **Figure 10**, the regulatory ring scheme.
- 6.1 takes **Figure 11**, the two-panel long-term banking impact.

The pattern is one exhibit per subsection, and the exhibit always lands *after* the paragraph
that has finished the argument and *before* the sentence that draws the conclusion from it. No
subsection carries two exhibits except where the pair is the point (3.3, 4.1).

## When it goes 3-D

**Never.** Twelve figure rows, zero perspective projection, zero surface, zero isometric
drawing, no colourbar anywhere. The paper's three variables - return, cost, weighting - are
handled by three separate two-variable schematics and three flat grids, never by a third axis.
The only depth cue in the whole file is the drop shadow under the pictogram cells of Figure 2,
which is decoration on a pasted image.

## Layer defects found in the measured layout

- **The pasted tables do not exist in the text layer.** Tables 1, 2 and 3 are embedded images, so
  their own words ('Factors', 'Membership', 'Great(v1)', 'Small(v2)', 'Impact') appear on no page
  of the text layer while their captions do. Tables 1 and 2 additionally re-print the same nine
  numbers below as three typeset matrices numbered (17), and it is those matrices - not the
  tables - that the text layer holds.
- **The printed footer is offset by one**: PDF page 4 prints `Page 3 of 22`.
- **The table of contents parses as headings**: p3 carries '3.1.1 Model of Currency Value………6'
  and '6.3 Long-term Impact on International Relationship………20' as numbered section entries, so
  the heading index lists sections on the page they are listed on, not the page they start.
- **The caption detector misses a colon with no space**: `Figure 3:interest rate analysis in short
  term`. A caption regex demanding ': ' reads p9 as carrying an uncaptioned figure.
- **Sentences run together without a space** at several figure boundaries: `figure 2This trust
  mechanism`, `will rise Fixed Exchange Rate`, `Cr1 , Cr2 and Cr3 .Through easy analysis`. The
  paragraph break disappears while the period survives, so a sentence splitter that keeps
  sentence case will see one 60-word sentence where the page printed two.
- **The p6 roster's columns drift in the text layer**: `S(w,σ ) w` and `RT Ri labor input` are
  two rows whose meaning column has run into the next row's symbol, so the roster reads as 17
  rows in text against 15 printed.
