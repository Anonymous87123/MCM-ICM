# P-B-2011-12114-03fd7440 — distilled rules

- a coverage picture on unitless -40..40 axes is scatter, while a plan view whose axes are titled in Miles is map-or-plan-view: the axis titles, not the marker type, decide between the two families [geometry.family]
- an outline construction (circles, hatched wedges, ray diagrams) is flowchart-or-schematic even when it carries no text at all, because it draws a mechanism rather than data [geometry.family] [geometry.data_labels]
- a caption printed inside a body line, or split over indented lines with a hyphen break, is quoted as printed and never reflowed: Figure 2's caption sits at the end of a prose line, Figure 10's breaks 'distribu- tion' across three lines [caption.verbatim] [caption.words]
- each panel of a multi-panel figure is measured as its own region and recorded as its own item sharing the figure's caption, so Figure 9 yields three items on p20 and Figure 13 three more on p22 [geometry.panel_count] [caption.verbatim]
- program listings in a ruled title bar are excluded as not-an-exhibit in the same class as algorithm listings, even where the listing is printed on a page that also carries figure panels [geometry.family]
