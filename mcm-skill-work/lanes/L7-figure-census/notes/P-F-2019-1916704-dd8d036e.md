## Templates this paper hands you

## What this paper is

2019 ICM F, team 1916704: a four-model argument about a central-bank-issued global digital
currency. Model 1 is a DSGE feasibility test; section 4 screens fourteen indicators by PCA;
Model 2 classifies money-laundering sectors by kNN on a vector-space embedding; Model 3 forecasts
the spillover with a spatial autoregressive model on 163 countries. Twenty-one pages, ten of which
carry art, thirteen measured regions.

**All thirteen are accounted**: 6 figure rows and 7 grids, and **nothing refused** - the first
paper in this stretch where every measured region is an exhibit. The eighth printed table,
**Table 2 "Parameter Calibration Table of Model 1" on p8, prints a full grid and owns no measured
region at all**; it is declared in the deficits list and in this note rather than given an
invented bbox.

## Which section takes which exhibit

- 1.2 "Our work" takes **Figure 1**, the roadmap, after the fourth numbered task and before the
  heading of section 2. Nothing introduces it.
- 3.2 "Variable Nomenclature" takes **Table 1** - but the grid prints ABOVE the heading, so the
  page opens with the glossary and the section it belongs to is announced underneath it.
- 3.4 "Implementation and Results" takes **Tables 2 and 3**. Table 2 is the calibration
  (unmeasured), Table 3 the steady-state answer. The reader meets them in the order
  calibration-then-result, which is what makes the missing box a loss rather than a nuisance.
- 3.5 "Future Discussion" takes **Figure 2**, the impulse responses; it is the only figure whose
  panels sit in the section whose conclusion they support rather than in the implementation one.
- 4 "Key Factors: PCA" takes **Tables 4 and 5** back to back at the top of one page, and no
  heading intervenes: the variance decomposition and the loading matrix are one exhibit split
  into two grids.
- 5.2.3 and 5.2.5 take **Table 6** (glossary) and then **Figures 3 and 4** (the scatter and the
  classified scatter), one per page.
- 6.2 takes **Table 7** (glossary); 6.4 takes **Table 8** (coefficients) and **Figure 5** (the
  ten-per-cent test); 6.5 takes **Figure 6** (the five-per-cent test).

The pattern is a **house style**: every model opens with a glossary grid (Table 1, Table 6,
Table 7) and closes with one results object. The three glossaries are the same construction,
cut down each time - 31 rows, then 13, then 7 - as the models get smaller.

## When it goes 3-D

**Never.** Six figures, no perspective projection, no surface, no isometric drawing, no colourbar
anywhere. The DSGE output is two flat panels, the kNN output is a scatter in a plane, the SAR
output is six flat time series. The paper's own third dimension - the fourteen PCA dimensions -
is collapsed to two by the projection and then never plotted as depth.
