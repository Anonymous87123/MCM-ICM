## Templates this paper hands you

## What this paper is

2019 ICM E, team 1916129: an ecological cost accounting model. A modified Cobb-Douglas
production function prices natural-resource consumption, five non-market valuations (carbon
dioxide, soil, water, dust, biodiversity) are stacked on top, pollution control and
degradation costs are added, a BP neural network supplies the long-term self-recovery term,
and the whole thing is run as a cost-benefit analysis of a house, a subway and a steel mill,
with Green GDP as the closing discussion.

A 23-page file, 17 of whose pages carry art, 25 measured regions. **All 25 are accounted**:
5 printed figures, 16 printed tables of which 15 are measured (Table 14 is the declared
deficit), 2 unnumbered halves of one appendix grid, and 3 refused vector regions. Every
figure is a single raster paste; every table is vector paths. Families: line 3, node-link 2,
table-grid 17.

## Which section takes which exhibit

- 1.3 "Symbol Description" takes **Table 1**, introduced by its heading alone.
- 2.3.1-2.3.4 take **Tables 2, 3, 4 and 5**, one constant table per valuation sub-model, all
  four behind the identical pointer sentence.
- 3.1/3.2/3.3 take **Tables 6, 7 and 8** - the control-cost grids, one per pollution type.
- 4.3 takes **Table 9**, the biodiversity service-value lookup.
- 5.2 takes **Figure 1**, the neural network diagram; 5.4 takes **Figures 2 and 3**, the two
  environmental-degradation curves; 6.2 takes **Figure 4**, the long-term cost components.
- 6.5.1 takes **Tables 10 and 11**, 6.5.2 **Tables 12 and 13**, 6.5.3 **Tables 14 and 15** -
  each case study is a cost grid followed by a benefit grid, one pointer for both.
- 7 takes **Table 16** (the sensitivity grid) and **Figure 5** (K against K/2).
- The appendix's data-sheet section takes the one unnumbered ecosystem-service grid, split
  over p22 and p23.

So the shape is: a table per priced quantity, and a figure only where a quantity varies over
time or over a network. Sections 2-4 are all tables, section 5 is all figures, section 6 is
tables again, section 7 gives one of each.

## When it goes 3-D

Never. All five figures are flat two-dimensional pastes and all seventeen grids are flat
two-column-or-more tables: no perspective, no surface mesh, no isometric projection, and no
colourbar in the file. Depth is not even faked with shading.

## What the figures do

Three line charts and two diagrams, and the split is by job:

| figure | job | how it is dressed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Figure 1 network | the model itself | eleven pollutant nodes into three media into one environment node, w_ij on every edge |

## Caption template

`Table N.` and `Figure N.` - a PERIOD on 21 of 21 and a colon on none, the inverse of the
usual convention - then a bare noun phrase of 6 to 13 words. No caption states a unit, names
a variable, gives a condition or draws a conclusion, and every one of them is in the passive
or nominal register (`voice` = none on all 21).

Every caption prints ABOVE its exhibit - all 5 figures, all 16 tables, no exceptions - which
is what lets the caption be typeset flush against the paragraph that introduces it and, in
Table 10's case, run onto the header line itself.
