## Templates this paper hands you

## What this paper is

2019 ICM F, team 1904381: a three-market macroeconomic model built to answer what happens when
a digital currency is introduced. Money demand is derived at the individual level from a
Cobb-Douglas utility over two currencies, the LM/IS/FE triple is assembled from it, two
exchange-rate institutions are run through a policy-assignment analysis, and R anova output is
offered as the sensitivity and robustness check. Twenty-eight pages, twenty-one of which carry
art, forty measured regions.

**All forty are accounted**: 2 native tables, 37 figure rows (of which 17 are panels of the
eleven numbered figures, 9 are uncaptioned body plates, 11 are uncaptioned appendix plates) and
1 refusal. The refusal is p22, a full-page raster of the team's letter to the judges.

## When it goes 3-D

**Only where a third variable has to be shown, and only in the appendix plus one body plate.**
Twelve of the thirty-seven figure rows are 3-D, and eleven of those twelve are the appendix;
the body's one 3-D plate is the Sweden domestic-product model on p18. Every 3-D object is an R
`persp`-style box with vertical stems standing on a base plane, ticked on all three axes, with
the depth variable named only in the rotated right-margin title (`M/P` on money-market plates,
`G/TAN` on domestic-product plates, a bare `I` on foreign-exchange plates). The paper never
fakes depth: the fifty-eight flat 2-D objects (schedules, matrices, grids, flowcharts) carry no
shading, no perspective and no colourbar anywhere.

## Caption template

`Figure N:` or `Table N:` — a COLON on all thirteen numbered captions, and no period anywhere.
Then a bare noun phrase: `The slope`, `LM Curve shifts`, `The analysis of the IS model`,
`Mechanism`, `linear regression anova result`, `Central bank's assets and liabilities`. Four to
nine words, no unit, no variable, no condition, no conclusion, nominal in voice. The caption
prints BELOW its exhibit on eleven of thirteen (Table 1 is the exception, above), and the same
caption word is reused without embarrassment: `Figure 6: Mechanism`, `Figure 7: Mechanism`,
`Figure 8: Mechanism` over a flowchart and two Word tables.

## Context connectives

- The dominant pointer is **"<Number> <verb>"** inside a full sentence: `Figure 1 explains the
  economic meaning of points that are not on the curve.`, `Figure 2 demonstrates that the slope
  of the LM curve is determined by ...`, `Figure 3 explains why LM curve will shift to the right`,
  `According to the Figure 11, Thailand should pursue a tight fiscal policy`. Six exhibits are
  named this way, and the number is always capital-F, always inside a sentence, never in a
  parenthetical.
- The second form is **"The mechanism works as follows."** — five words, deictic, naming
  nothing — used for Figures 6 and 7, the two objects of completely different genus.
- The third is **"the figure above"**, used twice: for the chevron timeline, which has no
  caption at all, and for the p18 3-D plate, whose sentence opens `From the figure above and
  Firgue9` — a misspelling of the very figure number two pages later.
- The read-backs are verdicts: `From the linear regression result above, we can conclude that
  each variables of the model is indispensable`, and for the results pair, a named policy
  recommendation per country.

## Layer defects found in the measured layout

- **The layout pass files every panel of Figures 1-3 against a list heading.** All six panels
  come back as `3 Balance: ...` because the numbered list items of 3.2.3 are parsed as headings;
  the real owners are 3.2.2 and 3.2.3.
- **The whole appendix is filed under `References`**, because `Appendix A Real Data Result`
  first prints on p26 while nine of the eleven plates sit on p24-p25.
- **Figure 9's panel letters begin at (c).** The p19 pair prints `(c) result for Sweden` and
  `(d) result for Israel`; there is no (a) and no (b) anywhere in the file. Panel letters
  restart at (a) for Figure 10 on the same page, so the letters are per-figure and the first
  two of Figure 9 were simply never printed.
- **The text layer hyphenates within sentences** (`international mone- tary system`), so every
  quoted lead and trail in this record is a literal slice of the layer, hyphen and all.
- **p22 has no text layer at all** — 29 characters, all running head — because the page is a
  scanned letter.
- **The pointer on p17 names the wrong genus**: `the following table can be summarized` stands
  over a flowchart, a grid and a second grid, and only two of the three are tables.
- **Table 1's foot rule falls outside its measured region**, so the closing rule under the last
  row (`γ, n, σ, φ`) is not inside the bbox even though the table is.
