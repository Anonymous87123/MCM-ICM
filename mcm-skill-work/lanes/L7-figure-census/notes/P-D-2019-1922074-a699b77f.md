- Read-backs are one sentence and one number each: 'The evacuation time is 453.3', 'route A can be totally evacuated in 172s, and route B is 110s'. The paper never interprets a chart without quoting a value. [mention.trail_sentence]

- Units ride on the individual value or in the header, never in a note row: '66s', '110s' on each cell of Tables 3 and 4, 'Shortest evacuation time(s)' in the header of Tables 5 and 6. No table in the paper has a note row. [table.units_in_header]

## Templates this paper hands you

## What this paper is

2019 ICM D, team 1922074: a Visitor Emergency Evacuation (VEE) model of the Louvre. Improved
ant colony optimisation plans visitor routes on a floor topology, improved Dijkstra routes
emergency personnel through the same graph, and a queueing time model turns both into
evacuation times; the last third is sensitivity and policy. 31 rendered pages at 595.3 x
841.9 pt, 29 printed, 54 measured regions, 16 numbered figures and 6 numbered tables plus two
unnumbered appendix table images - 24 exhibits, 32 refusals, **two printed exhibits with no
region at all** (Figure 7 and Table 3).

## Which section takes which exhibit

- 2.2 carries **Table 1**, the symbol table, and it runs over the page break.
- The section-3 intro carries **Figure 1** (the VEE concept diagram) - the paper's only
  exhibit that is not evidence but a table of contents drawn as a picture.
- 3.1 carries **Figure 2** (visitor count, two panels by day type); 3.2.3 carries
  **Figures 3 and 4** (the topology, then the optimal route on the five-floor plan).
- 3.4 carries the whole emergency-personnel block: **Table 2** (clearing times - the orange
  and yellow grid), **Figure 5** (Dijkstra flow chart), **Figures 6, 7 and 8** (the abstract
  route chains, then the same two routes on one map) and **Tables 3 and 4** (their time
  costs).
- 3.5.1 carries **Figure 9** (the language pie) and **Figure 10** (the disabled route);
  3.5.2 carries **Figure 11**, **Figure 12** and **Table 5** (the reopened-entrance
  scenario); 3.5.3 carries **Table 6**, **Figure 13** and **Figure 14** (the closed-entrance
  scenario and the B-stair case).
- 6 carries **Figures 15 and 16**, two sensitivity charts of three curves each.
- Appendix 1 carries the two full clearing-time grids as raster images.

So the pattern is: a claim gets a plan, a plan gets a table of times, and a scenario gets a
(plan, table) pair. The two sensitivity figures are the only exhibits in the paper that are
neither a floor plan nor a schematic.

## When it goes 3-D

Never. Sixteen figures and not one has a third dimension, an isometric projection, a
colourbar or a photograph: the drawings are Word/PowerPoint floor plans and box chains, and
the four charts are stock Excel/Matplotlib. The paper expresses depth by stacking two floor
frames inside one outer box (Figures 3, 8, 12, 13), which is a layout device, not depth.

## What the tables do that the figures cannot

Every table states a TIME; every plan states a PATH. The pairing is exact and the paper never
breaks it: Table 5 with Figure 12, Table 6 with Figure 13, Tables 3/4 with Figures 6-8. The
tables are small - two or three rows, two or three columns - because they are meant to be
read as one sentence ('closing this entrance costs 12.9 seconds'), and the only big grid in
the paper (Table 2, and its appendix twin) is a clearing-time matrix that no prose sentence
reads.

## Caption template

`Figure N.` with a **PERIOD on 16 of 16** (the tables use periods on 5 of 6; only Table 1
prints a colon), then a bare noun phrase of 3-10 words: "The number of visitors", "The new
route map", "Change the value of speed". No verb, no unit, no variable, no conclusion, no
condition - the caption never does more than name the object, and the reader has to go to the
prose for the finding. Table captions sit ABOVE their grid, figure captions BELOW their art,
and neither is separated from it by a rule.

## Context connectives

- **"showed in the Figure N"**: "the change of visitors' number is showed in the Figure 2",
  "is showed in the Figure 14". The paper's default verb is "showed", not "shown".
- **"The pointer as the last token"**: "The shortest route we get is the same and showed in
  the following figure: Figure 6." and "is showed in the Table 3." - the number arrives AFTER
  a colon or a full stop, as a bare label, rather than inside a clause.
- **"Figure N is ..."**: "Figure 12 is the route map of the 0th floor and the 1st floor after
  opening the entrance." - the pointer sentence states the content rather than pointing.
- **"the following figure/table"**: the deictic fallback for the six exhibits that are never
  named (Figures 1 and 3, Tables 1, 2, 5, 6): "the following figure is an example of the 1st
  and 2nd floor", "the result is showed in the following table".
- Read-backs are short and quantitative: "The evacuation time is 453.3", "So route A can be
  totally evacuated in 172s, and route B is 110s", "the prime time for emergency evacuation is
  15 minutes when the number of visitors is 28000".

## Layer defects found in the measured layout

- **Section paths are welded FORWARD**: the pass files a region under the deepest heading
  printed BELOW it. On p9 that puts Figure 4 - whose lead sentence is the last paragraph of
  3.2.3 - into 3.3, and on p5 it files the VEE diagram under 3.1. Six of the twenty-four
  records were re-assigned from the prose that introduces them.
- **The symbol table is measured twice**: p4r1 is the headed half, p5r1 the headless tail;
  no repeated header, identical column rules. Filed as one table plus one refused
  continuation.
- **Table 3 is split across the page break and measured NEITHER side**: the boxed header row
  ('Stairs | B3 | A3 | A1') prints at the foot of p14 and the boxed data row
  ('Time | 110s | 46s | 172s') at the head of p15, directly under the running head. The
  region pass sees two small boxes and claims neither.
- **Figure 7 has no region at all**: the route-B chain (Entrance -> T -> B -> 101) sits
  between Figure 6's strip and Figure 8's map on p14, and nothing is measured over it.
- **The caption detector reads a body sentence as a caption**: on p17 "Figure 12 is the route
  map of the 0th floor and the 1st floor after opening the entrance." is counted as a caption
  in addition to the real caption below the art, so Figure 12 has two entries in the raw
  caption list.
- **The folio band is a region on every page**: 31 identical 74-pt-tall bands (y=767-841),
  containing nothing but the centred page number. The footer says "Page N of 29" while the
  file has 31 pages, and the printed folios run two behind the render index.
- **The appendix's tables are rasters pasted into code pages**: the two clearing-time images
  sit above and beside Python source, are unnumbered, and are reproduced at a resolution
  where the cell borders alias.
