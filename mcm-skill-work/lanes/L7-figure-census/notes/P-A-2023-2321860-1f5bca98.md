# P-A-2023-2321860-1f5bca98 — distilled rules

- One figure can be measured as a whole block and its art as an inner layer: Figures 4, 5 and 12 each arrive as a vector frame (r1, r2) plus a raster of the drawing inside it, and the record books the frame and excludes the inner raster as a fragment, so the bbox stays the region the layout actually measured. The same happens three times inside one page for Figures 8 and 9, where one frame region plus three panel rasters each are one exhibit. [place.area_frac]
- A block that mixes prose and art is still one measured region and cannot be split: Figure 4's region also carries three paragraphs of model derivation, and Figure 5's carries equation (19) and the site description, so their area_frac (0.31 and 0.45) describes a page block rather than the drawing inside it. That has to go in the note, not be silently corrected. [place.area_frac]
- A pointer can be misprinted with the digits run together: Figure 4 is announced on page 10 as 'Figure13 shows the three main factors', which looks like a forward reference to Figure 13 on page 19 but describes the three factors of the soil-water cartoon. The spelling is recorded as printed and the row is bound to the exhibit the sentence describes, not to the number it types. [mention.style]
- The caption is not always the whole title: Figure 4's text-layer caption stops at 'The Factors Influencing the Soil' and the page continues 'Water Content', Figure 16's breaks as 'Decrease-' / 'ment', and Figure 18's wraps to 'Species and Derivation'. The verbatim slice runs to the last printed word, and where the wrap splits a word the record keeps the hyphenated fragment exactly as printed. [caption.verbatim]
- A caption and its own prose can disagree on a year: Figure 7's caption says 'Predicted Population Densities in 2020' while the sentence announcing it projects '2022'. Nothing in the layout resolves this, so both strings are recorded from their own layer and the row does not pick a winner. [caption.words]
- The pie chart is the family the enums do not have: Figures 10 and 11 are three pies each with per-slice percentage labels and a group total in kg/m2 or mmol/(m2 s), so they are recorded as 'other' with an other_note that says why, and data_labels is true while printed_numbers is true for a different reason. [geometry.family]
- Dual y axes are usually a duplicated scale and occasionally a real second variable: Figures 6, 7, 13, 14, 15, 17 and 18 print the same numbers twice on opposite spines with no second title, while Figure 16 titles its two axes 'Pinus' and 'Quercus' - the one justified dual axis in the paper. Reading a dual axis as two quantities is the default error this field exists to prevent. [geometry.printed_numbers]
- Panels of one exhibit are comparable exactly when the numerals are repeated for each panel: Figure 14 prints -0.10 to 0.10 on all three panels and can be compared, Figure 8 prints its y scale on panel a only, Figure 9 prints it on the left spine of a, the right spine of c and nowhere on b, and Figure 7's six siblings share nothing. shared_axes is read off the printed numerals, never assumed from the arrangement. [geometry.shared_axes]
- Printed panel letters are optional and inconsistent: Figure 8 letters its panels 'a', 'b' and 'c' under the frames, Figure 9 puts the letters only inside the x titles ('Mode a', 'Mode b', 'Mode c'), Figures 10 and 11 print no letters at all and rely on the caption's 'under Mode a, b and c', and Figure 14 uses the panel's legend as its title. [geometry.panel_layout]
- Series are separated by more than colour wherever the author expected a print: Figure 7 and Figure 17 assign each of eight species its own marker shape as well as its own hue, Figure 13 separates four legend entries into two lines and two filled bands, and Figure 12 separates its three modes by hue alone. The scheme is recorded per exhibit because the colour-only ones are exactly the ones that fail in grayscale. [geometry.legend]
- A table can be laid out as two side-by-side blocks rather than one grid: Table 3 prints eight species as two four-column blocks with the species names promoted into the header band and AVG/SD as the stub, separated by the paper's only vertical rule. Counting it as one nine-column table invents a column that does not exist. [table.rule_style]
- Grey fills in a table are not zebra striping: Table 2 shades alternate class groups (HDTP, LDTP) around the stub column that vertically merges two rows each, so 'zebra' is false while 'merged_cells' is true. Both fields are read from the crop, and the merge is semantic - it is the species class, not a layout accident. [table.merged_cells]
- Section paths follow the section that uses the exhibit, not the heading printed above the float: Figure 5 and Figure 6 both sit under 5.3.1 Data although Figure 6's page opens under 5.3, Figures 15 and 16 belong to 7.3 (pollution and habitat reduction) although they print on the page that opens section 8, and Figure 9's page carries the 6.1 heading only in its running head. [place.section_path]
- The paper's citation apparatus is thin: nine of eighteen figures have no numbered pointer at all and are introduced by a stub sentence ('Results are shown below:', 'The results are shown as follows:', 'The data are as follows:'), one is announced by a lowercase 'as figure 15', and two tables are introduced by the words 'are as follows' with no figure noun. Pointer count is therefore read per row and zero is a legitimate value. [mention.count]
- Two exhibits can be announced by one sentence and printed under two captions in one row: Figures 15 and 16 share the page, the 'two species' sentence points at the pair, and Figure 17/18 share a rule-blocked pair of captions under two side-by-side plots. Neither pair may be merged into one row - each has its own caption - but neither gets its own pointer. [mention.first_page]
- An equation is not an exhibit: the paper's displayed equations are detected as measured regions on pages 12 and 20 because they carry vector paths and a number at the margin. They are excluded as 'not-an-exhibit' rather than counted as exhibits with an empty caption, which keeps the census's exhibit count equal to the number of captions. [caption.verbatim]

### 关键读法

1. **外框与被含内层要分开记账**。Figure 4/5/12/15/16 各是"矢量外框 + 内层栅格图画"，Figure 8/9 各是"一框 + 三联栅格"；行记外框、内层按 `fragment-of-other` 排除，否则 43 个区域会算出 40 张图，见 [place.area_frac]。
2. **一块区域可以既是正文又是图**。Figure 4 与 Figure 5 的外框同时含推导段与公式（式 19），`area_frac` 0.31 / 0.45 描述的是页面块；这个事实写进 note，不改数，见 [place.area_frac]。
3. **指针可以连写误印**。p10 的 `Figure13 shows the three main factors` 看着像前指 p19 的 Figure 13，实际说的是土壤含水量那张卡通的三要素；记下原拼写，行绑到句子描述的展品而不是它打出的号，见 [mention.style]。
4. **图注不是全称**。Figure 4 文本层止于 `The Factors Influencing the Soil`，全句还有 `Water Content`；Figure 16 断成 `Decrease-`/`ment`；Figure 18 断成 `...between`/`Species and Derivation`。取到最后一个印刷词为止，断词按原样保留，见 [caption.verbatim]。
5. **饼图是枚举里没有的族**。Figure 10/11 各三张饼，逐扇区印百分比、另印组总量，记 `other` 并把理由写进 `other_note`；`data_labels` 与 `printed_numbers` 同时为真但理由不同，见 [geometry.family]。
6. **双轴默认是复制而非第二变量**。Figure 6/7/13/14/15/17/18 都在对侧印同一套数字且无第二轴题；只有 Figure 16 用 `Pinus`/`Quercus` 各标一轴才算真双轴。把双轴读成两个量是本文最容易犯的错，见 [geometry.printed_numbers]。
7. **三联可比与否，看刻度数字是否逐联重印**。Figure 14 三联都印 −0.10…0.10 故可比；Figure 8 只在 a 联印；Figure 9 左脊/右脊/不印各占一联；Figure 7 的六个同伴什么都不共享。`shared_axes` 从印刷数字读，不从版式推，见 [geometry.shared_axes]。
8. **panel 字母是可选件**。Figure 8 在框下印 `a`/`b`/`c`；Figure 9 只把字母塞进 x 题；Figure 10/11 一个字母都不印，靠图注 `under Mode a, b and c`；Figure 14 拿图例当联题，见 [geometry.panel_layout]。
9. **表格的灰底不是斑马纹**。Table 2 的浅灰按类群（HDTP、LDTP）交替，且首列单元格纵向合并两行承载类群名，故 `zebra=false`、`merged_cells=true`，合并是语义的，见 [table.merged_cells]。
10. **表格可以排成两块而非一张**。Table 3 把八个物种排成左右两块四列，物种名升进灰底表头带，存根是 AVG/SD，全套表格里唯一的竖线用在这儿；按九列表读会凭空多出一列，见 [table.rule_style]。

### 两条可复用结论

- **"一张图"和"一个测量区域"在本篇里几乎从不对齐**：43 个区域对应 21 个展品，剩下 22 个是层级切分（外框/内层栅格、上下两段表、左右两半饼）或根本不是展品（两条显示公式）。蒸馏记录必须先判"这块区域属于谁"，再判"这是什么图"；先判族再找区域的顺序会让三分之一的页面无法记账。
- **轴的可比性是印出来的，不是排出来的**。Figure 14 三联重印刻度所以可比，Figure 9 三联把刻度分放在左脊、右脊和不存在三处所以不可比，Figure 8 只在 a 联给数字。任何"三种模式下谁更高"的读法，在这篇里都要先回答"这个数字印在哪一联"，否则读到的是轴。
