- The exhibit text layer is a minefield of welded lines: one caption line can carry the caption, a formula fragment and the next sentence ('... n = 2400 49, Figure 2: Quadratic Function Image of Q 95 and c = - 49.'), and four captions here are hyphenated across lines ('Mutu- alism', 'Drought-Intolerant Species'), so the verbatim slice must stop at the last unbroken word or the row must be typed from the crop. [caption.verbatim]
- Caption side is not uniform across kinds in the same paper: the 17 figure captions print BELOW the art, and the two tables split - Table 1's caption prints ABOVE its grid while the notation list prints no caption at all. [caption.position]
- A figure caption can announce the wrong count: Figure 3 is titled 'Basic Information of 8 Species' while the Table 1 it pairs with lists ten, and the art shows the eight. The caption word count is what exposes it - the number in the title is a claim the row should not silently repeat. [caption.words]
- Data labels and tick numbers are different kinds of evidence: Figure 4 prints the coefficient inside every cell and Figure 2 prints the three marked points as coordinate pairs, while the eleven line charts print ticks only. Both booleans are set per row from the crop, never inherited. [geometry.data_labels]
- A missing legend is a finding, not a gap: Figure 5 draws one species on one stroke with no key and no name anywhere in the frame, so the caption is the only attribution, while every other line exhibit in the paper keys itself in the upper right - usually INSIDE the plotting area, sometimes under the trajectory of its own first series (Figure 6) and once over the data zone with five to seven rows of entries (Figure 7). Two exhibits key something that is not a line: Figure 8's third entry is a translucent fill with no outline, 'Sum of the change rates', and Figure 4's key is a continuous titled colour bar. [geometry.legend]
- A colour bar can live outside the frame with only its two end ticks labelled: Figure 4's ramp is drawn to the right of the matrix with '0' and '1' and the title 'Absolute value', so 'colorbar' is true while 'printed_numbers' is also true for a different reason - the cells. [geometry.colorbar]
- Multi-panel exhibits are ONE measured region six times out of six: Figures 7, 9, 10, 13, 14 and 15 each print two to SIX panels inside a single raster with one caption, so they get one row with panel_count 2, 3 or 6 and the panel map lives in the caption ('(left), (middle), (right)') or in printed panel labels ('k', '2k', '3k'). Figure 7 is the extreme case - a 2x3 block of six panels in one measured region, distinguished only by the number of legend entries. Splitting any of them into a row per panel would invent regions the layout pass never measured. [geometry.panel_count]
- Panel labels are printed as the parameter values, not as letters: Figures 13, 14 and 15 print 'k / 2k / 3k', '1 dT/dt / 2 dT/dt / 3 dT/dt' and 'D / 2D / 3D' above their frames, and Figure 9 prints nothing at all and relies on the caption's 'left/middle/right'. Any cross-panel reading - 'this case collapses faster', 'this one is safe' - is therefore an artifact of the axis, and shared_axes is recorded per row from the crop rather than assumed. [geometry.shared_axes]
- A caption that promises a chart can carry a diagram: Figure 16 is titled 'Weight of Each Factor' and read as 'the weight of each factor is shown', but it is an AHP hierarchy of labelled boxes with the weights as printed text and the ranks as outlined numerals. Family is decided from the crop, never from the caption's noun. [geometry.family]
- Photographs and botanical drawings are exhibits too: Figure 3 is a hand-placed montage of eight plant portraits with text blocks, one measured region, no axes and no legend, and its labels collide with the neighbouring drawings. Recording it as photo-or-raster-image keeps the census honest about what the paper actually printed. [geometry.family]
- Section paths must follow the reading sentence, not the heading that happens to sit above the float: Figure 1 floats to the top of p4 under no heading while its sentence closes 1.3 on p3, Table 1 floats to the top of p8 and belongs to 4.2 on p7, and Figure 16 is announced at the end of 7.1 (p20) and printed at the top of p21 under 7.2. [place.section_path]
- A section heading can be invisible to the heading detector when it wraps across two lines ('4.2 Relation Between Plant Species Model Based on Lotka-Volterra Model and Spearman Correlation Analysis'), so the deeper heading is missing from the context dump while its body exists; the record falls back to the printed line rather than to the parent section. [place.section_path]
- Pointers cluster: eight of the seventeen figures are announced by the same pattern 'The results are shown in Figure N', 'we can get the curve in Figure N', 'We obtain the curves in Figure N', and two of those announce a pair in one sentence ('Figure 7 and Figure 8'), so a single pointer legitimately supports two rows. ### 关键读法

1. **说明文字与正文共用一行**。p7 印 "…n = 2400 49, Figure 2: Quadratic Function Image of Q 95 and c = − 49."，说明被夹在公式碎片中间；整行取用会让 neighbours 全部污染，只能截带自己编号的子串，见 [caption.verbatim]。
2. **标题里的数目可以撒谎**。Figure 3 题为 "Basic Information of 8 Species"，而它与 Table 1 是同一批植物，表里列了 10 种；插画确实是 8 幅。词数统计是最硬的证据，见 [caption.words]。
3. **没有图例本身就是结论**。Figure 5 一条线、框内无键、图上无物种名，读者无法从图上知道画的是谁；其余直线图全部在图内右上自键。缺键要记 `legend=none` 并写进注，而不是按邻图继承，见 [geometry.legend]。
4. **一个测量区域可以是六联**。Figure 7 的 2×3 六联共用一个 region、一条说明，唯一的分联依据是图例条数与框下 `ΔICV` 标注；拆成六行会凭空造出布局没有量到的区域，见 [geometry.panel_count]。
5. **同一展品的各联不共轴**。Figure 7 顶排 0–100 / 0–10 / 0–200，Figure 10 是 180 vs 160，Figure 13/15 末联翻倍，Figure 9 拉高左联。跨联的"谁先崩溃"全是轴造成的，`shared_axes` 必须逐行从裁切读，见 [geometry.shared_axes]。
6. **图注的名词不等于族**。Figure 16 题为 "Weight of Each Factor"、正文说 "the weight of each factor is shown"，画的却是带权重文字的 AHP 层级框。族只从裁切判，见 [geometry.family]。
7. **章节号可以断行**。4.2 的标题被换成两行（`… Lotka-Volterra Model and` / `Spearman Correlation Analysis`）而图注检测不到，第 7 章标题更是被连字符切成 `Plant Com-` / `munity Based on AHP`；路径只能落到印出的那一行，不能落到父节，见 [place.section_path]。
8. **表格的账要分两笔记**。Table 1 说明在表上方、单位在表头括号里、数值右对齐但不小数点对齐；Notations 表 24×3 却完全没有说明与编号，下方的定义句不是指针，故 `first_page=null`、`count=0`，见 [mention.first_page]。

### 两条可复用结论

- **轴尺度的不一致是这类论文最容易被吞掉的谎**。Figure 15 第三联把 y 翻倍，才让"改变 D 无物种灭绝"看起来成立；Figure 9 把左联拉到 0–300，才让强竞争像"下降更快"。蒸馏记录逐联写轴上限，读者才复现得出原判。
- **说明文字的词数与断词位置是两项独立证据**。词数暴露"标题说的数目≠表里数目"（Figure 3 的 8 对 Table 1 的 10），断词位置（`Mutu-/alism`、`Drought-Intolerant `/`Species (right)`、`dT/dt` 竖排分数）决定该行能否用文本层切片——不能就必须 `typed-from-crop`，两者都不许猜。
