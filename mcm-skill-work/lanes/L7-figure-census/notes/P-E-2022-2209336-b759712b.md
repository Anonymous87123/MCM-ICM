# P-E-2022-2209336-b759712b — distilled rules

- A figure caption does not have to own its printed line: two panels can share one line ('Figure 1: ... Figure 2: ...', 'Figure 6: a Figure 7: b'), so each exhibit slice must be the substring that carries its own number and never the whole line, or two exhibits end up quoting the same verbatim text. [caption.verbatim]
- A caption can also be contentless - the panel pair here prints the single letters 'a' and 'b' - and then the only statement of what the panels show is the body sentence 'In Figure(a) and (b), the blue full line represents ...'. Caption word count is the check that catches this: two words mean 'look elsewhere for meaning'. [caption.words]
- Pointer spelling is a datum of its own. In this paper the two opening figures are cited only as a lower-case parenthetical glued to the preceding word with a ligature inside it - 'year( ﬁgure 1)' - and the two 2020 score figures as '(ﬁgure8 , ﬁgure9)' with no space before the numeral, so the search has to be case-folded and ligature-folded or both pointers are lost. [mention.style]
- A pointer can also name no number at all: 'plot the result in Figure as follow.' and 'The result is shown below.' leave first_page null and count zero, which is the honest record for a figure the prose refers to but never numbers. [mention.first_page]
- Lead lag runs up to two pages here: Figures 8, 9 and 10 are all cited on page 19 and printed on pages 20, 21 and 21, so 'lead_pages' is a measured page distance and not a constant. [mention.lead_pages]
- One cross-reference is broken in the text layer itself: page 15 prints 'In Figure ??,' where a number was lost. No exhibit can be attached to it, and it must not be read as a pointer to any of the eleven printed figures. [mention.count]
- Two figures can be screenshots of a data website rather than plots. The choropleths here carry their own title, subtitle, source and logo inside the raster, and their binned colour strip is legend and colour bar at once, so 'legend: below' and 'colorbar: true' describe the same object. The printed caption then adds nothing measurable: no unit, no source, no word that the exhibit is a map. [geometry.colorbar]
- Captions again print below the art on all eleven figures, at a measured distance from the region box, including the shared lines; polarity is a measurement, never an assumption. [caption.position]
- Printed numbers live on the shapes, not on the axes: the radar prints a value at every vertex of four polygons and the stacked bar a value in every segment, while the four line charts print none. The two booleans 'printed_numbers' and 'data_labels' can therefore be true together and false together, and here they are exactly that. [geometry.printed_numbers]
- A close vocabulary forces honesty: a five-spoke radar is not a line chart and not a heat map, so it is recorded as 'other' with the reason written into other_note instead of being widened into a neighbouring family. [geometry.family]
- A notation list can be a real table with no caption, no number and no citation, and its measured region can be only its header band: the two opening rules sit 28.3 pt apart, just above the floor that would extend the region to the closing rule, and the numeric-row absorption cannot extend it because not one body cell carries a number. Record the grid from the crop, record the band as the region, and say in the note that the two do not coincide. [place.area_frac]

### 七个判断

1. **说明文字不完全归某一张图所有**。p4 一行印 "Figure 1: Annual CO2 emissions(2020)1 Figure 2: Forest area(2020)2"，p17 一行印 "Figure 6: a Figure 7: b"。逐图必须截取带自己编号的子串，取整行会让两张图引用同一段 verbatim，见 [caption.verbatim]。
2. **说明文字可以只剩一个字母**。Figure 6/7 的说明就是 "a" 与 "b"，全篇唯一交代两图画的是什么的句子在正文："In Figure(a) and (b), the blue full line represents …"。词数统计正是捕捉这种情况的检查：两个词意味着"含义在别处"，见 [caption.words]。
3. **指针拼写本身就是数据**。前两图只有全小写、连字团块形指针 "year( ﬁgure 1)"；p19 是 "(ﬁgure8 , ﬁgure9)"，数字前无空格。不折叠大小写与连字（ﬁ），这些指针全部漏检，见 [mention.style]。
4. **指针可以不写编号**。p17 "plot the result in Figure as follow."、p22 "The result is shown below."、p21 "The result is shown below." 都指向图却不带号：这种图 `first_page` 记 null、`count` 记 0，见 [mention.first_page]。
5. **指针迟到最多两页**。Figure 8/9/10 全在 p19 被同一句话点出，实际印在 p20、p21、p21：`lead_pages` 是量出来的页距（1、2、2），不是常数，见 [mention.lead_pages]。
6. **文本层自带一条断链**。p15 印的是 "In Figure ??,"，编号在排版时就丢了。它不能挂到任何已印的图上，也不能当成第十二张图，见 [mention.count]。
7. **表格的账要分两笔记**。符号表是 9×2 的 booktabs 网格，但布局只量到表头带——两条开口横线相距 28.3 pt，仅比触发闭合线延伸的 25 pt 地板高 3.3 pt，而数值行吸收要求每行至少两个数字单元格，散文表一格都没有，于是无法延伸。网格行数以裁切为准、区域以测量为准，两者不重合必须写进 note，沿用 8362 修复时的同一判据，见 [place.area_frac]。

### 两条可复用结论

- **网站截图不是图，是证据**。Figure 1/2 是 Our World in Data 的网页导出：标题、副标题、来源、logo、分级色带全在栅格内。此时 `colorbar=true` 与 `legend=below` 描述的是同一条色带；说明文字里没有单位、没有来源、也没有说明这是地图，但 `caption.units=false` 只表示说明文字没给单位，不表示图无数值——两者必须分开记，见 [geometry.colorbar]。
- **族名不够用的地方就写 other_note**。五辐雷达既不是 `line`（轴是径向的），也不是 `heatmap-or-contour`（没有连续色域），记 `other` 并把理由写进 `other_note`，比塞进邻近族更可复核，见 [geometry.family]。
