# P-A-2016-44398-137ab9e6 — distilled rules

- Measure first, then read the pixels: sixty-four measured regions resolve into thirty-six figure rows, twelve tables and sixteen exclusions, and no region is counted twice [place.page_order].
- A panel measured as its own box is its own row: the four multi-panel exhibits of this paper (Figures 7, 11, 13, 24) are split into two, five, two and four measured boxes, so the figure count follows the boxes and the panel_count names the exhibit they belong to [geometry.panel_count].
- A key or colour bar that the region pass boxes on its own is an exclusion, not a figure: the legends of Figures 6, 10 and 12 and the colour bars of Figures 7, 11, 13 and 24 are fragments of the exhibits they serve [place.area_frac].
- A label or lettering strip drawn inside a diagram and boxed separately is a fragment of that diagram: the four 'Layers of thermal insulation materials / Inside the tub / Outside the tub / ft ft2' strips of pp12 belong to Figures 3 and 4 [place.area_frac].
- A display equation boxed as a vector block is not an exhibit: p34 r4 holds inequality (45) and carries no data, so it is excluded rather than counted as a table or figure [place.section_kind].
- A strip of body text boxed as a region is not an exhibit: p49 r4 holds two columns of prose from the summary sheet [place.section_kind].
- A graphic sliced twice on one page is counted once: p49's banner is measured as a full raster and as an overlapping slice, and the second is a duplicate of the first [place.page_order].
- A caption transcribed off the crop is typed-from-crop when the text layer cannot produce it: seven captions print their subscripts as glyph runs (phi_q and T2 come out of the text layer as 'q \uf046' and '2 T'), so those seven are read off the pixels [caption.verbatim].
- A caption that the text layer prints intact is text-layer and is quoted from it, including the missing space in 'Table7' and the space in 'Fig. 21', which are what the page prints [caption.numbering].
- A caption naming its variables in symbols counts them: the T2/phi_q and qm1/qm2 captions all carry two variables, the rest carry none [caption.variables].
- Every printed caption in this paper sits BELOW its figure and ABOVE its table, without exception, so position is read off the block order and not guessed [caption.position].
- A pointer that names 'the above figure', 'the following figure' or 'the above picture' is as-shown-in and its count is one per figure even when the same sentence serves a pair of panels [mention.lead_pattern].
- An exhibit that no sentence names is recorded with first_page None and count 0 rather than attributed the nearest 'as follows': eleven figure rows and eleven of the twelve counted tables are in that state [mention.count].
- An exhibit named after it prints takes a negative lead_pages: Figure 2 prints on p9 and is first named on p10, and Figure 10 prints on p24 and is first named on p25 [mention.lead_pages].
- A pointer that prints the wrong number is recorded against the number it prints: p16 says 'The curve of residual is shown in Fig.5' for the figure captioned Fig.6, so the pointer counts under Figure 5 and Figure 6 stays uncited [mention.lead_sentence].
- A colour-mapped field with no axis, no graduation and no numbers is heatmap-or-contour even when its colour bar is a separate region, and printed_numbers stays false [geometry.printed_numbers].
- A figure whose marks are joined by drawn strokes is line even when each stroke is short and straight between two categories, as Figure 17's five-tub comparison is [geometry.family].
- A dual-ordinate plot is line and takes legend inside when the key box is drawn within the plot area, which is Figures 14 to 22 [geometry.legend].
- A schematic, sketch or flow chart with no numeric axis is flowchart-or-schematic: Figures 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 18 and 23 all draw structure rather than data [geometry.family].
- A screenshot of a mesh viewport is photo-or-raster-image and not a schematic: Figure 5 is a screen export whose grid aliases at print size [geometry.family].
- A banner, pull-quote word or decorative glyph has no vocabulary class and takes other with an other_note naming what the lettering says [geometry.family].
- A table caption that omits its parameter is recorded as printed, not reconstructed: Table 15 reads 'The variation of parameters' and h3 survives only as a column head [caption.verbatim].
- A closed box with vertical strokes between columns is boxed, and a grid with a rule above the header only is booktabs - ten of the twelve counted tables take booktabs [table.rule_style].
- A merged band header is recorded with the header row count it needs: Tables 12, 17 and 18 all carry merged cells, Table 17 carries two header levels [table.header_rows].
- A table whose cells each carry their own percent sign is decimal_aligned when the signs form a colon, which Tables 14 and 15 do and Table 13 does not [table.decimal_aligned].
- A footnote under a grid is recorded as the table's note_sentence only when it is a printed sentence: Tables 11 and 18 both carry one, Table 8's sketches carry none [table.note_sentence].
