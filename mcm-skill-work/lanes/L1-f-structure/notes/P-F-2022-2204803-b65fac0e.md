## sensitivity-analysis — 6 Sensitive Analysis (pp. 22-22, 2 blocks)

`route → compute`

Two blocks. 257 states the design: perturb the task-1 model by changing the variance of important indicators. 258 reports that increasing the variance of military spending, capital formation, R&D, national income and higher-education enrolment one at a time and re-running EWM gives a weight change rate for each, and that 'only the weight of military spending decreases with the increase of variance' while the other four gain weight as cross-country dispersion grows. That ordering is backwards for one indicator relative to the method: under EWM a larger dispersion lowers entropy and therefore raises the weight, so a weight that falls as variance rises points either to a sign error in the perturbation or to a coding error, and the paper offers no explanation. No weight change rates are printed, no table or figure accompanies the section, and the sensitivity of the fuzzy evaluation (the alpha/beta judgements, the 0.7/0.3 memberships, the outcome scores) - which is where the paper's conclusions actually live - is not tested at all. 258 carries the '7 Strengths and Importance' heading in its tail.
