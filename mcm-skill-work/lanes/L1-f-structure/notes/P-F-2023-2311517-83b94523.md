## sensitivity-analysis — 8 Sensitivity Analysis (pp. 22-22, 4 blocks)

`route → check → read-figure → check`

4 blocks, 222 words. 270 states the motivation in the paper's own terms: the energy-consumption data come from different sources and “often fluctuate due to artificial error and unpredictable factors”. 271 is the experiment: +3%, +5% and +10% disturbances are added to EC and run through the prediction model. 272 is the Figure 16 caption. 273 is the reading, and it contains the claim the summary repeats: the results stay similar qualitatively, sacrifice is short-term and recovery comes later and faster, and “the greater the sacrifice in the short term, the sooner the recovery will be achieved”.
