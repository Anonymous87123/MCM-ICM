## 5. Reusable writing action

When you inherit a category scheme, delete one category on the record and give the double-counting
reason — the omission is the modelling decision, so it has to be argued, not silently skipped.

> "Supply services, regulation services, and cultural services often have a rel- atively direct short-term impact on humans. Support services are the back- bone of these three types of ecosystem services. Therefore, we recommend not evaluating support services to avoid double the value of ecosystem ser- vices."

Companion move from unit 27: attach a sensitivity index to every coefficient imported from
elsewhere, so the model states how wrong the coefficient may be before the answer changes —
"if CS > 1, it indicates that ESV is sensitive and ﬂexible to VC; if CS < 1, it indicates that ESV is
inelastic to VC."
