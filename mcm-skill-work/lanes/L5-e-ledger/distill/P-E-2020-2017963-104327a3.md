## 5. Reusable writing action

When a quantity is bounded by more than one resource, compute the bound for each and take the
minimum on the record — the binding constraint then falls out of the model instead of being assumed.

> "The maximum capacity of the environment … to bear the toxic gases released by the incineration of plastics is the minimum values of PIVCO2 and PIVtox." … "Therefore, we can get the maximum plastics mitigation volume: MPMV = V1 + V2 + PIV"
