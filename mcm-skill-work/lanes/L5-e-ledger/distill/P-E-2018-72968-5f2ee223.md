## 5. Reusable writing action

Build the composite so that its sign convention is visible in its structure: put the factors that
increase fragility in the denominator and the ones that decrease it in the numerator, then say that
aloud in one sentence.

> "Equation (9) shows the relation between the SPEC score and indicators of four core fields regarding fragility: security, politics, economics and climate… We only put the indicators considering extreme weather condition in the denominator, showing the negative correlation with the SPEC score. Bigger the value of extreme weather indicator, smaller the value of the SPEC score and more fragile of a state."

Companion move from units 22 and 25: split indicators by their time signature before averaging —
instantaneous ones take the latest value, slow ones take a decaying average with a stated constant —
"Indicators acts differently to the fragility of a country, some of them has abrupt and sudden
impact, while others has on-going influence."
