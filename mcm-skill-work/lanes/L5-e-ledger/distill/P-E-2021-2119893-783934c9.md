## 5. Reusable writing action

Give a large indicator set two layers — five named superiors over twenty-four inferiors — and let the
scoring formula be the linear combination of the superiors only.

> "As shown in the ﬁgure, a total of 24 inferior indicators are considered in our basic model. We use V to describe the result of linear combination."

Companion move from unit 11: say why the chosen weighting method is insufficient and pair it with a
second one in the same sentence — entropy is objective but cannot reflect attention, so AHP supplies
`αi` while EWM supplies `βi` — which turns a method choice into a defended joint estimator.
