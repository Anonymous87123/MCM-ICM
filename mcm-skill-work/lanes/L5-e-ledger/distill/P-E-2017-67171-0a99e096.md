## 5. Reusable writing action

Define one composite index as the product of a direction term and a magnitude term, then give the
reader a single tunable weight that shifts emphasis between them and a table saying which value the
weight should take for which kind of city.

> "SGI is calculated from Current Growth(CG) and Smart Growth(SG) with cos , CG SG SGI  1" … "where the first term of the numerator is the cosine function of the angle between the current growth vector and the ideal smart growth vector. This term represents whether the direction of current growth is close to smart growth" … "If growth rate is more important than growth direction(such as a developing country), …"

Companion move from unit 17: keep the decision rule and the measurement separate — the minimum of
the three axes answers yes/no, the composite answers how much — "in this model the lowest value of
EDI, SJI and EPI is utilized to determine whether the growth of a city is smart growth or not, SGI
is utilized to measure the level of smart growth."
