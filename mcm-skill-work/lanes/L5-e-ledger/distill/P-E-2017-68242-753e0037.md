## 5. Reusable writing action

When indicators are qualitative to begin with, borrow a published decomposition wholesale, name the
source case study, and let its own component names become your middle level — then the indicator
choices argue themselves.

> "In this section, we refer to the case study Using Smart Growth Strategies to Foster Economic Development: A Kelso, Washington, Case Study to analyze the success of economy. In the study, the economic development consists of three components, respectively are Supporting Businesses, Supporting Workers and Supporting Quality of Life.…" … "We construct a metric incorporating the extent to success of a city's smart growth, named Success of Smart Growth(SSG). It measures how successful the city’s smart growth is.…"

Companion move from unit 10: annotate every indicator with a direction arrow and state the reading
rule once — "The target ↑ (↓) indicates that the increase (decrease) of the value will help promote
the extent to success of economical prosperity in smart growth."
