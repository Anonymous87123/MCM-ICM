## 5. Reusable writing action

Make the model's own validation part of the model chapter, not the discussion: refit with the
target country removed, compare the two predictions, and let the small gap be the argument.

> "Because India was included in the 147 countries in our model, we re-ran the regression with India's values removed in order to perform and out-of-sample test." … "With India's values removed, our model suggests that 83.2% of India's population should have had access to water in 2012, while the version with India's values included suggests that 84.2%…" … "they are close enough to each other that we don't feel the model is significantly influenced by the presence of India's"

Companion move from unit 9: name the coefficient whose sign is wrong and attribute it to the
method rather than hiding it — "the coefficient for air pollution is a positive number, where one
would intuitively expect a negative number, but we consider this a byproduct of the multiple
regression, rath…"
