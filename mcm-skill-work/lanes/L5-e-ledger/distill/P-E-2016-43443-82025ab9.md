## 5. Reusable writing action

When the model needs a quantity that does not exist in the data, rewrite the model in terms of a
published index that does, and show the algebra in one line.

> "A variable in our model that is not readily available for the country… the calculation of the published WSI statistic requires an estimate of a total MAR for the country, so we can manipulate the formula for WSI to write the EWR in terms of WSI rather than MAR…"

The display that follows is not quotable — it prints as loose symbols (`WSI = withdrawals MAR withdrawals EWR = MAR −withdrawals 1 WSI −1 = population population`), so the identity is described above rather than quoted.

Companion move from unit 15: state the simplifying assumption and immediately convert it into a
bound, so the assumption becomes a floor rather than a weakness — "we assume that in the
relatively short time span of 15 years, the mean annual runoff for India will remain relatively
constant. We note that this assumption is not fully accurate… it will still be helpful because it
places a lower bound on the state of India's water scarcity."
