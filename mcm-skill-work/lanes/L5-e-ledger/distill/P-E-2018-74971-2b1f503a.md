## 5. Reusable writing action

Give a physical signal a piecewise response curve instead of a linear score, and justify each
segment by the size of the anomaly rather than by convenience — the shape itself becomes the
argument.

> "As the Fig.3 shows, the annual anomaly di σi, it is reasonable and common because of the micro disturbance, thus this function obeys exponential distribution and tends to climate change rapidly, setting the critical value φi=0.1. With the increasing value of annual anomaly, the speed will slow down… φi=0.5 , to logarithmic gradually, from the linear shape"

Companion move from unit 15: let geography set the parameter rather than averaging over it —
"Considering the landlocked countries without the effects of sea, we are required to make a
difference between landlocked and coastal states."
