## 5. Reusable writing action

Chain three sub-models by giving each one's output a name that the next one consumes, and say so at
the seam rather than at the end.

> "In the model established in 4.1, we considered the output of different crops or animals. This production model will be used in subsequent transportation and sales models."

Companion move from unit 7: put the policy coefficient inside the price law as an interpretable
constant instead of a free parameter — the influence coefficient of supply and demand on prices is
"usually related to national policies and stability", so the market model can absorb a policy scenario
without changing form.
