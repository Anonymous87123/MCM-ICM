mean the same thing at every stage — biomass `Pb` stays `Pb` from the logistic curve to the per-cell
stem count.

call the constraints soft, so the plan is chosen lexicographically rather than by weights — "the
priority factor reflects the difference in the importance of goals … they are soft constraints."
