## 5. Reusable writing action

Before building, spend a page dismissing the published indicators one by one with an explicit
(a)/(b)/(c) list of flaws, then let the survivor's flaw be the thing your construction fixes.

> "Due to its extreme simplicity and the easy access to the data, the Falkenmark Indicator becomes the most widespread metric of water scarcity[6]. However, its main limitations are obvious: (a) it fails to take enough environmental and social factors into account, thus it's not convincing even as an indicator of physical scarcity; (b) the identification of the thresholds is too arbitrary."

Companion move from unit 15: when a borrowed index conflates two things, split the concept
explicitly and keep only the half you can measure — "We divide water demand into two components,
the "object" component and the "subject" component… we consider the "subject" component in the
ESM, and remain the "object" component here. By this way, we completely incorporate water demand
in our model."
