## 5. Reusable writing action

When a model inherits a published framework, rename it and justify the substitution one letter at a
time — the borrowed structure keeps its authority, and the replaced term becomes your contribution.

> "In line with the characteristics of PSR, we first divide fragility into three aspect-s: Pressure, Sensitivity, Adaptation." … "As for Sensitivity, we determine indicators according to Xu's work[16]. Finally, we choose indicators for Adaptation referring to Fragile State Index(FSI)."

Companion move from unit 11: give the subjective weighting method a stated share and let the
objective method correct the rest, in one equation — "As we all know, AHP has some subjectivity due
to the score matrices. Hence, we calculate the weights using Entropy Weight Method(EWM)… Here we
suppose λ1 = 0.8 and λ2 = 0.2"
