## 5. Reusable writing action

Build the chapter as a chain of rejected sources — name each published model, state its validity
range, then the single case where it fails, and let that case force the next model.

> "In summary, N&M Model[6] considers the relationship between evacuation speed and ﬂow density[7], and gives diﬀerent models such as the staircase model and corridor model for diﬀerent road sections, but it is not suitable for the situation where the ﬂow density is relatively large, such as the narrow exit of the room. So we try to ﬁnd a better model to replace it."

Companion move from unit 12: fix the free parameters with literature body dimensions before
running anything, so the model has no dial left to tune — "the adult body thickness in the front
and rear direction is about 0.32 m, and the body width in both directions is 0.5 m. Considering
that the contact between the human body is the highest density, it can be considered that the
maximum density ρ1m and ρ2m in the front and side directions are 3 persons/m and 2 persons/m."
