## 5. Reusable writing action

Choose a growth model by elimination: reject linear for the observed explosive regime, reject
exponential for its blow-up, then adopt the S-shaped model and name the two regimes it spans
in one sentence.

> "We intuitively notice the development of high technology products as a simple positive feedback process. Such an explosive trend is unfeasible in fitting a linear model." … "However, an exponential increase in the positive feedback is explosive. Comparing with the analogy with species reproduction in ecology, an exponential model ignores the limited antecedents of the environmental resources we live in." … "This model tends to form an s-shaped curve in the transition from positive feedback to negative feedback."

Companion move from unit 4: when a formulation is correct but unsolvable at scale, say so with
the count that breaks it, then convert — "since there are more than |V|…2 variables in the above
linear programming model, it is difficult to solve this large-scale LP model using standard
algorithms like Simplex. Thus, the LP model is converted into an equivalent minimum cost
max-flow model".
