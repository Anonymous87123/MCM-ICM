## 5. Reusable writing action

Before estimating anything from a mixed data source, run one significance test whose whole
purpose is to justify pooling, and report it as a single inequality against a stated critical
value.

> "The given datasheet involves timing of two diﬀerent TSA oﬃcers, the signiﬁcance test here is to verify that individual variability does not contribute much to the service time." … "At 5% level, sw 1/n1+1/n2 = 1.375 ≤ t0.025(14) = 2.145, the null hypothesis is not rejected."

Companion move from unit 24: name the model by its Kendall string and let it do the
explaining — "ﬁrst of which is an (M/M/c) queueing process, and the second is an (M/Ek/c′)
queueing. Arrival rate of the second is the service rate of the ﬁrst."
