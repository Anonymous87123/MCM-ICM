## 5. Reusable writing action

When one chapter must carry several loosely related models, open each section with a
`route` unit that states the question and the three to five steps, then let the sections be
read as a checklist rather than as one argument.

> "Task one requires us to determine whether Tesla is on track to allow a full switch to all-electric in the US, and give the distribution of charging stations based on all-electric condition. Firstly, we searching the population density and car ownership per capita of different states of America. According to population density, we divided the United States into urban, suburban and rural areas."

Companion move from the 3.2 → 3.3 seam: announce the multi-objective frame and then label each
goal as its own paragraph — "we establish a multi-objective model with flexible constraints.
Goal of minimum cost: … Goal of minimum waiting time: … Constraint of distance: … Constraint
of convenience …".
