## 5. Reusable writing action

Define a statistic once, show it on one match, then in the next section re-describe the very
same statistic as a new named indicator and give it its own symbol — the chapter grows by
relabelling rather than by adding models.

> "In the first question, we introduced the weighted standard to reflect the dispersion of the team members in the whole game. It can measure whether a team is able to pass the ball at different positions on the court within the al…" … "This index is denoted as 𝑡𝑝B."

Companion move from unit 19: when importing a construct from another discipline, name the source
science in one clause and then immediately state the property that makes it usable here —
"In biology, the motif is used to explain the spatial structure of proteins. Here it can be used
in the analysis of football passing network to obtain the local configuration of the team."
