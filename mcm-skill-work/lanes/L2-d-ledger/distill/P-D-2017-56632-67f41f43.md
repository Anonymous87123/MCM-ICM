## 5. Reusable writing action

Write each modelling assumption as a bolded `Assumption n:` claim, then a `Remark.` that
says why it is defensible and when it will be dropped; put it inline where the derivation
needs it, not in a list at the front.

> "Assumption 2: For the ﬁrst version of our model, we assume that the system includes only the top row in Figure 1, and that there is only one service spot at each of S1 (ID check) and S2 (scan station). Later on we will remove this assumption. Remark." … "However, omitting the max operation allows for an explicit solution to the recurrence T (n) Q1 and simpliﬁes calculations immensely. Later on we will remove this assumption."

Companion move from unit 23: when a simplification distorts the quantity you report, say so
at the equation, not in the limitations section — "This remark is applicable to all boxed
equations (5), (6), and (7). Once Assumption 6 is removed in section 3, the times will be
strictly non-negative."
