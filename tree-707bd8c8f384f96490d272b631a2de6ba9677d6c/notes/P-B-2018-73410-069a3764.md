# P-B-2018-73410-069a3764 — distilled rules

- Numbering is one colon grammar twice over - every figure caption prints 'Figure N:' and every captioned grid prints 'Table N:' with the same colon, no period and no roman - but only five of the paper's twelve grids carry a number at all: the three symbol grids of sections 2.1.1, 3.1.2 and 4.2, the two 4.3 office grids and both Memo grids are bare [caption.numbering].
- Caption side follows exhibit kind without exception: all twelve figure captions print BELOW the artwork and all five captioned grids print BELOW their last rule, so this paper puts every caption at the foot - #145's convention, and the opposite of #146 and #147, whose tables carried theirs above [caption.position].
- Caption length is one line almost everywhere: the twelve figure captions run 9 to 19 words and the five grid captions 9 to 15, print in roman sentence case with no full stop and no bold, and no caption anywhere states a unit, a conclusion or a numeric condition [caption.words].
- Symbols reach the captions only on the figure side - three of them name a parameter ('When k=0.1', 'When k=10', 'Top 6 to 16 Language Groups') - while none of the five captioned grids names a variable, so a reader holding only the table captions cannot recover a single column heading's units or argument order [caption.variable_count].
- Three captions are wrong at the level of the noun and one is empty where a symbol belongs: Figure 9 is captioned 'Birth Rate' while its own axes read 'Death rate/%', Table 7 repeats Table 6's caption word for word, Table 1 promises 'Projections' for columns that mix prediction with realisation, and Figure 2 prints 'Rate ( )' with the parameter dropped out of the brackets [caption.verbatim].
- No caption prints a unit and no caption states a result; the units that exist in this paper live inside the artwork (axis titles) or inside two cells of the section 4.2 symbol grid ('(Unit: million)', '(Unit: ten billion USD)') [caption.units].
- The drawing is one engine and three idioms: seven of the twelve figures are MATLAB line plots, two are hand-laid transition graphs, two are 2x5 panel plates and one is a pie chart [geometry.family].
- Nothing in the paper is three-dimensional and nothing carries a colour scale - no surf plot, no heatmap, no contour - so every quantity is read off a position against a printed tick, and the twelve figures together contain no key legible at print size [geometry.three_d].
- Keys are rare and never inside the frame: two figures (7 and 8) key their twenty-six curves in a bordered box placed OUTSIDE the plotting area at the upper right, seven figures (1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 11, 12) label their marks inline, and the other three carry no key and no labels - a single-trace plot and the two plates whose panels are titled instead [geometry.legend].
- Panel grammar has exactly two states: a single frame, or a 2x5 grid of ten titled panels in the two appendix plates. Only those two use panels, they do not share axes - every panel repeats its own 'Time/year' and 'Death rate/%' titles - and no panel letter (a)/(b) exists anywhere in the paper [geometry.panel_count].
- Shared axes are used zero times: every multi-panel exhibit repeats its full axis labels panel by panel, and every multi-curve exhibit carries one y scale for all curves, which is why the eighteen bottom curves of Figures 7 and 8 merge into a single band below 350 million [geometry.shared_axes].
- Printed values are absent from every figure - no data labels, no annotated points, no value strip under the plots: the only printed numbers in the artwork are axis ticks, so the three figures that matter most (4, 7, 8) present their twenty-six curves as indistinguishable hairlines [geometry.printed_numbers].
- Every grid in the paper is booktabs: three horizontal rules (top, below the header, bottom), no vertical rule at any cell boundary, no outer box, no zebra shading and no slanted corner cell - the style is invariant across twelve grids, including the four whose content reaches the layout as a raster rather than as vectors [table.rule_style].
- Merged cells appear exactly once: Table 1's 'Language' cell spans both header rows while the four measure labels wrap inside the second row. No other grid merges a cell, and no grid prints a diagonal header [table.merged_cells].
- Header depth is one row for eleven of the twelve grids; only Table 1 needs two, and only the 2017 and 2067 office grids add a body band - a rule above their 'Accumulated scores' totals row - which is the sole footer structure in the paper [table.header_rows].
- Grid width splits by function, not by size: the single comparison grid (Table 1) is set at about 60 percent of the measure while the nineteen-row economic table (Table 8) and the seventeen-row symbol grid run the full measure, so the paper lets its widest tables run wide and only the one it wants read against prose wrap [table.width].
- Decoration is absent on the grid side as completely as on the figure side: no grid is striped, none is turned into landscape, none continues onto a second page, and not one of the twelve prints a source line or a table note that reaches the text layer - the only note-like row in the paper is the closing line of the section 4.2 grid, and it is inside the raster [table.note_sentence].
- Announcement is same-page for four cited rows and one page early for five: Figures 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 are named in the prose on the page before their artwork prints, while Figures 1, 2, 7 and Table 1 are cited on their own page, so this paper's habit is to lead its exhibits by a page [mention.lead_pages].
- Fifteen of the twenty-four rows are never cited from the body at all - Figures 9 to 12 and the eleven uncaptioned or appendix grids - and every one of them sits either at the foot of a section or inside the appendices, reached only by the bare gestures 'See: Apendix B', 'See: Appendix A' and 'the data in 2013 is in Appendix A' [mention.count].
- Citation vocabulary is a parenthetical rotation: eight of the nine cited rows are introduced by '(Figure N)' or '(Table N)' glued to the end of a sentence, and only Figure 3 gets a full as-shown-in construction ('Example graph ... is shown below (Figure 3)') [mention.lead_pattern].
- Placement follows the argument down the page: thirteen rows sit in the top third, seven in the middle band and four at the foot, and the four foot cases are the third panel of the appendix plate, the section 2.1.1 symbol grid that closes its page and two grids that fill the bottom half of a page of tables [place.page_zone].
- Exhibit density is where the work is: six model rows, one result row, two sensitivity runs and three symbol grids fill sections 2 and 3, four uncaptioned grids belong to the application section and its Memo, and the appendices hold eight rows - four grids and four plates - while the introduction, the strengths section and the references carry nothing at all [place.section_kind].
