# P-A-2011-9582-99696cfc — distilled rules

- a caption is quoted as the page sets it, so a bare printed label carries numbering Figure N and a label with a full stop carries Figure N. [caption.numbering]
- a panel measured as its own region still carries the shared caption of its exhibit, so the panel count lives in [geometry.panel_count] and the shared caption in caption.verbatim rather than in eleven different caption strings. [geometry.panel_count]
- an exhibit printed without any caption keeps caption_source none and zero caption.words instead of borrowing the nearest paragraph or heading. [caption.words]
- a colour-field panel with no key is recorded as heatmap-or-contour and its missing key is stated by a false [geometry.colorbar], not by widening the family. [geometry.colorbar]
- whether numeric ticks print decides if a crop is legible on its own, so it is recorded per exhibit in [geometry.printed_numbers]. [geometry.printed_numbers]
- a legend is recorded only where a key box keys series, so the single in-frame key of Figure 29 panel (c) is inside and every other plot in this paper is none. [geometry.legend]
- panels of one exhibit that sit on different pages keep their page in place and are ordered by [place.page_order] within the page, which is how the Figure 25 and Figure 30 blocks stay one exhibit across a page break. [place.page_order]
